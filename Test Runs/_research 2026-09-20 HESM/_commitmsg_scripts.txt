intangibles plus equity-method investments, FY2021-25 means, twelve filers, every
cell from that filer's own company facts.

and it carries the dual PP&E tag list that the PAGP fold showed is necessary for
PAA -- while documenting that HESM itself does not need it, so the trap is
filer-specific rather than industry-wide.

The Antero Midstream row is this run's addition to PAGP's list, and it is the row

against HESM's 25.6% on the same metric over the same window.
