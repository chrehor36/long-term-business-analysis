**Definition used, applied identically to all five:**
`NTOA = Total assets − goodwill − intangible assets, net − (cash + all investments) − NIBCL`,
where `NIBCL = accounts payable + accrued liability captions + deferred/unearned revenue
(current + non-current)`.

Two disclosures about the definition, so another analyst can rebuild it:
- **"Other current liabilities"**, a mixed bucket, is **excluded** everywhere, which makes
  NIBCL smaller and NTOA **larger**, that is, the conservative direction given the negative
  result below. Amounts excluded: CRWD FY2026 $68,811K; MSFT FY2026 $26,738M.
- **Deferred tax assets** are left inside total assets as filed. This materially inflates
  NTOA for two peers, PANW FY2025 carrying $2,424.2M and FTNT FY2025 $1,314.9M, and both
  still come out negative anyway.

### Reading it honestly: the negatives are reported, not suppressed
**Four of the five filers carry NEGATIVE net tangible operating assets in both years.** This
is the expected shape and it is not a defect in the arithmetic. These are asset-light
subscription filers whose customers pay in advance, so deferred revenue alone (CRWD $4.75bn;
PANW $12.75bn; FTNT $7.12bn; S $0.63bn at the latest date) exceeds the entire tangible
operating asset base. **The business is funded by its customers.**

The consequence for Q2 is strict and is not dodged here: **an unleveraged return on net
tangible operating assets CANNOT be computed for CRWD, PANW, S or FTNT, because the
denominator is negative, and a "return" on a negative denominator is an artifact rather than
a return.** Dividing CRWD's FY2026 operating loss of $(293,292)K by NTOA of $(1,149,805)K
yields a positive 25.5%, which is arithmetically true and economically meaningless: it is a
loss divided by a negative number. **It is not reported as a return anywhere in this file.**
The correct statement is that this test does not discriminate among the four pure-plays, and
the franchise question must be decided on the margin structure, the retention series and the
competition language instead.

Only MSFT has a positive denominator, and only because $313.1bn of datacenter property and
equipment sits inside it. That makes MSFT's 41.5% non-comparable to the others rather than
better than them: a different asset shape, not a different quality of business. Note also the
direction. MSFT's return on NTOA **fell from 67.7% to 41.5%** across the window as the AI
capex build inflated the denominator faster than operating income grew.

FTNT FY2022 additionally shows **negative total stockholders' equity of $(281.6)M**, the
result of repurchasing $1,991.2M of stock in that year against a $6,228.0M balance sheet.
That is a capital-allocation fact, not a distress signal.

## AUDIT NOTES AND LIMITS OF THIS ROW

1. **PANW window offset, restated.** PANW's latest is FYE 2025-07-31 against CRWD's
   2026-01-31, a **12-month** stagger rather than the roughly 6 months the fiscal calendars
   alone imply, because PANW's FY2026 10-K was not filed as of 2026-09-07. Every PANW-vs-CRWD
   comparison above is therefore CRWD-FY2026 against PANW-FY2025. Re-run when
   `panw-20260731.htm` files.
2. **Cross-checks performed, operator rule 4.** One figure per company verified in the
   **primary 10-K document text**, not only in XBRL or the R-file rendering:
   CRWD `Total revenue 4,812,005` and `Stock-based compensation expense 1,096,679`;
   PANW `Total revenue $9,221.5` and `Net cash provided by operating activities $3,716.0`;
   S `Revenue $1,001,278` and `Total stock-based compensation expense $297,587`;
   FTNT `Revenue $6,799.6` and `Net cash provided by operating activities $2,590.6`;
   MSFT `331,839` and `12,405`. **All ten matched.**
3. **Not like-for-like, and the row says so.** FTNT sells appliances and carries inventory,
   $399.5m at 2025-12-31; CRWD, PANW and S do not. FTNT's 80.5% gross margin and 30.7%
   operating margin are therefore **not** a pure-SaaS comparable. Its 86.8% *service* gross
   margin is the closer read, and it still beats CRWD's 77.7% subscription gross margin.
4. **S is not split.** SentinelOne files a single `Revenue` line with no subscription/services
   split on the face of its income statement, so no subscription gross margin can be computed
   for it. It is not estimated here.
5. **S FY2026 tax anomaly, flagged not explained.** SentinelOne reports a **$171,024K
   provision for income taxes on a pre-tax LOSS of $(279,711)K**, turning a $(279.7)m pre-tax
   loss into a $(450.7)m net loss. The tax-footnote cause was **not read** for this row and is
   out of scope here. Flagged as UNRESEARCHED for the S file, and it is the reason S's net
   income line should not be compared to its operating line without that footnote.
6. **SBC comparability is clean.** All five figures come from the same place, the
   `Stock-based compensation` or `Share-based compensation` add-back in the operating section
   of the audited cash flow statement, over the same fiscal periods as their OCF denominators.
   This is the one metric in the row with no definitional slack, which is why it carries the
   weight.
7. **What this row does NOT establish.** It does not measure market share, win rates, pricing,
   or displacement, none of which is filed by anyone here. A franchise claim built only on
   this row would be built on margins, retention and self-description.

## THE ROW IN ONE LINE

loss**, so the entire GAAP deficit is a fraction of what is paid out in stock.
