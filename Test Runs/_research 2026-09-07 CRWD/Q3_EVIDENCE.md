# 1. PAY VERSUS PERFORMANCE — the target-below-actual test

## 1.1 The annual cash bonus: the Corporate Incentive Plan (CIP)

The CIP is paid **quarterly**, on three metrics. Weightings, thresholds and disclosed
targets, from the three proxies:

| | FY2024 (2024 proxy) | FY2025 (2025 proxy) | FY2026 (2026 proxy) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Non-GAAP operating income weighting | 20% | 30% | 30% |
| Net new ARR weighting | 80% | 70% | 70% |
| Non-GAAP OI funding threshold | 85% | 85% | **80%** (lowered) |
| Net new ARR funding threshold | 80% | 80% | 80% |
| Net Retention Rate modifier band | — | 115%–120% | **110%–115%** (lowered) |

**Disclosed targets and disclosed achievement:**

| Metric | FY2024 | FY2025 | FY2026 |

## 1.2 THE TEST: was the new year's target set BELOW the prior year's actual?

**Actual net new ARR, from the 10-K Item 7 (MD&A), verbatim:**

- FY2023 10-K: *"ARR increased 48% year-over-year and grew to $2.6 billion as of January 31, 2023, of which **$828.4 million** was net new ARR added during fiscal 2023."*
- FY2024 10-K: *"ARR increased 34% year-over-year and grew to $3.4 billion as of January 31, 2024, of which **$875.5 million** was net new ARR added during fiscal 2024."*
- FY2025 10-K: *"ARR increased 23% year-over-year and grew to $4.2 billion as of January 31, 2025, of which **$806.7 million** was net new ARR added during fiscal 2025."*
- FY2026 10-K: *"ARR increased 24% year-over-year and grew to $5.3 billion as of January 31, 2026, of which **$1.0 billion** was net new ARR added during fiscal 2026."*

### FINDING 1A — the FY2025 net new ARR target was set BELOW the FY2024 actual. **HIT.**

| | $M |
|---|---|
| FY2024 **actual** net new ARR (FY2024 10-K) | 875.5 |
| FY2025 **target** net new ARR (2025 proxy, aggregate of quarterly targets) | **775.0** |
| Target minus prior-year actual | **−100.5 (−11.5%)** |
| FY2025 actual net new ARR | 806.7 |
| Disclosed FY2025 achievement on the metric | 104.1% |
| CEO FY2025 bonus paid | 106.0% of target, $1,325,064 |

The FY2025 CIP net new ARR target — carrying **70% of the whole cash bonus** — was set
11.5% below what the company had actually delivered in FY2024. The company then delivered
LESS net new ARR in FY2025 ($806.7M) than in FY2024 ($875.5M), a **year-over-year decline
of 7.9%**, and the CEO's cash bonus was paid at **106.0% of target**. The target reset is
what converted a declining year into an above-target payout.

### FINDING 1B — the disclosed CIP achievement percentage cannot be reconciled to the disclosed target. Three years running, non-GAAP operating income came in BELOW the aggregate of the quarterly targets, and the plan paid ABOVE 100% on that metric.

| Fiscal year | Aggregate of quarterly non-GAAP OI targets (proxy) | Actual non-GAAP OI (PvP table col. (i), current definition) | Actual ÷ aggregate target | Disclosed "Average of Quarterly Achievement Percentages" |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FY2024 | $715.6M | $679.6M | **95.0%** | **105.4%** |
| FY2025 | $886.5M | $879.9M | **99.3%** | **108.6%** |
| FY2026 | $1,146.4M | $1,046.5M | **91.3%** | **105.0%** |

**The mechanism is disclosed and is the explanation.** The proxy states the payout is the
*"Average of Quarterly Achievement Percentages"* — an **unweighted** average of four
quarterly percentages. CrowdStrike's operating income is heavily back-loaded (Q4 FY2026
non-GAAP operating income was $325.8M of the $1,046.5M full year, 31% of it — ER 2026-03-03).
An unweighted average of quarterly percentages therefore gives the small Q1 the same
weight as the large Q4, so a plan can miss the full-year aggregate target by 8.7% and
still record 105.0% "achievement." **The proxy never discloses the actual result against
the aggregate target it discloses**, so the arithmetic from target to payout is not
checkable from the proxy alone.

This is a structural asymmetry, not an accusation of misstatement — but it is the single
most important thing in the CrowdStrike pay disclosure, and it recurs in all three years
examined.

### FINDING 1C — the PSU performance band is set EXACTLY equal to the company's own public guidance range. **Exact match, to the dollar and the cent.**

2026 proxy, *"Fiscal 2026 PSU Targets and Results"*:

| Metric | Min — Max Achievement Range | Fiscal 2026 Actual Achievement |
|---|---|---|
| Non-GAAP EPS | **$3.33 – $3.45** | $3.75 |
| Revenue ($M) | **$4,743.5 – $4,805.5** | $4,812.1 |
| Overall payout as a percentage of target | — | **200%** |

ER 2025-03-04 (8-K, accession 0001535527-25-000005), *"Financial Outlook"*, Full Year FY26 Guidance:

> Total revenue | **$4,743.5 - $4,805.5 million**
> Non-GAAP net income per share attributable to CrowdStrike common stockholders, diluted | **$3.33 - $3.45**

**The PSU threshold-to-maximum band IS the guidance range, identical on both metrics.**
The 50%-of-target threshold is the low end of guidance; the **200%-of-target maximum is the
high end of guidance**. Executives earn the maximum PSU payout by beating the top of a
guidance range that management itself sets and publishes. Section 4 below shows CrowdStrike
has beaten the high end of its revenue guidance in **every one of the last 10 quarters**.

Two further points on the same table:
- The revenue band is **1.31% wide** ($4,743.5M to $4,805.5M). A 1.31% move in revenue
  spans the entire distance from 50% payout to 200% payout. Actual revenue landed
  **0.14% above the maximum**.
- For FY2026 the Compensation Committee **raised the threshold payout from 25% to 50%** of
  target, stating: *"For fiscal 2026, we increased the PSU threshold payout from 25% to 50%
  of target for both metrics to align with competitive market practices observed in our peer
  group analysis. This change creates more meaningful incentive value at threshold
  performance while our underlying revenue and non-GAAP EPS performance requirements remain
  equally rigorous."* (2026 proxy)

**Band width over three years — the band has been narrowed sharply:**

| | Revenue metric band | Non-GAAP EPS band | Payout |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY2024 (2024 proxy) | Revenue Growth Percentage **30% — 50%** (20 points wide) | $2.21 — $2.61 | 154.4% (actual RGP 36.3%, EPS $3.16) |
| FY2025 (2025 proxy) | Revenue Growth Percentage **28% — 32%** (4 points wide) | $3.77 — $4.15 | 75.6% (actual RGP 29.3%, EPS $3.95) |
| FY2026 (2026 proxy) | Revenue **$4,743.5M — $4,805.5M** (≈1.3% wide, ≈1.6 points of growth) | $3.33 — $3.45 | **200%** (actual $4,812.1M, EPS $3.75) |

The 2026 proxy explains the change of unit: *"For fiscal 2026, we transitioned from
expressing our revenue growth goal as a percentage to an absolute figure. As our revenue
base has grown, absolute dollar targets better distinguish between achievement levels and
align with our operating plan."*

**Caveat, stated honestly:** the FY2026 EPS maximum of $3.45 is nominally below the FY2025
PSU "actual achievement" of $3.95, which would be a second target-below-actual hit. But
CrowdStrike changed its non-GAAP methodology for FY2026 (ER 2025-03-04 carries a section
headed *"Changes in Presentation of Non-GAAP Measures"*), and the FY2026 earnings release
recasts FY2025 non-GAAP diluted EPS as **$3.24**, not $3.95 (ER 2026-03-03: *"Non-GAAP net
income attributable to CrowdStrike per share, diluted, was $3.73, compared to $3.24 in
fiscal 2025."*). Against the recast $3.24 base the FY2026 band of $3.33–$3.45 sits above
prior-year actual. **The EPS leg of the target-below-actual test is therefore
NOT CLEAN and I do not claim it.** The net new ARR hit in Finding 1A is clean; the
guidance-equals-band finding in 1C is clean.

## 1.3 FY2026 Net Retention Rate, verbatim (2026 proxy)

> "For fiscal 2026, our quarterly Net Retention Rate target range was 110% – 115%. Our Net
> Retention Rate was 112%, 111%, 114%, and 115% for the first, second, third, and fourth
> quarter of fiscal 2026, respectively, falling within our target range, and therefore no
> modifier was applied to the final payout under the CIP."

And the reason the band was lowered from 115%–120%:

> "Net Retention Rate Range: We adjusted the target range to 110% – 115% to reflect the
> expected performance dynamics of our expanding customer base. As companies scale, net
> retention rates typically moderate from early-stage levels, and this range reflects
> challenging but achievable targets that maintain our focus on best-in-class customer
> retention and expansion."

2025 proxy, for contrast:

> "For fiscal 2025, our quarterly Net Retention Rate target range was 115% – 120%. Our Net
> Retention Rate was 119%, 118%, 115%, and 112% for the first, second, third, and fourth
> quarter of fiscal 2025, respectively, falling within our target range, other than with
> respect to the fourth quarter."

**The band was moved down to 110%–115% after the metric fell out of the bottom of the old
band in Q4 FY2025.** The Q4 FY2025 reading of 112% would have been a miss under the FY2025
band; it is mid-range under the FY2026 band.
