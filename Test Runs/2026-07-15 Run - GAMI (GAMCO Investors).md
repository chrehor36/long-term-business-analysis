### The real finding: executive compensation and related-party structure
The original run's Gate 3 said alignment was "about as tight as it gets." **That
was too quick.** From the 2026 proxy statement (Summary Compensation Table for
2025):

**Mario Gabelli's total 2025 compensation was $28,694,119** — **40.6% of the
company's entire 2025 net income**, paid via a formula in place since the 1999
IPO: 10% of aggregate annual pre-tax profits as an "incentive management fee"
($5.5M of the total, after reallocating pieces to other executives), PLUS
separate "Portfolio Manager and Other Variable Remuneration" (~$23.2M) for
running specific funds/accounts personally. He draws no salary, bonus, or stock
awards — the entire package is profit-linked, which aligns incentives with
performance but also means minority shareholders share economics with him twice:
once through his ~80% equity stake, and again through this compensation layer
that comes off the top before reported net income exists at all.

**Related-party relationships, all disclosed but worth naming as a pattern, not
individually dismissing:**
- GAMCO has leased its ~60,000 sq ft Rye, NY headquarters since 1997 from **M4E,
  LLC, owned by Gabelli family members** (2025 rent: $1,548,351).
- Sub-leases London office space from **AC (Associated Capital Group)**, a
  separate public company majority-owned by the same Holdings entity that
  controls GAMCO ($366,078 paid in 2025).
- Provides administrative/management services to **Teton** (spun off from GAMCO
  in 2009) under a continuing services agreement.
- **The controlling ownership vehicle itself was restructured twice in the last
  ~6 weeks of the data window**: GGCP Holdings → GGCP (Dec 29, 2025) → a newly
  created LLC, "GAMCO Gabelli Asset Management Company LLC" ("GGAM"), majority
  owned/controlled by Gabelli (Jan 14, 2026). As of Dec 31, 2025: **92% of voting
  power, ~80% of equity interest** — both up from the 2023 figures (93%/74%) and
  well above the 2017 figures (91%/63%) used in the original run. **The rationale
  for this rapid restructuring was not found in the documents reviewed** — flagged
  as an open question, not a conclusion either way.

**Mitigant found on key-person risk:** the original run understated the bench.
Gabelli (84) is Chairman/Founder and Co-CEO, but **Douglas R. Jamieson is also
Co-CEO** (2025 comp $4.4M) and **Christopher Marangi is President and
Co-Chief Investment Officer** — there is a real, named operating leadership
layer beyond Gabelli alone, not just family board seats. Key-person risk remains
real (his personal brand and 45%+ voting/economic dominance are irreducible) but
"no named successor" was not quite accurate.

**No dedicated Legal Proceedings item with case-level disclosure was found** in
either the proxy or the OTC-K filing (only generic forward-looking-statement
boilerplate uses that phrase) — read as "nothing material to disclose currently,"
not confirmed as a clean record with certainty, since absence of a section is not
the same as an audited attestation of no litigation.

### Revised Gate 3 verdict
**PASS still stands, but Alignment (Dimension 3) is downgraded from "tight" to
"formulaic and disclosed, but extractive at scale."** The compensation structure
and related-party web are not integrity violations (Dimension 1: everything found
was properly disclosed in the proxy, which is itself a candor point in GAMCO's
favor) — but they represent a real, quantified agency cost that the original
run's quick "owner-operator, aligned" read missed. This is exactly the kind of
thing a superficial pass would wave through and a real dig catches.

### Revised overall verdict
**Unchanged in direction, strengthened in reasoning:** eligible for a position on
price (now on cleaner, primary-source-verified numbers), but sized deliberately
below what the margin of safety alone would justify — not only for key-person
concentration (as originally stated) but now explicitly **also for the ~41%-of-NI
compensation extraction and the recent, unexplained restructuring of the control
vehicle.** Two genuinely new items for the thesis-breaking watchlist: (1) any
further increase in Gabelli's take as a percentage of NI, (2) clarification of
the GGCP → GGAM restructuring's purpose, if it ever becomes public.
