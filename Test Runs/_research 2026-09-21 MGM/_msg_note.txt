MGM fold: record why the untracking needed a second commit

Corrects one sentence in the MGM narrative fold. It said the deletions and the widened
gitignore patterns were both in the fold commit; the patterns are in 43f0276 and the
thirteen deletions are in 37aceb7, because `git commit -- <pathspec>` takes working-tree
content rather than the index and therefore cannot record a deletion for a file that is
still on disk - it re-added all thirteen. Written up as a gap in the fold's step 6 rather
than glossed: a pathspec isolates your commit from other sessions, it does not isolate the
repository from you, and it cannot express a deletion at all.
