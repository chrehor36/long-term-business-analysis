MGM: the fold verifier, and its own case-sensitivity bug

verify_fold.py reads all six fold steps back from disk independently: the register
count and MGM's position in it, the roster line count, the narrative fold and its
contents, the absence of any alerts.json band or PORTFOLIO.md row, the acceptance
test, and the tree's cleanliness. It reported one FAIL on its first run and the
fail was its own: it looked for a lowercase "shape #13" where the fold writes
"Shape #13 THE TENANT". Fixed and re-run; all checks PASS. Recorded rather than
quietly corrected, because a checker that returns a plausible wrong answer is the
defect class this project keeps finding in its own tooling.
