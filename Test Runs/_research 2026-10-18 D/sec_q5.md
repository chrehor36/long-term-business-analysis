## Q5 — WHO RUNS IT: able, honest, in love with the business, the same after being paid. STOP on integrity.
Robert M. Blue has been President and CEO since October 2020 and Chair since April 2021; Steven D. Ridge CFO since November 2022 (FY2025 10-K, executive officers). The board has an independent lead director, Susan N. Story (DEFM14A, background).

- **The first yardstick: the record against the hand dealt** **[M1994-008]**. The hand was hard: Blue took over in the year the Atlantic Coast Pipeline was cancelled ($2,405M loss, 2020) and the gas transmission business was sold to BHE; the company carried the SCANA legacy of $2.0 billion of refunds over twenty years (FY2019 10-K); and the Virginia legislature rewrote the rate rules in 2023. What was done with it: the gas utilities were sold in 2024 for $9.2 billion of cash plus $4.9 billion of debt assumed (Item 1), at a loss on Questar ("a charge of $236 million ($231 million after-tax) primarily for an impairment of associated goodwill", Note 3); half of CVOW was sold to Stonepeak; the credit ratings were held at BBB+ (S&P, Fitch) and Baa2 (Moody's, outlook Negative). The return on equity of 2021-2025 averaged 8.3% against Southern's 11.9% and NextEra's 13.0% (Q2), and the deciding part earned 7.77% against 9.70% allowed in 2023-24 (Note 13). The board's own pay committee scored the result against peers: the 2023 performance grant "paid at 46.5% of target primarily due to missed relative TSR goal (CEO performance-based stock award did not pay out)" (DEF 14A 2026, `0001193125-26-115180`).
- **The second yardstick: how they treat the owners** **[M1994-009]**. "read the proxy statements [...] see how they treat themselves versus how they treat the shareholders" **[M1994-009]**. Blue's SEC total pay was $6.28M (2023), $12.90M (2024) and $16.04M (2025), and realized pay $5.99M, $6.51M and $9.09M (Summary Compensation Table); the CEO's stock award that depended on relative TSR paid nothing, which is pay following results down. Against that: one-time bonuses and restricted stock "in connection with the company’s business review" to three officers (2025), a review that consisted of selling businesses; and the merger. On a change in control with termination, the merger proxy puts Blue's potential payments at **$72,793,216** (DEFM14A, golden parachute table, assuming a July 9, 2026 close), and some officers' restricted stock "will single-trigger vest pro rata upon the closing". At the September 3, 2026 special meeting the merger itself passed 671,317,253 to 8,566,156, but the advisory vote on the merger pay passed only 392,021,223 to 287,047,146 (8-K `0001193125-26-381919`): 42% of the votes cast against.
- **The habits carried from Q4** (operating earnings featured and used for pay and dividends; annual EPS guidance and a 5% to 7% long-term operating EPS growth promise). The proxy's letter opens: "In 2025, we reached our financial goals, delivered on our forecasts and retained our strong credit profile". The rows: "Managers that always promise to "make the numbers" will at some point be tempted to make up the numbers" **[L2002-041]**; guidance is "the scourge" that encourages managers "to "hit the number."" **[L2019-006]**. Read with the rest of the record **[M2004-067]**: in the filings read I found no instance of numbers made up. The one misstatement of the span was disclosed by the company itself: "during the second quarter of 2025, the company and Dominion Energy Virginia identified misstatements in their previously issued consolidated financial statements related to income taxes associated with investments held within their qualified nuclear decommissioning trusts", judged not material and corrected by revision, and the pay committee found nothing to claw back (DEF 14A 2026). No investigation, enforcement action or penalty against the company or its officers was found in the FY2025 10-K (text search of the 10-K for subpoena, investigation, Department of Justice, grand jury, consent order and civil penalty: the only hit is an environmental-site sentence).
- **How they talk about mistakes** **[L2024-003]**. A text search for "mistake" in the FY2025 10-K, the 2026 proxy and the February 2026 earnings release found no instance. The proxy's opening pages read as "the product of some investor relations department" **[M2007-083]**: highlights, "constructive regulatory outcomes", the forecast delivered. The pipeline, the Questar loss and the earned-ROE shortfall are reported in the notes, not discussed as errors.
- **Love of the business, and after the sale.** For a hired management of a listed company the test is read from the reports: is it "telling us about the things that we would want to know about if we owned a hundred percent of the company" **[M1998-036]**? The board did not shop the company (it "determined that outreach to potential alternative parties was not merited at this time, as Dominion Energy was not being offered for sale") but, once approached, it pressed NextEra for a higher premium and customer commitments, and ran a second bidder, Party A, whose offer "remained materially below the NextEra Energy proposed premium" (DEFM14A). After the merger Blue would join NextEra's board, and the management he leads would no longer answer to Dominion's owners; the "same after being paid" question is the one the golden-parachute vote put, and 42% of the votes cast answered it no.

- **VERDICT on integrity: IN.** "If you’ve got doubts, forget it" **[M2013-088]**: my doubts are about ability and about a manner of speaking to owners (the adjusted figure, the guidance, and the forecasts the proxy says were delivered: **[L2002-041]**, **[L2019-006]**), not about honesty; the filings read show no deception, a self-reported immaterial misstatement, pay that fell when relative results fell, and a board that bargained with two bidders (DEF 14A 2026, DEFM14A). The habits stay a weight against. **Ability WEIGHS AGAINST**: on the record against a hard hand **[M1994-008]**, returns below two of three comparables and below the allowed return, a pipeline write-off and a goodwill loss on a sale, and a management whose talk is of forecasts met rather than of mistakes **[L2024-003]**; "if the CEO's talents or motives are suspect, today's value must be discounted" **[L2010-002]**, which is carried to Q7 as certainty, not as a second discount.
