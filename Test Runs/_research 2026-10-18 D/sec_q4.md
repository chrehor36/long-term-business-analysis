**What moved and why** ("what the figures are saying and what they don’t say" **[M2025-032]**):
- **Equity against goodwill.** Equity doubled from $14.6 billion to $29.1 billion, but not from earnings: retained earnings fell from $6,854M to $2,318M over the decade. The equity came from share issuance, above all the all-stock SCANA combination of 2019 (goodwill up $2.5 billion and regulatory assets up $5.0 billion that year, the NND legacy), and from at-the-market sales since. Dividends exceeded earnings over the span; the 2020 loss (pipeline write-off) and the 2022 impairments ate retained earnings.
- **Goodwill fell** from $8,946M (2019) to $4,143M (2022 onward) as the gas businesses were impaired and sold; what is left is the SCANA and Virginia goodwill.
- **Debt** rose from $35.1 billion to $48.9 billion (+39%) while basic shares rose 44% (2015 to 2025, Q2); debt to debt plus equity was 70.6% in 2016 and 62.7% in 2025, the improvement bought with shares. In 2023 the debt due within a year was $6,589M, high because of the 364-day term loans that bridged the gas sales (Item 7, 2023 cash flows).
- **Receivables against sales.** Customer receivables rose 66% (2016 to 2025) against operating revenue up 41% ($11,737M to $16,506M); the 2025 jump ($862M in the cash-flow statement) sits with a 14% rise in revenue and higher fuel costs. Deferred fuel under-recovery swung operating cash by -$939M (2021), -$2,021M (2022), +$975M (2023), +$727M (2024), -$754M (2025): a working-capital item the averages must carry, not a recurring earnings item.
- **Cash** is a few hundred million throughout; the company runs on commercial paper ($2,035M at 2025-12-31) and the capital markets (Item 7, Credit Facilities).
