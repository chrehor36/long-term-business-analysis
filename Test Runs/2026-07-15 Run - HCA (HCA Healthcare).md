The OE figures alone look healthy (lowest tier $5,116.8M, FY2023) — but
this run does **not** treat that as sufficient to clear the Fortress test,
because of what the balance sheet and debt notes show:

- **Stockholders' equity is negative and rapidly worsening**: -$1,774M
  (FY2023) → -$2,499M (FY2024) → **-$6,027M (FY2025)** — the deficit
  roughly tripled in two years, not a stable or improving trend.
- **Total indebtedness was $46.492 billion** as of December 31, 2025 (HCA's
  own 10-K, verbatim), against $6.97B of FY2025 OE (or $5.1B at the lowest
  verified tier) — a debt load an order of magnitude larger, in absolute
  terms, than any other name run today.
- **No "investment grade" language appears anywhere in the risk-factor or
  debt-note sections searched this pass** — consistent with HCA's known
  history (public record, not independently re-verified this pass) as a
  post-LBO company that has carried a high-yield/non-investment-grade-
  adjacent capital structure as a permanent feature since its 2011 re-IPO,
  not a temporary condition.
- **Available liquidity**: $5.779B under the senior unsecured credit
  facility (HCA's own 10-K), a real and substantial cushion — but this run
  could not determine, in the time available, the **near-term (≤24 month)
  debt maturity schedule** — precisely the data point Ruling 2's survival
  test requires ("undrawn liquidity vs. near-term maturities... termed-out
  vs. cliff-concentrated schedule") to distinguish "large but manageable,
  well-laddered debt" from "large and dangerously concentrated debt."

**This is a genuine, first-order Gate 4 question, not a peripheral data
gap** (unlike, e.g., an unverified CEO biography elsewhere in today's
batch) — the framework's Fortress test exists specifically to catch a
profitable, well-run operator whose capital structure could still produce
an existential outcome under stress. Per the Operator Protocol, a
CONDITIONAL or unresolved verdict is **not** a pass, and this run does not
manufacture one by assumption.

**VERDICT: UNRESOLVED — RUN STOPS AT GATE 4.** No Gate 5–8 material may be
produced. This is neither a clearance nor an elimination; it is an honest
stop pending the specific maturity-schedule data this pass did not obtain.
