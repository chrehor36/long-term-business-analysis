 Our Company 
 We are a global, diversified, and highly differentiated provider of benchmarks, data, analytics and workflow solutions in the global capital and energy and commodity markets. The capital markets include asset managers, investment banks, commercial banks, insurance companies, exchanges, trading firms and issuers and the energy and commodity markets include producers, consumers, traders and intermediaries within energy, chemicals, shipping, metals, carbon and agriculture. We serve our global customers through a broad range of products and services available through both third-party and proprietary distribution channels. 
 Our operations consist of four reportable segments: S&P Global Ratings, S&P Dow Jones Indices, S&P Global Energy and S&P Global Market Intelligence. 
 Our Strategy 
 We are a global, diversified, and highly differentiated provider of benchmarks, data, analytics and workflow solutions in the global capital and energy and commodity markets. Our mission is Advancing Essential Intelligence.
