## GATE 2 — MOAT
Franchise test: needed/desired — yes, mobile connectivity is essential;
no close substitute — **real, structural**: Japan's mobile market is a
concentrated three-carrier oligopoly with high spectrum/infrastructure
barriers to new entry (a fourth carrier, Rakuten Mobile, entered recently
and has struggled — a real, disclosed, generally-known data point
supporting the barrier-to-entry read, not independently verified in
KDDI's own financials this pass); unregulated — **no**, Japan's telecom
regulator has actively pushed for lower mobile pricing in recent years (a
real, disclosed government-pressure risk, cutting against pure pricing
power). **Class: NARROW** (oligopoly structure, tempered by regulatory
price pressure).
**VERDICT: PASS with NARROW classification** — the most bridge/toll-like
business model in this Japan batch outside the sogo shosha, but genuinely
capped by regulatory involvement, not a clean wide moat.
