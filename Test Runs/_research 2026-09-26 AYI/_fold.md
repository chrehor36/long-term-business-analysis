### The finding
- **[E3-03] criterion (2) fails in the registrant's own words.** FY2015 Item 1: *"highly competitive"*, the largest makers
  *"have over half of the total market share"*, *"the Company also competes with hundreds of manufacturers of varying
  size"*. FY2025 Item 1A: *"competitive pricing pressures may not allow us to pass on any more than the cost increases.
  Alternatively, if costs were to decline, the marketplace may not allow us to hold prices at their current levels."*
- **The price/mix record, stated by the filer for a decade**: about −4 points (FY2010), +2 (FY2011, FY2012), then negative
  every year FY2013-FY2018 (−1 to −3 while volume grew 6-16% a year): *"a reduction in the sales price of certain LED
  luminaries reflecting the continued decline in the cost of purchased LED components"*; FY2018 *"lower pricing on certain
  luminaires as a result of increased competition in portions of the market for more basic, lesser-featured products"*.
  FY2022's increases *"mitigated"* costs while the gross margin fell 80 basis points; FY2024's margin rose 310 basis points
  on *"favorable material and import costs"* with sales down in almost every channel.
- **[E3-43] names the class.** Returns are very high every year (44-82% pre-tax on net tangible operating assets, 15-30% on
  total operating capital including goodwill, FY2008-FY2025), earned while price followed cost: *"a business earns
  exceptional profits only if it is the low-cost operator ... With superior management, a company may maintain its status
  as a low-cost operator for a much longer time, but even then unceasingly faces the possibility of competitive attack."*
- **The competitor row is the strongest evidence against, and it is recorded in full**: LSI Lighting −5% to 13%
  (FY2009-FY2026), Cree Lighting loss-making before its 2019 sale, Philips Lighting −4.7% to 9.2% (2009-2015), Signify
  adjusted EBITA 8.9-11.6% (2021-2025, IR site, flagged), Hubbell C&I Lighting 3.4-9.9% (sold 2022 at 0.69 times sales),
  Eaton Lighting sold 2020 at 0.875 times sales; Acuity 9.3-15.0% every year. Answered: it shows the best operator, not a
  price-setter; the peers sell interchangeable fixtures through the same distributors.
- **Position, not only price**: ABL sales $3,548.4M (FY2019) to $3,612.2M (FY2025) through the 2022 price increases; retail
  $273.0M to $170.7M, corporate accounts $312.8M to $156.7M; Home Depot, once *"approximately 10% or more"* of sales, no
  longer named.
