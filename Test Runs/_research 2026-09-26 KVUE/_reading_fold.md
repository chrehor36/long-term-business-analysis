
## UPDATE 2026-09-26 - KVUE: Q2 OUT, and the quote is a merger spread. Kenvue is J&J's former consumer-health arm (Tylenol, Zyrtec, Listerine, Neutrogena, Band-AID), agreed to be bought by Kimberly-Clark for $3.50 cash plus 0.14625 Kimberly-Clark shares a share, with the close expected in the fourth quarter of 2026; at $17.80 it trades 0.5% below the consideration, so the price says nothing about Kenvue's owner earnings. The business question was run anyway and failed at the company level.
- **What the filings show.** The Self Care names carry real franchise evidence in the filer's own figures: in 2022 store brands took only about 19% of global pain care and 27% of allergy care against Tylenol and Zyrtec, the same molecules at a lower price, and the segment earns about a third of its sales before depreciation. The company does not show a franchise: in the three years it has filed as a standalone company it raised price 10.7% and sold 6.1% fewer units, with volume negative every year; in 2025 it cut prices ("strategic price investments") and still lost units; skin care lost share in the filer's own words and its margin fell from 15.5% to 11.6%; segment profit fell from $3,989M to $3,762M in nominal dollars; and the business sold $15.1bn in 2025 against J&J consumer's $15.8bn in 2009. That is the Precision Steel shape [E4-55], and the Clorox run's.
- **Refuted priors.** The screen's blank `deal_note` (a live merger), its `cap_flag` (cap and float fourteen months apart, neither wrong), and its `wc_note` (the "2022 payables" year is fiscal 2021, and what moved the cash was $3.2bn of US talc payments that J&J kept, not payables). The screen's band ($1,191-1,687M) reproduces on both ends.
- **What a reader should take.** The store-brand maker (Perrigo) earns about 3% margins while the brand earns 58% gross: the brand margin over the molecule is wide. It has not protected the units. If the merger closes, the business lives inside Kimberly-Clark, and a future Kimberly-Clark run should read the combined unit series before admitting it.
