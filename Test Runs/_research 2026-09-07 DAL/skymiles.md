# SKYMILES AND THE AMERICAN EXPRESS RELATIONSHIP — THE TWO-BUSINESS TEST
### Working paper for the DAL run of 2026-09-07. **No verdict is drawn in this file.**

The brief's instruction: *"Delta discloses the Amex remuneration figure annually. Find it,
quote it, and test whether it can be separated. The UAL run established that no audited
standalone loyalty-program financial statements exist — do not assume Delta is different;
verify."*

---

## 1. DOES AN AUDITED STANDALONE SET OF SKYMILES FINANCIALS EXIST? **NO. VERIFIED THREE WAYS.**

**(a) EDGAR full-text search.** `efts.sec.gov` for `"SkyMiles IP Ltd"` returns 27 documents,
all filed by Delta Air Lines, Inc. (CIK 0000027904). The only ones carrying standalone
financial data are the two exhibits to the **Form 8-K filed 2020-09-14, accession
0001193125-20-244688**:
- Exhibit 99.1 `d27099dex991.htm` — "Delta Air Lines SkyMiles Investor Presentation,
  September 14, 2020"
- Exhibit 99.2 `d27099dex992.htm` — the press release announcing the offering
The remaining 25 hits are subsidiary lists (Exhibit 21.1 of successive 10-Ks) and a credit
agreement exhibit to the 10-Q of 2026-07-10.

**(b) The presentation says so itself.** Exhibit 99.1, "Important Notice and Disclaimer":

> *"Financial Information — **Pro forma financial information included in this Presentation is
> provided for informational purposes only, has not been prepared in accordance with the
> standards for such information established by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission**
> and may not be an indication of the financial condition or results of operations of Sky Miles
> IP Ltd. in the future."*

And the summary financial page: *"Amounts represent SMHL **Unaudited** Pro Forma Consolidated
Financial Information."* And the cash-flow mechanics page: *"**To be created.** Pro forma for
financing transaction."* And every cash-sales figure: *"Cash sales are pro forma for sale of
miles to Delta that **would have occurred had the structure been in place** in 2019."*

**(c) SkyMiles IP Ltd is not an EDGAR registrant.** Exhibit 99.2: *"SkyMiles IP Ltd. **a newly
formed Cayman Islands exempted company** incorporated with limited liability and an **indirect
wholly-owned subsidiary of Delta**."* An EDGAR company-name search
(`browse-edgar?action=getcompany&company=skymiles`) returns an **empty result set**. There is
no filer, so there is no audit report, so there is nothing to read.

**The brief's warning was correct and it now generalises across the two largest US carriers:
neither Delta nor United has ever published audited standalone loyalty financial statements.
What exists in both cases is an unaudited pro-forma exhibit prepared to sell Rule 144A debt.**

---

## 2. WHAT THE 2020 PRESENTATION DOES DISCLOSE (Ex. 99.1, all figures pro forma and unaudited)

- *"In 2019, SkyMiles IP Ltd. ('SMIP'), an indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of Delta, would
  have generated **~$6.1 billion of cash sales** and **$2.4 billion of net cash from
  operations**, on a pro forma basis."*
- *"Mileage Sales: **$6.1B**. Redemption and Travel Benefits Provided: **$3.6B**. Small amount
  of non-air travel redemption (~3%)."*
- *"**In 2019, 97% of redemptions were on Delta**, allowing the flexibility to manage costs by
  modifying inventory levels and value."*
- *"SkyMiles can **adjust the redemption value of miles based on demand** on any given day …
  **Dynamic pricing model** adjusts the redemption value of miles based on demand strength or
  weakness, seasonality and other factors."*
- The mechanism: *"Delta pays SMIP for each mile earned by Members"*; *"**SMIP purchases seats
  from Delta**"*; *"**Perpetual operating agreement** from closing"*; *"Amex co-brand agreement
  to 2029"*; *"Average 1 to 3 year partner contracts (for partners excluding Amex)."*
- *"**Amex remuneration to Delta in 2019 totals $4.1 billion**, up 21% from 2018 and 116% from
  2014 … **Amex remuneration to Delta in 2014 totals $2.0 billion**."*
- *"The Delta co-brand credit card portfolio represents ~**8% of Amex customer card spending**
  and ~**22% of card loans** in 2019."*
- *"In 2019, **SkyMiles Members contributed 60%+ of Delta's ticket revenue** with premiums
  reaching 1.5x higher for Medallion Members … Current average tenure of 16 years for Medallion
  Members."*
- Adjusted Cash Sales for Pre-Purchase, 2015-2019: **$3,094 / $3,211 / $3,337 / $3,662 /
  $3,824**; *"2011-2019 CAGR of ~9%, 2015-2019 CAGR of ~12%."*
- Prior Amex pre-purchases of miles: $1bn in 2008 (amortised 2012-2014); $675M annually in
  December 2011, 2012, 2013 and 2014.
- *"Members to earn miles with **69 partners**, and redeem miles with **32 partners**."*
  *"SkyMiles launched in 1981, has **over 100 million total Members**."*

The offering itself (Ex. 99.2): Delta and SkyMiles IP Ltd. as **co-issuers** of senior secured
notes plus a term loan, *"expected to be $6.5 billion"* in aggregate, *"secured on a pari passu
senior basis by a **first-priority security interest in Delta's SkyMiles program**, including
Delta's rights under certain related agreements, intellectual property, and other collateral."*
The financing ultimately priced at $9.0 billion.

**FY2025 10-K, Item 1:** *"In 2025, **12% of revenue miles flown on Delta were from award
travel**, as program members redeemed miles in the loyalty program for approximately **35
million award tickets**. Our most significant and valuable contract to sell miles relates to our
co-brand credit card relationship with American Express. In 2025, **remuneration from American
Express totaled $8.2 billion, which we expect to grow to $10 billion over the next few
years**."*

**Guidance history of the $10bn target:** the FY2023 10-K said Amex remuneration *"totaled $6.8
billion, which we expect to increase by 10% in 2024 and grow to $10 billion **over the
long-term**."* The FY2025 10-K says *"over the next few years."* The number has not moved; the
horizon has shortened.

## 5. THE ENCUMBRANCE, AT 2025-12-31

| | $m |
|---|---:|
| SkyMiles Notes (4.75%+, maturing 2026-2028) | 3,422 |
| SkyMiles Term Loan (SOFR + 1.50%, maturing October 2028) | 588 |
| **Total secured on the SkyMiles programme** | **4,010** |

Covenants (Note 6 and Item 1A): a **$2.0 billion minimum-liquidity covenant**; an aggregate
**$550 million limit on the sale of pre-paid miles**; minimum coverage ratios whose breach
*"could trigger an early amortization event or … require us to post additional collateral"*;
and a restriction on Delta's ability *"to change the policies and procedures of the SkyMiles
program in a manner that would reasonably be expected to materially impair repayment of our
SkyMiles debt."*

In September 2025 Delta amended the SkyMiles Credit Facility: refinanced at SOFR + 1.50%,
maturity extended from October 2027 to October 2028, and **principal amortisation reduced from
20% to 1% per year.**
