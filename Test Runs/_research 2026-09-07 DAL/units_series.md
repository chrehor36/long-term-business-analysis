## 6. CAVEATS

1. **CASM-Ex is Delta's own definition** — fuel, expenses related to third-party refinery
   sales, "and other items." It is not comparable to any other carrier's ex-fuel measure
   (see the UAL file's caveat 1, which stands).
2. **As-filed TRASM includes third-party refinery sales.** Only the adjusted line is used
   for the price test above, for exactly that reason.
3. **Load factor** is reported to one decimal in the FY2019 10-K and to the whole point from
   FY2021 onward. The precision difference is Delta's.
4. **2020 and 2021 rates are pandemic rates**, printed because the brief asked for the test
   to run through 2020, and excluded from every trend claim above, which is stated at each
   window rather than assumed.
5. **PRASM, TRASM, yield, CASM and CASM-Ex are ratios Delta computes and files.** They are
   transcribed, not recomputed. Derived figures (real values, percentage changes) are
   labelled as such.
6. **The 2017 operating-income basis differs by source.** `sources.annual()` returns FY2017
   operating income of $6,114M on revenue of $41,244M (as originally filed); the FY2019
   10-K comparative, after the ASC 606 full-retrospective restatement, carries $5,966M on
   $41,138M. Section 4 uses the latter for consistency with the peer table. On the former,
   the 2017-19 real mean is $7,834M and 2025 is 25.7% below it instead of 23.8%. The
   direction does not move; the basis is disclosed because **[E4-38]** requires it.
