- Real price per acre 2016→2025: **~+43%** (~4.1%/yr real); 2016→2024 peak ~+63% real.
- Wobbles named: 2020 (pandemic), 2022 (rate shock, volume trough 322.7 acres), 2025
  (nominal $/acre −10% on Summerlin superpad mix while volume +39%).
- Cross-checks: FY2024 figures in FY2024 10-K ($441,044k / 445.4 / $990) tie exactly to
  the FY2025 10-K comparative column. 2018 figures tie between FY2018 and FY2020 10-Ks.
- Caveat: 2016-2020 include The Woodlands residential (now sold out); Summerlin custom
  lots skew $/acre upward in small-volume years; series is total-company as filed.
