- cap_flag is a defect in the screen and it is general. It compares a cap struck
  at today's price against a float struck at the prior half-year end and calls
  the gap arithmetically impossible. Here it was a 12.5% drawdown. Affiliates
  hold about 3.6%; on the same date float is 96.2% of the screen's cap.
- acq_note understated the perimeter: $667.3M over five years, 19.5% of cap, not
  $643M. The screen missed the separately-tagged Van's Foods line.
- newest_filing and newest_periodic of 2026-05-30 are both correct, not stale:
  a May fiscal year end means the 10-K is the newest periodic filing.
- wc_note did not fire on a 29.1% receivables swing, just under the measured 30%
  threshold. Recorded as a near-miss, not proposed as a change.
