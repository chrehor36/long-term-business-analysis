## 1. CORRECTION TO THE BRIEF — the three-segment split began in Q4 2020, not FY2021

The FY2020 10-K, Note 15, states verbatim:

> "Beginning in the fourth quarter of 2020, we report our segment results as Google Services, Google Cloud, and Other Bets"

and

> "Information about segments during the periods presented were as follows (in millions). For comparative purposes, amounts in prior periods have been recast"

So the Google-Services / Google-Cloud / Other-Bets series **starts filed at FY2018** (recast in the FY2020 10-K), not FY2021. Google Cloud has a filed operating-income line back to 2018.

## 6. WHAT ALPHABET DOES NOT DISCLOSE BY SEGMENT

- **No segment assets.** Every 10-K from FY2015 says the CODM "does not evaluate operating segments using asset information" / "Our operating segments are not evaluated using asset information."
- **No segment capex after FY2019.** FY2016–FY2019 10-Ks had a segment capital-expenditure table; the three-segment presentation dropped it.
- **No segment depreciation.**
- Consequence for the framework: **segment ROIC / return on tangible capital cannot be computed from the filings.** Google Cloud's capital intensity in particular is unmeasurable from the filed statements. Mark that UNKNOWABLE, not UNRESEARCHED — the document that would resolve it does not exist.
