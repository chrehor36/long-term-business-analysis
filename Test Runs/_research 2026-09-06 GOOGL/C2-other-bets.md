# 10. THE FULL FILED SERIES OF OTHER BETS REVENUE AND OPERATING LOSS

Segment disclosure begins with the FY2015 10-K. That filing gave 2013, 2014 and 2015, and stated
the reason:

> "In conjunction with the Alphabet reorganization, in the fourth quarter of 2015, we implemented
> legal and operational changes in how our Chief Operating Decision Maker (CODM) manages our
> businesses, including resource allocation and performance assessment. Consequently, we have
> multiple operating segments, representing the individual businesses run separately under the
> Alphabet structure."
> *(FY2015 10-K, Note 16, Information about Segments and Geographic Areas)*

> presentation.**"

> *(FY2023 10-K, Note 15)*

predominantly internal capital transfers, not external validations of value, and Alphabet does not
disclose the external portion. **The split between Alphabet money and external money is
NOT DISCLOSED in any of the four rounds.**

Note also that the $2.1 billion Q4 2025 charge is described as "based on estimated stock valuation."
Alphabet therefore internally values Waymo equity for compensation purposes, but **the valuation
itself is NOT DISCLOSED.**
