## WHAT IN THE FRAMEWORK WAS WRONG OR UNCLEAR

OUT, and a change for the better is credited only on a full cycle, TOO HARD (NATURE) until then. A field with an ordinary

the money test, the pricing test and the low-cost comparison, which do not wait for a downturn, and said so. (2) The

window; a company formed in 2020 and public since 2025 has four and a half years on the record, and the text gives no rule
for a short history (shorter window, or TOO HARD (WORK) for want of a cycle). (4) The Up-C and its TRA: the text says nothing
on whether a TRA liability is net debt, how "the company's own tax" applies when 85% of the tax savings go to former owners,
or whose stock pay counts when the sponsor's vehicles settle it; each was confessed as a CONVENTION of the run. (5) The
serial-acquirer convention takes "organic growth as the filer reports it"; this filer reports none for the window years,
and the run derived it from the MD&A's attribution percentages.
