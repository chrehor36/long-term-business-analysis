## Sub-bottling / CBA facts (FY2025 10-K)
- CBA (as amended; with KO and CCR): exclusive rights to distribute/promote/market/sell KO
  brands in acquired territories; quarterly acquisition-related sub-bottling payments to CCR
  "on a continuing basis"; contains termination and default rights; REQUIRES minimum ongoing
  capex and minimum volume; KO approval rights over sale of company/distribution business;
  prohibits handling non-KO beverages without consent. System Transformation completed Oct 2017.
- RMA (2017-03-31): manufacturing; KO "unilaterally establishes ... the prices, or certain
  elements of the formulas used to determine the prices" for finished-goods sales to other
  bottlers/KO.
- Incidence-based pricing agreement: establishes KO's concentrate prices to COKE; "prices
  charged by The Coca-Cola Company are impacted by a number of factors, including the incidence
  rate in effect, our pricing and sales of finished products, the channels in which the finished
  products are sold, the package mix and, in the case of products sold ... in finished form, the
  cost of goods for certain elements used in such products. The Coca-Cola Company has no rights
  under the incidence-based pricing agreement to establish the prices, or the elements of the
  formulas used to determine the prices, at which we sell products."
- Contingent consideration liability: $717.9M at 2025-12-31 (Level 3; PV of expected sub-bottling
  payments over ~40-yr life of distribution assets, discounted at WACC). FY2023 10-K guidance:
  next-five-years annual payments ~$50–70M. Level-3 roll-forward FY2023: begin 541,491 −28,208
  payments −3,300 reclass +159,354 FV = 669,337.
- 15% of total bottle/can volume 2023–25 is non-KO brands (Dr Pepper, Monster etc.).
- Customer concentration FY2025: Walmart+Kroger = 36% of bottle/can volume, 29% of net sales.
- Funding support from KO and other beverage companies: $209.5M (2025), $186.5M (2024).
- Input costs excl. concentrate ≈ 20% of COGS; labor ≈ two-thirds of SD&A.
- Approx. 60 million consumers in territories; ~2/3 post-mix gallons via ARTM.
