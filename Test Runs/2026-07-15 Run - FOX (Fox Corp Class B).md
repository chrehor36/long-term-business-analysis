## STEP 0 — RATE REFRESH
US 30-yr **5.10%**. Mkt cap (Class B) $20.80B — no size premium.

## GATE 1 — CIRCLE OF COMPETENCE
Cable news (Fox News), sports (Fox Sports, including NFL/college football
rights), and broadcast (Fox broadcast network, Tubi streaming) content
company. Unit economics: affiliate/carriage fees plus advertising revenue,
increasingly supplemented by streaming (Tubi). **VERDICT: PASS.**

## GATE 2 — MOAT
Franchise test [E3-03], from Fox's own 10-K: **"Cable network programming
is a highly competitive business. Cable networks compete for content,
distribution, viewers and advertisers with a variety of media, including
broadcast television networks; cable television systems and networks;
direct-to-consumer streaming and on-demand platforms and services; mobile,
gaming and social media platforms; audio programming; and print and other
media."** — an explicitly broad, ever-widening competitive set (essentially
"all media/entertainment attention"), consistent with the well-documented
industry-wide cord-cutting/streaming-disruption narrative (general
knowledge, not further quoted here).
- **No close substitute**: **real for live content specifically** — Fox
  News' loyal news audience and Fox Sports' live-sports rights portfolio
  (NFL, college football) carry genuine appointment-viewing value that is
  harder to time-shift or substitute than scripted content, supporting
  real affiliate-fee negotiating leverage. This is a real, if narrowing,
  moat mechanism, not the same weak position as a general entertainment
  cable network with no live-content anchor.
**Class: NARROW**, **Direction: NARROWING** — the pay-TV bundle that
funds affiliate fees is in structural decline industry-wide (general
knowledge), even for content with real live-viewing value.
**VERDICT: PASS with NARROW/NARROWING classification.**
