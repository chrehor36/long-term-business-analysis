# Company Run — Invitation Homes (INVH) — 2026-07-15
Framework v3.0. From the REIT mechanical screen. OE from real FY2025 10-K
data (Archives/edgar/data/1687229).

## STEP 0 — RATE REFRESH
US 30-yr **5.10%**. Mkt cap $17.71B — no size premium.

## GATE 1 — CIRCLE OF COMPETENCE
Owns, leases, and manages single-family rental homes (SFR) across ~16 Sun
Belt/growth markets, plus a growing third-party property-management fee
business (management fee revenue $87.3M in 2025, up from $13.6M in 2023 —
a genuinely new, fast-growing revenue line worth naming, not just averaging
into "total revenue"). Different product from the apartment REITs (detached
single-family homes, not multifamily buildings) but same underlying
economics: rent × occupancy − opex − capex. **VERDICT: PASS**, with the
management-fee segment noted as a second, smaller thing to understand
alongside the core SFR portfolio.

## GATE 2 — MOAT
Franchise test [E3-03]: needed/desired — yes; no close substitute — **weak**
(SFR homes compete with apartments and other SFR operators, and increasingly
with homeownership itself as rates move); unregulated — largely yes (SFR has
less rent-control exposure than urban apartment REITs to date, a genuine
point of differentiation from AVB/EQR, though state-level SFR-specific
regulation has been a live legislative topic). Real moat candidate: **scale
in local market operations** (maintenance/turnover cost advantages from
density within a metro) — genuinely different moat logic from the
"submarket land scarcity" story at AVB/EQR. **Class: NARROW** (operational
scale, not pricing power). Revenue trend not independently pulled this pass.
**VERDICT: PASS with NARROW classification.**

## GATE 3 — MANAGEMENT
CEO **Dallas B. Tanner — Co-founder, President & CEO** (founder-led, not a
professional-manager succession like AVB/EQR — a genuinely different
management-risk profile: stronger founder alignment, but no independent
succession track record yet). Governance from the 2026 proxy (filed
2026-03-26): documented related-person-transaction policy, stock ownership
guidelines with board-level compliance review, whistleblower policy, defined
enterprise risk management oversight. No litigation/restatement/integrity
flags found. **Limitation**: exact compensation figures and founder-share
concentration not independently pulled this pass.
**VERDICT: PASS**, founder-CEO structure noted explicitly as a different
risk/alignment profile than the other REITs in this batch, not better or
worse by default.

## GATE 5 — INVERSION
1. **Moat destruction** — SFR-specific regulatory risk (several states have
   floated institutional-SFR-ownership restrictions) is a real, live
   political risk distinct from AVB/EQR's rent-control exposure. Scored
   **[P]**, not [D] — this is a genuinely less-settled risk than multifamily
   rent control.
2. **Management/key-person** — founder-CEO concentration is a real
   dependency (no succession precedent exists yet, unlike AVB/EQR). Scored
   **[P]**.
3. **Balance-sheet stress** — 1.95:1 assets/equity, comfortably under
   ceiling. Scored **[D]**.
4. **Demand-shift** (homeownership affordability cycle driving SFR demand
   up or down) — addressed via geographic/market diversification. Scored
   **[D]**.

No [U], but **two [P]s instead of one** — a genuinely higher qualitative
risk profile than AVB or EQR, even though the mechanical numbers look
similar. **Thesis-breaking metric:** adverse state-level institutional-SFR
legislation in a top-5 market, or two consecutive years of negative
same-store NOI growth.
**VERDICT: PASS**, but flagged as the weakest Gate 5 read of the three REITs
run so far.

## GATE 6 — VALUATION
**6-B Statute** (lowest tier, $945.732M): yield = 945.732 / 17,710 =
**5.34%** vs. hurdle **5.10%** → **PASSES**, but by only **0.24pp** — the
thinnest cushion of any REIT run so far, and combined with the two [P]
scores at Gate 5, this is the least comfortable pass in the batch.
**Book Two DCF (Ruling 4 — built 2026-07-15 PM)**: Gate 3 carried a real
caveat (founder-CEO structure, no independent succession precedent,
compensation/founder-ownership not independently pulled) → discount rate =
5.10% + 2.5% NARROW spread + 0.75% caveat bump = **8.35%**. g1: most-recent
YoY (2024→2025) = +15.45% vs. 2-year CAGR (2023→2025, which correctly
smooths over the non-monotonic 2024 dip already flagged at Gate 4) = +5.71%
— used the lower, **g1 = 5.71%**. 10yr NARROW fade to 2% terminal.
- **IV = $17,739.0M** | Max buy (20% MOS) = **$14,191.2M**
- Current mkt cap $17,710M → essentially AT IV (100.2%), well above max buy.

**Scream Test**: Book One PASS by only 0.24pp (already the thinnest cushion
in the batch). Book Two independently lands the price almost exactly AT
intrinsic value — no margin of safety at all, let alone the 20% floor.
**Books technically both say "not cheap"** → **WHISPER, pass.**
**VERDICT: STATUTE-ONLY (starter size)**, and the Book Two result confirms
this was already the marginal pass of the batch — do not size up.

## GATE 8 — REVERSE DCF

## GATE 7 — SIZING
