## [E4-55] physical series per segment, and the [E2-49] switch
- FY2015 10-K published Kaplan University enrollment COUNTS: 39,848 (2015) vs 42,469
  (2014), with program mix table, and KTP enrollment % changes.
- FY2025 10-K publishes NO enrollment counts anywhere: only "Average enrollments at Purdue
  Global... up 4%". KI: qualitative only ("lower student enrollments in US Pathways...").
  TV: no subscriber counts (direction only: "overall cable and satellite subscribers are
  down"); Nielsen DMA ranks given. Healthcare: no patient census. The structural excuse is
  real (KU was transferred to Purdue in 2018 — the students are no longer Kaplan's), but no
  physical series replaced it in any segment. Absence per segment is the finding.
- Retrans economics = the Precision Steel case [E4-55]: per-sub rates RISING while the
  sub count falls; "retransmission revenue net of network fees declined in 2025... this
  trend is expected to continue" (10-K, MD&A). Dollar revenue flattered by pricing while
  units shrink is how a shrinking franchise hides.
