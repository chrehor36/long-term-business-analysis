# Company Run — T. Rowe Price Group, Inc. (TROW) — 2026-07-15
Framework v3.0. From the mechanical screen. Source: FY2025 10-K (CIK
1113169).

## STEP 0 — RATE REFRESH
US 30-yr **5.10%**. Mkt cap $24.87B — no size premium.

## GATE 1 — CIRCLE OF COMPETENCE
Active asset manager — investment management fees on client AUM (mutual
funds, institutional accounts, retirement products). Unit economics: fee
rate × AUM, minus compensation/distribution/operating costs. Genuinely
simple, though AUM itself is a function of both market performance and net
flows, both outside direct control. **VERDICT: PASS.**

## GATE 2 — MOAT
Franchise test [E3-03], from T. Rowe Price's own 10-K: **"[We face]
competition in all aspects of our business. A significant number of
proprietary and other sponsors' investment products are sold to the public
by other investment management firms, broker-dealers, mutual fund
companies, banks, and insurance companies. We compete with brokerage and
investment banking firms, insurance companies, banks, traditional and
alternatives asset management companies, hedge funds, and other financial
institutions and funds in all aspects of our business... Some of these
financial institutions have greater resources, may have more developed
brand awareness in particular markets..."** — a direct concession of
competitive disadvantage against larger, better-resourced rivals.
- **No close substitute**: **FAILS on product substitutability** — this
  is an active manager in an era of well-documented, industry-wide
  active-to-passive AUM migration (general knowledge, not filing-quoted
  this pass, but a real and highly relevant structural headwind for any
  active manager). Existing client relationships carry some real switching
  friction (retirement-plan default options, advisor relationships), a
  narrower and weaker moat mechanism than a consumer brand.
**Class: NARROW**, **Direction: NARROWING** — the passive-investing
secular shift is a real, ongoing, well-documented pressure on active-
manager fee rates and flows industry-wide, not a hypothetical risk.
**VERDICT: PASS with NARROW/NARROWING classification.**

**LOWEST VERIFIED TIER: $1,320.3M (FY2022)** — understated by the missing

**Fortress**: this run treats TROW under the **non-financial survival
track**, not Ruling 2's financial-business leverage ceiling — a pure asset
manager holds client AUM off its own balance sheet (unlike a bank's loan
book or an insurer's claims reserves) and does not take on the same kind of

Total assets $14,341.8M ÷ equity $10,860.1M (2025) = **1.32:1** — an
extremely low ratio, well under even the mechanical 10:1 ceiling had it
applied, supporting the survival-track classification rather than
contradicting it.
**VERDICT: PASS.**
