### The finding

**BellRing owns two trademarks and no factories.** *"We primarily engage third-party contract
manufacturers … for an agreed-upon tolling charge for each item produced."* Capital expenditure
is guided at **$10 million on $2.3 billion of sales**. **74.0% of FY2025 net sales went to
Walmart, Costco and Amazon.** The majority of its milk-based protein comes from *"one
supplier"*, **46.3%** of Premier Protein RTD shake supply from one contract manufacturer with
**28.0%** from a single facility, and the 11-ounce bottle from *"only one supplier"*.
**Administered cost on one side and administered price on the other, and no door on either
[E2-58, E2-59].**

**Q2 OUT on [E3-03] criterion (2), from the subject's own filed numbers.** Nine months to
2026-06-30: Premier Protein RTD shake net sales *"increased 0.5%, driven by 5.2% increase in
volume and 4.7% decrease in price/mix."* **The flagship gave back price into the worst input
year in its record while its volume rose.** Consolidated gross margin went from **34.9% to
28.5%, 647 basis points**, on +2.28% sales. The self-described *"clear leader in ready-to-drink
shakes"* lost **680bp** in a quarter in which its own dollar consumption grew **6.0%**, which
disproves **[E2-53]**'s dominance class in one line: the marketplace, not the company, is
setting how good this business will be.

### THE REFUTED PRIOR, and it is the reason this run mattered

The SMPL run (entry 135) recorded from outside this company: *"the pass-through failure is a
category fact and [E2-58]'s equation is operating on the layer beneath the trademarks."*

**The arithmetic reproduces exactly** from BellRing's own filings at BellRing's own definition
of gross margin: 647bp on +2.28%, against SMPL's "640bp on +2.3%". **The inference does not.**
A seven-name competitor row, matched at the calendar quarter ended 2026-06-30 and computed by
hand from each filer's own statements, found **two filers the SMPL row did not have**, and both
refute "category fact":

**Post Holdings is the decisive one and it was sitting in plain sight**: identical fiscal
calendar, the same nine-month window to 2026-06-30, the same US retailers, the same commodity
environment, and it is BellRing's own former parent. **Its gross margin went UP 17 basis
points** (29.6% against 29.4%, cross-checked on the filed Condensed Consolidated Statements of
Operations, accession 0001530950-26-000077). **Abbott's Nutritional Products segment sells
Ensure into the same aisle and lost 130bp.**

**So the pass-through failure is not a fact about the category. It is a fact about who owns the
conversion step.** Where the brand owner also owns the plant, the input shock landed on a cost
base it could manage; where the brand owner buys finished goods at *"an agreed-upon tolling
charge"* from a private co-packer, the shock arrived as a price it had to accept. **[E2-59]**
names this half of the mechanism explicitly, listing administered **costs** beside administered
prices, and it is the half the SMPL run's wording left implicit.

**And inside BellRing the boundary is narrower still.** Nine months to 2026-06-30: Premier
Protein RTD price/mix **-4.7%**; Dymatize price/mix **+4.7%**, and in the June quarter alone
*"increased 26.7%, driven by 6.0% increase in volume and 20.7% increase in price/mix."* **One
company, one input shock, one management, two opposite pricing outcomes.** The brand that could
not price is the one sold into the three customers who are 74% of sales; the brand that took
twenty points of price is the specialty and international powder brand. **The failure tracks
CHANNEL CONCENTRATION, not category and not brand.** This strengthens SMPL's own Q2 OUT rather
than weakening it, because the mechanism it names is sharper than the one SMPL wrote down.

**[E4-36]/[E3-51], wave-riding, and this run can prove it rather than assert it.** Net sales
went $854.4M (FY2019) to $2,316.6M (FY2025). The decisive fact is that **the wave has not
stopped** -- Premier Protein RTD consumption is still +6.0% -- **and the economics collapsed
anyway.** When the wave is still running and the surfer is drowning, the advantage was never the
surfer's.
