## (d) Verbatim MD&A / risk-factor language on PND decline and smartphone substitution

1. **FY2008 10-K, Risk Factors (0001144204-09-010751):** "The demand for personal navigation devices (PNDs) may be eroded by replacement technologies becoming available on mobile handsets and factory-installed systems in new autos." (Risk factor heading; repeated verbatim in the FY2009 and FY2011 10-Ks.) Body text, same vintage: "Mobile handsets are frequently GPS-enabled and many companies are now offering navigation software for mobile devices."

2. **FY2011 10-K, Risk Factors (0001144204-12-012007):** "We have historically experienced substantial growth in the automotive/mobile segment of our business as the products have become mass-market consumer electronics in both Europe and North America. This market has peaked as penetration rates increase and competing technologies emerge. This has resulted in lower revenues and lower earnings per share." Same vintage, PND risk factor body: "The acceptance of this technology by consumers has halted our growth and could further reduce margins."

3. **FY2013 10-K, Risk Factors (0001144204-14-010483):** "The demand for personal navigation devices (PNDs) has been and continues to be reduced by replacement technologies becoming available on mobile devices and factory-installed systems in new autos." Body, same vintage: "The acceptance of this technology by consumers has halted our growth in this segment and reduced margins." And: "Our financial results are dependent on the automotive/mobile segment, which represents approximately 50% of our revenues, is maturing and expected to further decline in 2014."

4. **FY2016 10-K, MD&A (0001615774-17-000765):** "Auto segment revenue decreased 17% from the year-ago period, primarily due to the ongoing PND market contraction."

Supporting decline mechanics stated in MD&A (verbatim fragments, attributed): "Automotive/mobile segment revenue declined 19.1% in 2009 as the average selling price declined 18% and volumes declined 2%. Average selling price declines continue to be attributable to the competitive environment in which our automotive/mobile products compete." (FY2009 10-K MD&A, 0001144204-10-009642). "The declining unit sales volume in 2011 was attributable to a decline in automotive/mobile units as the North American PND market slowed due to penetration rates and competing technologies" (FY2011 10-K MD&A). "Total unit sales decreased 3% to 15.4 million units in 2012 from 15.8 million units in 2011. The declining unit sales volume in 2012 was primarily attributable to a decline in automotive/mobile units as the North American and European PND markets slowed due to penetration rates and competing technologies." (FY2013 10-K MD&A).

## PND / unit-volume disclosures in the early vintages

**Checked absence:** neither the FY2007 10-K (0001144204-08-011821) nor the FY2008 10-K (0001144204-09-010751) discloses PND-only or Auto/Mobile-only units sold. Both disclose **total company units sold across all segments**, verbatim:

- FY2007 10-K MD&A: "Total units sold increased 128% to 12,300,000 in 2007 from 5,400,000 in 2006." And, in the 2006-vs-2005 comparison: "Total units sold increased 78% to 5,400,000 in 2006 from 3,028,000 in 2005."
- FY2008 10-K MD&A: "Total unit sales increased 38% to 16.9 million in 2008 from 12.3 million in 2007."

The closest filed proxy for PND weight in the mix is in the FY2011 10-K, Item 1 (0001144204-12-012007), verbatim: "In fiscal years 2011, 2010, and 2009, the nüvi class of products represented approximately 48%, 54%, and 63% of Garmin's total consolidated revenues, respectively." (The nüvi is described in the same filings as "Garmin's popular thin-profile personal navigation device (PND).")

The full total-units series FY2005-FY2016, with each figure's vintage, is transcribed in `grmn_segment_history.md`.
