## 2. Does Apple disclose Apple Watch units or Apple Watch revenue separately?

**Vintage checked: FY2025 Form 10-K, accession 0000320193-25-000079, primary document
`aapl-20250927.htm`, filed 2025-10-31. Cross-checked against FY2019 Form 10-K, accession
0000320193-19-000119.**

**No. Neither.**

- **Apple Watch revenue: not disclosed separately.** The smallest revenue unit of account in
  the FY2025 10-K is the five-line category table (iPhone / Mac / iPad / Wearables, Home and
  Accessories / Services). Apple Watch is inside "Wearables, Home and Accessories" together
  with AirPods, Beats, Apple Vision Pro, Apple TV, HomePod and accessories. No dollar figure
  is attached to Apple Watch, to "Wearables" alone, to "Home" alone, or to "Accessories"
  alone anywhere in the filing.
- **Apple Watch units: not disclosed.** A full-text scan of the FY2025 10-K for the word
  "unit"/"units" returns **only** references to *restricted stock units* (RSUs) — no product
  unit-sales figure of any kind, for any product line. Verbatim, the only "unit" hits are of
  the form "Approximately 24 million restricted stock units ("RSUs") were excluded from the
  computation of diluted earnings per share…" and exhibit-index references to "Restricted
  Stock Unit Award Agreement".
- The FY2019 10-K likewise carries no product unit counts. Its only qualitative unit language
  is: **"iPhone net sales decreased during 2019 compared to 2018 due primarily to lower iPhone
  unit sales."** and **"Products gross margin and Products gross margin percentage decreased
  during 2019 compared to 2018 due primarily to lower iPhone unit sales and the weakness in
  foreign currencies relative to the U.S. dollar."** Both are directional statements about
  iPhone with no number, and neither concerns Apple Watch.
- The FY2025 10-K names Apple Watch six times in total. All six are product-name mentions in
  the business description or in the "Fourth Quarter 2025" product-announcement list
  (verbatim: **"Apple Watch Series 11, Apple Watch SE 3 and Apple Watch Ultra 3"**). None
  carries a figure.

**Recorded plainly as an absence:** the filings do not contain the disclosure. Any Apple Watch
revenue or unit number in circulation is a third-party estimate and is not on the filing rung.

## 4. Category-movement sentences (verbatim, MD&A)

The 10-K explains the category's movement only in these one-sentence terms. No number is
attached to any sub-product.

- FY2019: "Wearables, Home and Accessories net sales increased during 2019 compared to 2018 due
  primarily to higher net sales of AirPods and Apple Watch."
- FY2020: "Wearables, Home and Accessories net sales increased during 2020 compared to 2019 due
  primarily to higher net sales of AirPods and Apple Watch."
- FY2021: "Wearables, Home and Accessories net sales increased during 2021 compared to 2020 due
  primarily to higher net sales of accessories and Apple Watch."
- FY2022: "Wearables, Home and Accessories net sales increased during 2022 compared to 2021 due
  primarily to higher net sales of Apple Watch and AirPods."
- FY2023: "Wearables, Home and Accessories net sales decreased 3% or $1.4 billion during 2023
  compared to 2022 due primarily to lower net sales of Wearables and Accessories."
- FY2024: "Wearables, Home and Accessories net sales decreased during 2024 compared to 2023 due
  primarily to lower net sales of Wearables and Accessories."
- FY2025: "Wearables, Home and Accessories net sales decreased during 2025 compared to 2024
  primarily due to lower net sales of Accessories and Wearables."

## 5. Presentation changes flagged (not reconciled)

1. **FY2022 → FY2023, footnote dropped.** Through FY2022 the category table carried footnote
   (2) enumerating the category's contents ("…net sales include sales of AirPods, Apple TV,
   Apple Watch, Beats products, HomePod mini and accessories."). From FY2023 that footnote is
   gone; the contents are described only in Item 1.
2. **FY2022 → FY2023, business description split.** The single "Wearables, Home and
   Accessories" paragraph becomes three sub-headings ("Wearables", "Home", "Accessories").
   **The revenue line was not split** — it remains one combined figure in all seven vintages.
3. **FY2021 → FY2022, iPod touch and full-size HomePod drop out of the enumeration**;
   "HomePod mini" replaces "HomePod" in the FY2022 footnote. Flagged, not reconciled.
4. **FY2024, Apple Vision Pro enters "Wearables"** with no separate figure and no restatement
   of prior years.

No dollar amount in the table was restated across vintages.
