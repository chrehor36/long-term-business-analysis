# GRMN — THE FILED PHYSICAL SERIES [E4-55], FY2005-FY2025
**The brief predicted this would be an absence finding. It is the opposite.** Garmin has
filed a **total unit sales figure in MD&A every year for twenty-one years**, in the same
sentence form, through both the PND boom and the PND collapse. It is the honest series the
corpus asks for, and this company has one.

## The decompositions that matter
- **FY2015→FY2025 (10 years):** units **+27.8%** (+2.48%/yr) AND real rev/unit **+48.0%**.
  **Both halves of [E2-44] move up together over the corpus's decade window.** Revenue
  +156.9% (+9.89%/yr).
- **FY2016→FY2022 (6 years):** units **−10.7%** while revenue **+61.0%** and real rev/unit
  **+47.9%**. This is the Precision Steel shape **[E4-55]** — dollar revenue rising on a
  falling physical series — and it is the six years in which the run's bear case lives.
- **FY2022→FY2025 (3 years):** units **+38.0%** (+11.3%/yr) with real rev/unit **−1.8%**
  (essentially flat). **The recent surge is VOLUME at a held real price, not a price wave.**
  This is the opposite signature from EAT/CTAS/BMI, whose recent booms were price or a
  lapped crest.
- **FY2008 (the PND peak) → FY2025 (17 years):** units +22.5%, revenue +107.4%, real
  rev/unit +13.2%. Garmin sells more physical units today than at its PND peak.

## The honest qualifier — this is MIX, not proven pricing power
Garmin's own MD&A attributes the rev/unit rise to **"shifts in segment and product mix"**
every year it explains the gap, and **never to price**. The $180→$350 move is the company
exiting ~$100 PNDs and selling ~$500-1,000 watches and chartplotters. So:
- **[E2-44](1) — raise prices with flat demand and unused capacity — NOT PROVEN.** No filed
  price/volume split exists, and the filed attribution is mix. (Same finding shape as BMI.)
- **[E2-44](2) — grow dollar volume with only minor additional capital — PASSES loudly.**
  Revenue +156.9% over the decade on capex averaging ~3.4% of revenue.

## The [E4-55] absence that remains
**Segment-level units are not filed in any vintage checked** (FY2005-FY2025, twenty-one
10-Ks). Only the company total. So the wearable-unit question — did Apple Watch take
Garmin's fitness units? — cannot be answered from Garmin's filings directly; it is answered
by segment revenue and margin instead. Registered-user / Garmin Connect actives: **no
numeric series filed in any 10-K vintage checked**. Garmin Connect and Garmin Connect+ are
described qualitatively in Item 1; no user count, no subscriber count, no ARPU. Recorded as
a checked absence.
