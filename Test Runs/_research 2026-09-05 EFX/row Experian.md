## Experian Verify / Employer Services (The Work Number attacker)

- **Active record count: 66 million** (FY26). Verbatim, CEO review AR p31: "**We continue to scale Employer Services and Verification Solutions, growing our active record count to 66 million.** We introduced enhancements such as Experian Verify Preview to provide mortgage lenders with earlier visibility into borrower employment data and, following the financial year-end, broadened distribution through a new integration with the TazWorks background-screening platform."
- NA regional review, AR p34: "**Verification Solutions also contributed to growth, with our record count increasing to 66 million.**"
- **Verification revenue: NOT FOUND** — no dollar revenue disclosed for Employer Services/Verification Solutions anywhere in the AR; it sits inside NA Financial Services/Verticals lines.
- Scale check against the incumbent (FILED): EFX FY2025 10-K (filed 2026-02-19, accession 0000033185-26-000010): "The Work Number® held about **209 million active and 813 million total (active and historic) employment records** at December 31, 2025." Experian's 66M active = ~32% of EFX's active count. The best-resourced attacker, still a distant second on records.

## Mortgage / scores (company-IR rung)

- NA regional review, AR p34: "**Mortgage profile revenue grew very strongly, primarily due to higher pricing.**" — same price-led mortgage growth pattern as TRU and FICO report in filings.
- CEO review, AR p31: "**We introduced VantageScore 4.0 into the conforming mortgage market in the USA. Following the recent announcement from the Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA), Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac are now able to accept VantageScore 4.0, introducing score competition for the first time. We have begun delivering VantageScore 4.0 to lenders participating in the initial FHFA pilot…**" (VantageScore is the bureaus' jointly-owned FICO alternative; Experian is pushing it into conforming mortgage.)
- Own Up acquisition (post-FY-end): "an AI-powered mortgage platform, extends our consumer marketplace into the mortgage space" (AR p30).

## Notes for the row
- Experian's segment margin structure (NA 34.2% Benchmark) and group statutory ~24% bracket EFX's operating margin; it is the largest of the three bureaus by revenue ($8.4B vs EFX ~$6.1B FY2025, TRU $4.6B).
- No statement found in the AR that names Equifax or The Work Number as the verification competitor; the competitive claim is implicit ("Verify… mortgage lenders… borrower employment data").
