### The freeze, dated
- LAST PRE-BREACH INCREASE: quarterly rate went from $0.33 to $0.39 effective Q1 2017
  (FY2017 10-K quarterly dividend table shows $0.39 in each quarter of 2017, $0.33 in each
  quarter of 2016). The breach was announced 2017-09-07; the $0.39 rate was already in place.
- FREEZE: $0.39/quarter ($1.56/year) held for 33 consecutive quarters, Q1 2017 through
  Q1 2025 — eight full years of $1.56 declared (2017–2024).
- THE INCREASE: FY2025 10-K, MD&A: "On April 21, 2025, the Board of Directors approved an
  increase in our quarterly cash dividend to $0.50 per share beginning in the second quarter
  of 2025. We paid out $232.8 million, or $1.89 per share, in dividends to our shareholders
  during 2025." ($1.89 = 1×$0.39 + 3×$0.50.)
- Every vintage FY2017–FY2024 carries the identical boilerplate: "We anticipate continuing
  the payment of quarterly cash dividends... There can be no assurance that the Company will
  continue to pay quarterly cash dividends at current levels or at all." No vintage ever
  stated a freeze policy in the 10-K; the freeze is visible only in the numbers.

FY2025 10-K, MD&A: "On April 21, 2025, the Board of Directors terminated the existing share
repurchase authorization and approved an authorization to repurchase up to $3 billion of
shares of common stock. We repurchased 4,006,173 shares of our common stock on the open
market for $927.4 million, excluding brokerage commissions and excise taxes of $8.4 million,
during the twelve months ended December 31, 2025. We did not repurchase any shares from
public market transactions during the twelve months ended December 31, 2024 or 2023. At
December 31, 2025, approximately $2.1 billion was available for future purchases of common
stock under our share repurchase authorization."

So: buybacks stopped dead after the 2017 partial year and stayed at zero for seven years
(2018–2024, except the small $69.9M purchase in 2021), and restarted at scale only in 2025,
alongside the dividend increase — the same April 21, 2025 board action.
