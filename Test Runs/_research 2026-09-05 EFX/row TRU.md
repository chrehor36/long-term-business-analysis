### Mortgage
- Mortgage is a **line of business inside the Financial Services vertical**; TRU does **NOT** disclose mortgage revenue or its share of consolidated revenue in the 10-K. **NOT FOUND** as a filed number.
- MD&A FY2025: "For 2025, Financial Services revenue increased $250.8 million, or 17.5%, compared to 2024. **The Mortgage line of business grew due primarily to increased pricing.**"
- MD&A FY2024: "**A majority of the growth in Financial Services came from our Mortgage line of business, due primarily to increases in price and batch activity.**"
- Cost side, MD&A FY2025: "an increase of approximately **$118.0 million in product and fulfillment costs due primarily to an increase in certain product cost pricing** in our U.S. Markets segment…"; FY2024: "approximately **$138.0 million** … resulting from **an increase in certain product cost pricing** primarily in our U.S. Markets segment" — the pass-through of score/data input pricing, not named as FICO in MD&A.

For example, we compete with FICO in the Financial Services vertical, with Verisk Analytics, Inc. in the Insurance vertical, and with LiveRamp and Experian in the marketing solutions space."

## Verbatim — FICO royalty / third-party scores

Item 1, Intellectual Property:
> "We also license certain intellectual property that is important for our business from third parties. For example, **we license credit-scoring algorithms and the right to sell credit scores derived from those algorithms from third parties for a fee.**"

Forward-looking factors list (Cautionary Notice):
> "**uncertainty related to Fair Isaac Corporation's ('FICO') new Mortgage Direct License Program**;"

This is the only named FICO-program mention in the filing; the term appears once. (FILED evidence that the FICO direct-license move is material enough for TRU to list among factors that "could materially affect our financial results.")

Risk factor on data suppliers (Item 1A):
> "**In some cases, we compete with our data providers.** Our data providers could stop providing data, provide untimely data or **increase the costs for their data** for a variety of reasons, including … **a desire to generate additional revenue** or for regulatory or competitive reasons."

## Verification services (Work Number competitor?)

**NOT FOUND.** The FY2025 10-K claims no income/employment verification business. TRU does not compete with Equifax's The Work Number on employment/income verification per its own 10-K.
