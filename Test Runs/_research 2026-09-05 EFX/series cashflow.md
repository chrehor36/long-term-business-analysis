## THE CAPEX CAPTION ANSWER

Every vintage FY2016–FY2025 has ONE combined investing-activities line, captioned exactly:
**"Capital expenditures"**. There is NO separate software line, NO separate PP&E line, and
no "additions to internal-use software" caption anywhere in the cash flow statement, in any
vintage. Capitalized internal-use software IS INSIDE the capex caption:
- PP&E note (FY2025 vintage): "Capitalized internal-use software and system costs |
  3,098.2 | 2,817.5" ($M gross, Dec 31 2025 / 2024) — the dominant component of gross PP&E.
- FY2025 MD&A: "Capital expenditures decreased in 2025 and 2024 from 2024 and 2023,
  respectively, due to lower capitalized software costs and lower spending on technology
  infrastructure..." and "Our capital expenditures are used for developing, enhancing and
  deploying new and existing software in support of our expanding product set..."
- Depreciation policy: "generally three to seven years for data processing equipment and
  capitalized internal-use software and systems costs."
So owner-earnings work can take the single capex line as containing the software/data
capitalization; nothing is hiding in a second caption. CAUTION: there are TWO capex series —
the cash flow statement line ("total cash outflows for capital expenditures") and the
MD&A-highlights/segment-note series footnoted "Amounts include accruals for capital
expenditures." Both are tabulated below.

Reading notes:
- 2019 OCF of $313.8M is the breach-settlement year (payments of $341.5M made in 2019; the
  settlement accrual itself boosted 2019's "Current and long-term liabilities, excluding
  debt" +$527.7M, then 2022's massive -$464.6M swing includes the January 2022 payment of
  the final $345.0M into the Consumer Restitution Fund).
- D&A nearly tripled 2016→2025 (268.7 → 726.9) while capex peaked in 2022 (624.5) — the
  cloud/technology-transformation capitalization cycle rolling through the income statement:
  "increased amortization of capitalized internal-use software costs resulting from
  technology transformation capital spending incurred previously" (FY2025 MD&A).
- SBC roughly doubled 2016→2024 (37.1 → 81.6).
