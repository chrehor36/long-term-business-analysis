Reading notes (facts, not judgments):
- FY2018 and FY2019 both say "approaching 380 million" — the count disclosure did not move
  between those two vintages.
- The FY2023 vintage is the first to split CURRENT records (168M) from TOTAL (657M); prior
  vintages disclosed only the combined "current and historic" figure. FY2024 renames
  "current" to "ACTIVE". So the pre-2023 series and the post-2023 "total" series are the
  comparable line: 330 → 380 → 380 → 460 → 535 → 600 → 657 → 734 → 813 (million).
- Contributor disclosure jumped from "tens of thousands" (FY2019) to "over one million"
  (FY2020): the FY2020 10-K is also the vintage after EFX began ingesting from payroll
  processor partnerships; the 10-K itself does not explain the jump — it changed the
  denominator ("organizations" including data received via payroll providers) without
  comment. NOT EXPLAINED IN ANY VINTAGE.
- FY2024 vintage also rewrites the platform description: "key repository of employment and
  income data" becomes "key employment and income data platform providing a service on
  behalf of our employer customers."

## Verbatim quotes by vintage

### FY2017 — 10-K filed 2018-03-01, accession 0000033185-18-000011
> "The Work Number. The Work Number is our key repository of employment and income data serving our verifier business and enabling employer human resource services. We rely on payroll data received from over 10,600 organizations to regularly update the database."

> "The fees we charge for these services are generally on a per transaction basis. We have not experienced significant turnover in the employer contributors to the database because we generally do not charge them to add their employment data to the database and the verification service we offer relieves them of the administrative burden and expense of responding to third-party employment verification requests. The database is approaching 330 million current and historic employment records at December 31, 2017."

### FY2018 — 10-K filed 2019-02-21, accession 0000033185-19-000007
