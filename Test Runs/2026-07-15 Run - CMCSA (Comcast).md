## STEP 0 — RATE REFRESH
US 30-yr **5.10%**. Mkt cap $83.91B — no size premium.

## GATE 1 — CIRCLE OF COMPETENCE
Two genuinely different businesses under one roof: Residential
Connectivity & Platforms (cable broadband/video) and NBCUniversal (media,
theme parks, film/TV studios). The cable/broadband business is the larger,
more understandable core; the media business carries its own distinct
secular dynamics (cord-cutting, streaming competition) not detailed in the
excerpt reviewed this pass. **VERDICT: PASS**, treating broadband as the
dominant business and media as a second, separately-evaluated segment.

## GATE 2 — MOAT
Franchise test [E3-03], from Comcast's own 10-K (Broadband competition
section): **"We compete with a number of companies, many with significant
financial resources, that offer internet services, including: wireline
telecommunications companies, wireless telecommunications companies,
municipal broadband networks and power companies, satellite broadband
providers. Certain wireline telecommunications companies, such as AT&T,
Lumen and Verizon in the United States... have built and are continuing to
build fiber-based wireline network infrastructure further into their
networks, which enables them to provide data transmission speeds that
exceed those that can be provided..."** — Comcast's own filing names fiber
overbuild as a competitor advantage over its own cable-based network in
speed terms, a direct, disclosed technology-disadvantage admission.
- **No close substitute**: **weakening** — cable broadband historically
  enjoyed a real local-market infrastructure advantage over DSL-era
  telecom, but the company's own words concede fiber overbuild and fixed-
  wireless are closing or exceeding that speed advantage in an increasing
  number of markets.
**Class: NARROW**, **Direction: NARROWING** — same fiber-overbuild/fixed-
wireless erosion pattern already documented at Charter Communications
(run separately, eliminated at Gate 4 on leverage grounds, not moat), and
the same secular dynamic named in Verizon's filing (run separately) from
the opposite competitive direction.
**VERDICT: PASS with NARROW/NARROWING classification** — the media segment
(NBCUniversal) was not independently assessed for its own moat this pass,
a real scope limitation given it's a meaningful part of the business.

## ADDENDUM — ANCHOR VERIFICATION + NEWS SCREEN, 2026-07-16
Run as a fat-pitch check on the widest arithmetic headroom in the wide
screen. Four findings, mixed directions, verdict at the end:

**1. FY2022 anchor cause CONFIRMED one-time**: SEC XBRL shows an **$8,100M
goodwill impairment in FY2022** (the Sky write-down) and none in any other
year since 2021. Underlying FY2022 earnings ≈ $13.5B, in line with
surrounding years. The anchor's depression was non-cash and non-recurring —
kept UNADJUSTED as the anchor per the VZ precedent (we don't smooth), now
known to be conservative rather than suspicious.

**2. But FY2025 had the OPPOSITE distortion**: the income statement shows
"Investment and other income (loss), net" of **$9,503M in 2025** vs $1,252M
(2023) and $(490)M (2024) — a ~$9.5B largely one-time boost (Hulu-sale
related). Operating income actually DECLINED: $23,314M → $23,297M →
$20,672M (2023→2025). The wide screen's +24.2% g1 was an artifact of the
gain; **underlying growth is ≈ −7.4%/yr**. g1 corrected accordingly.

**3. Versant spinoff COMPLETED January 5, 2026** — the cable networks
(MSNBC/CNBC/USA etc., ≈$1.3B/yr earnings run-rate, already declining −16%
YoY) left the company. Today's market cap excludes Versant but our tier
table (FY2022-25 consolidated) includes it → all tiers overstate the
go-forward company by ≈$1.3B. Anchor haircut applied: $12,018M → ≈$10,718M
(approximation flagged — Versant's exact FY2022 contribution not
separately disclosed in the data pulled).

**4. June 29, 2026: Comcast announced it will ALSO separate NBCUniversal
and Sky from the connectivity business** — a second, much larger breakup,
announced two weeks before our screen. Post-separation, "Comcast" becomes
substantially a broadband pure-play — the segment losing 711k subscribers
in FY2025 (Q1 2026 −65k, "less bad") to fiber/fixed-wireless. This is
HON-style separation-execution risk at larger scale, layered on the
NARROWING moat already classified.
