## Pricing and conduct in NVIDIA's own filings (the [E3-03] pricing sentence, [E2-44](1), [E4-37])
- No unit, ASP or price series is filed. Counts: "unit shipments" 0 and "price increase" 0 in the 10-Ks FY2023-FY2026 and the Q2 FY2027 10-Q.
- 10-K FY2023 and FY2024: *"In the past, we have experienced a reduction in average selling prices, including due to channel pricing programs that
  we have implemented and may continue to implement, as a result of our overestimation of future demand ... We have had to increase prices for
  certain of our products as a result of our suppliers' increase in prices"*. FY2023 MD&A: *"We introduced pricing programs for our channel partners
  and started undershipping GPU supply to the partners so that we could lower inventory in the channel."*
- 10-Q Q2 FY2027 on the licensed H200: *"any H200s shipped under the new licensing program are subject to a 25% tariff upon importation into the
  United States. We have been unable to pass along any of the tariff to our customers, and do not anticipate doing so"*.
- 10-K FY2026 on Rubin: *"delivering up to a 10x reduction in cost per token compared to Blackwell"*; the product is priced against the customer's
  cost of computing, which each generation cuts.
- Gross margin by quarter (releases): Q1 FY26 60.5% (with the $4.5bn H20 charge), Q2 FY26 72.4%, Q3 FY26 73.4%, Q4 FY26 75.0%, Q1 FY27 74.9%,
  Q2 FY27 75.0%; guided Q3 FY27 74.0%.
