## GATE 2 — MOAT
Franchise test [E3-03], from Zoetis's own 10-K: **"Although our business
is the largest based on revenue in the animal health industry (which
includes medicines, vaccines and diagnostics), we face competition in the
regions in which we compete... Our primary competitors include animal
health medicines, vaccines and diagnostic companies such as Boehringer
Ingelheim Animal Health... Merck Animal Health..."**
- **Needed/desired**: yes — animal health products for livestock
  (production economics) and companion animals (owner willingness to pay)
  are both real, recurring needs.
- **No close substitute**: **real, for on-patent products specifically** —
  pharmaceutical-style patent protection creates genuine periods of
  exclusivity for specific molecules (general pharma-industry knowledge;
  this specific filing excerpt does not itself quote a patent-duration
  figure, flagged as not independently confirmed this pass), though
  competitors named above operate in the same categories with their own
  patent-protected portfolios.
- Zoetis's own claim to being the **largest** player by revenue is a real,
  disclosed scale position, not a bare assertion of quality.
**Class: NARROW-to-WIDE** (patent-protected proprietary products plus
scale/market-leadership, tempered by named large pharma-backed
competitors), **Direction: STABLE**.
**VERDICT: PASS with NARROW-to-WIDE classification**, using the more
conservative NARROW end for Gate 6 sizing given the patent-protection claim
rests partly on general industry knowledge rather than this filing's own
words.
