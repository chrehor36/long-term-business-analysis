FY2025 and H1-2026 are extracted elsewhere; this file covers FY2016-FY2024.

**Source:** SEC EDGAR, CIK 0000077476. Each year's block is headed by the accession number
of the 10-K it was transcribed from. Anything not found in the filing is marked NOT DISCLOSED.

**Status:** COMPLETE for FY2016-FY2024. Built newest first, appended after each 10-K.
Note on "—" inside tables and value lines: it transcribes the filed table symbol for a
zero/nil impact, as printed in the 10-K.

## Promotional activity / price-investment language, FY2022-FY2024 10-Ks (verbatim, max 4)

No sentence using the phrase "price investments" appears in the MD&A of the FY2022, FY2023
or FY2024 10-Ks; explicit promotional-activity discussion in these filings is limited to
the items below. NOT DISCLOSED beyond these.

1. FY2022 10-K (0000077476-23-000007), Europe division operating profit discussion:
"Operating profit performance was also negatively impacted by a 91-percentage-point impact
of higher commodity costs, primarily packaging materials, raw milk and potatoes, certain
operating cost increases, the organic volume decline, a 4-percentage-point impact of less
favorable settlements of promotional spending accruals compared to the prior year and a
4-percentage-point impact of payments to employees for a change in pension benefits."

2. FY2024 10-K (0000077476-25-000007), MD&A strategy discussion:
"Bringing Our Business Closer to the Consumer: We are continuously making investments that
aim to help us provide consumers with more value, more personalization, and more choices.
