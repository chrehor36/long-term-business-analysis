Formula (project standard, applied exactly):
NTOA = total assets − cash and equivalents − short-term investments − goodwill − all intangible assets (incl. trademarks/franchise rights) − operating-lease ROU assets − (total current liabilities − current debt/short-term borrowings − current operating-lease liabilities).
Return on NTOA = GAAP operating income ÷ NTOA. ROIC incl. goodwill = GAAP operating income ÷ (NTOA + goodwill + intangibles).
