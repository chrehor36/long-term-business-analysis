## THE CROSS-CHECK THAT DID NOT AGREE, AND IT IS THE FINDING OF THE RUN
The FY2016 10-K reports FY2016 *"Total Operating Activities | 1,475"*. The FY2018 10-K reports
**`(6,508)`** for the same year. Both are ADM's own filed statements. The reconciling line is
**"Deferred consideration in securitized receivables"** - $8,063M in 2016, $8,177M in 2017,
$7,838M in 2018, $7,681M in 2019, $4,603M in 2020 - added to **operating** cash on the
securitization-classification change, with an **exactly equal offset in investing** in every
year (FY2018: -6,957 + 14,795 = +7,838). ADM states the offset in its own FY2020 MD&A:
*"Deferred consideration in securitized receivables of $4.6 billion and $7.7 billion in 2020 and
2019, respectively, was offset by the same amounts of net consideration received for beneficial
interest obtained for selling trade receivables."*

**So every negative operating-cash year ADM carries - 2016 through 2020 - is a classification
artifact.** The screen row's `level_note_oe` reports the pre-window years running down to

than the 5-year window; rebuild it [E4-25]"*. Rebuilt, the same five years run **+$1,555M to
+$3,054M** of operating cash. **The caveat was right to send a reader there and wrong about what
they would find.**

## The (c) judgment, the LIFO test, and the number that actually matters
**(c) is judged at total capex**, a disclosed judgment under **[E5-20]**: ADM calls itself *"a
capital intensive agricultural commodity-based business"*, capex exceeded D&A in four of five
years, and 2026 capex is guided at *"approximately $1.4 billion"* against FY2025 D&A of $1,181M.
The D&A end is shown as a memo line and named INVALID for this class.

**[E2-23]'s LIFO carve-out was tested and DOES NOT APPLY.** The parenthetical exempts LIFO
filers. ADM's inventory note: *"Certain merchandisable agricultural commodity inventories ... are
**stated at market value**. In addition, the Company values certain inventories using the
**first-in, first-out (FIFO)** method"* - market inventories **$6,222M of $10,369M**. Under
market and FIFO accounting a price rise demands working capital at flat unit volume, so the
increment is real.

**And it is half of FY2025.** The net working-capital contribution to operating cash was
**-$267M (2023), -$492M (2024) and +$2,763M (2025) - 50.7% of the year's operating cash** - with
inventory down from $14,771M (2022) to $10,369M (2025) on falling commodity prices. **FY2025's
$4,121M of owner earnings is a de-stocking high-water mark, not a run rate.**

**The honest cross-check:** cumulative ten-year owner earnings **$21,758M** against cumulative
reported net earnings **$21,245M** - within **2.4%**. Working capital swings violently and nets
to roughly nothing across a cycle with flat unit volume. **The defensible long-run figure for ADM
is about $2.2 billion a year**, which on a $41bn cap is **5.3-5.6%** against a 5.34% sovereign.
**The ten-year mean is 29.4% below the five-year mean, and what the five-year window hid is the
2016-2020 half at $1,271M a year** - the default window sits exactly on the supply-tight half of
the crop cycle, which is **[E4-38]**'s terminal-date distortion arriving by accident.

**Wilmar handled explicitly.** Equity in earnings of unconsolidated affiliates was **$648M in
FY2025, 60% of the $1,078M of net earnings**, of which only dividends are cash: the filed line
*"Equity in earnings of unconsolidated affiliates, net of dividends | (224) | (180) | (143)"*
puts **dividends received at $424M / $441M / $408M**. **[E3-04]'s look-through was deliberately
NOT applied**, because spending arithmetic in the favourable direction on an already-failed name
is what **[E4-27]** warns about.

## Tooling defects found - reported, not patched (three)
1. **`working_capital_flag()` tests SINGLE LINES and missed a 50.7% aggregate.** ADM's `wc_note`
   is **blank**. Every individual line sits under the 30% threshold - inventories 27.7%,
   brokerage payables 19.5%, receivables 15.7%, accrued 15.1% - while the **net sum is 50.7% of
   operating cash.** This is **not** the annual-facts limit already recorded in the resume state
   (that one is about periodicity); it is about **aggregation**, and it will under-fire on every
   merchandiser, where the working-capital story is many correlated lines rather than one big
   one. **Suggested fix, offered and not applied:** fire on the **net sum** of the
   working-capital detail lines as well as on any single line. It adds no step and no number the
   filing does not already carry.
2. **The tagged operating-cash series is unusable before 2021 for any filer that ran a
   deferred-purchase-price receivables securitization.** Recorded as a **data-shape hazard for a
   reader**, not proposed as a tool, because only a reader can tell a classification change from
   a business change. **The five-year window is unaffected and no queue ranking changes.**
3. **`RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax` returns 31% of ADM's revenue** -
   $24,956M tagged against $80,269M filed under `Revenues` - because most commodity sales are
   accounted for under derivative rather than ASC 606 rules. **Measured on three of three
   merchandisers**: Bunge $16,944M against $70,329M filed, Andersons $1,531M against $11,009M.
   **No owner-earnings number depends on revenue, so nothing in the queue is wrong today**, but
