### THE SKIP REASON, TESTED - A THIRD CAUSE, AND IT IS IN THE TOOL
*"Foreign 20-F filer ... too few annual periods because their XBRL history is short."* **Wrong for Ericsson, and not for the reason the
four Asian filers found.** `companyfacts` holds eleven IFRS annual periods and **had ingested the FY2025 20-F** (filed 2026-03-12).
`python tools/run.py ERIC` still prints *"no overlapping OCF/D&A/capex annual facts. UNRESEARCHED"*, because **`tools/run.py`'s tag lists
(`OCF`, `DA`, `CAP`, `SBC`, `NI`) name only US-GAAP elements** and `sources.annual()` looks them up in `ifrs-full` too, where
`CashFlowsFromUsedInOperatingActivities`, `PurchaseOfPropertyPlantAndEquipmentClassifiedAsInvestingActivities` and
`DepreciationAndAmortisationExpense` are never asked for. **Every IFRS 20-F filer fails the screen by construction**; the lag the SONY, TM,
HMC and TSM runs found is real for them but was not the whole cause. **Flagged to the operator; not fixed in a run.**

### THE QUESTIONS THE BRIEF ASKED
- **What Ericsson sells:** radio networks to ~500 operators (ten largest 46%, largest ~14%), sold one generation at a time and re-priced
  yearly without committed volumes or prices, plus SEK 14.5bn of patent licences; a core-software and managed-services leg that lost money
  five years in six; and an enterprise leg built from Cradlepoint (2020) and Vonage (2022: SEK 53.3bn plus SEK 5.9bn of debt repaid,
  SEK 46.6bn written off in 2023-24).
- **Did share and margin hold from 4G to 5G?** **Margin did, and better than held - against Nokia.** Ericsson's radio segment
  ex-restructuring ran even with Nokia's in 2014-2017 and 6-18 points ahead in every year 2018-2025 (20.4% against 2.8% in 2025). **Share
  moved, both ways, by region and by price:** US sales 20.5% of Ericsson's total (2011) -> 40.8% (2025); China SEK 18.7bn (2020) -> 8.2bn
  (2025); Nokia's filings record *"market share loss and price erosion in North America"* (2020-21) and AT&T's move to *"an alternative RAN
  vendor"* (2024), and Ericsson's record *"strategic contracts"* at *"challenging"* initial margins to win the 5G footprint (2018-19).
- **Is there filed evidence of no close substitute once a network is installed?** **The filed evidence runs the other way:** Nokia,
  *"Almost all CSPs dual source, giving vendors no pricing power unless they offer some technology advantage"* (FY2021); both vendors on the
  European modernisation swap wave (FY2013); Nokia's own 4G-to-5G *"conversion rate … 93.5%"* (FY2019); Ericsson's *"due to open
  interfaces, Ericsson's customers are no longer required to purchase from one vendor"* (FY2025).
- **Huawei and Samsung under the source rules:** Huawei's English annual reports carry a KPMG ISA 810 report on a *summary* of audited
  statements and **no segment profit in any year** - used at the evidence-ladder rung of a competitor's own published report, for revenue
  only, with the limit stated. **Samsung Networks is not separately disclosed in any audited or unaudited Samsung Electronics document for FY2016-FY2025** (inside the DX segment; decks show only "MX / Networks") - the limit of the source, stated in the row.
