### 6b. Pricing pressure and price erosion

- **FY2013, D13, Item 5 "Pricing and Price Erosion":** "The pricing environment remained intense in 2013. ## 7. Limits

1. **No radio-only operating profit exists for FY2016-FY2018 or FY2019-FY2020 as first reported.** D16-D18 give UBN (radio products + Fixed Networks) and D19-D20 give "Networks" (radio + services + fixed + IP/optical). The only radio-level profit for 2019-2020 comes from the D21 recast. Pre-2016 Mobile Broadband included Core; D16's Mobile Networks included services; D17-D18 Mobile Networks excluded services; D21+ Mobile Networks includes associated services but not managed services; D25 adds managed services. **No two adjacent breaks are like-for-like.**
2. **Measure basis is never IFRS at segment level.** All segment profits are non-IFRS (contribution / non-IFRS / segment = comparable). IFRS operating profit exists only for the Group.
3. **Global Services 2016-2018** is part of the mobile radio economics (it was carved out of Mobile Networks) but is shown separately; it also carries company-wide managed services. Summing UBN + Global Services is NOT a radio segment either (UBN includes Fixed Networks).
4. Stated margins were taken where Nokia printed them; all "computed" margins are arithmetic by this transcriber and are not filed numbers. Computed margins use sales including inter-segment sales.
5. **Market-share statements** are Nokia's own claims, several sourced by Nokia to Dell'Oro, Omdia, Analysys Mason; the underlying analyst reports were not read. Basis shifts (global vs excl. China vs excl. China, Russia and Belarus; 4G+5G vs 5G RAN vs Mobile RAN) are carried verbatim and are not comparable to each other.
6. **Search method for quotes:** keyword search (market share, price erosion, pricing pressure, competitor names, frame/framework agreement, commitment, diversity policies, dual source, vendor, lost customers) over text renderings of all 13 filings, with repeated paragraphs de-duplicated; relevant hits then read in context. Wording repeated year after year in risk factors is quoted once with "repeated" noted. A statement not containing those terms could have been missed.
7. **Price commitments:** searched "commit", "minimum purchase", "volume commitment", "obligation to purchase", "frame agreement", "fixed commitment". Found statements that framework agreements carry "no fixed commitment on the overall project scope" and are "non-committed", and that pricing "varies depending on the different needs of each customer"; found NO filed statement that prices are fixed or committed. NOT FOUND is a finding of absence in these searches, not proof of absence.
8. **Capex basis:** D13-D16 capex taken from cash flow statement (D13) and Item 5B text (D14-D16); the precise definition in those years (whether intangibles were included) was not transcribed. From D18 the line is "Purchase of property, plant and equipment and intangible assets". FY2013 capex includes discontinued Devices & Services.
9. **D&A:** FY2013 is Note 7 continuing (560) vs Note 33 total (728); FY2014-FY2025 are the segment-note "Total" column, which includes amortization of acquired intangibles. Not transcribed: right-of-use (IFRS 16, from 2019) depreciation split.
10. Group "first reported" 2013 and 2014 include HERE (sold 2015); 2013-2014 exclude D&S. 2022-2023 first reported include Submarine Networks (discontinued in D24).
11. Text rendering of D23-D25 splits sentences across lines; quotes from those years were rejoined from the rendering. The en dash / typographic apostrophes were kept as in the source where visible; console rendering showed some as replacement glyphs, and those were restored to the typographic apostrophe (’). This is a possible transcription artifact, flagged.
12. Not done: 6-K quarterly reports, Nokia annual reports outside the 20-F, and the Alcatel Lucent 20-F (pre-2016 ALU radio business) were not read.
