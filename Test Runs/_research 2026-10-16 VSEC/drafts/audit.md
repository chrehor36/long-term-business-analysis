## WHAT IN THE FRAMEWORK WAS WRONG OR UNCLEAR
Four things. **(1) Two Q2 rules pointed at different boxes on the same facts.** The item "the standing castle that protects only ordinary returns" (section C) sends a field whose durable players earn ordinary returns over a full cycle to OUT; the change-for-the-better paragraph (also section C, amended 2026-10-10) says that until a full cycle confirms an improvement "the file is TOO HARD (NATURE) on that question". VSE has both: ordinary returns over 2019 to 2025 and a margin improvement since 2022 that has not met a downturn. The text does not say which governs when the uncredited improvement is the only thing standing between the evidence and OUT. This run read the change-for-the-better rule as deciding only whether the improvement is credited (it is not), so the full-cycle evidence stands and the box is OUT; it also rested the OUT on the money test, which the improvement does not touch. A one-line rule would settle it. **(2) The serial-acquirer specific assumes the filer reports organic growth over the window.** VSE reports a figure once, for one quarter (about 14%), and none for the window years; the run used that figure and showed a like-for-like 11% computed from the 10-K's own pro forma tables beside it. The text should say what to use when the filer gives no window figure. **(3) Specific (e) does not say how to charge growth spending against growth when the spending is working capital rather than capex.** The run charged working capital at the latest year-end intensity (50.2% of each added dollar of revenue) plus growth capex for each dollar of revenue added, which makes the 14% growth case worth far less than an uncharged case would be; another analyst could charge it differently and get a different top of the range. **(4) A deal closed after the window is valued "on its own filed record" but the text does not say how to join that record to a window whose own years describe a different company.** Here the window's early years carried two sold segments and PAG is a third of the combined revenue; the run used the three continuing years of VSE plus PAG's two audited years, averaged separately and added, and said so. Smaller: the brief's competitor rule ("the rivals the 10-K itself names") has no fallback for a 10-K that names none; this run confessed its own choice.
