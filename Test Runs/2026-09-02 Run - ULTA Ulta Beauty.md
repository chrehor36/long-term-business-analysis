## Q1 — CAN I UNDERSTAND HOW THIS MAKES MONEY? **[E3-31]**

- **Unit economics in my own words, no management language.** The company rents **1,591 boxes** —
  1,505 in the United States averaging about **10,000 square feet** each, of which **950 square
  feet is a hair salon**, plus 84 in the U.K. and 2 in Ireland at about 2,000 square feet — fills
  them with **cosmetics, skincare, haircare and fragrance that other companies manufacture and
  brand**, and sells them at a **39.1-cent gross margin on the dollar** struck after the cost of
  the goods, the distribution centres, the store rent and the salon payroll. Out of those 39.1
  cents, **26.6 cents goes to selling, general and administrative expense**, leaving **12.4 cents
  of operating income** — the highest operating margin of any specialty retailer this project has
  run. Roughly **4% of the dollar is services** (haircuts, colour, brows), **38% cosmetics, 24%
  skincare and wellness, 19% haircare, 13% fragrance**. It owns no stores and no factories: it
