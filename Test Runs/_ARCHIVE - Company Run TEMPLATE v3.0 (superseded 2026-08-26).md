## GATE 6 — VALUATION
> **FRAMEWORK CONVENTION — Buffett does not run this calculation.** Munger, 1996 annual
> meeting: "Warren talks about these discounted cash flows. I've never seen him do one."
> Book Two exists here to impose explicit assumptions and make reasoning auditable, not
> because the corpus endorses DCF as a decision procedure. **It can veto a purchase; it
> cannot justify one.** (Ruling 4-B, 2026-08-01)
- **PRE-MODEL VERDICT (record BEFORE running Book Two; binding):** is it obvious this
  will WORK OUT WELL — i.e. will the business very likely be materially larger and still
  advantaged in ten years? [ ] OBVIOUS [ ] NOT OBVIOUS
  · **This tests the OUTCOME, not the price** (Munger 1996: "pluperfect obvious that it's
    going to work out well"). It may be YES for a business trading at a full price —
    cheapness is a separate question below. Do not smuggle price into this line.
    (Ruling 4-C, correcting 4-B.)

    record Book Two anyway — it is evidence, and it guards against a lazy "obvious".
