## 2. PRICING TAKEN 2021-2025 vs TRAFFIC (did guests hold?)

Caveat: BLMN's disclosed "average check per person" is pricing + mix + discounts, not pure menu pricing.

Compounded from the filed yearly figures (arithmetic mine, inputs above):
- **Outback avg check FY2021-FY2025 cumulative: +26.5%. Outback traffic same years: +0.2%** off the collapsed 2020 base; **vs the 2019 base (chaining 2020's (17.6)%), Outback traffic is (17.4)% by FY2025.**
- Excluding the 2021 reopening rebound: **FY2022-FY2025 Outback avg check +19.3%, traffic (15.1)%.** Four consecutive negative traffic years (2022, 2023, 2024, 2025).
- Combined U.S.: avg check FY2021-25 +31.6%; traffic +4.4% vs 2020 base, **(14.0)% vs 2019 base**; FY2022-25 check +19.9%, traffic (13.5)%.

**Answer: guests did not hold.** After the 2021 reopening rebound, every year of check growth was
accompanied by a traffic decline; roughly a seventh of 2019 traffic had not returned by FY2025, and check
growth decelerated to +0.7% (Outback, FY2025) while traffic was still negative. Management's own FY2025
framing: a "comprehensive turnaround strategy" announced November 2025 whose first platform is to
"drive in-restaurant traffic growth" (FY2025 10-K MD&A).

### Dividend record (the question: did BLMN cut/suspend in 2020? YES, twice in the window)
- Began paying quarterly dividends 2015. FY2017 $0.32/yr, FY2018 $0.36, FY2019 $0.40 ($0.10/qtr).
- Q1 2020: raised to $0.20/share, declared and paid ($17,480 total paid in FY2020).
- **2020 suspension:** among COVID liquidity measures BLMN "suspended our quarterly cash dividend and stock
  repurchases" (FY2020 10-K MD&A). The Amended Credit Agreement of **May 4, 2020** restricted dividends
  "until after September 26, 2021" (FY2020 10-K). No dividends paid in FY2021.
- **February 2022:** Board "reinstated quarterly dividends after a temporary suspension during the COVID-19
  pandemic" at $0.14/qtr (FY2022 10-K). Feb 7, 2023: raised to $0.24/qtr. FY2024: $0.96/yr, $82,574 paid;
  plus $265,695 of buybacks under the $350M Feb 2024 authorization (announced Feb 23, 2024; expired Aug 13, 2025).
- **2025 cut and second suspension:** quarterly dividend cut to $0.15 for Q1-Q3 2025 ($38,266 paid), then
  "In October 2025, our Board suspended the quarterly dividend as a component of our turnaround strategy"
  (FY2025 10-K). Q4 2025 dividend: $0. No FY2025 buybacks.
- Record for the competitor row: two suspensions and one cut inside six years, against DRI's record.

### Brazil Sale Transaction (in window)
- **December 30, 2024:** sold 67% of Brazil operations, retained 33%; all Brazil restaurants became
  unconsolidated franchisees under amended and restated franchise agreements. Brazil reported as
  discontinued operations for all periods in the FY2025 10-K.
- Proceeds: **$103.9M** cash (net of withheld income taxes, 52% of total) received at closing, applied to
  the revolver in Q1 2025; **$123.5M** (remaining 48%, net of withheld taxes, including accumulated
  interest) received in Q4 2025, also applied to the revolver. $14.4M interest income on the final
  installment. FX forwards on the installments: +$15.1M cash in 2024, ($25.7)M in 2025; matured Nov 2025.
- Remaining 33%: put-call in Q4 2028 "at a multiple of earnings defined in the shareholders agreement".
  Carried as equity method investment, $63,967 at Dec 28, 2025; FY2025 loss from equity method
  investment, net of tax, $(4,742).
- P&L of discontinuation: FY2024 loss from discontinued ops $(75,982) (includes $68.3M held-for-sale
  impairment and $33.8M deferred tax expense per the non-GAAP notes); FY2023 income $41,629; FY2025 $(537).
  FY2025 comprehensive income includes **$217,548 reclassification of accumulated foreign currency
  translation loss into earnings due to the sale** (non-cash).

## 6. COMPETITION LANGUAGE (verbatim, short)

FY2025 10-K, Item 1: "The restaurant industry is highly competitive with a substantial number of
restaurant operators that compete directly and indirectly with us in respect to price, service, location
and food quality, and there are other well-established competitors with significant financial and other
resources." And: "At an aggregate level, all major casual dining restaurants in markets in which we
operate would be considered competitors of our concepts." (Identical framing in the FY2020 10-K Item 1.)

FY2025 10-K, Item 1A: "Further, if this competitive environment and the breadth of alternatives result in
a decline in casual dining customer traffic, it could make our financial operations dependent on our
ability to increase our market share within the hyper-competitive casual dining segment. We believe all
of the above factors have increased competitive pressures in the casual dining sector in recent periods
and we believe they will continue to present a challenging competitive environment in future periods."

FY2020 10-K, Item 1A: "A substantial number of restaurant operators compete directly and indirectly with
us with respect to price, service, location and food quality, some of which are well-established with
significant resources."

BLMN nowhere claims a durable competitive advantage; its own filings describe casual dining as
"hyper-competitive" and its FY2025 posture is a turnaround (November 2025 plan, dividend suspended,
remodel "nearly all" Outbacks by end of 2028, new-unit development slowed, free cash flow to debt paydown).

## COMPETITOR-ROW SUMMARY (for the DRI file)
- Traffic: Outback U.S. traffic negative in 8 of the last 10 fiscal years (positive only 2018 and the 2021
  reopening rebound); cumulative FY2022-25 Outback traffic (15.1)% while average check rose +19.3%.
- Scale: system 1,460 restaurants (967 company-owned) vs DRI's ~2,100+ company-owned; Outback U.S.
  company-owned units shrank 568 to 548 over five years; Bonefish shrank 180 to 156 with a $28.2M FY2025
  goodwill impairment.
- Returns: FY2025 OI/NTOA 4.5% ex-goodwill (2.6% including goodwill and intangibles), on 0.9% operating
  margin; restaurant-level margin 11.7% and falling.
- Capital record 2020-2025: two dividend suspensions (May 2020 COVID-forced by credit agreement;
  October 2025 turnaround), one cut ($0.24 to $0.15, 2025), $135.8M loss extinguishing converts (2024),
  Brazil majority sold Dec 30, 2024 with a $217.5M accumulated FX loss recycled through earnings.
