additive as filed in every year (check + traffic = comps; pricing + mix = check). Standing definition

## 2. MENU PRICING TAKEN 2021-2025 vs TRAFFIC (the price/traffic trade)

| FY | Effective pricing | In-year actions as filed | Traffic same year |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2021 | +3.0% | approx 1.5% Q1 + 1.5% Q3 | +41.4% (reopening) |
| 2022 | +5.9% | approx 3.25% Q1 + 4.25% Q3 + 2.8% Q4 | +6.8% |
| 2023 | +9.4% | approx 3.5% Q1 + 2.0% Q3 | (1.0)% |
| 2024 | +4.7% | approx 2.5% Q1 + 2.0% Q3 | (0.7)% |
| 2025 | +4.3% | approx 2.4% Q1 + 1.5% Q3 | (2.3)% |

Planned next (FY2025 10-K): "We are in the process of implementing an approximate 1.5% menu price
increase in the first quarter of fiscal 2026."

Computed (arithmetic on filed numbers):
- Cumulative effective pricing FY2021-FY2025: 1.030 x 1.059 x 1.094 x 1.047 x 1.043 - 1 = **+30.3%**
- Cumulative effective pricing FY2022-FY2025: **+26.5%**
- Cumulative average check FY2022-FY2025: 1.002 x 1.040 x 1.017 x 1.024 - 1 = **+8.5%**
- Cumulative traffic FY2022-FY2025: **+2.6%** (and (4.0)% over FY2023-FY2025)

The gap between pricing (+26.5%) and realized check (+8.5%) is negative mix in all four years
((5.7)%, (5.4)%, (3.0)%, (1.9)%): customers absorbed the menu price by trading down. Verbatim on the
mechanism (FY2025 10-K, Item 1A): "our menu price increases may be insufficient to meaningfully
offset increased costs and may, if not accepted by customers, result in reduced customer traffic and
unfavorable menu mix shifts (i.e., customers reducing their spend by purchasing fewer menu items or
lower cost menu items)". Policy history, verbatim (FY2025 10-K): "Prior to fiscal 2022, we targeted
menu price increases of approximately 2% to 3% annually ... In the last three fiscal years, we
implemented price increases above our historical levels, to help offset significant inflationary cost
pressures."

Contrast for the DRI row: over FY2022-FY2025 CAKE priced ~26.5% and got traffic +2.6% with three
straight negative years after the rebound; TXRH (see peer_TXRH.md) priced ~18.5% over the same window
and compounded traffic +15.3%. CAKE is the peer that shows what pricing above the value position
costs in traffic and mix.

## READ-ACROSS FOR THE DRI RUN (the competitor row)

1. **Traffic:** CAKE's flagship has shed order traffic in seven of nine years FY2017-FY2025
   (cumulative approx (21.5)% on the filed order-count basis). The premium AUV ($12.4M/unit, approx
   $1,151/productive sq ft) rests on check, not on guest growth.
2. **Pricing discipline:** approx +30.3% effective pricing FY2021-FY2025 bought +0.1% to +3.0% comps
   in the last three years, with mix negative every year since 2022. This is the trade Darden's
   value-forward concepts (and TXRH) are positioned against.
3. **Returns:** FY2025 return on NTOA 23.2% (ROU-in 9.0%, incl. goodwill 17.7%) against DRI/TXRH
   equivalents; FY2020 was an operating loss year with a (54.7)% return on the same basis.
4. **Capital record:** dividend suspended March 2020, emergency preferred sold at a $22.23 strike
   in April 2020 and bought out fourteen months later for $443.8M plus 2.4M shares; dividend
   reinstated below the old rate and unraised for four years; the FRC earnout (no cap, seller
   financed by CAKE) still open through 2027. The 2020 stress exposed a balance sheet and a deal
   structure materially weaker than DRI's through the same storm.
