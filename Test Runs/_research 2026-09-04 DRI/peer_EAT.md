## 2. PRICING TAKEN FY2022-FY2026 vs TRAFFIC (did guests hold?)

Chili's company-owned, filed yearly figures compounded (arithmetic mine, inputs in table above):
- **Price FY2022-FY2026: +3.3, +9.2, +7.4, +4.5, +4.4 = 28.8 pts simple sum; compounded 1.033 x 1.092 x
  1.074 x 1.045 x 1.044 = 1.3217, i.e. cumulative +32.2%.**
- **Traffic FY2022-FY2026: +2.7, (6.9), (0.6), +16.0, +3.6 = +14.8 pts simple sum; compounded 1.027 x
  0.931 x 0.994 x 1.160 x 1.036 = 1.1422, i.e. cumulative +14.2%.**
- Mix same years compounded: 1.026 x 1.047 x 1.006 x 1.048 x 1.012 = 1.1461, +14.6%.
- Against the pre-COVID FY2019 base (chaining FY2020 (8.8)% and FY2021 +10.5%): 0.912 x 1.105 x 1.1422 =
  1.1510, **Chili's traffic +15.1% above FY2019 by FY2026** - with the (b) caveat that the FY2021 link
  includes It's Just Wings orders.

**Answer: guests held, and then some.** Brinker priced +32% cumulative over five years and ended the
period with MORE traffic (+14.2%), all of the gain arriving in FY2025-FY2026 after two mid-decade price-
heavy, traffic-negative years (FY2023 +9.2% price / (6.9)% traffic, FY2024 +7.4% / (0.6)%). This is the
opposite shape of BLMN (FY2022-25 check +19.3%, traffic (15.1)%) and the direct standard of comparison
for DRI's price-vs-traffic record.

## 6. COMPETITION AND VALUE-STRATEGY LANGUAGE (verbatim, short)

FY2026 10-K, Item 1A (competition risk factor): "The restaurant business is highly competitive as to
price, service, restaurant location, convenience, and type and quality of food. We compete within each
market with locally-owned restaurants as well as national and regional restaurant chains. The casual
dining segment of the restaurant industry has not seen significant growth in customer traffic in recent
years. If these trends continue, our ability to grow customer traffic at our restaurants (including
through off-premise) will depend on our ability to increase our market share within the casual dining
segment." And: "If we are unable to compete effectively, our gross sales, guest traffic and profitability
may decline." The forward-looking statements list names "the impact of competition, including competitors
employing our same strategies or discounting their offerings."

FY2026 10-K, Item 1 (the value strategy): Chili's "is dedicated to delivering delicious and craveable
food with value-centric offerings such as '3 for Me' starting at only $10.99". "Our '3 for Me' platform
allows guests to enjoy a non-alcoholic drink, an appetizer and certain entrées starting at just $10.99.
We believe our value offerings will continue to be an important traffic driver in the current economic
circumstances, and we will continue to highlight this value in our marketing efforts. We have increased
menu pricing in other areas in light of the inflationary challenges". Menu focus: "we have simplified our
menu to focus on core equities we believe can help grow sales--burgers, fajitas, Chicken Crispers,
margaritas, and the Triple Dipper" and "the social-media-famous Triple Dipper®" (called "five core
equities"). Same $10.99 "3 for Me" language in the FY2025 10-K.

Lineage of the platform (FY2021 10-K, Item 1): "Our '3 for $10' platform allows guests to combine a
starter, a non-alcoholic beverage and an entrée for just $10.00 as part of the every-day base menu",
plus "our Margarita of the Month promotion that features a premium-liquor margarita every month at an
every-day value price of $5.00." So the value platform predates the turnaround; what changed in
FY2025-FY2026 was execution, staffing ("scheduling more team members per shift"), simplification, and
social-media marketing, per the filings' own framing.

## COMPETITOR-ROW SUMMARY (for the DRI file)
- Traffic: the one demonstrated traffic turnaround in the comp set. Chili's traffic FY2025 +16.0%,
  FY2026 +3.6% (filed), after (6.9)% and (0.6)%; cumulative FY2022-26 price +32.2% compounded WITH
  traffic +14.2% - guests held through the pricing. Maggiano's, run by the same company, lost traffic
  five straight years (FY2026 (9.3)%): the turnaround is a brand phenomenon, not a corporate one.
- Scale: system 1,635 restaurants, 1,163 company-owned (nearly all Chili's, 95%+ domestic); Chili's AUV
  $2.9M (FY2021) to $5.0M (FY2026).
- Returns: FY2026 OI/NTOA 82.1% ex-goodwill (33.6% ROU-in; 64.3% incl goodwill) on a 10.7% operating
  margin - but on a leased-asset, negative-to-thin-equity base; FY2021 same ratios 34.3% / 13.4% / 25.2%
  on a 6.0% margin.
- Capital record: dividend suspended Q4 FY2020 (COVID, revolver-enforced) and never reinstated through
  FY2026; deleveraged $1,225.0 (FY2020) to $448.0 (FY2026) total debt incl. finance leases, then
  redeemed the 8.25% notes July 15, 2026; returns now via buybacks ($381.9 treasury-stock increase in
  FY2026).
- Its own filing concedes the industry fact that frames the DRI run: "The casual dining segment of the
  restaurant industry has not seen significant growth in customer traffic in recent years" - Brinker's
  answer is taking share via value ("3 for Me" at $10.99) plus social media, i.e., a share-shift game,
  not category growth.
