 Exhibit 99.5
 To announce the status of trade secrets disputes between UMC and Micron
 1. Date of occurrence of the event: 2021/11/26
 2. Company name: UNITED MICROELECTRONICS CORPORATION
 3. Relationship to the Company (please enter "head office" or "subsidiaries"): head office
 4. Reciprocal shareholding ratios: N/A
 5. Cause of occurrence:
 The Company and Micron Technology, Inc. (Nasdaq: MU) announced a settlement agreement between the two companies worldwide. The companies will globally withdraw their complaints against the other party.
 6. Countermeasures: No
 7. Any other matters that need to be specified:
 United Microelectronics Corporation (TWSE: 2303; NYSE: UMC) and Micron Technology, Inc. (Nasdaq: MU) today announced a settlement agreement between the two companies worldwide. The companies will globally withdraw their complaints against the other party, and UMC will make a one-time payment of an undisclosed amount to Micron. UMC and Micron look forward to engaging in mutual business cooperation opportunities. The amount of settlement has no material impact on the Company's business operations and financial conditions.
