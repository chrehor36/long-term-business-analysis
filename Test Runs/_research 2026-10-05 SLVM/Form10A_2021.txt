 Item 10. | 
 Recent Sales of Unregistered Securities. 
 | 
 In the three years preceding the filing of this registration statement, the registrant has not sold any securities that were not registered under the
Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Securities Act”), except for the issuance of 100 shares of common stock, par value $1.00 per share, of the registrant to International Paper Company for aggregate consideration of $100 on
April 13, 2021, in a transaction exempt from registration pursuant to Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act.
