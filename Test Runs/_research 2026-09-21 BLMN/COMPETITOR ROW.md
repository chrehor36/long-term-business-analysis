## ROW 4 — RETURN ON NET TANGIBLE OPERATING ASSETS [E3-46]
*One construction, applied identically to all eight, and it is mine, not the filers':*
**NTOA\* = Total assets - cash - goodwill - intangibles ex-goodwill - operating-lease ROU asset -
total current liabilities.** It treats all current liabilities as non-interest-bearing, which
understates NTOA slightly wherever current debt exists, i.e. it flatters everybody equally.
All inputs from the accession named above.

## ROW 5 — WHAT THE FILER SAYS ABOUT SUBSTITUTION, verbatim

  restaurants."*

  hyper-competitive casual dining segment."*

## THE ROW'S OWN LIMIT [E3-61]
*"In some businesses, the participants behave like a demented Kellogg. In other businesses, they
don't … I think you'd have to know the people involved …"* The row shows position. It cannot show
conduct: it does not tell me whether Chili's traffic gain is a permanent share shift or a promotional
cycle, and Buffett says even Munger had no model for predicting that. The row is used below for what
it can carry - relative position - and nothing more.
