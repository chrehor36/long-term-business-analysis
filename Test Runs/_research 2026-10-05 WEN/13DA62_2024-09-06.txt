 Item 4. Purpose of Transaction
 Item 4 is hereby amended and supplemented as follows:
 On September 6, 2024, Mr. Peltz informed the Company of his resignation from the Board, effective immediately, to devote more time to other board commitments and Trian Management’s future activities. Following his resignation, the Board appointed Mr. Peltz as Chairman Emeritus, in recognition of his long-time leadership, counsel, guidance, and contributions to the Board and the Company. Mr. Peltz expressed his strong support for the Company’s CEO, Kirk Tanner, and his belief that the Company was well positioned for future success under Mr. Tanner’s leadership and the Board’s stewardship with two of Trian Management’s partners, Peter May and Matthew Peltz, on the Board and serving as the Senior Vice Chairman and Vice Chairman, respectively. Mr. Peltz further noted he was proud to have played a role in recruiting Mr. Tanner as CEO. Mr. Peltz informed the Company that with Mr. Tanner as CEO, and with Arthur Winkleblack’s deep experience on the Board, along with the experience of Mr. May and Mr. Matthew Peltz, he is confident that the Company is in good hands for further growth and success. In addition, Mr. Peltz looks forward to discussions with the Company as Chairman Emeritus and as a significant shareholder.

 Dear Art and Kirk, 
 It is with much emotion that I am writing this letter to you, after serving as Chairman of Wendy’s since 2008 and as CEO and Chairman of the Company’s predecessor, Triarc Companies, prior to that time.
 As you know, I believe that Wendy’s is well positioned for future success under your leadership and the stewardship of our Board. 
 However, in order to devote more time to other board commitments and Trian’s future activities, I have reluctantly decided to resign from Wendy’s Board, effective today. 
 I wish to express my strong support for Kirk, whom I am proud to have played a role in recruiting to Wendy’s. Kirk has transitioned seamlessly into the CEO role, and with Art’s deep experience on the Board along with that of my partners Peter May and Matt Peltz, Senior Vice Chairman and Vice Chairman, respectively, I am confident that Wendy’s is in good hands for further growth and success.
 Kirk, I have enjoyed working with you and getting to know you. Art, it has been my great pleasure to lead the Wendy’s Board for more than 15 years and to work closely with you during your time on the Board. I have also enjoyed the many close friendships I have developed with Board members over the years and the many interactions I have had with the Wendy’s management team.
 I look forward to continuing our discussions as a large shareholder.
 Kindest regards.
