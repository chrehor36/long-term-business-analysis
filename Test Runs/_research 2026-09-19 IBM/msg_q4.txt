THE KYNDRYL PERIMETER. The revenue line was recast to continuing operations for FY2019 onward;
the CASH-FLOW statement was NOT. The FY2021 annual report says the statement includes the cash
flows of discontinued operations, with one aggregate figure per year ($1.6bn/$4.4bn/$4.5bn for
2021/2020/2019) and no Kyndryl cash-flow statement. The CNR rule's "nothing is estimated; the
pro forma is addition" condition is not met, so FY2019-FY2021 are REFUSED, not adjusted. Clean
perimeter: FY2022-FY2025 plus the TTM, and the five-year default is short by one year and says
so. Confluent ($11,268M cash, closed 2026-03-17) sits inside the TTM and is flagged.

audited line. Not the [E4-41] flag (the reconciliation is on the same page and the offsetting debt

look-through construction is explicitly NOT attempted and the reason is stated, so no reader adds
the $521M of segment profit twice.
