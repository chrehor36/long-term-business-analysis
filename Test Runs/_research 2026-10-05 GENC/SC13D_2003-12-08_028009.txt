 Domestic has been an active market maker in
 Gencor stock for many years. It has
 purchased the stock of Gencor for its market making activities, as an
 investment for its own account, and on behalf of its customers. Domestic’s management is opposed to
 Gencor’s recent self-tender offer because they believe the price to be
 grossly inadequate and the offer to be not in the best interests of Gencor’s
 public shareholders.
