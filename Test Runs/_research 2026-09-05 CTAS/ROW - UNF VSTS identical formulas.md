## CROSS-CUTTING FINDINGS AND ABSENCES

1. **Nobody files a retention rate.** All three describe retention qualitatively. UNF: "improved customer retention." VSTS: strategy sections about improving it. Absence is uniform across the industry's filings - any retention number in an analysis is company-IR or estimate, not a filed figure.
2. **Only Vestis files a route count** (3,300+) and a facilities count (325+). UNF files neither in the FY2025 10-K. Neither files wearer counts.
3. **The "300,000" coincidence:** UNF "over 300,000 customer locations"; VSTS "more than 300,000 customer accounts." Different units; not comparable head-to-head.
4. **Comparability trap in the formulas:** VSTS' "Cost of services provided" is *exclusive of D&A* (D&A its own line, includes vehicle/plant depreciation); UNF's cost of revenues *includes* merchandise amortization but D&A is also a separate line. Operating income is comparable; gross margins are NOT.
5. **53rd weeks:** UNF FY2024 (53w) and VSTS FY2025 (53w) each inflate the year they land in; both filings quantify the effect (UNF: organic +1.8% ex-week; VSTS: -4.4% ex-week).
6. **VSTS FY2023 is a pre-spin carve-out** - allocated costs, near-zero interest, "may not be indicative." Its 7.7% FY2023 margin is the Aramark-era baseline, not a standalone achievement.
7. **OCF is polluted at Vestis** by the A/R securitization running through operating activities ($202.5M of receivables sold and derecognized at FY2025 year-end).
8. **Both non-Cintas nationals had a control/integrity flag in the same year:** UNF an unremediated ICFR material weakness (adverse E&Y opinion on controls), VSTS two securities class actions past (in whole or part) motions to dismiss, plus four derivative suits.
9. **Verification note:** all figures above transcribed from the four text conversions on disk; no external sources touched. Accession numbers are not present in the text conversions (absence noted) - the parent run file should carry document dates instead: UNF 10-K FY ended 8/30/2025; UNF 10-Q Q ended 5/30/2026; VSTS 10-K FY ended 10/3/2025 (auditor Deloitte & Touche LLP, Atlanta, report dated December 2, 2025); VSTS 10-Q Q ended 7/3/2026.
