## 3. THE POKEMON COMPANY (private Japanese KK; publisher of the Pokemon TCG)

### What public financial documents exist

1. The Pokemon Company (Minato-ku, Tokyo) is a private kabushiki kaisha. It files no securities report (no EDINET filing) and publishes no annual report with financial statements. Its only statutory financial disclosure is the annual settlement announcement (kessan kokoku) under the Companies Act, published via the official gazette / electronic public notice.
2. Secondary reporting of the 28th-term kessan kokoku (fiscal year ended February 2026, published ~June 1, 2026): net sales ¥531.4 billion (+29.3% YoY); operating profit ¥143.97 billion; net income ¥120.05 billion (+70.7% YoY); total assets ¥532.26 billion. Sources: GameBusiness.jp, June 1, 2026, https://www.gamebusiness.jp/article/2026/06/01/27087.html ; Nikkei, "決算:ポケモンの純利益が71%増の1200億円 30周年効果、26年2月期", https://www.nikkei.com/article/DGXZQOUC02B590S6A600C2000000/ . ### What Nintendo's English annual report discloses (checked directly)

Document checked: Nintendo Co., Ltd. "Annual Report 2026 (for the fiscal year ended March 31, 2026)" (English translation of the securities report), https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ir/pdf/2026/annual2603e.pdf , 103 pages. Also checked: "Annual Report 2025", https://www.nintendo.co.jp/ir/pdf/2025/annual2503e.pdf .

- Affiliates table ("Associates accounted for using equity method"): The Pokémon Company, Minato-ku, Tokyo; share capital ¥365 million; principal business "Sales and licensing of Pokémon related goods"; percentage of voting rights held by Nintendo: 32. "(Note) There are two associates accounted for using equity method other than the one shown above."
- Notes to consolidated statements: "Number of associates accounted for under the equity method: three companies. The main associate accounted for under the equity method is The Pokémon Company."
- Consolidated income statement line "Share of profit of entities accounted for using equity method": FY2025.3 ¥35,125 million; FY2026.3 ¥82,792 million (USD 520 million). This line is an aggregate across the three associates and is NOT broken out by company. No named per-company profit figure for The Pokémon Company exists anywhere in the report.
- MD&A, verbatim: "Net non-operating income was ¥182.0 billion (USD 1,144 million), mainly due to the share of profit of entities accounted for using equity method for The Pokémon Company, as well as interest income and foreign exchange gains."
- NEW in FY2026.3 — "2. Note about significant associates" (report p. 97), verbatim: "Condensed financial information for all associates accounted for under the equity method (three companies), including The Pokémon Company, a significant associate, is shown below." Current fiscal year (FY2026.3) combined figures for the three associates: total current assets ¥908,355 m; total non-current assets ¥327,744 m; total current liabilities ¥236,538 m; total non-current liabilities ¥144,691 m; total net assets ¥854,870 m; net sales ¥1,102,861 m; profit before income taxes ¥344,304 m; profit ¥255,139 m. Previous-fiscal-year column shows "-" (not presented). Note, verbatim: "The Pokémon Company has been designated a significant associate from the fiscal year ended March 31, 2026, due to an increase in materiality, and condensed financial information is presented, including The Pokémon Company."
- Cross-check against the prior year: Annual Report 2025 (annual2503e.pdf), same note section, reads "2. Note about significant associates — Not applicable." So the condensed disclosure is new with FY2026.3.

Summary of what was checked and found: Nintendo's English annual reports for FY2025.3 and FY2026.3 were downloaded and searched for "Pokémon Company", "equity method", and "significant associate". No single named profit figure for The Pokémon Company alone is disclosed. The closest disclosures are (i) the aggregate equity-method profit line (¥82,792 m in FY2026.3), which the MD&A attributes "for The Pokémon Company" by name, and (ii) the new FY2026.3 condensed financial information for the three equity-method associates combined, in which The Pokémon Company is the named significant associate. Note the three-associate combined "profit" of ¥255,139 m is the associates' own profit on a 100% basis (arithmetic check: 32% of 255,139 = 81,644, close to the ¥82,792 m equity-method line), and its measurement period differs from TPC's own kessan kokoku (TPC's fiscal year ends February).
