**THE [E4-55] FINDING — a RECORDED SWEEP, worded per the absence-claim rule.** Sweep run
by the analyst over every vintage on disk (FY2016 Ex-13, FY2017 Ex-13, FY2018, FY2019,
FY2020, FY2022, FY2023, FY2024, FY2025, FY2026 10-Ks and the Q2 FY2027 10-Q) for the
terms: "average ticket", "ticket", "transactions increased", "transaction growth",
"traffic", "number of transactions", "average transaction", "customer traffic",
"comp transactions". **No numeric transactions/ticket decomposition was found in any
10-K or 10-Q vintage.** Every hit is either (a) a risk-factor boilerplate list item
("customer traffic and average ticket in Walmart's stores and clubs..."), or (b) a
qualitative MD&A attribution sentence of the form quoted above. Walmart files the
DIRECTION of both components and never the magnitude of either.

**BUT the split IS filed quarterly — in the 8-K earnings release, not the 10-K.** The
"Business Highlights and Strategic Initiatives" table of ex-99.1 gives numeric quarterly
transactions % and average ticket %; the FULL-YEAR columns of the same table read
**"NP - Not provided."** Filed examples:
- Q4 FY2026 ER (8-K acc 0000104169-26-000032, 2026-02-19): Walmart U.S. comp ex-fuel
  +4.6% / transactions **+2.6%** / average ticket **+2.0%** / eComm contribution ~520bps.
  Prior-year quarter: +4.6% / **+2.8%** / **+1.8%** / ~290bps. FY columns: NP.
  Sam's Club U.S. Q4: comp ex-fuel +4.0% / transactions **+5.3%** / ticket **−1.3%**.
- Q2 FY2027 ER (8-K acc 0000104169-26-000145, 2026-08-20): Walmart U.S. comp ex-fuel
  +2.6% / transactions **+1.5%** / ticket **+1.1%** / eComm ~510bps. Prior-year quarter
  +4.6% / **+1.5%** / **+3.1%** / ~420bps. Sam's Q2 FY27: +4.4% / **+7.0%** / **−2.5%**.
So: the decomposition exists at rung 2 (SEC 8-K exhibit) quarterly, and is deliberately
withheld annually. That is the honest statement of the [E4-55] position for this filer.

## E. THE HIGH-MARGIN STREAMS, as filed
- **Global advertising business: "grew 46% to nearly $6.4 billion, including VIZIO"**
  (FY2026 full-year highlight, Q4 FY26 ER, 8-K acc 0000104169-26-000032, 2026-02-19).
  Q4 FY26 alone +37%; "Walmart Connect in the U.S. up 41%". Q2 FY27: Walmart Connect
  ex-VIZIO +41%; International advertising +20% "led by Flipkart Ads".
  **CRITICAL FILED FOOTNOTE:** "Our global advertising business is recorded in **either
  net sales or as a reduction to cost of sales**, depending on the nature of the
  advertising arrangement." So advertising is NOT a disclosed revenue line; part of it
  lands in the gross-margin rate. It is also inside the comp: the 10-K's comp definition
  includes "certain other business offerings that are part of our ecosystem, **such as
  our advertising net sales**."
  **ABSENCE (recorded sweep):** searched all 10-K vintages FY2022-FY2026 for a dollar
  advertising-revenue figure — none found. The only "advertising" dollars in the 10-K are
  advertising **COSTS** (expense): FY2026 $5.4bn · FY2025 $5.1bn · FY2024 $4.4bn ·
  FY2023 $4.1bn · FY2022 $3.9bn · FY2021 $3.2bn · FY2020 $3.7bn. The $6.4bn revenue
  figure exists only in the earnings release (rung 2, 8-K exhibit).
- **Membership income:** filed only inside "Membership and other income" — consolidated
  $6,750M FY2026 / $6,447M FY2025 / $5,488M FY2024; Walmart U.S. $2,624 / $2,594 /
  $1,985; International $1,565 / $1,478 / $1,408; Sam's the remainder (~$2,525M FY2026).
  ER: "Membership fee revenue grew 15.1% globally" (Q4 FY26). MD&A: Walmart U.S.
  increases "primarily driven by **double-digit growth in membership fee revenue from
  Walmart+**". **ABSENCE: Walmart+ revenue, member count and renewal rate are not
  quantified in any 10-K vintage read (FY2022-FY2026); Sam's Club paid-member count and
  renewal rate are likewise not disclosed** (the COST run recorded the same absence).
- **Marketplace / fulfillment services:** no revenue quantification found in any 10-K.
- **eCommerce:** ER only — global eCommerce +24% in Q4 FY26, +17% International Q4,
  Walmart U.S. eComm +27% in Q4 FY26.
