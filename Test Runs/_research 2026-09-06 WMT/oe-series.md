### Flags (oddities, not smoothed)

1. **FY2018 restricted-cash recast (ASU 2016-18).** In the 18K vintage the FY2018 cash-flow statement reconciles "Cash and cash equivalents" (end of year $6,756M; "Net cash used in investing activities (9,060)"; "Other investing activities (58)"). In the 20K vintage the same FY2018 column reconciles "Cash, cash equivalents and restricted cash" (end of year $7,014M; investing total (9,079); other investing (77) — a $19M shift into investing). The eight target lines are unaffected.
2. **FY2024 investing reclass between vintages.** 24K vintage shows for FY2024: "Proceeds from disposal of certain operations 135" and "Other investing activities (1,057)". 26K vintage shows for FY2024: "Proceeds from disposal of certain strategic investments —" and "Other investing activities (922)"; the certain-operations line is gone. 135 + (1,057) = (922); "Net cash used in investing activities (21,287)" is identical in both. Line-level reclass only.
3. **Caption drift on the investment gains add-back:** "Net unrealized and realized (gains) and losses" (23K/24K) became "Investment (gains) and losses, net" (26K). FY2024 amount identical (3,193) under both captions.
4. **SBC scope wording drift:** 18K footnote says "compensation expense recognized for all plans"; 20K and later say "for all stock incentive plans, including expense associated with plans of the Company's consolidated subsidiaries granted in the subsidiaries' respective stock." FY2018 total is 626 in both vintages, so no measured discontinuity at the seam.
5. **PhonePe modification inside FY2026 SBC.** 26K, Note 3 area (share-based compensation footnote and preceding note): "During fiscal 2026, the Company's PhonePe subsidiary modified certain of its share-based payment arrangements in contemplation of a potential initial public offering. Upon modification, the Company recorded a non-cash charge of $0.7 billion (a portion of which was based on grant-date fair value) in operating, selling, general and administrative expenses within the Walmart International segment, primarily related to previously unrecognized share-based compensation expense under these arrangements." The footnote states this $0.7B is included in the "Other" line of the SBC-by-award-type table, i.e. inside the FY2026 total of $3,603M.
6. **FY2019 net income outlier is as filed**: NI attr. to Walmart $6,670M (20K vintage) against "Other (gains) and losses 8,368" on the FY2019 income statement (unrealized losses, principally the JD.com/investment mark and Asda-related items per that vintage's MD&A). Not an OCR error.
