# Notes

## A. Two definitional breaks, in the company's own words

These are the reason the series is **not** continuously comparable end to end. Both are transcribed
verbatim from the release footnotes.

**Break 1 — "Traffic" becomes "Transactions", Q1 FY20 (release 2019-05-16, acc 0000104169-19-000021, footnote 2):**

> "In the first quarter of FY20, we updated our definition of traffic as a component of comparable
> sales to be all sales transactions in our stores as well as for eCommerce. Traffic will now be
> called transactions. For comparability, we revised this metric for FY19 and have provided a
> quarterly summary on our website at http://www.stock.wa[lmart.com]"

Consequence: every FY19 quarter carries **two** published values — the as-first-reported "Traffic"
figure (in the table above) and the restated "Transactions" figure (visible only as the prior-year
column of the FY20 releases). Both are listed in section B.

**Break 2 — eCommerce net sales redefined, Q1 FY21 (release 2020-05-19, acc 0000104169-20-000016, footnote 4):**

> "Beginning in Q1 FY21, we revised our definition of eCommerce net sales to include certain pharmacy
> transactions. Accordingly, we revised prior period amounts for Walmart U.S. and Sam's Club to be
> consistent with the current year's presentation."

Consequence: every FY20 quarter's **eCommerce contribution to comp** carries two published values.
Both are listed in section B.

**Definitional note on advertising:** every release carries the same footnote — "Our global advertising
business is recorded in either net sales or as a reduction to cost of sales, depending on the nature of
the advertising arrangement." The advertising growth rate therefore cannot be tied to a single income
statement line. The only absolute dollar figures ever given are: $2.1bn (FY22), $2.7bn (FY23),
$3.4bn (FY24), $4.4bn (FY25), nearly $6.4bn (FY26). From Q1 FY26 the global figure **includes VIZIO**
(acquired), so it is not comparable to prior years without the stated ex-VIZIO Walmart Connect number.

**Definitional note on membership:** the label changed from "Membership income" (through FY25) to
"Membership and other income" with membership income broken out (Q1-Q3 FY26) to "Membership fee
revenue" (Q4 FY26 forward). These are not the same base. Sam's Club U.S. membership growth is a
separate and materially lower number than the global figure in every quarter where both are given.

## E. Other oddities recorded, not smoothed

1. **"flat" appears as a value, not a number.** Q1 FY23 Walmart U.S. Transactions = "flat";
   Q1 FY25 Walmart U.S. Average Ticket = "flat". Transcribed as written.
2. **"< 10 bps" appears once.** Q3 FY22 Walmart U.S. eCommerce contribution. Not a point estimate.
3. **Negative bps printed in parentheses in some vintages, with a minus sign in others.**
   Q4 FY22 = "~(80) bps"; Q1 FY23 = "~-30 bps" in its own release but "~(30) bps" in the Q1 FY24
   prior-year column. Same value, two presentations.
4. **Stray spaces inside figures in the Q3 FY22 release** ("24.0 %", "6.8 %", "4.0 %"). Typesetting
   artifact in the filed document; preserved in the raw `.txt`, normalised only for the table cell.
5. **eCommerce contribution is always approximate.** Every value is prefixed "~" and rounded to
   10 bps. It is a company estimate, not a measured figure.
6. **The comp-sales retail calendar is not the fiscal quarter.** Footnote in every release: comps are
   a 13-week retail ("4-5-4") period that does not coincide with the fiscal quarter end. The Q4 FY26
   release adds: "In certain years, it becomes necessary to add a 53rd week to our comparable sales
   reporting calendar, which occurred in fiscal 2025."
7. **Sam's Club comp is materially affected by tobacco de-emphasis in several quarters** and the
   company says so: Q1 FY22 "Reduced tobacco sales negatively affected comp sales by approximately
   340 basis points"; Q1 FY21 "approximately 410 basis points"; Q1 FY19 "approximately 140 basis
   points". The reported comp is not a clean read on member demand in those quarters.
8. **Sam's Club average ticket has been negative for four straight quarters** (Q3 FY26 -0.1%,
   Q4 FY26 -1.3%, Q1 FY27 -2.2%, Q2 FY27 -2.5%) while transactions ran +3.9% to +7.0%. Recorded as a
   transcription fact; no interpretation offered here.
9. **The Walmart U.S. comp row is labelled "Comp sales", not "Comp sales (ex. fuel)", in the four
   FY17 releases** (Q1 FY17 - Q4 FY17); Sam's Club carries "(ex. fuel)" in the same releases. Checked
   against the "U.S. comparable store sales results" supplemental in the Q1 FY17 release
   (acc 0000104169-16-000083): Walmart U.S. "Without Fuel" 1.0% / "With Fuel" 1.0% / "Fuel Impact"
   0.0% — identical, so the figure in the table above is ex-fuel in substance. The label differs, the
   number does not. Sam's Club in that same quarter: Without Fuel 0.1% vs With Fuel -1.9%, a 2.0 pt
   fuel impact — for Sam's the distinction is material and the "(ex. fuel)" row is the one transcribed.

## F. Method and provenance

- Source of the filing list: `submissions.json` and `submissions-001.json` (SEC EDGAR submissions API
  for CIK 0000104169), filtered to form 8-K carrying Item **2.02**.
- 58 earnings 8-Ks identified from 2012-05-17 to 2026-08-20. All 58 filing indexes fetched; the
  EX-99.1 document was identified from the index type column in every case — no ambiguity, no
  fallback heuristic used.
- Documents fetched from `https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/104169/<acc-no-dashes>/<doc>` with
  header `User-Agent: BRK-research chrehor36@gmail.com`, saved to disk, then tag-stripped to `.txt`
  locally. No aggregator was used at any point.
- Extraction is regex over the local text, anchored on the "Transactions"/"Traffic" row; the first
  such block in each release is Walmart U.S., the second is Sam's Club U.S. Verified against the
  explicit segment headers ("Walmart U.S.", "Sam's Club U.S.") where the release prints them as text
  rather than as an image, and against segment net-sales magnitude in every release.
- Independent check on the whole table: the year-ahead prior-year column agreement described in
  section B. It is a genuine check because the two figures come from different documents filed a
  year apart.
- Helper scripts left in this directory: `fetch_releases.py`, `dl_releases.py`, `extract2.py`,
  `final.py`, `xcheck.py`, `opinc.py`, `labels2.py`, `metrics.py`, `seg_bullets.py`, `writemd.py`.
  Intermediates: `final.json`, `labels.json`, `manifest.json`, `manifest2.json`, `narrative.txt`,
  `seg_bullets.txt`, `metrics.txt`.
