# Company Run — HydroGraph Clean Power (HGRAF) — 2026-07-20
Framework v3.0. User-requested single-name run. Canadian (CSE) graphene-
materials company; OTC foreign-ordinary ticker. **Data limitation stated
up front**: no auditable financial history available through this
project's sources (stockanalysis carries no income statement; Canadian
filings live on SEDAR, not EDGAR) — price ~$3.48 and cap ~$1.19B are
aggregator reads (2026-07-20, flagged). The verdict below is robust to
that imprecision.

## STEP 0 — RATE REFRESH
US 30-yr 5.10%; micro/illiquid premium would set the hurdle at 7.10% —
**never reached** (see below).

## GATE 1 — CIRCLE OF COMPETENCE
The business as described is understandable (manufacture graphene via
detonation synthesis; sell it to industrial users). But Gate 1 requires
**evaluable unit economics** — and a pre-commercial company has
projections, not economics: no established price, volume, cost curve, or
customer base to evaluate. What exists to analyze is a technology claim
and a story. **VERDICT: FAIL — STOP**, on the classification question
both frameworks share:
- The corpus's standard: the framework analyzes BUSINESSES with owner
  earnings; a pre-earnings venture has nothing for Gates 2-8 to grip —
  no moat evidence (the franchise test's "proven by pricing power +
  returns on capital" prong is unfulfillable where there are no
  returns), no OE tier table (years of losses fail range anchoring
  automatically), no fortress question that share-issuance dependence
  doesn't answer against it.
- Graham's ledger anchor [SA, book p.54, eye-verified]: *"An investment
  operation is one which, upon thorough analysis, promises safety of
  principal and a satisfactory return. Operations not meeting these
  requirements are speculative."* Thorough analysis is not possible on
  projections; therefore no promise of safety of principal is available
  at any price. **This is a speculation by definition — not pejoratively,
  taxonomically.**

## RUN TERMINATED AT GATE 1 — NOT ANALYZABLE AS AN INVESTMENT
No Gate 2-8 material may be produced; no valuation exists to compute
(a DCF on projected commercialization would be exactly the invented-
precision this framework forbids). For the record, the mechanical facts
that would each independently terminate later gates anyway: negative
worst-year earnings (auto-fail, the same test that rejected 370 of 881
S&P small/mids), equity funded by issuance rather than retention, and a
~$1.19B price on a pre-commercial revenue base.

## What would change this verdict
Nothing about the technology — only the arrival of the thing the
framework analyzes: **several consecutive years of positive, audited
owner earnings from commercial operations.** If graphene wins and
HydroGraph becomes a business, the framework will be able to see it then
— at the cost of missing the venture-stage upside, which is the explicit,
accepted price of the discipline (the corpus's no-called-strike rule:
pitches outside the zone are not strikes against the watcher).
