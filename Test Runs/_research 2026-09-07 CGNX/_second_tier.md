**THIS SEGMENT MARGIN IS NOT COMPARABLE TO NI'S OLD OPERATING MARGIN. Do not put them in the
same column of a competitor table.** Four reasons, three of them sourced from Emerson's own
definition:

1. **Purchase accounting.** Emerson wrote up $5,275M of intangibles and $3,442M of goodwill
   at the acquisition. The amortization of those intangibles is a cost that did not exist in
   NI's own income statement and never could have. NI's FY2022 operating margin of 11.6%
   was computed on a book with no acquired-intangible amortization; Emerson's (19.8)% in
   FY2024 is computed on a book carrying it.
2. **Emerson's segment measure is defined differently.** Verbatim, FY2025 10-K segment note:
   > "The primary income measure used for assessing segment performance and making operating
   > decisions is earnings before interest and income taxes. In the statements below, Other
   > deductions is largely comprised of restructuring, intangibles amortization and foreign
   > currency transaction (gains) losses."
   "Other deductions, net" was $644M in FY2024 and $439M in FY2025, i.e. 44.0% and 29.5% of
   segment sales. That single line is the whole difference between a ~74%-gross-margin
   business and a loss-making segment.
3. **Different cost allocation.** Verbatim, same note:
   > "Certain expenses are reported at Corporate, including stock compensation expense and a
   > portion of pension and postretirement benefit costs. Corporate and other includes
   > unallocated corporate expenses, acquisition/divestiture costs, first year acquisition
   > accounting charges (which include fair value adjustments related to inventory, backlog
   > and deferred revenue) and other items."
   So some costs NI used to bear itself are now at Emerson Corporate and are outside the
   segment, while first-year acquisition charges are also outside the segment. The segment
   line is neither NI's old P&L nor a clean carve-out of it.
4. **No R&D line at all.** Emerson's segment table gives Net sales, Cost of sales, SG&A,
   Other deductions and Earnings. **R&D expense for Test & Measurement is UNAVAILABLE**;
   obstacle: Emerson does not disclose R&D by segment. The last observable NI R&D figure is
   FY2022, $331.7M / 20.0% of sales.

**The one comparison that does survive.** Gross margin. NI's own FY2022 gross margin was
67.7%; Emerson's Test & Measurement gross margin was 73.6% in FY2024 and 74.4% in FY2025.
Both are computed the same way (net sales less cost of sales), and gross margin is the line
least disturbed by purchase accounting, though the FY2024 figure is still affected by
inventory step-up amortization, which Emerson says it recorded but reports at Corporate under
"first year acquisition accounting charges", not in the segment.

**One presentation change to be aware of.** In the FY2025 10-K Emerson merged the AspenTech
segment into Control Systems & Software and restated prior years (AspenTech's remaining
shares were bought in on 2025-03-12). **Test & Measurement was not affected**: FY2024 sales
of $1,464M and earnings of $(290)M are identical in both the FY2024 and FY2025 10-Ks.

## B. PRIVATE / NON-SEGMENTED PLAYERS

### B.1 MVTec SOFTWARE GmbH (Munich): **PRIVATE**

**What it does.** MVTec sells the machine-vision software layer rather than cameras: HALCON,
a general-purpose machine-vision library, plus MERLIC (a configuration-based vision product),
a Deep Learning Tool, embedded vision runtimes, and calibration plates. Source: MVTec's own
About Us page, https://www.mvtec.com/about-us , which also states, verbatim: "With over 300
experts, we innovate and turn cutting-edge technologies into global successes" and "Founded in
1996 as a spin-off of the Technical University of Munich and FORWISS, MVTec is a family-owned
company." This makes MVTec a **direct competitor to Cognex's software stack (VisionPro,
In-Sight software, VIDI)** rather than to its smart cameras, which matters for how the
competitor row should be read: the two overlap on the highest-margin part of Cognex's product.

**STATUS: PRIVATE. Revenue, gross margin, operating margin and R&D are all UNAVAILABLE from a
primary source.**

**What was checked and what the obstacle is.**
1. **Company disclosure: none.** The MVTec press-release archive at
   https://www.mvtec.com/company/press-room/press-releases carries only product and
   trade-show announcements. No fiscal-year results release exists on the company site. The
   About Us page states an employee count and no financial figure.
2. **Bundesanzeiger: filings exist but are not retrievable without an interactive session.**
   MVTec Software GmbH is registered at Amtsgericht München, **HRB 114695**. Annual accounts
   are on file for fiscal years **2005 through 2025**, the most recent being the annual report
   for the year ending 31/12/2025, filed **11 August 2026**. Obstacle: the Bundesanzeiger
   search is a session-based JSF application that cannot be reached by direct URL, and the
   third-party mirror that indexes those filings (northdata.com, HRB 114695) paywalls the
   figures. The existence, register number and filing dates above come from that index; the
   underlying numbers were not obtained.
   *Further obstacle to note even if the filing is later pulled:* a German GmbH of MVTec's
   size may qualify as a small or medium-sized company under HGB and file abridged accounts,
   in which case the filing may contain a balance sheet and notes but **no profit and loss
   account**, so revenue and operating margin may not exist in the public filing at all. Do
   not assume the Bundesanzeiger filing will yield a revenue line until it has been read.
3. **Trade-press figures exist. They are NOT primary and must be flagged.** German trade
   publication wileyindustrynews.com has reported MVTec revenue of approximately **EUR 38
   million** for a recent fiscal year (reported as a 4-5% increase) and **EUR 29.1 million**
   for fiscal 2019 (reported as a near-10% increase). **SECONDARY / TRADE PRESS ONLY, not
   sourced to an MVTec release or filing.** Do not put either number in the competitor table
   as a fact.
4. **Aggregator figures exist and are worse.** getlatka.com carries "$32.4M revenue with a 216
   person team" and growjo/ZoomInfo/Tracxn carry their own estimates.
   **AGGREGATOR-ONLY. Unusable.** Note that the aggregator employee count (216) contradicts
   MVTec's own "over 300 experts", which is itself a reason to distrust the revenue estimate
   attached to it.

**How to write the row:** MVTec, PRIVATE, German GmbH, HRB 114695 Munich, machine-vision
software (HALCON/MERLIC), 300+ employees per company statement, **financials not publicly
available**. Order of magnitude on trade-press reporting is roughly EUR 30-40 million of
revenue, i.e. **about 2% of Cognex's scale**, which is the only honest thing to say about
size, and it should be labelled as trade press.

lower-margin, sensor-hardware business. It is not the margin comparable for Cognex; it is
evidence about what happens to a sensor business without Cognex's software attach.
