## GATE 2 — MOAT
Franchise test [E3-03], from EOG's own 10-K: **"EOG competes with major
integrated oil and gas companies, government-affiliated oil and gas
companies and other independent oil and gas companies for the acquisition
of licenses, concessions and leases, properties and reserves and access to
the facilities, equipment, materials, services, and employees and other
personnel... necessary to explore for, develop, produce, market and
transport crude oil, NGLs and natural gas. Certain of EOG's competitors
have financial and other resources substantially greater than those EOG
possesses..."**
- **No close substitute**: **FAILS** — a barrel of EOG's oil is priced off
  the same global benchmark as any competitor's barrel of comparable
  grade; the company's own filing frames competition as being for inputs
  (leases, equipment, personnel), not for a differentiated output.
- **Unregulated**: **FAILS** — same industry-wide regulatory exposure
  (environmental, drilling, production regulation) already documented at
  APA.
- **Proven by pricing power + returns on capital**: **FAILS** — the
  company's own words concede competitors "have financial and other
  resources substantially greater," a disadvantage, not evidence of
  pricing power. Net income declined from $7,759M (FY2022) to $4,980M
  (FY2025), a 36% decline over three years tracking commodity-price
  movement, not operational execution.
**Class: NONE.** **VERDICT: FAIL — STOP** — same structural conclusion as
APA Corporation, independently re-derived from EOG's own filing language
rather than assumed by sector.
