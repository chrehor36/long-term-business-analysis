### WHY Q2 CLOSED, ON SMCI'S OWN RECORD (the DELL run's SMCI figures were not imported)
- **The company says it has no pricing power, twice.** FY2026 and FY2025 MD&A: gross margin fell *"primarily due to our strategy to offer competitive pricing to
  gain market share"*; Item 1A: *"a continued decline of average selling prices across our business and we expect that these historical trends will continue"*, with
  ODMs *"that benefit from their scale and very low-cost manufacturing"* named as the pressure.
- **The product is the supplier's.** *"One supplier accounted for 63.1 %, 64.4 %, and 65.4 % of total purchases"*; every FY2026 system line is an NVIDIA platform,
  which Dell, HPE, Lenovo and the ODMs also sell.
- **The row**: gross margin **SMCI 13.75%, 11.06%, 10.82% (FY2024-26)**; Dell ISG 38.19% to 25.98%; HPE Server 29.41% to 20.89%; **Celestica 9.5% to 12.1%**; NVIDIA
  71-75%. **SMCI now earns less gross margin than the contract manufacturer in the row.** Operating margin before stock compensation 7.1-9.6% against Dell ISG
  11.7-12.8%, HPE Server 7.6-12.7%, Celestica CCS 6.2-8.2%. Incremental gross profit **9.2 cents per incremental dollar** FY2023-26. Two of the seven rivals the 10-K
  names are in the row; the Taiwanese ODMs are not on EDGAR, and the gate does not close on that gap.
- **The full-strength case, recorded**: pre-tax return on invested capital **21-43% (FY2022-26)**, capital turning 3.5-4.5 times a year; Q4 FY2026 gross margin
  **17.5%** against the company's own 8.2-8.4% guidance; share taken from larger rivals. Read as turnover times a thin margin that moves with the wave (10.7-17.5% in
  FY2016-21) [E4-36, E3-51], and as one quarter.
- **[E2-44](2) fails on working capital**: operating cash **-$6,809.9M** in FY2026 on $9,648.3M of working-capital build; FY2024 -$2,486.0M. **[E4-04]** engaged: the

  [E4-23]**: *"We currently do not have a succession plan for the replacement of Mr. Liang."*
