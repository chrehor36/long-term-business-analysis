## Q2 — IS IT A FRANCHISE? **[E3-03]**
*The analyst's incentive, stated before the reading (operator rule 9, [E4-27], [E4-26]): the quote fell 17.9% on one day this month and half the market value is cash, which invites a reader to hurry the business through to the price question to see how cheap it is; the register also holds more Q2 closes than gate-clearers, which invites the opposite lean. Neither the price nor the tally belongs in this question. Because the reading below closes the file, **the case for IN is written first and at full strength** [E4-51], and [E3-47]'s warning is carried: a wrongly closed file inside the circle is the error the corpus rates most expensive.*

### THE STRONGEST EVIDENCE AGAINST THIS VERDICT (the case for IN), stated as its holders would state it [E4-51, E4-26, E3-47]
1. **The installed base is sticky and pays in advance.** Contracts run about three years (*"We generally offer these services through a 36-month contract"*), are invoiced a year ahead, and carry $87.0M of remaining performance obligations against $91.1M of annual revenue; deferred revenue has held at $48.5-50.5M for five years. Switching means rewriting and re-approving a regulated company's whole library of answers and rewiring it into the call-centre desktop: the classic enterprise switching cost.
2. **The business needs no capital.** Property spending was $0.1-0.6M a year FY2017-FY2026 (0.2-0.9% of revenue); customers fund the working capital. Equity less goodwill, deferred tax assets and cash is **negative every year FY2019-FY2026 (about −$21M to −$30M)**, so operating income on net tangible capital [E2-43, E3-46] is not a finite ratio: every dollar the business earns is earned on the customers' money. [E2-44]'s second characteristic (*"an ability to accommodate large dollar volume increases in business ... with only minor additional investment of capital"*) passes plainly.
3. **A platform owner has paid a royalty on it for years.** Cisco embeds eGain in its contact-centre product and pays per agent ($10.9M in FY2026, up 3%); the FY2019 10-K: *"Our key channel partner today is Cisco"*. The IRS runs on it (*"The Internal Revenue Service uses our solutions served from the eGain Cloud."*), and it holds FedRAMP.
4. **Third-party recognition at the moment the category forms.** *"In July 2026, Gartner named eGain a Leader in the first-ever Magic Quadrant for Customer Service Knowledge Management Systems, positioned highest for Ability to Execute and furthest for Completeness of Vision."*
5. **Gross margin rose and the AI product is growing.** Gross margin 63.6% (FY2017-FY2018) to 73.4% (FY2026); *"AI customers | 55,129 | 45,920"* (+20%); AI customer annual recurring revenue +13% to $54.3M. Operating margin 8.7% in FY2026 is the best since FY2011.
6. **The competitor row places it above most of its public rivals** (below): Upland, Five9, LivePerson and Pegasystems lost money in most of FY2017-FY2025 while eGain earned a small profit in seven of the last eight years.
