# COMPETITOR ROW: alternative asset managers, FY2021-FY2025, filing-sourced

Research file for the BN run of 2026-09-13. Facts only; no investment conclusion is drawn here.
All figures USD millions unless stated. Every figure below was read in the filed document text
(HTML primary document downloaded from EDGAR, converted to text and saved in this folder as
`<TICKER>_<FORM>_FY<YEAR>.txt`). Fee rates are my arithmetic, shown in full.

Fee rate convention used for every firm (arithmetic, not a filer figure):
`management fees for year / ((fee-paying AUM at prior year end + fee-paying AUM at year end) / 2)`.
A two-point average; filers that report their own fee rates or quarterly averages will differ.

### Perimeter by year
- **FY2025 10-K:** "For accounting purposes and for periods prior to the closing date of the 2025
  Arrangement ... references to the historical financial information of BAM are to the historical
  financial statements of Brookfield Asset Management ULC (the "Asset Management Company") as the
  "Predecessor" of BAM." GAAP 2025 = combined entity after 2025-02-04; 2023-2024 = ULC predecessor.
- **FRE table, all years:** "Base management fees and direct costs are presented on a 100% basis for
  BAM and BAM's investment in Oaktree" (FY2025 10-K note 1). So Fee Revenues, direct costs and the
  pre-NCI FRE line are the whole asset management business including Oaktree at 100%, in every year,
  regardless of whether BAM Ltd owned 25% (2022-2024) or 100% (2025) of ULC.
- **FY2022 20-F and FY2023 40-F:** "The "Asset Management Company", the "asset management business",
  "BAM ULC", or the "Company" refers to Brookfield Asset Management ULC" (40-F). The FRE table is the
  asset management business at 100%; BAM Ltd (the "Manager") held ~25%.

### FRE definition (FY2025 10-K, Key Financial and Operating Measures)
"Fee-Related Earnings is comprised of Fee Revenues less direct costs associated with earning those
fees, which include employee compensation and professional fees as well as business related technology
costs, and other shared services costs. Fee-Related Earnings exclude revenues and expenses of
consolidated funds." Fee Revenues "include base management fees, incentive distributions, performance
fees and transaction fees. Fee Revenues exclude carried interest".

**SBC treatment:** FRE is NOT stated before equity-based compensation. Equity-based compensation is
added back only in the step from FRE to Distributable Earnings: "Add back: equity-based compensation
costs 4 | 44 | 38 | 39" with note "4. This adjustment adds back equity-based compensation costs"
(FY2025 10-K). In the NI-to-FRE reconciliation there is also "Compensation costs recovered from
affiliates (h) | 298 | 218 | 156" (2025/2024/2023). BAM also includes IDRs from BEP/BIP in Fee Revenues.
**FRE margin is not stated as a percentage in BAM's own 10-K** (searched "margin": only operating
margin and credit facility margins). Margin below = my arithmetic on the pre-NCI line.
