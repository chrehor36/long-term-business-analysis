# Company Run — Mid-America Apartment Communities (MAA) — 2026-07-15
Framework v3.0. From the REIT mechanical screen. OE from real FY2025 10-K
data (Archives/edgar/data/912595).

## STEP 0 — RATE REFRESH
US 30-yr **5.10%**. Mkt cap $15.72B — no size premium.

## GATE 1 — CIRCLE OF COMPETENCE
Owns and operates apartment communities across the Sun Belt (Texas,
Florida, Georgia, the Carolinas, Tennessee) — a different geography from
AVB/EQR's coastal-gateway focus, but the same underlying rent × occupancy
− opex − capex economics. Single business type, one segment in substance.
**VERDICT: PASS.**

## GATE 2 — MOAT
Franchise test [E3-03]: needed/desired — yes; no close substitute — **weak**
(Sun Belt apartment supply has actually been unusually elastic in recent
years — heavy multifamily construction pipelines in Texas/Florida specifically
are a well-known, real risk to this thesis, more so than in AVB/EQR's more
supply-constrained coastal markets); unregulated — largely yes, Sun Belt
states generally lack the rent-control exposure AVB/EQR carry. **Class:
NARROW-to-NONE** — genuinely weaker moat case than the coastal peers,
given the more elastic supply response in MAA's core markets. Revenue trend
not independently pulled this pass.
**VERDICT: PASS with NARROW (weak) classification — the softest moat read
of the REITs run so far.**

## GATE 3 — MANAGEMENT
CEO **Brad Hill, appointed President and CEO effective April 1, 2025** — a
genuinely recent transition (roughly 15 months of tenure as of this
analysis), the least-tenured CEO of the REITs run so far. Governance
includes a standard anti-hedging/derivative-trading prohibition; a
related-person-transaction policy was not found under that exact phrase in
this pass (not confirmed absent, just not located — flagged). No litigation/
restatement/integrity flags found.
**VERDICT: PASS**, but the CEO-tenure and related-party-policy gaps are
real, not cosmetic — this is the weakest Gate 3 evidence base of the batch.

## GATE 4 — FINANCIAL QUALITY (full OE formula, real maintenance capex)
Used "Capital improvements and other" from the cash flow statement (separate
from "Purchases of real estate" [acquisitions] and "Development costs"
[new construction] — MAA's own 10-K text notes this line blends "recurring
capital replacements" with "redevelopment and repositioning" activity, so it
may overstate true maintenance-only spend somewhat):

| FY | Net income (common) | Depr. (real estate) | Capital improvements | **OE = NI+D&A−capex** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | $523.855M | $579.927M | $322.372M | **$781.410M** |
| 2025 | $443.221M | $616.774M | $360.238M | **$699.757M** |

**Only 2 years pulled this pass** (not 3) — a real limitation flagged
honestly rather than papered over; FY2023 not independently verified.
**LOWEST VERIFIED TIER (of years available): $699.757M (FY2025).**
Fortress (financial/leverage-ceiling track): Total assets $11,975.383M ÷
Total equity $5,819.243M = **2.058:1** — far under the 10:1 ceiling.
**VERDICT: PASS** (financial-quality gate; the OE trend itself — declining
year over year — is a real yellow flag carried into Gate 5/6, not grounds
for a Gate 4 fail on its own).

## GATE 5 — INVERSION
1. **Moat destruction** — Sun Belt oversupply is not hypothetical; it is
   the live, current explanation for MAA's declining OE (see Gate 4 trend).
   Scored **[DS]** — being addressed via geographic mix, but not resolved.
2. **Management/key-person** — brand-new CEO (15 months), no track record
   yet under this framework's lens. Scored **[P]**.
3. **Balance-sheet stress** — 2.06:1 assets/equity, comfortably under
   ceiling. Scored **[D]**.
4. **Regulatory** — comparatively low rent-control exposure in Sun Belt
   states. Scored **[D]**.

No [U], but the combination of a **declining OE trend + new CEO + the
weakest moat read in this batch** is a materially different risk profile
than AVB/EQR/INVH, even before reaching valuation.
**Thesis-breaking metric:** a third consecutive year of OE decline, or
same-store NOI growth turning negative in a majority of core markets.
**VERDICT: PASS**, but flagged as the weakest Gate 5 case in the batch —
the qualitative picture already points toward caution before Gate 6 is run.

## GATE 6 — VALUATION
**6-B Statute** (lowest tier, $699.757M): yield = 699.757 / 15,720 =
**4.45%** vs. hurdle **5.10%** → **FAILS.** This is the first REIT in this
batch that does not clear the Statute even on the corrected OE (consistent
with the earlier NI+D&A screen, which also showed MAA failing at 3.58% —
the only REIT to fail on both bases in that preliminary comparison).
**Book Two DCF**: not built — moot given the Statute failure and the
declining-OE trend already flagged at Gate 5; a DCF would need to assume a
turnaround this run has no evidence for yet.
**VERDICT: WAIT.** Not eliminated (Gates 1–5 all passed), but does not
clear the bar for any entry-permission language at current price.
