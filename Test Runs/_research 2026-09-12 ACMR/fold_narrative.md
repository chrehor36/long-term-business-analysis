### THE GHC PROBLEM IN ITS LISTED-SUBSIDIARY FORM — AND THIS TIME IT WAS FOUND FIRST

ACM Research is a Delaware holding company whose operating business is **ACM Research (Shanghai),
STAR Market 688082.SS**. The brief named the GHC and BAM/BN precedents and asked what share of owner
earnings belongs to ACMR's own holders. **The filings answer it in four places, all read:**
- **Net-income attribution: 77.2% (FY2025)**, 77.7–79.9% in FY2022–24.
- **Ownership: 82.5% (FY2022) → 82.1% → 81.5% → 74.6% (FY2025) → 73.2% (2026-06-30)** — the September
  2025 private offering (38,601,326 shares at RMB 116.11, net $623.0M), ACM Research's own sale of
  4,801,648 Shanghai shares at RMB 160.00 on 2026-02-06, and 2,431,900 Shanghai options exercised by
  Shanghai employees on 2026-05-12.
- **Parent-only statements (Rule 4-08(e)(3), note 20):** registrant operating cash +$7,555k (2025);
  **90.5% of registrant net income is an equity-method pick-up**; registrant cash $96,184k.
- **Cash by jurisdiction (note 2):** at 2025-12-31 **8.5% of the group's $1,132.6M sat at the registrant**;
  52.6% in mainland China behind SAFE approval and fixed deposit terms; 37.2% in Hong Kong inside
  CleanChip — **which ACM Research sold to ACM Shanghai in December 2019 for $3.5M, before the STAR
  IPO**. **Restricted net assets of $1,675,187k exceed ACM Research's entire equity.**

**The difference from GHC is the useful part.** GHC's distortion was a mandatorily redeemable NCI — a
liability. ACMR's is an **ordinary minority in a listed subsidiary that keeps issuing its own shares**,
so it shows up as a *falling percentage* and a *rising APIC* (the subsidiary issues above book, so ACM
Research's equity went from $904.6M to $1,464.4M in FY2025 without an ACMR share being sold). **The
dilution looks like value creation in the consolidated balance sheet and like dilution only in the
ownership footnote.** Combined with the registrant's own issuance, **a 2024 ACMR share's look-through
claim on the operating business is 0.812 as large nineteen months later.**

### THE STRONGEST SINGLE FACT AGAINST THE VERDICT **[E4-51]**
**ACMR's ~353M ACM Shanghai shares at the filed February 2026 transfer price (RMB 160.00 ≈ $23.05) carry a
quoted value of roughly $8.1bn — against ACMR's own $5.0bn cap**, before any registrant cash. A
sum-of-the-parts buyer calls that a 38% discount. **It is a spread between two quotes, not a yield**; the
stake cannot be distributed (*"Stockholders of ACM Research may never directly own equity interests in ACM
Shanghai"*); and the only way the registrant has ever realised it is by selling Shanghai shares, which
lowers the 73.2%. And it prices a company with negative owner earnings at ~$11bn in Shanghai, which is
the quote [E2-28] tells an owner to sell into.
