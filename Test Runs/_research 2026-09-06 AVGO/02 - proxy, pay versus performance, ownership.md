# C — THE METRICS, AND WHETHER THEY ARE GAAP

All from the 2026 proxy, 0001193125-26-085691, unless noted.

## C.1 The annual bonus — the "APB Plan"

The plan is the **Annual Performance Bonus Plan ("APB Plan")**. Three pillars:

| Pillar | Metric(s) | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate Financial Goals | **Revenue** and **Adjusted non-GAAP operating income (as a % of revenue)**, evenly weighted 50/50 | 150% of target per component |
| Division Financial and Strategic Goals | direct expense and fiscal responsibility of the division; divisional strategic goals — **"confidential and proprietary metrics"**, not disclosed | 120% of target |
| Individual Multiplier | strategic (M&A, new product development, innovation, growth); operational excellence; leadership, values & culture | 50% - 150% (0% possible) |

Formula: **Base Salary × Bonus Target (% of salary) × Attainment % of Corporate & Division
Goals × Individual Multiplier.**

**Is it GAAP? Half of it is not.** One of the two corporate financial metrics is explicitly a
company-defined non-GAAP measure — the proxy names it **"Adjusted Non-GAAP Operating Income
(as a % of revenue)"** and directs the reader to a reconciliation: *"See Appendix A for a
reconciliation of non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable GAAP financial
measures."* Revenue itself is GAAP. Note also the proxy uses two different labels for the same
metric — the goal table says "Adjusted Non-GAAP Operating Income (as a % of revenue)" while
the attainment narrative says "adjusted non-GAAP **operating margin** was 68.0%", and the PVP
tabular list calls it "Adjusted Non-GAAP Operating Margin".

Verbatim on metric selection:

> "For fiscal 2025, revenue and adjusted non-GAAP operating income were selected as the
> metrics as they are readily measurable, comparable to peers, relevant to investors and, most
> importantly, short-term indicators of long-term value creation. The target levels were
> designed to require substantial effort to achieve and drive a significant increase in
> stockholder value with the same level of rigor as in prior performance periods."

Hock Tan received **no APB Plan payout** in FY2023, FY2024 or FY2025, and none is intended
through FY2027 — the 2023 Tan PSU Award was front-loaded to cover five years of cash bonus as
well as five years of equity.

## C.2 What the PSUs vest against

Broadcom runs **three different PSU designs simultaneously**:

### (a) 2023 Tan PSU Award — absolute stock-price hurdles
$825 / $950 / $1,125 (pre-split) 20-day average closing price; one-third of shares per hurdle;
**no interpolation**; Earning Period 3rd-to-5th anniversary of the 2022-10-31 grant; vest
2027-10-31. Grant-date price $470.12, so hurdles are +75.5% / +102.1% / +139.3%. Tracking
**above target** as of FY2025 year end; the outstanding-awards table now shows it at
**maximum**. See A.2.

### (b) 2025 Tan PSU Award — "AI Revenue", a company-defined revenue subset
Threshold $60bn = 0%; Target $90bn = 100%; Stretch $105bn = 200%; Maximum ≥$120bn = 300%.
Measured as **the highest aggregate AI Revenue for any four consecutive fiscal quarters**
within FY2028-FY2030, with linear interpolation between levels. See A.2 for the full curve.

The definition of "AI Revenue" is Broadcom's own and is broad — verbatim:

> "...the independent directors selected revenue from our technologies and products used or
> expected to be used by our customers for AI enablement, including but not limited to our
> custom AI accelerators, XPUs, ASICs, networking and connectivity solutions, and AI racks or
> systems based on our XPUs ('AI Revenue') as the performance metric for the 2025 Tan PSU
> Award."

**"used or expected to be used by our customers for AI enablement" and "including but not
limited to" are elastic.** This is not a line on any financial statement; it is a
management-scoped subset of GAAP revenue. Broadcom's mitigation is the last-word clause:
*"the independent directors ... will ultimately determine whether the performance goal has been
achieved under GAAP reporting."* For scale, Broadcom discloses FY2025 AI semiconductor revenue
of **$20.2 billion** (up 65% from $12.2bn in FY2024), and FY2025 total net revenue of
**$63.887 billion**.

### (c) NEO (non-CEO) PSUs — Relative TSR versus the S&P 500 plus an absolute-TSR gate

> "...each PSU award may be earned, if at all, based on our TSR performance relative to the
> S&P 500 ('Relative TSR') and absolute TSR performance, over four overlapping performance
> periods, commencing on March 2nd immediately preceding the applicable March 15th vesting
> start date and ending on March 1st of the first, second, third and fourth years following
> the performance period commencement date."

Payout curve, verbatim:

| Relative TSR | Achievement Factor — periods 1, 2, 3 |
|---|---|
| At or above 50th percentile of S&P 500 | 1 |
| At 25th percentile | 0.5 |
| Below 25th percentile | 0 |

| Relative TSR | Achievement Factor — period 4 |
|---|---|
| At 75th percentile | if absolute TSR negative: **4 minus Prior Achievement Sum**; if neutral or positive: **8 minus Prior Achievement Sum** (aggregate cap 200% of target) |
| At 50th percentile | 4 minus Prior Achievement Sum |
| At 25th percentile | if Prior Achievement Sum ≥ 1.5: 0.5; if < 1.5: 2 minus Prior Achievement Sum |
| Below 25th percentile | 0 |

Periods 1-3 are capped at 25% of target each *"even if performance exceeds target
performance"*; up to **200% aggregate** is only reachable in period 4 at the 75th percentile
with non-negative absolute TSR. And: *"If our absolute TSR is negative, the maximum payout over
the four-year period is 100% of the total target number of shares."* This is a defensible
design — relative TSR against a broad index with an absolute-return gate.

# SUMMARY OF WHAT THE RECORD SHOWS

**The pay is very large and it is not disguised.** FY2025 SCT total for Hock Tan is
**$205,278,006**; compensation actually paid under Item 402(v) is **$2,268 million**; the pay
ratio is **543:1** (Broadcom's own smoothed alternative is 93:1). A single grant carries a
maximum grant-date fair value of **$607,053,241**.

**The performance is real.** Broadcom's TSR beat the NASDAQ 100 in every one of the five PVP
years and by a widening margin — $1,182.35 against $243.37 on a $100 base at FY2025. Revenue,
cash from operations and free cash flow all set records. The plan paid at maximum because the
business cleared the maximum.

**The things that would concern a Buffett-framework reader, in order:**

1. **Target-setting.** FY2025 bonus maximum revenue was set at +16.6% on prior-year actual; the
   business delivered +23.9%. Both corporate metrics capped out. The proxy asserts rigor but
   shows no prior-year benchmark.
2. **A company-defined metric with elastic scope.** The 2025 Tan PSU Award pays against
   "AI Revenue" — *"used or expected to be used by our customers for AI enablement, including
   but not limited to..."* — a management-scoped subset of GAAP revenue, adjudicated by the
   same independent directors who granted the award, with $607m at stake at maximum.
3. **The pay quantum was benchmarked to pay, not to value.** The 2025 award's annual target
   value was set to *"the top decile of current chief executive officer compensation for
   similarly-situated large technology peer companies based in the Silicon Valley region."*
   That is a ratchet by construction.
4. **The CEO owns almost nothing outright.** 908,474 shares, roughly 0.019% of the company,
   against $205m of FY2025 pay. Alignment is entirely through unvested PSUs. Broadcom
   evidently knows this — the 2025 grant's whole consideration was a post-vesting holding
   requirement on the 2023 award's shares.
5. **Shareholders object, loudly, and only about pay.** Say-on-pay 61.7% (2024), 92.5% (2025),
   **66.4% (2026)** — the two low years follow the two mega-grants exactly. Tan himself is
   re-elected with 99.7%.

**The things that are clean:**

- Never filed a 10-K/A in eight years as a registrant.
- Both FY2025 cover-page error-correction and clawback-recovery boxes unchecked.
- Unqualified dual opinion from PwC; one CAM (VMware software revenue recognition).
