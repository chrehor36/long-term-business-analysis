# Competitor row — XYLEM (XYL), Measurement & Control Solutions segment
Research date: 2026-09-05. For the BMI run competitor row (Q2). Xylem owns Sensus (water/gas/electric smart meters, acquired 2016 for ~$1.7bn).

## Composition within M&CS (Part I business description, FY2025)
- "Smart Metering and Other" applications: **$1,726 (83%)** of segment revenue. Brands listed: Ebro, **Sensus**, Sentec, Smith Blair, WTW, YSI, Xylem Vue.
- "Analytics": **$360 (17%)**.

## Is Sensus water-meter revenue separable?
**No.** "Sensus" appears in the filing only as a brand name in the application table and in an executive bio. Revenue is disaggregated by segment and by application ("Smart Metering and Other" vs "Analytics"); the smart-metering line mixes water, electricity, and gas and is not split by commodity. **The row holds M&CS as the closest disclosed unit**, with "Smart Metering and Other" ($1,726M) as the closest sub-disclosure — still commodity-mixed.

## Smart-meter demand / backlog / AMI-cycle language (MD&A)
- M&CS FY2025 revenue +11.5% ($215M): organic +$172M, acquisitions +$30M, FX +$13M. "Smart metering and other applications had $171 million of organic growth, **led by energy growth in North America** due to strong volume and price realization as well as water growth from project revenue in western Europe. This growth was partially offset by **declines in water in North America due to lower demand following strong prior year backlog execution**." — the water-AMI backlog unwind, named.
- M&CS orders FY2025: **$1,873** vs 2024 **$1,672** (+12.0%; organic +9.4%). Segment book-to-bill ≈ 0.90 (1,873/2,086).
- Total Xylem backlog: **$4,615 at Dec 31, 2025** vs **$5,070 at Dec 31, 2024**, "a decrease of 9.0%"; ">60% ... will be recognized as revenue during 2026." (Backlog is not broken out by segment.)
- M&CS FY2025 margin decline driven by "270 basis points unfavorable mix and 260 basis points of inflation" — the energy-meter-heavy mix is lower margin.

## Pre-tax operating return on net tangible operating assets (FY2025, total company)
Segment-level assets are not disclosed ("Disaggregated asset information by segment is not provided to the CODM"), so **only a total-company RONTA is computable — it is not an M&CS figure**:
- Operating income (GAAP): **1,223**
- Total assets: **17,634**
- Cash and cash equivalents: **1,479**
- Goodwill: **8,332**
- Other intangible assets, net: **2,272**
- Non-interest-bearing current liabilities = total current liabilities 2,856 − short-term borrowings and current maturities of long-term debt 534 = **2,322** (accounts payable 1,013; accrued and other current liabilities 1,237; liabilities held for sale 72)
- Denominator: 17,634 − 1,479 − 8,332 − 2,272 − 2,322 = **3,229**
- **RONTA = 1,223 / 3,229 = 37.9% pre-tax** (total company, not M&CS)

Caveats: assets held for sale (176) remain in the denominator and liabilities held for sale (72) in the NIBCL; goodwill+intangibles of 10,604 is 60% of the balance sheet — Xylem is an acquisition vehicle, and stripping the acquisition ledger flatters the operating return.

## Row relevance
M&CS at $2.1bn revenue is ~2.5x BMI's whole company, but its GAAP segment margin (11.7%) is roughly half BMI's operating margin, its FY2025 growth was energy-led while **North America water declined**, and its water-meter franchise is buried in a conglomerate segment that management reports on an adjusted basis. Sensus has not compounded visibly since 2016; BMI's utility segment more than doubled over the same span.
