## Segment structure note
Four reportable segments as of the FY2025 10-K: **Device Solutions, Networked Solutions, Outcomes, Resiliency Solutions** — "Resiliency Solutions is a new reportable segment starting in the fourth quarter of 2025" (from an acquisition; Nov 3–Dec 31, 2025 only). ## Water-specific revenue
**Not disclosed in the FY2025 filing.** Revenue is disaggregated by segment and product/service only, not by commodity (electricity/gas/water). The only water color in MD&A: Device Solutions revenue decline was "due to the planned decrease in electric residential sales in [EMEA] and water meter sales, partially offset by higher smart water shipments."

However, the **FY2015 10-K** reported under the old Electricity/Gas/Water segment structure:
- **Water segment FY2015: revenue $519,422; gross profit $145,680 (28.0% GM** — depressed by a $29.4M special warranty charge for prematurely failing communication modules; ex-charge ~33.7%).
- Water segment FY2014: revenue $576,668; gross profit $202,178 (35.1% GM).
- "Our Water operating segment includes both our global water and heat solutions."

## Water AMI position per the 10-K
Networked Solutions "includes automated meter reading (AMR) and advanced metering infrastructure (AMI) for electricity, water, and gas" on a multi-purpose IIoT network; Outcomes lists "non-revenue water reduction" among use cases. No water-specific market-position, share, or unit claims anywhere in the filing. Named primary competitors: "LM Ericsson Telephone Company, Landis+Gyr, Advanced Energy Industries, and Xylem, Inc." — **Badger Meter is not named** (Itron's frame is electricity-first grid networks, not water metering).

## Decade record — the [E2-45] attacker question
- FY2015 total revenue **$1,883,533** → FY2025 **$2,367,194**: **+25.7% over ten years, ≈2.3%/yr CAGR.**
- Worse from an earlier base: the FY2015 10-K's selected data shows FY2011 revenue of $2,426,137 — Itron's FY2025 revenue is still **below** its 2011 revenue.
- FY2015 gross margin 29.6% (556,685/1,883,533) → FY2025 37.7%. Real margin repair, negligible growth.
- Contrast held in the row: BMI utility segment ~$350M (2016) → $816M (2025) at 42% GM, vs Itron's water business at $519M in 2015 — **larger than BMI's then** — under the old structure, and no longer separately visible. Itron's growth engine claim (Networked Solutions) went 1,450,291 (2023) → 1,650,075 (2024) → 1,557,321 (2025): the 2024 spike was catch-up of supply-constrained backlog, and 2025 declined 6%.
