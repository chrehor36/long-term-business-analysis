## 3. [E2-49] — the two withdrawn yardsticks, dated
1. **Comparable guest counts**: numeric table by segment, three years, in every 10-K
   through FY2019 (filed 2020-02-26). FY2020 10-K (filed 2021-02-23): table gone,
   replaced by one qualitative sentence in a −7.7% comps year. FY2021 10-K on: even the
   definition bullet gone; guest counts named only as a comps *driver*. The metric was
   withdrawn AFTER five negative US years in six, in the year it was worst. It has not
   returned through FY2025 or the Q2-2026 10-Q (which still concedes the sign).
2. **The Euromonitor IEO share benchmark**: "about 8% of the sales" (FY2013 10-K, 2012
   data); "7.0%" (FY2016, 2015 data); "8.2%" (FY2019, 2018 data). FY2022 and FY2025
   10-Ks: no Euromonitor citation, no quantified share anywhere. Withdrawn between the
   FY2019 and FY2022 vintages. (The share it last showed was RISING, 7.0→8.2 — this
   withdrawal does not follow deterioration on its own last reading.)

## 5. THE FRANCHISE TERM STRUCTURE (Franchise Arrangements note, FY2025 10-K)
- "franchise/license agreements that generally have 20-year terms" (Item 1).
- "At the end of the 20-year franchise arrangement, **the Company maintains control of
  the underlying real estate and building** and can either enter into a new 20-year
  franchise arrangement with the existing franchisee or a different franchisee, or
  close the restaurant." (Franchise Arrangements note.)
- "Minimum rent payments are based on the Company's underlying investment in owned
  sites and parallel the Company's underlying leases and escalations on properties
  that are leased."
- **Future gross minimum rent due TO the Company under existing conventional
  arrangements: $31,451M** ($15,843M owned sites, $15,608M leased sites); 2026 $3,045M.
- Net P&E under franchise arrangements $22.8bn (incl. land $7.1bn), accum. dep $16.9bn.
- Owned land at cost $8,169M; buildings on owned land $22,202M; on leased land $15,506M.
- No renewal RATE is disclosed in any 10-K read (FY2013-FY2025).
- Franchisee pricing control, filer's words (FY2016 Item 1): franchisees "maintain
  control over all employment-related matters, marketing and **pricing decisions**."
