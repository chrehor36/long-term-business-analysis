### THE FINDING - five things that sit behind the negative sign
- **Q1, the asymmetric cost curve.** Fluence buys cells (the FY2025 10-K: *"including battery cells from China"*) and rents
  contract manufacturers. Its price per GWh falls with cell cost (*"lower average price per GWh ... as the cost of lithium-ion
  batteries has continued to decline"*, FY2025 MD&A) while cell-cost rises on signed fixed-price contracts land on Fluence
  (FY2022 supplier repricing, gross margin -5.2%; 9M FY2026 *"increases in battery prices"*, gross margin 6.5%, Q3 5.1%). On
  two-year GWh windows the customer took **~83%** of the cost decline - better than BE's 104%, but the sharper finding is the
  asymmetry: falls passed on, rises absorbed [E3-62].
- **Q2, the substitutes, from both sides.** Fluence names Tesla, Wärtsilä, Sungrow and CATL; **Eos's 10-K names Fluence**;
  Fluence's own risk factor says Chinese integrators price below it. **Tesla's energy segment went 18.9% -> 29.8% gross margin
  (2023-25) on 46.7 GWh while Fluence went 13.1% -> 6.5% on 5.0 GWh.** Stem exited battery hardware; Powin went bankrupt.
- **Q2, the borrowed moat.** The strongest bull input - bankability - is the sponsors': performance guarantees and $100.0M of
  supply-chain-finance guarantees from AES and Siemens, and a risk factor that says a reduction in the Founders' ownership *"may
  prompt business partners to reprice"*. The Founders sold 23.0M shares at $21.00 in May 2026 [E4-23].
- **Q4, the treadmill.** Customers and suppliers lent **$1.13bn** of working capital from FY2021 to 2026-06-30 and cumulative
  operating cash was still **-$1,092.0M**. Deferred revenue is **$1,014.0M, 2.8x cash**, discharged at a 5-13% gross margin
  below ~$350M of overhead. FY2024's only positive operating cash was **payables +$370.1M (the screen's 464%) and accruals
  +$160.2M**.
- **Q4, the perimeter (the ACMR question in Up-C form).** Class A owns **77.55%** of LLC owner earnings, then pays corporate tax
  on its share and owes the Founders **85% of basis-step-up tax savings (TRA: $165.1M on $194.2M, unrecorded)**. The yield is
  unchanged by the split (whole-LLC count against whole-LLC earnings); the TRA is a claim on the success case only.

power over Fluence's price is filed regardless of how safe Fluence is.

### TOOLING DEFECTS
1. **`tools/run.py` priced an Up-C on its non-economic class.** No `dei` cover count resolves (a multi-class cover is dimensioned
   and companyfacts drops it); `cover_count()` fell through to `us-gaap:CommonStockSharesOutstanding`, whose only undimensioned fact
   is **51,499,195 - the Class B-1 comparative** from the 10-Q filed 2026-08-05. Output: `shares 51.5M, cap 0.51B`, 3.6x low. The
   docstring's *"DOES NOT SUM SHARE CLASSES"* is honoured, but the function does not refuse either; **a refusal when the dei cover
   count is absent and the fallback is a single class of a multi-class filer** is the testable fix, not measured here.
2. **`deal_note` counts an 8-K and its 8-K/A as two Item 1.01 events.** A de-duplication on the same period-of-report is testable,
   not measured here.
3. **`wc_note` names payables but cannot see customer deposits or supply-chain finance** - the larger lines in this file.
4. **The owner-earnings ends are from different windows and (c) ends a seventh time.**
