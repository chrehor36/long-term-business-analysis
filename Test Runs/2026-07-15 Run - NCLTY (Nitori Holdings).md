## GATE 3 — MANAGEMENT / FORTRESS
Original pass (2026-07-15): not independently verified — provisional.

**VERIFICATION COMPLETED 2026-07-16 (web research, pre-entry, at user
request):**
- **Succession already happened, cleanly, a decade ago**: founder Akio
  Nitori (b. 1944, age 81) stepped down as president in **2016**;
  **Toshiyuki Shirai has been President & CEO since** — ~10 years of
  operating tenure, not a fresh or untested handoff. Both were reappointed
  in the June 25, 2026 board restructuring, which also added a new internal
  director (Hiroshi Nagai) and a new outside director (Ryoko Yamazaki) —
  an orderly refresh, not entrenchment-by-stasis.
- **Chairman and CEO roles are SEPARATE** (Akio Nitori chairman, Shirai
  CEO) — no combined-role caveat.
- **Alignment is exceptional**: the founder retains a **~21% ownership
  stake** — among the strongest insider alignment of any company run in
  this project.
- **Integrity**: no scandals, restatements, or governance controversies
  surfaced in searches; auditor is Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu; governance
  framework published.
- **Competence**: 36 consecutive periods of revenue AND profit growth
  through ~FY2023 — one of the most remarkable operating records in global
  retail — **followed by a real, ongoing break in the streak**: FY2024
  operating profit -5.8% (¥120.3B) and FY-Mar-2025 net profit -11.1%
  (¥76.89B), driven by **yen weakness** (roughly 90% of product is imported
  from Vietnam/China, so yen depreciation directly compresses margins).
  This confirms the vertical-integration model's one structural
  vulnerability and sharpens the thesis-breaking metric below.
- **Residual watch item, not a caveat**: the 81-year-old founder remains
  Representative Director & Chairperson — a board-level key-person
  consideration, mitigated by the 2016 operational handoff and the sitting
  CEO's decade of tenure.

**VERDICT: PASS — VERIFIED CLEAN** (succession precedent real, roles
separate, alignment exceptional, no integrity flags). Under Ruling 4 this
removes the Gate-3 caveat bump from Book Two's discount rate — see the
Gate 6 recompute note.
