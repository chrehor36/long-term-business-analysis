Notes:
1. **Dell's book equity has been NEGATIVE in every year of the window** (Dell Technologies
   Inc. stockholders' equity: -$1,685M, -$3,122M, -$2,404M, -$1,482M, -$2,470M). ROE and
   RONTA are not computable and are not reported. A row that quoted a large positive ROE
   for Dell would be an artifact of a small denominator crossing zero, not a return. This is
   recorded as **n/m**, not as a number.
2. Net tangible equity is equity less goodwill ($19,547M at FY2026) less intangibles
   ($4,533M): **negative $26.6 billion**. Dell's entire balance sheet carries $24.1bn of
   goodwill and intangibles against negative equity. Apple carries **zero** goodwill.
3. FY2022 figures reflect the **VMware spin-off (November 2021)**; VMware is presented as
   discontinued operations. This is why R&D drops from $5,275M (FY2021) to $2,577M (FY2022)
   and goodwill drops from $40,829M to $19,770M. The window from FY2022 is therefore
   comparable within itself but NOT continuous with FY2021 and earlier. Flagged, not smoothed.
4. FY2026 is the AI-server year: revenue up 18.8% to $113,538M, and **gross margin FELL to
   20.00% from 22.24%** - the lowest in the window. The extra revenue came in at a lower
   margin than the business it displaced.

consecutive years, at the largest commercial PC vendor in the row.** Apple's consolidated
operating margin in the matching year is 31.97%. Apple's PRODUCTS gross margin alone (36.8%)
exceeds Dell's whole-company gross margin (20.00%) by 16.8 points.

> The markets in which we compete span countries around the world with customers that range

**NAMED COMPETITORS: NONE.** Dell names not one company. Dell's consumer PC business - the
segment that overlaps Apple most directly - shrank from $9,102M to $6,922M in two years
(-24%) while Dell's commercial PC business grew, and the 10-K does not attribute any of it
to Apple.
