# SAMSUNG ELECTRONICS CO., LTD. - competitor row entry
## RUNG FLAG: **NOT AN SEC REGISTRANT. THIS IS RUNG 3 EVIDENCE (COMPANY IR), NOT A FILING.**

**State it plainly:** Samsung Electronics Co., Ltd. is a Korean issuer listed on the Korea
Exchange. **It has no CIK, files no 10-K, no 20-F and no 40-F with the SEC, and is not an
SEC registrant in any form.** There is no ADR-driven 20-F either; the US-traded instrument
is an unsponsored OTC ADR (SSNLF), which creates no SEC reporting obligation. Nothing below
is a filed US figure and nothing below may be treated as one under operator rule 5.

**What the evidence actually is:** the audited-basis consolidated K-IFRS results Samsung
Electronics publishes itself, in English, on its own investor-relations site, as the
"Earnings Presentation" accompanying each quarter's results. That is **the company speaking
about itself in a document of its own design** - rung 3 on the evidence ladder. It is above
an aggregator and below a filing. The equivalent rung-2 document is the Korean-language
Business Report (사업보고서) filed with the Financial Supervisory Service on **DART**
(dart.fss.or.kr); that document is the regulator-filed original and is **NOT** what is
transcribed below.

## MX (MOBILE eXPERIENCE) DIVISION - THE iPHONE COMPARATOR

Samsung reports the smartphone business as **"MX / Networks"**. Operating profit is disclosed
only for the combined MX/Networks line; **MX standalone operating profit is NOT disclosed**.
MX standalone SALES are disclosed. Both are recorded; the margin is computed on MX/Networks.

| FY | MX / Networks sales (KRW tn) | MX only sales (KRW tn) | MX / Networks operating profit (KRW tn) | **MX/NW operating margin** | Source deck |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 2021 | 109.25 | 104.68 | 13.65 | **12.49%** | 2022_4Q (comparative) / 2021_4Q as "IM" |
| 2022 | 120.81 | 115.43 | 11.38 | **9.42%** | 2022_4Q |
| 2023 | 112.41 | 108.63 | 13.01 | **11.57%** | 2023_4Q / 2024_4Q |
| 2024 | 117.3 | 114.4 | 10.6 | **9.04%** | 2024_4Q / 2025_4Q |
| 2025 | 129.5 | 126.5 | 12.9 | **9.96%** | 2025_4Q |

Precision flag, not smoothed: the FY2021-FY2023 decks state segment figures to two decimals
(0.01 trn); the FY2024 and FY2025 decks state them to one decimal (0.1 trn). The FY2024 and
FY2025 margins therefore carry roughly +/- 40 basis points of rounding. **Samsung reduced
the precision of its own segment disclosure mid-window.**

Structure caveat in Samsung's own footnote, verbatim:
> "Sales and operating profit of each business stated above reflect the organizational
> structure as of December 2021 and the sales of business units include intersegment sales.
> The DX Division provides earnings call materials based on the business structure before the
> reorganization to prevent confusion and to improve understanding among investors."

**Intersegment sales are INCLUDED.** MX buys memory and displays from Samsung's own DS and
SDC divisions at transfer prices Samsung sets. The MX margin is therefore a
transfer-price-dependent number in a way that Apple's consolidated margin is not. Flagged.

roughly a third of Apple's consolidated margin, and Apple's consolidated figure carries the
loss-making-nothing drag of no memory fab.

## THE ATTACKER'S TEST - DOES SAMSUNG NAME APPLE?

**UNRESEARCHED - rung 3 (company IR). Obstacle:** Samsung's competition disclosure lives in
the Korean-language Business Report on DART, not in the English earnings decks. The English
earnings presentations contain no Item-1-equivalent competition section and name no
competitors. The English figures obtained above therefore answer the FINANCIAL half of the
attacker's test and not the NAMING half. **The document that would resolve it is named:
Samsung Electronics 사업보고서 (Business Report) for FY2025, filed with the FSS on DART.**
It was not obtained in this run.

**Third-party confirmation that Samsung is a real competitor exists inside the row and is
filed:** HP Inc's FY2025 10-K (accession 0000047217-25-000071) lists "Samsung Electronics
Co., Ltd." among its ten named Personal Systems competitors alongside Apple Inc.; and
Garmin's FY2025 10-K names Samsung in both its fitness and outdoor competitor lists
alongside Apple. Samsung's status as a peer of Apple is thus established from US filings by
third parties, even though Samsung files none itself.
