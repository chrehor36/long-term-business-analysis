## 3A. ANNUAL CASH BONUS METRICS — GAAP, AND SAID SO IN TERMS

**This is the strongest single fact in the Q3 file.** The plan's metric definition, verbatim:

> *"As in prior years, the People and Compensation Committee chose **net sales and operating
> income, calculated in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles**, as the
> financial performance measures for the 2025 Cash Incentive Plan awards. Net sales and operating
> income continue to reflect commonly recognized key measures of overall Company performance and
> profitability and are drivers of shareholder value creation."*

Mechanics, verbatim:
> *"Payouts ... are first determined quantitatively based on an **equal weighting** of the net
> sales and operating income performance measures. The target payout opportunity for each ... was
> **100% of annual base salary** ... **Threshold performance would result in a payout equal to 50%
> of target and maximum performance would result in a payout equal to 200% of target** for each of
> the performance measures. **If the threshold goal is not achieved for a financial performance
> measure, there is no payout for that measure.** The total payout opportunity for each financial
> performance measure is **capped at 200%**. Payouts ... are linearly interpolated for achievement
> between goal levels."*

**The goals themselves are disclosed in dollars — prospectively set, retrospectively published.**
Most large-cap proxies withhold these as competitively sensitive. Apple does not:
> *"For **net sales**, the threshold, target, and maximum goals were **$380 billion, $391 billion,
> and $402 billion**, respectively. For **operating income**, the threshold, target, and maximum
> goals were **$113 billion, $118.5 billion, and $124 billion**, respectively."*

Result, verbatim:
> *"For 2025, we reported net sales of **$416.2 billion** and operating income of **$133.1
> billion**, representing a year-over-year increase of six percent ($25.2 billion) and eight
> percent ($9.9 billion), respectively. ... **The net sales and operating income results delivered
> for the year exceeded the maximum financial goals considered, resulting in a maximum payout
> opportunity for each named executive officer.**"*

**VERDICT: no adjusted metric anywhere in the incentive plan.** No "adjusted EBITDA", no
"non-GAAP operating income", no add-backs, no FX-neutral overlay, no discretionary modifier
applied upward. The metrics are two line items lifted straight off the audited income statement,
equally weighted, with a hard 200% cap. This is the cleanest possible construction under [E4-52]:
the incentive cannot be gamed by redefining the measure, only by moving the actual number.

**The counter-observation, stated for balance.** The goals were set BELOW the prior year's actual
result. FY2024 net sales were $391.0bn; the FY2025 **target** was $391bn and the **maximum** was
$402bn — i.e. maximum payout required only **+2.9% growth**, and Apple delivered +6.4%. The
committee's own language concedes the framing: *"focusing on the underlying business performance
rather than the absolute growth rates ... Acknowledging the risks, challenges, and uncertainties
in the 2025 projections."* Every year 2021-2025 produced an above-target or maximum payout:
> *"Net Sales and Operating Income ... were consistently strong from 2021 to 2025, and this
> sustained performance led to **above target or maximum bonus payouts in each of 2021, 2022,
> 2023, 2024, and 2025**."*
**Five for five at or above target is a goal-setting question, not a measurement-integrity
question.** The metric is honest; the bar is soft. Record it that way.

**Company-Selected Measure = NET SALES**, per the proxy's own footnote:
> *"In addition to Relative TSR and Operating Income, **Net Sales in column (i) represents the most
> important financial performance measure used to link Compensation Actually Paid to Company

**The three "most important" measures listed under Item 402(v)(6): Net Sales · Operating Income ·
Relative TSR.** All three are GAAP or market-derived. None is a management construct.

**THE HEADLINE FACT IN THIS TABLE — and Apple discloses it against itself.** Over the five-year
window a $100 investment in Apple grew to **$233.88** while the **peer group grew to $279.51**.
**Apple UNDERPERFORMED its own peer group over the full 2021-2025 PvP window, and the gap widened
sharply in 2025** (Apple TSR +12.66% vs peer group +35.48%, stated verbatim in the proxy). Apple
had led the peer group at each of 2022, 2023 and 2024 and lost the lead in a single year.

> *"Over 2025, **Apple's TSR on an absolute basis was 12.66%, while the TSR of our primary peer
> group was 35.48%.**"*

Apple prints this without softening it, and separately concedes the disconnect on net income:
> *"**There is no direct correlation between Net Income and Compensation Actually Paid from 2021 to
> 2025 as we do not use Net Income as a financial measure in our executive compensation program.**"*

**Note the asymmetry for the run file.** The bonus plan's own peer comparison (the *Relative TSR*
used for PSU vesting) is measured against the **S&P 500**, not the primary peer group. Apple's
Relative TSR was at the **81.20th percentile** for the 2022-2024 period, producing **187% of
target** PSU vesting — while the primary peer group beat Apple over the same PvP window. Two
different benchmarks, two different stories. The S&P 500 benchmark is the one that pays.

## 3C. CEO COMPENSATION AND PAY RATIO

**FY2025 CEO total compensation (Summary Compensation Table): $74,294,811.**
Target vs actual structure, verbatim:
> *"**2025 Total Target CEO Compensation: $59 million** — Base Salary: $3 million (Mr. Cook's base
> salary has remained the same since 2016). Target Cash Incentive Plan Opportunity: $6 million
> (... remained at 200% of his base salary). Target Equity Award Value: $50 million. **Award Mix:
> 25/75** — Mr. Cook was granted an equity award with 25% subject to time-based vesting and 75%
> subject to performance-based vesting."*

Cash incentive range from the Grants table: threshold $3,000,000 / target $6,000,000 / **maximum
$12,000,000** — and the maximum was paid for FY2025.

**CEO PAY RATIO — 533 to 1**, verbatim:
> *"The 2025 annual total compensation of our CEO was **$74,294,811**, the 2025 annual total
> compensation of our **median compensated employee was $139,483**, and the ratio of these amounts
> is **533 to 1**."*
Method disclosed: base salary + bonuses + commissions + grant-date fair value of equity, applied
to the **global** employee population as of 2025-09-27, salaries annualized for partial-year
staff. A new median employee was identified for 2025.

**Say on Pay: 92% in favour at the 2025 annual meeting.** Committee made no structural changes and
*"maintained Mr. Cook's total target compensation between the 80th to 90th percentile range of
target CEO pay at our 2025 primary peer companies."*

**Note for the run file:** the FY2025 proxy still treats Cook as CEO. The **8-K/A filed
2026-09-01** supersedes it — **John Ternus became CEO on 2026-09-01**, five days before this
research date, with Cook moving to Executive Chair. See `q3_disclosure_flags.md` §1F for the new
pay terms. Ternus' FY2027 award is **75% performance-based**; Cook's Executive-Chair award is
**50%**.

## 3D. REPRICING, HEDGING, PLEDGING, SEVERANCE, CHANGE OF CONTROL

All verbatim from the proxy's "Executive Compensation Policies and Practices" table and the
"Potential Payments Upon Termination or Change of Control" section:

| item | Apple's position |
|---|---|
| **Repricing** | *"We do not allow repricing of stock options without shareholder approval."* The Director Plan carries a hard anti-repricing covenant (quoted in §1A of the flags file). |
| **Hedging / pledging / short sales** | *"We prohibit short sales, hedging, and transactions in derivatives of Apple securities **for all Apple personnel**, including directors, officers, employees, independent contractors, and consultants. In addition, we prohibit pledging of Apple stock as collateral by directors and executive officers."* Carve-out: *"We allow for certain portfolio diversification transactions, such as investments in exchange funds."* |
| **Severance** | *"Our named executive officers **do not have employment contracts or guaranteed cash severance arrangements** and are not entitled to acceleration of their equity awards or any other payments upon a change in control."* And: *"**We do not have any cash severance arrangements with our named executive officers**..."* |
| **Change of control** | *"**No change of control payments** — We do not provide change of control payments or gross-ups of related excise taxes."* Equity awards contain **no single- or double-trigger acceleration**; the only accelerators are **death, disability, and (for Cook) retirement** (defined as age 60+ with 10 years of service). |
| **Employment** | *"At-will employment — Our named executive officers do not have employment contracts and are employed at will."* |
| **Pension / SERP** | *"**We do not provide pension, supplemental executive health, or insurance benefits.**"* |
| **Tax gross-ups** | *"We do not provide tax gross-ups for the limited perquisites."* |
| **Clawback** | Rule 10D-1/Nasdaq mandatory policy **plus** a broader discretionary recoupment policy covering *"certain acts of misconduct."* |
| **Ownership guidelines** | CEO required to hold **10× annual base salary**. |
| **Perquisites** | Limited. Named: *"for security and efficiency purposes, Mr. Cook is provided personal security protection and is **required by the Board** to use private aircraft for all business and personal travel."* |
| **Dividend equivalents** | Not paid until the underlying vesting AND performance conditions are met. |

**This is a materially above-average governance sheet.** No employment contracts, no severance,
no CoC payments, no gross-ups, no pension, no pledging, mandatory + discretionary clawback,
anti-repricing. There is nothing here that reads as extraction.

## 3F. DOES THE PROXY CONTAIN GUIDANCE OR GROWTH TARGETS?

**NO forward earnings guidance and NO forward growth targets.** Full-text search returns:
- "guidance" — 2 hits, neither financial: one on the code of conduct providing *"clear,
  actionable guidance to our employees"*, one on SEC Staff guidance under Rule 14a-8.
- The only numeric goals in the document are the **fiscal 2025 Cash Incentive Plan goals,
  published after the fact**. FY2026 goals are **not** disclosed.
- Forward-looking-statement legend is the standard PSLRA boilerplate covering *"our goals,
  commitments"* on ESG/supply-chain matters, not financial projections.

Consistent with Apple's long-standing practice of not issuing formal annual guidance. **Nothing in
the proxy sets an expectation a future manager would be tempted to manage earnings to.** This is
the [E4-52] test passed on the disclosure side as well as the metric side.
