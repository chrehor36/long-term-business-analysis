## 1. FIRST, THE ASP DISCLOSURE QUESTION, RECORDED SWEEP

Terms searched across all 14 10-K vintages FY2012 through FY2025 (whole-word, case-insensitive,
on the stripped text of each primary document):

| term | hits, all 14 vintages |
|---|---|
| `ASP` | **0** |

**Apple has never disclosed an iPhone ASP number in a Form 10-K.** It disclosed the two inputs
(net sales and unit sales) and let the reader divide. From FY2019 it stopped disclosing one of
the two inputs, which makes the quotient uncomputable from the filings.

The narrative phrase itself also went to zero. `selling price` count by vintage:
FY2012 **9**, FY2013 **9**, FY2014 **9**, FY2015 **9**, FY2016 **9**, FY2017 **10**,
FY2018 **9**, FY2019 **0**, FY2020-FY2025 **0**.
Both the number and the word for the number left the document in the same year.

## 3. THE DIRECTION TEST, [E2-49]

### 3.1 Annual, side by side

| FY | iPhone units (000) | Δ units | iPhone net sales ($M) | Δ net sales | derived $/unit | Δ $/unit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2015 | **231,218** | +37% | 155,041 | +52% | 670.54 | +11.2% |
| 2016 | 211,884 | **(8)%** | 136,700 | (12)% | 645.16 | (3.8)% |
| 2017 | 216,756 | +2% | 141,319 | +3% | 651.97 | +1.1% |
| **2018** | **217,722** | **0%** *(Apple printed a dash)* | **166,699** | **+18%** | **765.65** | **+17.4%** |
| 2019 | withdrawn | n/a | 142,381 *(restated basis: 2018 = 164,888)* | (14)% | n/a | n/a |

**Read the last two lines together.** FY2018: the physical series did not grow; dollar revenue
grew 18%; the entire gain was price. FY2019, the first year with no unit disclosure, iPhone
revenue fell 14%. The metric was withdrawn between those two filings.

revenue rose 13%.

### 3.3 [E4-55], the Precision Steel comparison, on the numbers

The corpus case: pounds 69M → 46M while price rises held dollar revenue level, *"a serious
reverse, not likely to disappear in some 'bounce back' effect."* Apple's shape is the same
mechanism at a milder magnitude and on a larger base:

| | Precision Steel | Apple iPhone |
|---|---|---|
| physical series | pounds 69M → 46M, **(33)%** | units 231.2m (FY15) → 217.7m (FY18), **(6)%**, and flat 0% in the final year |
| dollar series | held level by price | **rose 18%** in the final year, entirely on price |
| what the dollar series hid | the volume reverse | the volume plateau |
| what happened next | n/a | units never disclosed again; iPhone revenue **(14)%** the following year |

**Verdict on the physical-series test: the physical series had stopped growing before the
disclosure was withdrawn, and the dollar series was being carried by price.** That is precisely
the condition [E4-55] exists to detect, and it is the condition under which the instrument for
detecting it was removed.

### 3.4 [E2-49], the metric-withdrawal test

The [E2-49] test *"turns on whether the yardstick was discarded while yielding favorable readings
or after deterioration."* On the filed record:

- iPhone units **peaked in FY2015** at 231,218 thousand and were never exceeded.
- FY2016 units **fell 8%**.
- FY2017 recovered **+2%**, still **6% below the FY2015 peak**.
- FY2018 was **0%**, still **6% below the FY2015 peak**, and the December quarter within it
  was **negative**.
- iPad units had fallen **39%** from FY2013 (71,033) to FY2018 (43,535).
- Mac units in FY2018 (18,209) were **below FY2014** (18,906).

**All three withdrawn unit series were flat or declining at the moment of withdrawal. None was
at a peak. None was growing.** The yardstick was not discarded while flattering; it was discarded
after the readings turned.

The one metric that WAS flattering at that moment, revenue per unit, up 17.4% in FY2018, is the
one Apple had never published as a number and therefore had nothing to withdraw. The company kept
the favourable half of the ratio in the filing (revenue) and removed the unfavourable half
(units), which also removes the reader's ability to compute the favourable one.

**[E2-49] reads: FLAG.** *"most managers favor disposition of the yardstick rather than
disposition of the manager."*

### 3.5 THE HONEST COUNTER-CASE, recorded, because [E4-26] requires it

Hunting disconfirming evidence hardest for the favoured hypothesis, four points cut the other way
and are recorded on the same filed evidence:

1. **The categories genuinely changed.** Wearables/Home/Accessories, introduced as a named
   category in the same Q1 FY2019 10-Q, is a multi-device category (AirPods, Watch, HomePod)
   where a single unit count is not meaningful. Services, 17.8% of FY2019 revenue and 30.0% of
   FY2019 gross profit, has no unit at all. A units table covering three of five categories is a
   partial instrument.
2. **Apple did not go dark on the direction.** The FY2019 10-K still states, verbatim,
   *"iPhone net sales decreased during 2019 compared to 2018 due primarily to lower iPhone unit
   sales."* Apple continued to tell the reader that units fell; it stopped telling the reader
   by how much.
3. **The withdrawal was not concealed in the aggregate.** Total, Products and Services revenue,
   and the geographic split, all remained; the FY2019 10-K also **added** the Products/Services
   gross-margin split, which is genuinely new information (see `segment_history.md`).
4. **The disclosure had never been required.** Unit counts are not a GAAP or Reg S-K requirement.
   Withdrawing them is lawful and unremarkable in isolation.

None of the four disturbs the timing finding. The question [E2-49] asks is not whether the
company had a defensible reason; it is whether the yardstick went away when the yardstick started
reading badly. **On the filed record, it did**, and no filed document offers the defensible reason
(see `unit_disclosure_sweep.md` §4.4: the reason exists only on the earnings-call transcript rung).
