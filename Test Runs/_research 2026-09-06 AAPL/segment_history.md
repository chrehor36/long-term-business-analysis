## 1. A DISCONTINUITY THE READER MUST CARRY

Apple's category revenue series **is not continuous across FY2018/FY2019.** Two changes land in
the Q1 FY2019 10-Q (0000320193-19-000010) and are carried into the FY2019 10-K
(0000320193-19-000119):

1. `Other Products` is renamed and redefined as **`Wearables, Home and Accessories`**.
2. The amortisation of deferred value of Maps, Siri and free iCloud moved **from Products to
   Services**. Apple's own words (Q1 FY2019 10-Q, verbatim):

> *"Beginning in the first quarter of 2019, the Company classified the amortization of the
> deferred value of Maps, Siri and free iCloud services, which are bundled in the sales price of
> iPhone, Mac, iPad and certain other products, in Services net sales. Historically, the Company
> classified the amortization of these amounts in Products net sales consistent with its
> management reporting framework."*

The effect on FY2018, as restated in the FY2019 10-K: **iPhone 166,699 → 164,888** and
**Services 37,190 → 39,748**. Same total (265,595). Both bases are given below; do not splice
them.

## 2. THE TABLE THAT MATTERS, PRODUCTS vs SERVICES GROSS PROFIT

### 2.0 WHEN THIS DISCLOSURE STARTED

**The Products/Services gross-margin split is FIRST disclosed in the FY2019 10-K
(0000320193-19-000119), filed 2019-10-31, the same filing that first omits unit sales.** The
FY2018 10-K (0000320193-18-000145) discloses one consolidated line only, verbatim caption:
*"Gross margin for 2018 , 2017 and 2016 was as follows (dollars in millions):"* with rows
`Net sales` / `Cost of sales` / `Gross margin` / `Gross margin percentage`. There is no Products
row and no Services row in FY2018 or any earlier vintage.

The FY2019 caption, verbatim: *"Products and Services gross margin and gross margin percentage
for 2019 , 2018 and 2017 were as follows (dollars in millions):"*, with FY2018 and FY2017
back-filled on the new basis, which is how FY2017 and FY2018 gross-profit dollars exist at all.

**This is the single most important row in the file.** Services is a quarter of Apple's revenue
and **over two-fifths of its gross profit**, and the gap is widening every year. The economics of
the business are not the economics of the revenue mix.

### 2.3 THE OTHER READING OF THE SAME TABLE, PRODUCTS GROSS PROFIT HAS BARELY MOVED IN SEVEN YEARS

| | FY2018 | FY2025 | change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Products gross profit $ | 77,683 | 112,887 | +45.3%, **+5.5%/yr compounded** |
| Services gross profit $ | 24,156 | 82,314 | +240.8%, **+19.1%/yr compounded** |
| Products gross profit, FY2021 → FY2025 | 105,126 | 112,887 | **+7.4% over four years, +1.8%/yr** |

Products gross profit **fell** in FY2019 (77,683 → 68,887, minus 11.3%) and has grown 1.8% a year
since FY2021. Essentially **all** of the growth in Apple's gross profit since FY2021 is Services:
of the +42,365 total gross-profit increase FY2021 → FY2025, **34,604 (81.7%) is Services.**

For the Q1/Q2 franchise questions this is the fact to carry: the hardware business, the one whose
physical volume series was withdrawn, has produced roughly flat gross profit for four years, and
the growth engine is a category with no unit, no volume series, and no physical measure of any
kind on the filing rung.

## 3. [E2-44], DOES APPLE'S MD&A ATTRIBUTE REVENUE CHANGE TO PRICE vs VOLUME?

Recorded sweep, all 14 10-K vintages, whole-word case-insensitive on the stripped primary
document. Terms: `volume`, `pricing`, `price increase(s)`, `higher price(s)`, `lower price(s)`,
`average selling price(s)`, `unit sales`.

### 3.1 What Apple used to do (FY2012-FY2018): price/volume attribution WAS given, verbatim

- FY2018 10-K (0000320193-18-000145): *"iPhone net sales increased during 2018 compared to 2017
  due primarily to a different mix of iPhones resulting in higher average selling prices."*
- FY2018 10-K, same document, on FY2017: *"iPhone net sales increased during 2017 compared to
  2016 due to higher iPhone unit sales and a different mix of iPhones with higher average selling
  prices."*
- FY2018 10-K, Gross Margin section, on FY2017: *"Gross margin increased in 2017 compared to 2016
  due primarily to a shift in mix to Services and an overall increase in product volumes."*
- FY2018 10-K, Mac: *"Mac net sales decreased during 2018 compared to 2017 due primarily to lower
  Mac unit sales, partially offset by a different mix of Macs with higher average selling prices."*

Both levers were named, in both directions, product by product.

### 3.2 What replaced it (FY2019-FY2022): "volume" survives ONLY in the gross-margin paragraph

- FY2019 10-K: *"Products gross margin and Products gross margin percentage decreased during 2019
  compared to 2018 due primarily to lower iPhone unit sales and the weakness in foreign currencies
  relative to the U.S. dollar."*
- FY2020 10-K: *"Products gross margin increased during 2020 compared to 2019 due primarily to
  higher Products volume and material cost savings…"*
- FY2021 10-K: *"Products gross margin increased during 2021 compared to 2020 due primarily to
  higher Products volume, a different Products mix and the strength in foreign currencies…"*
- FY2022 10-K: *"Products gross margin increased during 2022 compared to 2021 due primarily to a
  different Products mix and higher Products volume, partially offset by the weakness in foreign
  currencies relative to the U.S. dollar."*

### 3.3 FY2023, THE LAST VOLUME ATTRIBUTION IN ANY APPLE 10-K

FY2023 10-K (0000320193-23-000106), Products Gross Margin, verbatim:

> *"Products gross margin decreased during 2023 compared to 2022 due to the weakness in foreign
> currencies relative to the U.S. dollar and lower Products volume, partially offset by cost
> savings and a different Products mix."*

Its category commentary that year attributes revenue change to **model mix only, never to price or
volume**, verbatim:

> *"iPhone net sales decreased 2% or $4.9 billion during 2023 compared to 2022 due to lower net
> sales of non-Pro iPhone models, partially offset by higher net sales of Pro iPhone models."*
> *"Mac net sales decreased 27% or $10.8 billion during 2023 compared to 2022 due primarily to
> lower net sales of laptops."*

### 3.4 FY2024 and FY2025, RECORDED SWEEP, ZERO VOLUME ATTRIBUTIONS

`volume` count in the FY2024 10-K: **1.** `volume` count in the FY2025 10-K: **1.** In both, the
single hit is the boilerplate market risk factor, *"the stock market as a whole have, from time
to time, experienced extreme stock price and volume fluctuations"*, which is about the share
price, not the business. **There is no volume attribution anywhere in the FY2024 or FY2025 MD&A.**
`average selling price`, `ASP`, `pricing` as a revenue driver, `price increase`: **zero MD&A hits
in both vintages.**

What Apple says instead, verbatim in full:

**FY2024 10-K (0000320193-24-000123):**
> *"Products gross margin and Products gross margin percentage increased during 2024 compared to
> 2023 due to cost savings, partially offset by a different Products mix and the weakness in
> foreign currencies relative to the U.S. dollar."*
> *"Services gross margin increased during 2024 compared to 2023 due primarily to higher Services
> net sales."*
> *"Services net sales increased during 2024 compared to 2023 due primarily to higher net sales
> from advertising, the App Store and cloud services."*

**FY2025 10-K (0000320193-25-000079):**
> *"iPhone net sales increased during 2025 compared to 2024 due to higher net sales of Pro models."*
> *"Mac net sales increased during 2025 compared to 2024 primarily due to higher net sales of
> laptops and desktops."*
> *"iPad net sales increased during 2025 compared to 2024 primarily due to higher net sales of
> iPad Air, iPad mini and iPad, partially offset by lower net sales of iPad Pro."*
> *"Wearables, Home and Accessories net sales decreased during 2025 compared to 2024 primarily due
> to lower net sales of Accessories and Wearables."*
> *"Services net sales increased during 2025 compared to 2024 primarily due to higher net sales
> from advertising, the App Store and cloud services."*
> *"Products gross margin increased during 2025 compared to 2024 primarily due to favorable costs
> and a different mix of products, partially offset by tariff costs."*
> *"Products gross margin percentage decreased during 2025 compared to 2024 primarily due to a
> different mix of products and tariff costs, partially offset by other favorable costs."*

### 3.5 [E2-44] VERDICT

**Apple's MD&A attributed revenue change to price and volume through FY2018, to volume alone
(inside the gross-margin paragraph) through FY2023, and to NEITHER in FY2024 or FY2025.** The
FY2025 explanation of why iPhone revenue rose 4% is nine words: *"due to higher net sales of Pro
models."* That is a tautology, revenue rose because revenue of a sub-line rose. It names neither
how many were sold nor at what price.

The attribution language decayed on the same trajectory as the unit disclosure, four to six years
behind it:

| | units | ASP words | volume attribution | price attribution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| through FY2018 | disclosed | present | present | present |
| FY2019-FY2023 | **gone** | **gone** | present (gross-margin paragraph only) | gone |
| FY2024-FY2025 | gone | gone | **gone** | gone |

**Nothing in the FY2025 Form 10-K lets a reader decompose Apple's revenue into price and
quantity for any product, or state whether Apple sold more phones or fewer.**
