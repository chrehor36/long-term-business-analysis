## THE STRUCTURAL FACT THE ROW EXISTS TO SURFACE

**Apple's balance sheet carries no goodwill and no intangible-asset line at all.** The last
year Apple disclosed goodwill was FY2017 ($5,717M). From FY2019 forward there is no goodwill
caption. Return on net tangible assets therefore EQUALS return on equity for Apple, at
**171.4%** in FY2025.

Every other hardware company in the row is the opposite: Dell carries $19,547M of goodwill
plus $4,533M of intangibles against **negative $2,470M** of equity - **negative $26.6bn of
net tangible equity**. HP carries $8,706M of goodwill against **negative $346M** of equity.
Alphabet carries $33,380M of goodwill against $415,265M of equity, and still returns 39.16%
on net tangible equity - roughly a quarter of Apple's.

## US v. GOOGLE - both sides of the same fact

**Alphabet, FY2025 10-K, Antitrust Matters, verbatim:**
> "In August 2024, the US District Court for the District of Columbia ruled against Google. A
> final judgment was entered in December 2025, which, among other things, **imposes
> restrictions on how Google distributes its services** and requires Google to share certain
> search data with and offer syndication services to certain competitors. In January 2026, we
> appealed the final judgment and moved to pause implementation of certain remedies. In
> February 2026, the DOJ and state Attorneys General also appealed."

**Apple, FY2025 10-K, Risk Factors, verbatim:**
> "the Company earns revenue from licensing arrangements with Google LLC ('Google') and other
> companies to offer their search services on the Company's platforms and applications... A
> reversal of the order on appeal could result in imposition of certain remedies initially
> proposed by the DOJ, **such as those prohibiting Google from offering the Company commercial
> terms for search distribution. If implemented, these remedies could materially adversely
> affect the Company's ability to earn revenue from such licensing arrangements.**"

**Apple discloses the risk to the payment and not the size of it.** The payment sits inside
the Services line, which carries the 75.4% gross margin and all of Apple's margin expansion.
Alphabet's distribution model is additionally under order in Japan (JFTC cease-and-desist,
April 2025, Android agreements), settled with Australia's ACCC (August 2025, Android
agreements), and under the EU Android decision (EUR 4.1bn, on appeal at the CJEU).

## THREE THINGS THE ROW ESTABLISHES FOR THE APPLE RUN

1. **Apple is not the highest-margin business in its own row.** Microsoft's operating margin
   (46.78%) and Alphabet's gross margin (59.65%) both exceed Apple's. Alphabet's FY2025
   operating margin (32.03%) is six basis points ABOVE Apple's (31.97%). **Apple's distance
   from the field is at the BALANCE SHEET, not the income statement**: zero goodwill,
   171% return on net tangible equity, and 3.06% of revenue in capex against Alphabet's 22.70%.
2. **The hardware rung is 5-6% and falling.** Dell CSG 5.6%, HP Personal Systems 5.3%,
   Samsung MX/Networks ~10%. Apple's Products gross margin (36.8%) is not in the same
   business as these. That gap is the moat claim, and it must be argued at Q2 against the
   fact that Apple's Products margin moved only 1.5 points in five years.
3. **The margin expansion and the regulatory exposure are the same line item.** The Services
   line (75.4% gross margin, +59.5% in five years) carries the undisclosed Google payment
   (at risk from the DOJ search remedy, which Apple itself flags as materially adverse) and
   the App Store commission (at risk from the DMA and the US smartphone monopolization
   suits). **The most profitable part of Apple is the part the row cannot see and the courts
   can reach.**
