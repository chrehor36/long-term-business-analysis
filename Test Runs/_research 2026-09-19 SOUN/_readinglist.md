### THE PRIOR THE BRIEF ASKED TO BE ARGUED AGAINST, AND HOW IT FARED
The brief's likeliest error: that a loss-making, acquisitive, heavily stock-paid AI company is an easy close. **Half right.** The close came at Q2, not at Q4, and not
for free: the franchise case had real evidence, all from SoundHound's own documents or a rival's: design-win stickiness (*"very unlikely that a customer will change
complex technology until a product model is revamped"*), multi-year renewals with a carmaker and restaurant chains, per-unit royalties, and **Cerence, the auto voice
incumbent, naming SoundHound among competitors that *"have had some success selling into our customer base"***. Revenue guidance was met in every year FY2022-25. All of
it proves [E3-03]'s criterion 1 (needed or desired). Criterion 2 is answered by the filer itself.

### WHY Q2 CLOSED
- **The filer's own words on substitutes** [E3-03] criterion 2: alternatives *"offered at significantly lower costs or free of charge"*; rivals able to make the function
  *"available at lower cost or free of charge within the larger offering"*; carmaker contracts with *"annual price reduction commitments"*. Its own release celebrates
  displacing an incumbent voice assistant: in this field incumbents are displaced, which is the switching cost failing. Cerence's 10-K carries a near-identical paragraph,
  so it is the industry's condition.
- **The growth was bought and the owned businesses shrank**: reported revenue $45.9M to $168.9M (FY2023-25) through SYNQ3, Amelia and Interactions; **the filer's own pro
  forma, $153.6M to $143.5M (FY2023-24) and $225.6M to $211.7M (FY2024-25)**; Legacy SoundHound alone about $45.9M to $30.7M in FY2024. Then LivePerson, revenue down
  53% since FY2022, bought in September 2026.
- **Contracted revenue is thin and falling**: GAAP RPO $79.5M to $60.0M in two quarters, $37.3M due within a year against $203.2M of TTM revenue.
- **[E4-04]**: R&D 58% of revenue, and the capability that changed the field is rented (*"large language models like OpenAI's ChatGPT"*); five acquisitions since January 2024.
- **The row** (Cerence, LivePerson, Five9, NICE): SoundHound last on every margin and cash column; five-year owner cash -176.3% of revenue against NICE +14.7%.
