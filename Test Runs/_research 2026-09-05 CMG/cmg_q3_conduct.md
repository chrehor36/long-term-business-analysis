## Pay design and outcomes (DEF 14A 2026)
- 2025 AIP: CPF 75% / IPF 25%; payout 0-275%; **>200% must be paid in deferred stock units
  with 2-3yr mandatory deferral**; **food-safety modifier can only REDUCE (−20% max)** —
  "our executive officers cannot earn a higher bonus for strong food safety performance."
- **2025 CPF outcomes — the rigging check: CRS 40% weight, threshold +2.0%/target +5.0%,
  actual −1.7% → 0% payout. RCF Margin 40% weight, threshold 26.25%/target 27.0% (set ABOVE
  2024's 26.7% actual), actual 25.62% → 0% payout. SARs 20% weight paid at max → CPF 40%.**
  IPFs 70-100%. Pay moved down with the bad year; targets were not lowered to meet results
  — the DG/ULTA pattern does NOT replicate.
- 2023-25 PSUs paid 273% (the 2023-24 crest inside the window — pay rode the good years,
  symmetric with the 40% in the bad one).
- 402(v) four most important measures: RCF Dollars, CRS Growth, SARs, RCF Margin % — all
  operating metrics; no adjusted-EPS/EBITDA pay plumbing.
- TSR (10-K graph, 2020=100): CMG 134 vs S&P 500 196 vs S&P Restaurants 136 at 2025.
  Five-year CAP-vs-TSR: CMG under the index by 62 pts, at par with the restaurant index.
