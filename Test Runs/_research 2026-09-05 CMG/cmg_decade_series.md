### The [E2-44] price ladder, cumulative
- Menu price 2021-2025 filed: +8.5% × +12.0% × +5.2% × +2.9% × +2.1% = **+34.3% cumulative** (1.343x).
- Traffic (transactions) 2021-2025 filed: +10.3 × +0.9 × +5.0 × +5.3 × −2.9 = **+19.5% cumulative**.
- Traffic held POSITIVE through the steepest price years (2021-24: +23.1% cumulative) and broke
  in 2025 (−2.9) when pricing had already slowed to +2.1 — the 2025 break is NOT concurrent-price
  elasticity; it is demand/value-perception timed with slowed pricing. Dated: comps went negative
  Q1-2025 (H1-2025 T −3.7, Q2-2025 T −4.9 the trough), recovered to lapped-positive by Q2-2026 (+1.0).
- AUV: $2.424M (2015 peak) → $1.868M (2016, −23% E. coli) → $1.940M (2017) → $3.213M (2024 peak)
  → $3.104M (2025, −3.4%).

### E. coli base case [E3-30]
2015 Q4 food-safety collapse: comps 2016 −20.4%, AUV −23%. Recovery: +6.4/+4.0/+11.1 (2017-19);
AUV passed its 2015 peak ~2021. The brand survived an existential food-safety crisis with zero
franchisee buffer and recovered fully — filed evidence FOR brand resilience. Recovery took ~5 years.
