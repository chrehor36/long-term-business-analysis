## Q2 — IS IT A FRANCHISE? **[E3-03]**
*The analyst's incentive, stated before the reading (operator rule 9, [E4-27], [E4-26]): the quote has fallen 32.8% in twelve weeks, and a name that has just fallen invites a reader to pass it through to the price question to see whether it is cheap; the register also holds more Q2 closes than gate-clearers, which invites the opposite lean. Neither the price nor the tally belongs in this question. Because the reading below closes the file, **the case for IN is written first and at full strength** [E4-51], and [E3-47]'s warning is carried: a wrongly closed file inside the circle is the error the corpus rates most expensive.*

### THE STRONGEST EVIDENCE AGAINST THIS VERDICT (the case for IN), stated as its holders would state it [E4-51, E4-26, E3-47]
1. **Positions with no close substitute, for decades.** The 10-K: *"unique, sole-source positions"*; *"a presence on more than 400 defense platforms and more than 3,000 programs"*; the Electro-Mechanical Systems division, whose *"primary customer"* is *"the U.S. Navy"*, *"has served the U.S. Navy for over 60 years and is a supplier or sole source provider of pumps that are used in the nuclear propulsion system"* (FY2011 10-K); *"content on every reactor operating in the U.S., Canada, and U.K."*; FY2011: *"significant engineering expertise is a limiting factor to competition, particularly in the U.S. Government market."* A qualified part on a submarine reactor plant or a licensed nuclear station has no substitute for the platform's life. [E3-03] clause (2), at the level of the platform, passes.
2. **Returns on the capital the business uses are high and rose.** Operating income on net tangible capital (equity plus debt less cash, goodwill and purchased intangibles; `rotc.py` → `rotc_out.txt`): **48.7% (FY2015), 63.7%, 76.2%, 76.7%, 83.0%, 50.3% (FY2020), 54.0%, 52.4%, 56.8%, 63.0%, 70.8% (FY2025)** [E2-43, E3-46]. On all capital net of cash, goodwill included, 10.9-20.3%.
3. **Margins rose in every segment for a decade.** Consolidated operating margin **8.6% (FY2012) → 14.1% (FY2016) → 18.1% (FY2025)**, 19.3% in the second quarter of 2026; the defense segment 16.9% (FY2014) → 23.2% (FY2018), Defense Electronics 19.5% (FY2020 recast) → 27.3% (FY2025); Commercial/Industrial 14.0-15.8% (FY2016-FY2019), Aerospace & Industrial 15.5-17.0% (FY2021-FY2025); Power 13.8-16.9%, Naval & Power 14.2-17.2% (FY2021-FY2025) (`seg_out.txt`). [E4-32]: the direction has been one way.
4. **Gross margin held through the cost shock.** 36.1% (FY2019), 35.2% (FY2020), 37.1%, **37.3% (FY2022), 37.5% (FY2023)**, 37.0%, 37.2% (FY2025) (companyfacts `GrossProfit` over revenue, `series_out.txt`); the filer: *"We have consistently focused on mitigating inflation through pricing and operational excellence initiatives, and generally have been able to offset these cost increases, as a portion of our contracts contain terms and conditions that enable us to pass inflationary price increases to our customers."* Mercury Systems' gross margin fell from 41.7% to 23.5% over the same shock; Ducommun's dipped two points.
5. **The closest rival in its most technological segment collapsed while it widened.** Mercury Systems, the other US maker of open-standard rugged embedded computing, earned 7.8-11.7% operating margin in FY2015-FY2021 and **−17.7% to +3.2% in FY2022-FY2026**; Curtiss-Wright's Defense Electronics earned 19.5-27.3% in FY2020-FY2025 and 28.0% in the June 2026 quarter. A lead held through a technology generation against the same rival is the maintained class of [E4-04], not the rebuilt one.
6. **Demand is not the question.** Backlog $4,076M at FY2025 (+18%), book-to-bill 1.16 in the June 2026 quarter, *"record demand for our defense electronics products"*; naval backlog on the Columbia and Virginia programs; nuclear plant life extensions to 60 and 80 years.

### THE READING

  - (2) No close substitute: **for a platform already designed, passes; at the point where the price is set, the filer says otherwise.** The qualification that makes a part irreplaceable on a platform is won in a competition for the next platform, and the 10-K describes that competition in the same words every year: **"We compete globally, primarily based on technology and pricing"** (FY2014-FY2025 in these words; FY2013 *"We compete globally primarily based on technology and pricing"*; FY2011 *"We compete globally based on technology and pricing; however, significant engineering expertise is a limiting factor to competition, particularly in the U.S. Government market."*; the FY2010 and FY2012 files wrap their lines and the search did not reach them).   - **FY2016-FY2020 10-Ks, under the heading "Impacts of inflation, pricing, and volume":** *"We have not historically been and do not expect to be significantly impacted by inflation.
