### The four findings that matter

1. **`acq_note` was wrong in sign, in year and in mechanism, and the arithmetic that made it
   is recoverable to the dollar.** The screen said *"NET CASH INFLOW on the acquisition line
   ($11,783M, 242% of cap) - cash acquired exceeded cash paid."* **10,589 + 1,142 + 52 =
   11,783.** It summed three consecutive years of
   `PaymentsToAcquireBusinessesNetOfCashAcquired` on **absolute values** and then took the
   sign of the smallest of the three -- a genuine +$52M post-closing settlement in FY2023 --
   and reported it as the sign of the whole. The filed FY2022 statement reads *"Acquisitions,
   net of cash acquired | ( 10,589 )"*. **Celanese paid $11.7bn across 2021-22 for Santoprene
   and DuPont Mobility & Materials; it did not receive $11.8bn.** **This is a generalisable
   tooling defect, not a one-name typo: any filer with a multi-year acquisition programme and
   one negative year will produce the same inverted note.** Suggested fix, not applied:
   report the acquisition line **per year with its sign**, and refuse to characterise a
   direction on a multi-year sum at all.
2. **Q2 closed on the company's own published price-and-volume decomposition, and the
   competitor row is what made it a finding rather than a description of a downturn.**
   Engineered Materials -- the differentiated half -- **cut price in three consecutive years
   (-1%, -3%, -1%) while volume fell in three consecutive years (-5%, -4%, -3%)**, the 10-K
   naming *"competitive market dynamics"*. The Acetyl Chain is **[E2-58]** verbatim from the
   other side: *"an environment with greater supply than demand"*, printed in two successive
   10-Ks, with price **-17%, -6%, -6% and then +18% in one quarter**. The only escape
   [E2-58] allows is *"a cost advantage that is both wide and sustainable"*, and Celanese
   claims exactly that in Item 1. **The row refutes the second adjective:** on one metric
   (EBIT over capital employed) across seven SEC-registrant peers and eight years, Celanese
   is **second of eight on the FY2018-FY2025 mean (12.4%) and sixth of eight on the
   FY2023-FY2025 mean (1.2%)**, behind Eastman at 5.7%. Adding the impairments back and
   stripping the 2023 disposal gain gives 6.1%, 6.8%, 5.5% -- **still only the peer median.**
3. **The `deal_note` liveness check paid for the fourth time this month, and the brief told

### Tooling defects found

1. **`acq_note` characterises a direction on a multi-year absolute sum.** Reproduced exactly:
   10,589 + 1,142 + 52 = 11,783, sign taken from the 52. **It will misreport any filer with
   more than one acquisition year and one negative year.**
2. **`spread_caveat` was right that the 4-construction width could not see past five years,
   and understated the problem.** On this name the five-year window is not merely narrow, it
   is **non-comparable**: FY2021 and FY2022 are a different, unlevered company. A window
   caveat that talks about width should also talk about **perimeter**.
3. **`name_change_note` and `wc_note` were both empty on a filer that made two acquisitions,
   one disposal, one joint-venture formation and three plant closures inside the window.**
   Empty means the detector found nothing, and the detectors look for name changes and for a
   single working-capital line above 30% of a year's operating cash. **Neither looks for a
   perimeter move.** Recorded, not fixed.
4. **The register-entry count was re-derived by counting**, with both headings anchored to
   line start: **141 entries stood above this one, so CE is 142**, which agrees with the BRBR
   entry's own claim of 141 directly below it.
