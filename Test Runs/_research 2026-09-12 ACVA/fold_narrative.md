### TOOLING DEFECTS
1. **The queue price can be one event stale, and the brief carried it.** The pre-check struck ACVA at a close taken before an
   after-hours merger 8-K. **`deal_note` did count the Item 1.01 correctly**; what it cannot say is which 1.01 is a merger. **A
   testable refinement, NOT measured here:** the merger 8-K listed **Exhibit 2.1 "Agreement and Plan of Merger"** — Item 601(b)(2)
   exhibits are plans of acquisition — while the ASR 8-K listed only an Exhibit 10.1. Whether EX-2.1 separates mergers from other
   1.01s across the queue is for the next session to measure, not for this fold to assert (the Item 2.01 lesson of 2026-09-12).
2. **The screen counts seller float as owner earnings.** `wc_note` fired correctly (285%) and the row still carried the float inside
   both ends. For settlement businesses (auction houses, payment processors that hold funds on balance sheet) the ends are not owner
   earnings until the payables and receivables lines are removed; only a reader sees which filers are in the class.
3. **Operating cash omits floorplan credit losses** where finance receivables sit in investing. No tag-level guard can see the
   classification; a source limit, joining section 5 of the resume note.
4. **The D&A end double-counts capitalised SBC** — its amortization sits inside D&A ($6.2M FY2025) while the SBC line already carries
   it. A new variant of the acquired-amortization defect (SWK, ALKT).
5. **SBC resolves but is incomplete on the add-back tag:** ACV tags its CAPITALISED SBC under `AllocatedShareBasedCompensationExpense`
   ($7.6M FY2025), so the max-rule picks the add-back and silently drops the capitalised portion ($19.0M FY2021-25), which is in
   neither operating cash nor cash capex. The BOEING lesson in a third form: resolving is not complete.
6. **`cap_flag`'s two-date comparison, confirmed a second time** (ALKT defect 1): float struck at $16.22, cap at $6.76-equivalent.
7. **`oe_bottom` / `oe_top` from different windows and different (c) ends — the sixth instance.**
