# Company Run — American Homes 4 Rent (AMH) — 2026-07-15
Framework v3.0. From the REIT mechanical screen. OE from real FY2025 10-K
data (Archives/edgar/data/1562401).

## STEP 0 — RATE REFRESH
US 30-yr **5.10%**. Mkt cap $12.21B — no size premium.

## GATE 1 — CIRCLE OF COMPETENCE
Owns, develops (a genuine "built-for-rental" development pipeline, not just
acquisition of existing homes), and leases single-family rental homes across
Sun Belt/growth markets. Same core rent × occupancy − opex − capex economics
as Invitation Homes, plus a real development/construction business line
(distinct enough from pure-acquisition SFR to be worth naming explicitly).
**VERDICT: PASS**, with the development segment noted as a second thing to
understand, similar to INVH's management-fee business.

## GATE 2 — MOAT
Franchise test [E3-03]: needed/desired — yes; no close substitute — **weak**
(same SFR/apartment substitutability as INVH); unregulated — largely yes,
same lighter rent-control exposure as INVH relative to the urban-apartment
peers. Company's own commentary explicitly ties its build-for-rent strategy
to **easing the on-going per-home capex burden over time** (see Gate 4) —
a genuine, disclosed operating thesis, not marketing language, since newer
homes need less recurring capex. **Class: NARROW** (operational scale +
newer-build cost advantage), same category as INVH.
**VERDICT: PASS with NARROW classification.**

## GATE 3 — MANAGEMENT
CEO **Bryan Smith**, Chairman **Matthew J. Hart** — separate roles, cleaner
governance structure than UDR's combined Chairman/CEO. Neither individual's
tenure/background was independently verified against a primary-source
biography this pass. No litigation/restatement/integrity flags found.
**VERDICT: PASS**, verification-depth limitation consistent with the other
runs in this batch.

## GATE 4 — FINANCIAL QUALITY (full OE formula, real maintenance capex)
AMH uses the clearest possible terminology: **"Recurring and other capital
expenditures for single-family properties"** — separate from "Renovations to
single-family properties" (newly-acquired-home turnover costs) and "Cash paid
for development activity" (new construction, by far the largest capital
outlay at AMH: $810.5M in 2025 alone, correctly excluded from maintenance
capex):

| FY | Net income | Depreciation & amort. | Recurring capex | **OE = NI+D&A−capex** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | $468.142M | $477.010M | $121.751M | **$823.401M** |
| 2025 | $513.392M | $504.341M | $118.211M | **$899.522M** |

**Only 2 years matched cleanly this pass** (FY2023 capex figure exists —
$134.176M — but wasn't matched against a confirmed FY2023 NI/D&A pair in
this session; flagged, not fabricated). Recurring capex here is a notably
**smaller fraction of D&A (23–24%)** than the urban apartment REITs
(28–37% at AVB/EQR) — consistent with the company's own stated newer-housing-
stock argument.
**LOWEST VERIFIED TIER (of years matched): $823.401M (FY2024).**
Fortress (financial/leverage-ceiling track): Total assets $13,242.120M ÷
Total equity $7,709.506M = **1.718:1** — the **lowest leverage of any REIT
in this batch.**
**VERDICT: PASS.**

## GATE 5 — INVERSION
1. **Moat destruction** — SFR-specific institutional-ownership legislation
   risk, same category as INVH. Scored **[P]**.
2. **Management/key-person** — separate Chairman/CEO, no major red flags,
   but tenure/succession history not independently verified. Scored **[D]**.
3. **Balance-sheet stress** — 1.72:1 assets/equity, the lowest-leverage
   REIT in the batch. Scored **[D]**.
4. **Development-execution risk** (a real, AMH-specific inversion the pure-
   acquisition SFR/apartment peers don't carry: cost overruns, land-price
   inflation, or a housing-construction downturn affecting the build pipeline
   specifically) — addressed via the smaller, disclosed per-home capex
   argument, but not eliminated. Scored **[P]**.

No [U]. **Thesis-breaking metric:** SFR-specific adverse state legislation
in a top-5 market, or a sustained deterioration in build-for-rent margins/
delivery timelines.
**VERDICT: PASS**, with the SFR regulatory risk and development-execution
risk both carried forward as real (not hypothetical) watch items.

## GATE 6 — VALUATION
**6-B Statute** (lowest tier, $823.401M): yield = 823.401 / 12,210 =
**6.74%** vs. hurdle **5.10%** → **PASSES**, with a **1.64pp cushion — the
strongest cushion of any REIT run so far** (better than AVB, EQR, or INVH).
**Book Two DCF (Ruling 4 — built 2026-07-15 PM)**: Gate 3 carried a real
caveat (neither CEO nor Chairman's tenure/background independently verified)
→ discount rate = 5.10% + 2.5% NARROW spread + 0.75% caveat bump = **8.35%**.
g1: only 2 years of matched OE data exist (FY2024→FY2025), so a multi-year
CAGR cross-check isn't available — flagged as a real data-thinness limitation
(same one already flagged at Gate 4) rather than smoothed over. Single
available YoY = **+9.24%**, used as g1 for lack of a second, independent
figure to take the lower of. 10yr NARROW fade to 2% terminal.
- **IV = $17,862.3M** | Max buy (20% MOS) = **$14,289.9M**
- Current mkt cap $12,210M → **below the max-buy line** (mkt cap is 85.4% of
  max buy, 68.4% of raw IV) — genuinely cheap on Book Two, not just a bare
  Statute pass.

**Scream Test**: Book One PASSES with the widest cushion of any REIT in the
batch (1.64pp). Book Two independently confirms — price sits below the
20%-MOS-floored max buy, not just below raw IV. **The two books AGREE** →
this clears the Scream Test cleanly, no whisper.

**VERDICT: BUY-ELIGIBLE** (price $12,210M ≤ max buy $14,289.9M) — the first
REIT in this batch to clear both books at full size, not just a Statute
starter. Carry the g1 data-thinness limitation forward as the one real
caveat on this result: a third year of matched OE data would meaningfully
strengthen (or weaken) the g1 anchor used here.

## GATE 8 — REVERSE DCF
Implied g1 at current price = **+0.11%** — the market is pricing in almost
no OE growth at all, well below both the 9.24% single-year anchor AND AMH's
own disclosed newer-housing-stock/lower-capex operating thesis. Bear case
(g1=-5%): IV = $9,812.1M. Upside (Base IV − price) = $5,652.3M; downside
(price − Bear IV) = $2,397.9M. **Asymmetry ratio 2.36:1 — clears the 2:1
minimum**, the only REIT in this batch to do so.
**VERDICT: ENTER ELIGIBLE**, sized per Gate 7 below.

## GATE 7 — ENTRY COMMITMENTS
Thesis-confirming metric: recurring capex staying meaningfully below the
28–37% D&A fraction seen at the urban apartment REITs (currently 23–24%,
consistent with the newer-build-stock thesis). Thesis-breaking metric:
adverse state-level institutional-SFR legislation in a top-5 market, or
recurring capex rising toward AVB/EQR-style ratios (would undercut the core
"newer homes need less capex" argument this pass leans on). Given this is
the only name in the batch clearing Gate 8, and given the real g1
data-thinness caveat above, size as a genuine but not maximum-conviction
position — the 2.36:1 asymmetry clears the bar but isn't overwhelming, and
rests on a shorter matched-data history than AVB/EQR/INVH's OE figures.

## ADDENDUM — PRE-ENTRY VERIFICATION, 2026-07-16 — VERDICT DOWNGRADED
Run before a planned ~$3k entry, per the same discipline applied to NCLTY.
Two findings, both material, both adverse:

**1. The FY2023 anchor gap is now closed — and the anchor drops.** SEC XBRL
(CIK 1562401) provides the missing FY2023 pair on the same ProfitLoss
basis the tier table used (confirmed: the FY2024 $468.142M / FY2025
$513.392M figures match the ProfitLoss tag exactly): FY2023 ProfitLoss
$432.142M + D&A $456.550M − recurring capex $134.176M (already on file) =
**FY2023 OE $754.5M — the new lowest verified tier**, 8.4% below the
$823.4M anchor previously used. On the stricter available-to-common basis
(NI $366.224M), FY2023 OE is $688.6M. Recomputed:
- Book One: 754.5/12,210 = **6.18%** (strict basis 5.64%) vs 5.10% — still
  passes, cushion narrowed from 1.64pp to 1.08pp (strict: 0.54pp).
- Book Two (caveat rate 8.35%, g1 = 9.18%, lower of YoY/CAGR on the
  corrected 3-year table): IV $16,327M, max buy $13,062M — cap at **93% of
  max buy** on the ProfitLoss basis, but **101% (fails) on the strict
  common basis**, and **126% of max buy (above IV) at the 2% growth floor**.
- Under the wide screen's own robustness rule (2026-07-16: a BUY-ELIGIBLE
  verdict must survive the 2% g1 floor — the rule that demoted ZTS/FOX/
  FOXA/ADBE/CB), **AMH no longer qualifies: growth-dependent WHISPER →
  STATUTE-ONLY.** The original deep-dive verdict predated that rule; with
  the corrected anchor it fails the same test applied to everything else.

**2. Gate 3 verification hardens the caveat instead of clearing it.**
- CEO Bryan Smith took office **January 1, 2025** — an orderly internal
  succession from co-founder David Singelyn, with an experienced
  independent chairman (Matthew Hart, ex-Hilton/Disney/Host CFO) and the
  Hughes founding family aligned at ~10% (Tamara Gustavson, board member,
  largest individual holder). Structurally sound — but ~1.5 years of CEO
  tenure, not a seasoned record.
- **The Gate 5 regulatory [P] has partially materialized**: a DOJ lawsuit
  (filed 2025-01-07) against six large landlords over algorithmic-pricing
  practices, with AMH among the companies under scrutiny; a Senate Banking
  Committee letter addressed to AMH's CEO (March 2026) on institutional
  ownership of housing; and at least one tenant complaint referred to a
  state regulatory body (May 2025). None of this is adjudicated wrongdoing
  — but a pending federal action on pricing conduct is an unresolved
  integrity question, and the Ruling 4 caveat bump therefore STAYS.

**REVISED VERDICT: STATUTE-ONLY at most — effectively WAIT for entry
purposes.** The planned entry was NOT placed. What would change this:
(a) FY2026 results extending the OE recovery (raises the anchor and the
g1 confidence), and/or (b) resolution/clarification of the DOJ matter.
Until then the corrected arithmetic is whisper-grade and the framework's
rule is: pass.
