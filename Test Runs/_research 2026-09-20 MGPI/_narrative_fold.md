### The competitor row, and a census finding worth keeping

**The census came from a peer's own filing**, as **[E3-28]** and the brief require. Brown-Forman's
FY2026 10-K (accession `0000014693-26-000024`) names eight companies as the spirits industry —
*"Bacardi Limited, Becle S.A.B. de C.V., Davide Campari-Milano N.V., Diageo PLC, LVMH Moet Hennessy
Louis Vuitton SE, Pernod Ricard SA, Remy Cointreau, and Suntory Global Spirits"* — and **exactly one of
the eight files with the SEC.** Two things follow and both are recorded as standing facts for the next
run in this industry:

1. **American spirits is structurally unmeasurable from SEC filings.** Six of the eight are private or
   foreign-listed, and MGPI's *contract-distilling* rivals — the leg carrying 34% of its gross profit —
   are private Kentucky and Indiana distillers that file nothing. **The row cannot measure that leg and
   this run does not pretend otherwise.** [E2-58] reaches its conclusion without the row; the row would
   be needed to establish the *exception*.
2. **No SEC filer names MGP Ingredients as a competitor.** An EDGAR full-text search for
   `"MGP Ingredients"` in Form 10-K over 2024-01-01 to 2026-09-20 returns **38 hits, 21 of them MGPI's
   own filings**, the rest board-overlap and unrelated (Calavo, Canopy Growth, Clearway, Duckhorn,
   Farmer Brothers, FHLB Topeka, Laird Superfood, Whole Earth Brands). Run with `fts_count()` and the
   bare zero-padded CIK, so a malformed zero-hit is excluded.

**Five peers attempted, four producing the metric.** On operating margin from each filer's own XBRL,
MGPI is **third of four on the 9-year mean (11.4%, barely above Ingredion's 10.8%) and LAST OF FIVE on
the recent three-year mean (3.6%, against Ingredion 12.6%, Diageo 18.9%, Constellation 21.7%,

### Tooling defects found

1. **`cap_flag`'s diagnostic text asserts a contradiction that does not exist when the float date and
   the cap date are far apart in a falling market.** Three branches now exist (CE/CALM/EMBC: the float
   *is* the cap; MCFT: a real affiliate block; **MGPI: a real affiliate block AND a date gap large
   enough to invert the ratio**), and the flag's wording admits only the first two.
2. **`deal_note`'s advisory clause is now 0 for 3.** Recorded as a text defect, not fixed here.
3. **`sources.annual()` returns nothing for Diageo, and the brief's stated reason is not the operative
   one.** The brief said `floor_screen.annual()` reads only the `USD` unit, so a foreign filer returns
   nothing whatever its tags. **Measured on DEO's companyfacts: the USD unit IS present** — Diageo
   reports Revenue and ProfitLossFromOperatingActivities in USD from FY2022 and in GBP before that. The
   call returns nothing because the **IFRS concept names differ**: `Revenue` and
   `ProfitLossFromOperatingActivities`, not `Revenues` and `OperatingIncomeLoss`. **Four usable years of
   Diageo were recovered by naming the IFRS concepts directly, and they are in the row.** The limit is
   real but it is a *tag-name* limit, not a *unit* limit, and stating it the other way would have cost
   this run its only measurable foreign peer.
4. **ADM does not tag `OperatingIncomeLoss` at all**, so it is named in the row and marked
   UNAVAILABLE rather than dropped.
5. **`annual()`'s 340-380-day duration filter silently drops a fiscal-year-change stub.** MGPI's six
   months to 2011-12-31 exist in the filings and in companyfacts and appear in no annual series, so a
   "17-year record" has a hole in it that no flag reports. Recorded, not fixed.
6. **The register-entry count was re-derived by counting** with a line-start regex inside the slice from
   the `## COMPLETED FROM THE QUEUE` **heading line** (line 502, not its earlier textual occurrences) to
   `## THE WRITE-EARLY PROTOCOL`: **142 entries stood above this one, so MGPI is 143**, which agrees with
   the CE entry's own claim of 142 directly below it.
