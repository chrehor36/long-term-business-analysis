## 2. Martin Marietta Materials (MLM), CIK 916076. Cement product line (Texas; California in 2014-2015)

**Correction to the brief.** The filings do not show a February 2024 sale of MLM's cement business to Quikrete. The FY2023 10-K (Item 1) says: "On February 9, 2024, the Company closed the sale of it South Texas cement business and related cement distribution terminals" [sic, "it" for "its" as filed], and "The cement grade limestone reserves at the Hunter production facility in New Braunfels, Texas, were included with the February 2024 divestiture to CRH Americas Materials, Inc." The Midlothian, Texas plant stayed with MLM: the FY2025 10-K (acc 0001193125-26-059193) says an asset exchange with Quikrete was agreed on August 3, 2025 under which "QUIKRETE would receive the Company's Midlothian cement plant" and "The Company's cement operations are classified as assets held for sale as of December 31, 2025."

**What the line holds.** Cement was a separate reportable segment for 2014-2016 (TXI acquired July 1, 2014: two Texas plants, Midlothian and Hunter, plus "one plant in Southern California", divested September 30, 2015, per the FY2015 EX-13). From 2017 it is a product line inside the West Group segment; MLM files product-line revenue and gross profit (Note "Revenues and Gross Profit" and MD&A). The Lehigh West Region California cement plants (acquired Oct 1, 2021) were reported as discontinued operations throughout, so 2016-2023 continuing figures are the two Texas plants only. From FY2024 the product line is "Cement and ready mixed concrete" combined; no cement-only profit is filed for 2024 or 2025.

**Profit measure.** Product-line GROSS PROFIT (revenue less cost of revenues, which includes DD&A). This is below an EBITDA measure (D&A deducted) and above an operating-earnings measure (SG&A not deducted). Segment earnings from operations and DD&A exist only for the 2014-2016 segment years.

**Revenue basis break.** Through the FY2022 10-K, revenue is "products and services" revenue, excluding freight. The FY2023 10-K reclassified: "As of December 31, 2023, the Company combined products and services revenues and freight revenues into the Total revenues line item" and restated 2021-2022 on that basis. Both series are shown; they are not mixed within a row.

## 8. LIMITS (plain list)

1. **Only one filed pure-play cement segment with a profit measure over many years: Summit's Cement segment (2013-2023).** It is two Mississippi River plants (one before July 2015) plus terminals and a small waste-processing line. Summit filed no FY2024 10-K (Form 15-12G, 2025-02-21), so the Argos USA combination never appears in a filed Cement segment.
2. **MLM is cement-only but the measure is GROSS PROFIT**, after DD&A and before SG&A. It is neither an EBITDA nor an operating-earnings margin and should not be set beside Summit's EBITDA margin as if like for like. Revenue basis changes in the FY2023 10-K (freight added into revenue). 2024-2025 cement is bundled with ready mixed concrete. 2014-2015 include a California plant and partial-year effects.
3. **Bundled segments (margin not cement's):** Amrize Building Materials (cement roughly half of gross segment revenue; aggregates, ready-mix, asphalt, other; U.S. AND Canada). Cemex United States (cement, ready-mix, aggregates, urbanization solutions). CRH Americas Materials Solutions (Essential Materials about 30% of revenue, the rest readymix, asphalt, paving and construction services; North America). Titan America Florida and Mid-Atlantic (cement, aggregates, ready-mix, block, fly ash). None of these files a cement-only profit.
4. **Measure mismatch across peers:** Summit Adjusted EBITDA (also operating income); Amrize, CRH, Titan segment Adjusted EBITDA; Cemex Operating EBITDA (also operating earnings before other expenses, net); MLM gross profit; Monarch income from operations. Each company defines its EBITDA adjustments differently (definitions quoted in the filings; not harmonised here).
5. **Accounting basis mismatch:** Cemex and Titan America report under IFRS (IFRS 16 puts lease cost below EBITDA); the others under U.S. GAAP.
6. **Revenue basis mismatch:** Titan segment revenue is external revenue; Amrize segment revenue is net of interproduct eliminations; Cemex uses revenue including intragroup (U.S. intragroup is tiny); Summit segment revenue reflects external sales ("Intercompany sales are immaterial"); MLM product-line revenue is gross of interproduct eliminations.
7. **Missing years:** Amrize has only 2022-2025 (Form 10 plus one 10-K); no earlier SEC filings exist for the Holcim U.S. business. CRH 10-K data starts 2021 (earlier years are in 20-Fs with different segments; not pulled). Cemex 2017-2025 pulled; earlier 20-Fs exist but were not pulled. Titan America 2022-2025 only (IPO 2025). Summit and MLM end in 2023 for cement-only data. Monarch ends 2013.
8. **Volumes and prices are not on one basis:** Summit (tons sold, average selling price), MLM (total tons incl. internal; average selling price), Amrize (tons sold excl. trading; the Form 10 gives a different, rounded, 2023 figure), Titan (external tons; average external price). Cemex and CRH give only percentage changes for U.S. cement volume and price.
9. **Margins and means are computed here** (segment profit / segment revenue, simple means of yearly margins). Every other number is transcribed from the filed document named against it. One cross-check per peer against a second place in the same or a later filing is recorded in that peer's notes.
10. **Not examined:** 10-K/A amendments (Summit FY2018 10-K/A, MLM FY2018 10-K/A, CRH FY2023 10-K/A) and quarterly filings.
