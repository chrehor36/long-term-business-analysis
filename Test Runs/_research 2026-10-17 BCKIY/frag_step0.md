This is the Bank of England's fitted UK government nominal spot curve at 30 years on 15 October 2026 (5.8636%; file `latest-yield-curve-data.zip`, workbook "GLC Nominal daily data current month", sheet "4. spot curve"). Why this series: the protocol's table covers USD, JPY and EUR only. The Bank of England publishes the fitted gilt curve. The UK Debt Management Office publishes the yields of individual gilts, not a constant-maturity series. Either reading puts the rate between 5.7% and 5.9%, and nothing below turns on the difference.
