## GATE 1 — CIRCLE OF COMPETENCE
- Unit economics: a store leases space, employs staff, and sells a cheap commodity input
  at high gross margin. Owner earnings = transactions x ticket per store, less store labour
  and occupancy, less the capex to build and refurbish. Risk: price rises stop being
  absorbed and transactions fall.
- Informational edge: none claimed. This is a widely covered large cap; the only edge is a
  willingness to judge the turnaround on transaction data rather than headline comps.
- Segment count: 3 (North America, International, Channel Development) - each separately

## GATE 3 — MANAGEMENT
- Integrity: **PASS.** No fraud, accounting manipulation, cartel, bribery, or regulator
  finding of misconduct. NLRB matters examined and judged a contested labour dispute, not
  the dishonesty this gate tests.
- Competence: **the closest call here.** The prior regime's buyback record fails Buffett's
  standard outright - >$24B repurchased FY2018-25, $17.3B of it in FY2018-19 against a
  pre-announced $25B quota executed regardless of price and partly debt-funded. Result:
  **-$8.47B book equity** (2026-03-29) and $15.1B debt. The proof of error is the mirror
  image: with OE collapsed to ~$1.3B and the stock at $76.49, the company could repurchase
  only **$87M**. It bought heavily high and could not buy low - the exact inversion of
  [E5-01]. FY2025 dividends of $2.77B were **more than 2x owner earnings**.
  Offsetting: this gate judges the stewards going forward, and the current CEO's decisions
  are correct - buybacks halted, China sold at a full price (60% to Boyu, ~$4B, closed
  April 2026, $536.3M gain) rather than defended, ~$2.8B applied to debt, cash redirected
  into stores and labour.
- Alignment: comp structure and CEO package reviewed; nothing disqualifying found.
- **VERDICT: [x] PASS [ ] FAIL - STOP** - but a pass on a ~7-quarter record, with the
  inherited negative equity treated as a permanent constraint, not a cured one.
