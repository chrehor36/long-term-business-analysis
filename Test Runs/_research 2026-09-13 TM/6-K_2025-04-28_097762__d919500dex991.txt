 Regarding Certain Media Reports 
 Certain media reported that Toyota Motor Corporation ("TMC") is considering investing in the privatization of Toyota Industries
Corporation. However, while we are currently exploring various possibilities, including partial investment from TMC, this information was not disclosed by TMC. Regarding the Toyota Group shares held by TMC, we continuously evaluate the most optimal
approach, but no decisions have been made at this time. 
 If any matters requiring disclosure arise, TMC will promptly make an
announcement.
