 CHANGES TO BOARD MEMBERS, EXECUTIVE STRUCTURE, AND SENIOR PROFESSIONALS
 Changes to Board Members, Executive Structure, and Senior Professionals 
 Toyota Motor Corporation 
 TMC Announces Changes to Board Members, Executive Structure, and Senior Professionals/Senior Management 
 Toyota City, Japan, June 12, 2025—Toyota Motor Corporation (TMC) today assigned members of the board of directors at its ordinary general
shareholders' meeting, and appointed representative directors and directors with special titles at a subsequent Board meeting. In addition, effective July 1, 2025, TMC will implement changes to its executive structure and senior
professional/senior management employee assignments as described below.
