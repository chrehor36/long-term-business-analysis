 Toyota Motor Corporation 
 TMC Announces Changes to Board Members and Executive Structure 
 Toyota City, Japan, June17, 2026—Toyota Motor Corporation (TMC) today assigned members of the board of directors at its ordinary general
shareholders' meeting, and appointed representative directors and directors with special titles at a subsequent Board meeting. In addition, on the same day, TMC announced its executive appointments following the ordinary general
shareholders' meeting.
