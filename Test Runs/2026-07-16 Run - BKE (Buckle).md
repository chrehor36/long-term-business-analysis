## GATE 1 — CIRCLE OF COMPETENCE

## GATE 2 — MOAT
From BKE's own 10-K: **"The men's and women's apparel industries are
highly competitive with fashion, selection, quality, price, location,
store environment, and service being the principal competitive factors...
the Company believes it competes mainly on the basis of customer service
and merchandise selection. [Competitors include] Abercrombie & Fitch,
American Eagle Outfitters, Boot Barn, Dick's, Gap, Hollister..."** — a
generic competitive set with no disclosed structural claim, the same
filing pattern that eliminated LEN/PHM.
The countervailing filed evidence is the returns-on-capital prong:
**ROE ≈ 49% ($209.7M NI on $424.6M equity) on a debt-light balance
sheet** — sustained exceptional returns are the franchise test's own
proof standard, and they don't happen by accident in commodity apparel
(fit-service culture and niche denim positioning are the plausible but
undisclosed mechanisms). Weighed against: NI down 18% from the FY2022
peak ($254.8M → $209.7M), a real erosion signal.
**Class: NARROW (carried by the returns prong), Direction: NARROWING.**
**VERDICT: PASS**, the most reluctant Gate 2 pass of the sleeper set.
