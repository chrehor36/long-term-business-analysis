# THE COMPETITOR ROW — PINS, built 2026-09-07
**Required by [E3-28].** A moat is a claim about *relative* position and cannot be evidenced
from one company's numbers. Same metric, same window, filing-sourced.

**Sixth column added, not named by Pinterest: The Trade Desk** (10-K FY2025, accession
`0001671933-26-000014`). Included because it is the only pure-play *buy-side* advertising
platform of comparable size and it isolates the question of whether ad-tech economics or
Pinterest specifically explains the margin. It is **not a fair substitute comparable** — it
sells software to buyers of advertising, not advertising inventory — and that limit is
stated rather than blurred.

**Peers named: 5 of the 8 competitors the company itself names, plus 1 adjacent.** Buffett
says eight [E3-28]; the industry has eight and **three of them are unlisted**, which is a
hard limit on this row, not an omission.

**THE OPERATOR'S PRIOR IS REFUTED.** The CRWD run recorded that *"CrowdStrike ROSE into 68%
over three years where Pinterest's was a LEVEL."* **Pinterest's was not a level. It FELL into
68.6% from 105.7% — a 37-point improvement in two years, the steepest of any name in this
row except Reddit's IPO-distorted step.** The two companies arrive at the same ratio from
opposite directions, which is the opposite of the reading the CRWD run drew from it.

**But the half-year reverses it.** From the Q2 2026 10-Q (`0001506293-26-000104`): H1 2026
SBC **$555,963k** against operating cash flow of **$620,908k** — **89.5%**, against 72.6% in
H1 2025. Three years of improvement gave back in two quarters.

## THE USER AND PRICE SERIES — WHO FILES WHAT

| | user metric filed | latest | ARPU filed? | by geography? | **impressions / price-per-ad filed?** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **PINS** | **MAU (monthly)** | 619M (Dec-25), 640M (Jun-26) | **yes, $7.21** | **yes — 3 regions** | **NO — recorded sweep, 0 instances in 6 vintages** |
| META | DAP (daily) | 3.58bn avg Dec-25 | yes (ARPP) | yes — 4 regions | **YES — "ad impressions delivered" and "average price per ad"** |
| GOOGL | **NONE** | — | **no** | no | no |
| SNAP | DAU (daily) | 474M | yes, $3.62 (Q4) | yes — 4 regions | no |
| RDDT | DAUq (daily) | 121.4M | yes, $5.98 (Q4) | yes — US / RoW | no |

**THE DENOMINATOR WARNING, AND IT MATTERS.** Pinterest's ARPU is computed on a **monthly**
active base; Meta, Snap and Reddit compute theirs on a **daily** base. A monthly denominator
is several times larger than a daily one, so **Pinterest's $7.21 is not comparable to Snap's
$3.62-per-quarter or Reddit's $5.98-per-quarter and must not be put beside them.** Pinterest
discloses a WAU/MAU ratio of 62% but no DAU, so the conversion cannot be made from filings.
**Any cross-company ARPU comparison in this row is refused rather than estimated.** The
comparisons that ARE like-for-like — operating margin, SBC/OCF, capex/revenue, owner
earnings — are the ones the row is decided on.

**The sharper disclosure finding: Meta files the price series and Pinterest does not.**
Meta's 10-K reports **ad impressions delivered** and **average price per ad** separately, so
a reader can decompose revenue growth into volume and price. Pinterest reports only ARPU,
which is their product. **Recorded sweep of all six PINS 10-K vintages (FY2020–FY2025) for
"ad impressions", "impressions delivered" and "ad load": no instance found in any vintage.**
So the [E2-44](1) price test cannot be run on a true price series at Pinterest — only on
ARPU, which conflates price with ad load.

**NOTHING HAS BEEN WITHDRAWN IN SEVEN YEARS OF FILINGS. The only two changes both ADDED
disclosure:** the geographic split went from two buckets to three in Q1 2022 (more granular,
not less), and free cash flow was introduced in FY2024. **This is the fourth consecutive run
in which the operator's [E2-49] withdrawal prediction has failed** (QLYS, CRM, and now PINS,
after the CRWD case where it hit). **The FY2021 disclosure is the test case and Pinterest
passed it:** U.S. MAU fell 12% that year, from 98M to 86M — the single worst operating
datapoint in the company's history — and the company **published it as a number**, on the
face of the earnings release, with the decline shown in the growth column. [E2-49] demands
*"pre-set, long-lived and small bullseyes"*; Pinterest's have been pre-set and long-lived
across seven years including the year they were missed.

**And on Snap, as a control:** recorded sweep of Snap's FY2020–FY2025 10-Ks. DAU and ARPU
appear in all six vintages, by geography in all six. **Snap has withdrawn nothing either.**
Metric withdrawal is not a habit of this industry the way it is in enterprise software.

## WHAT THE ROW SHOWS

1. **Pinterest is fifth of six on the metric [E3-46] asks about.** GAAP operating margin
   7.6%, against Meta 41.4%, Alphabet 32.0%, Reddit 20.1%, Trade Desk 20.3%. Only Snap,
   which loses money, is below it.
2. **The single sharpest fact in the row: Reddit.** Public for two years, 52% of Pinterest's
   revenue, the same shape of business (advertising against user-generated content, near-zero
   capex, no data centres) — and **FY2025 owner earnings of $341.0M against Pinterest's
   $371.4M.** Reddit earns 92% of Pinterest's owner earnings on half its revenue, at a 20.1%
   operating margin against 7.6%, while growing revenue 69% against 16%.
3. **Pinterest's capital intensity is a genuine, measurable strength and it is real.** Capex
   0.77% of revenue, second-lowest in the row behind Reddit. Meta spent 34.7% and Alphabet
   22.7%. This is the [E2-44] second characteristic and Pinterest passes it outright.
4. **The scale asymmetry is not close.** Pinterest holds **0.7%** of the row's combined
   revenue ($4.2bn of $619.8bn) and **2.1%** of it excluding Alphabet's non-advertising
   businesses. Amazon's advertising *increment* in 2025 was 2.9x Pinterest's whole business.
5. **SBC/OCF: Pinterest is fourth of six, and moving in the right direction on the annual
   figure and the wrong one on the half-year.**

## THE ROW'S LIMIT — STATED [E3-61]

> *"In some businesses, the participants behave like a demented Kellogg. In other businesses,
> they don't … **I think you'd have to know the people involved.**"* — **[E3-61]**

The row shows position; it cannot show conduct. Three specific limits here:

1. **Three of the eight named competitors are unlisted** — TikTok/ByteDance, OpenAI and X.
   TikTok is, on any outside account, the most direct competitor for the attention of the
   demographic Pinterest monetises, and **it cannot be put in this row at all.** Anything
   the row says about Pinterest's relative position is silent on the largest single
   unmeasured threat.
2. **The ARPU denominators are not the same** (monthly vs daily) and the conversion is not
   available from filings, so the row **refuses** the ARPU comparison rather than estimating
   it. The most intuitive cross-company number in this industry is the one this row cannot
   honestly produce.
3. **Alphabet files no user metric at all**, so the largest advertising business in the world
   contributes nothing to the user-and-price half of the row. Recorded sweep of the FY2025
   10-K: no MAU, DAU, ARPU or equivalent; **no instance found.**
