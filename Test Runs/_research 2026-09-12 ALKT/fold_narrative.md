  $480.3M ARR; 18.9 cents of each revenue dollar is resold third-party service; organic growth 22.4% (FY2025),
  15.9% (Q2-2026, the first like-for-like quarter after MANTL).
- **Criterion (2), from the other side of the table.** Q2 Holdings' 10-K FY2025 names Alkami among ten
  digital-banking vendors (Candescent, Alkami, CSI, Backbase, Lumin Digital, Finastra, Bottomline, Fiserv, Jack Henry,
  FIS). Alkami's own MD&A: *"Each of our digital banking client wins is a competitive takeaway."* **The switching cost is
  real and it is the scarce input — for whichever vendor holds the client — and Alkami's growth is the proof that it
  can be crossed.**
- **Pricing, in the company's words:** *"in the past we have had, and expect to be required in the future, to change
  our pricing model, reduce our prices or accept other unfavorable contract terms."*

  **The overlay vendors have spent five years at or below zero; the core processors earn the returns — and control

### TOOLING DEFECTS
1. **`cap_flag` compares a current cap to a public float struck on a different date.** ALKT's float was set at $30.14
   (2025-06-30); the cap at ~$19.5. **Any name whose price has fallen by more than roughly a quarter since its last
   June 30 will fire "one of the two is wrong" when neither is.** The fix is to re-strike the cap at the float date
   (float date close x that date's cover count) before comparing. **Reported, not fixed.**
2. **The D&A end includes acquired-intangible amortization** — SWK's tooling defect 1, found again in the queue row:
   $22.3M of FY2025's $26.9M. On an acquirer it makes the "conservative" end over-conservative.
3. **The SBC max-rule double-counts cash-settled acquisition awards**: FY2025 P&L charge $80,098k includes $3.9M of MANTL
   awards settled in cash (already inside OCF); the cash-flow add-back is $76,188k. Same class as ARM FY2023; direction
   conservative.
4. **`deal_note` cannot see Schedule 13D.** An activist 13D seeking a sale and an 18.1% holder with a Section 203 waiver
   are control-shaped facts that move the quote (+40.6% since JANA's event date) and never reach the row. **A
   `13D` line in the deal note (form SCHEDULE 13D / 13D/A in the submissions index, with the Item 4 text) is a
   measured addition for the next session.**
5. **The SIGN CHANGE label measured operating cash**, not owner earnings; `level_note` and `level_note_oe` disagreed for
   that reason, and the row carries both without saying which series each reads.
