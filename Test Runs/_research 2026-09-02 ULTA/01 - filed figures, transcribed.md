  retailer in the U.S.**"* Plus **Space NK** (UK/Ireland subsidiary), a **joint venture in
  Mexico**, and a **franchise in the Middle East**.
- *"In 2025, this market represented **approximately $126 billion in sales** according to
  Euromonitor International and IBIS World Inc."* — *"management estimates there are
  **approximately 140 million beauty enthusiasts in the U.S.**"*
- *"In the fiscal year ended January 31, 2026 ("fiscal 2025"), **73% of Ulta U.S.'s loyalty
  members transacted solely within stores**."* · *"In fiscal 2025, **19% of loyalty members
  shopped both in Ulta U.S.** [stores and digital]"*
- **THE LOYALTY NUMBER, verbatim:** *"With **more than 46 million members as of the end of
  fiscal 2025 and approximately 95% of total sales coming from members in fiscal 2025**, Ulta
  U.S. …"* and *"Its data demonstrates that **loyalty members spend more per visit as compared
  to non-members**."*
- *"Our typical store is **approximately 10,000 square feet, including approximately 950
  square feet dedicated to our full-service salon**."* Smaller prototype **5,000–7,500 sq ft**.
  A typical **Space NK store is approximately 2,000 square feet**.
- **UB Marketplace**: *"more than 200 brands and 5,000 stock keeping units (SKUs)"*, brands
  fulfil directly, *"the Company earns a commission on these sales."*
- **THE TARGET PARTNERSHIP, verbatim and decisive:** *"the Company has partnered with Target
  Corporation ("Target") to create **Ulta Beauty at Target**, a "shop-in-shop" concept that
  offers a curated assortment of **more than 60 established and emerging prestige brands**…"*
  *"As of January 31, 2026, **Ulta Beauty at Target was available in over 600 Target locations
  and on target.com**."* — *"**On August 14, 2025, Ulta Beauty and Target announced a mutual
  agreement to not renew the Ulta Beauty at Target shop-in-shop partnership when the current

- **Competition (Item 1), verbatim:** *"The Company's major competitors for prestige and mass

  stores, mass merchandisers, and the online capabilities of national retailers and brands, as

- Strategy: *"In 2025, management unveiled the **Ulta Beauty Unleashed plan**"* — three
  priorities: Drive Core Business Growth; Scale New, Accretive Businesses; Align Its

## ITEM 1A — RISK FACTORS (the sentence that matters)
> *"The markets for beauty and wellness products and salon services are **highly competitive
> with few barriers to entry**. With regard to beauty and wellness products, we compete
> against a diverse group of retailers, both small and large, including mass merchandisers,
> specialty retailers, online capabilities of national retailers, pure-play e-commerce
> companies, online marketplaces (including those operated by social media platforms), and
> drug stores. … With regard to salon services, we compete primarily against high-end and
> discount salon chains as well as locally owned salons. We believe the principal bases upon
> which we compete are **the breadth of merchandise, our value proposition, the quality of our
> guests' shopping experience … and the convenience of our stores as one-stop destinations**…
> **Many of our competitors are, and many of our potential competitors may be, larger and have
> greater financial, marketing, and other resources** and therefore may be able to adapt to
> changes in guest requirements more quickly, devote greater resources to the marketing and
> sale of their products, generate greater brand recognition, or **adopt more aggressive
> pricing policies than we can. As a result, we may lose market share**…"*

**Note the difference from AEO/SHOE:** the competitive basis list here does **NOT end in
price** — it ends in *"convenience … as one-stop destinations."* Price appears only as a
competitor's weapon, not as Ulta's own basis of competition.
