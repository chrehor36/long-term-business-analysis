1. **The numerator is consolidated, the owner is not.** Consolidated operating cash includes 100% of every clinic
   partnership; the partners own the rest (the company's interests *"generally range from 65% to 75% (with an overall
   range of 30%-99%)"*). The partners' cash is the line *"Distributions to non-controlling interest, permanent and
   temporary equity"*, **$19,269K in 2025, $14.7-19.3M a year 2018-2025**, and net income attributable to them was

   same subtraction is consistent across the restatement.)

3. **Acquisitions are growth, not (c).** Mature-clinic revenue was flat in 2025 (+0.1%) and rose 3.1% in the first half
