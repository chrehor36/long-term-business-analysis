### WHY Q2 CLOSED, ON TESLA'S OWN RECORD
- **The company names its substitutes.** FY2025 10-K Item 1: Model 3 and Model Y *"compete with small to medium-sized sedans and compact SUVs, all of which are
  extremely competitive markets"*; Item 1A: *"Many of our competitors have significantly more or better-established resources than we do ... Increased competition could
  result in our lower vehicle unit sales, price reductions, revenue shortfalls, loss of customers and loss of market share."*
- **Three 10-Ks in a row explain lower revenue per car by price.** FY2023 *"overall price reductions year over year"*; FY2024 *"overall price reductions and attractive
  financing options"*; FY2025 *"higher customer incentives such as attractive financing options"*. Revenue per car delivered about $51.2k (FY2022) to $40.1k (FY2025);
  gross margin on cars 26.2% to 14.5%; deliveries 1.81m, ~1.79m, ~1.64m. **The cuts were made with idle plant**: the Q4 2023 Update listed about 2.35 million of
  installed capacity against 76% and 70% use in FY2024-25 [E2-58, E2-44].
- **The row-leading margin was two years of wave.** Consolidated operating margin 12.1 / 16.8 / 9.2 / 7.2 / 4.6% (FY2021-25), pooled 9.54%, the best in the TM run's
  eight-maker row; by FY2025 below Toyota (6.11%) and Hyundai (5.05%), and 2.6% in H1 2026. Pre-tax return on capital employed 57.5% (FY2022) to 9.4% (FY2025), 5.1%
  less credits, while capital employed rose $20.4bn [E3-46, E4-32].
- **46% of FY2025 operating income was regulatory credits** ($1,993M of $4,355M), a regime's product [E2-59]: *"In 2025, governmental and regulatory actions, such as
  OBBBA, have restricted certain regulatory credit programs tied to our products"*; H1 2026 credits -49%.
- **The company is buying its replacement advantage** [E4-04, E5-23]: *"The next phase of production growth will be initiated by advances in autonomy and the introduction
  of new products"*; Model S and X lines decommissioned for Optimus; capex $8.3bn in H1 2026.
- **Key-person dependence, as a moat defect [E4-23]**: *"we are highly dependent on the services of Elon Musk"* (10-K), and the 2025 proxy's *"endorsement of his singular
  role in leading Tesla into the future."*
- **The full-strength case, recorded first**: the row's best five-year margin; Superchargers adopted by rivals under NACS (8,704 stations, +18%); FSD subscriptions
  0.95m to 1.48m in a year; energy gross margin 29.8% against Fluence's 13.1% (the FLNC run's row); Q2 2026 deliveries +25%. The energy segment is at most a narrow,
  provisional position in 22% of gross profit and cannot carry a business that is 78% cars.
