## GATE 1 — CIRCLE OF COMPETENCE
Fujifilm Holdings operates three main segments: **Imaging** (consumer
photography, the historical core business — notably the Instax instant-
camera franchise, a genuine recent growth product), **Healthcare** (medical
imaging/diagnostics equipment, plus a large and growing **biopharmaceutical
contract development and manufacturing (CDMO) business, Fujifilm Diosynth
Biotechnologies**), and **Electronics/Business Innovation** (office
equipment, formerly the Fuji Xerox joint venture, materials and
semiconductor-related chemicals). This is a **genuinely well-known
corporate transformation story**: Fujifilm successfully pivoted away from
traditional photographic film (which collapsed with digital photography,
famously bankrupting its direct competitor Kodak) into healthcare,
materials, and electronics using adjacent chemistry/materials expertise
(film-emulsion chemistry has real technical overlap with cosmetics
ingredients and pharmaceutical manufacturing processes). **VERDICT: PASS**,
with the three-segment structure genuinely understandable at a reasonable
level, though the CDMO/biopharma manufacturing sub-business specifically
would require deeper technical understanding than this pass provides —
noted as a real limit on conviction, not glossed over.
