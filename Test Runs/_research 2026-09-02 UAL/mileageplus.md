**Two things fall out of this that the analyst should carry forward.**

(i) **The MPH standalone deferred-revenue liability is larger than UAL's consolidated
one**, at $6,151-6,161m versus $5,276m, a gap of roughly $875-885m. The standalone entity is
therefore not a simple carve-out of the consolidated line; intercompany positions with
United that eliminate on consolidation are present in the MPH column. Neither the 15 June

filed record.

books are not reconciled to each other.

Also worth carrying: MPH's disclosed "Revenue, net of redemptions" of $1,938m in 2019 sits
close to the $2.0bn UAL recognised in consolidated Other operating revenue that year from
partner agreements, but the two are **not the same measure** and the filings do not equate
them. MPH's line is net of the estimated future redemption cost of all miles sold; UAL's is
the non-deferred marketing/advertising/benefits slice only.

### 1l. The DEBT SERVICE COVERAGE RATIO
Source: **F** 8-K body (as closed) and **A-99.1** slide 32 / **A-99.2** (as proposed).
The two are identical in the schedule.

> "'Peak Debt Service Coverage Ratio' means the requirement that the debt service coverage
> ratio shall not be less than (A) so long as the DSCR Step-up Period is not in effect,
> (i) for the Determination Dates in September 2020, December 2020 and March 2021,
> **0.75 to 1.0**; (ii) for the Determination Dates in June 2021, September 2021 and
> December 2021, **1.0 to 1.0**; (iii) for the Determination Dates in March 2022 and June
> 2022, **1.5 to 1.0**; and (iv) for any Determination Date thereafter, **2.0 to 1.0** and
> (B) for any Determination Date on which a DSCR Step-up Period is in effect, 2.0 to 1.0."
> Source: **F**, accession 0001104659-20-080673, 8-K body, 2020-07-02

Definition of the ratio (**A-99.1** slide 32): "the ratio of (i) all collections received
in the Collection Account during a particular quarterly reporting period ('Collections') to
(ii) maximum quarterly debt service (which for the purpose of the calculation includes
scheduled quarterly amortization, even during the interest-only period)". MPH may cure a
shortfall by depositing funds into the Collection Account, subject to limitations.

**Consequence of a miss:** an Early Amortization Event, on which "50% of Collections (in
excess of required debt service) will be applied to repay a portion of the outstanding
principal" (**A-99.1** slide 32). Other Early Amortization triggers: an Early Amortization
Event under pari passu debt; an Event of Default; insufficient Reserve Account balance.

### 1m. Other covenants
From **F** (8-K body, 2020-07-02) and **A-99.1** slides 32 and 33:
- **UAL must maintain minimum liquidity of at least $2.0 billion** at the end of any
  business day. This is the only financial maintenance covenant at the parent.
- Reserve Account funded upfront equal to **3 months of interest service** ("DSRA Required
  Balance").
- Payment waterfall out of the Collection Account: **first** agents' fees and expenses;
  **second** interest and quarterly amortisation; **third** top up the Reserve Account;
  **fourth** if an Early Amortization Event exists, 50% of trapped amounts to principal;
  **fifth** all remaining amounts released to the Co-Borrowers. Each month, absent an Early
  Amortization Event, one third of the quarter's interest and fees is trapped in the
  Collection Account.
- Negative covenants limit the ability to: make restricted payments; incur debt; transact
  with affiliates; incur liens on the Collateral; merge/consolidate/sell assets; engage in
  certain business activities; convey the Collateral; **sell pre-paid miles in excess of
  $500.0 million in the aggregate**; "**exit from, terminate or substantially reduce the
  MileagePlus business or modify the terms of the MileagePlus program**" where a material
  adverse effect may be expected; and terminate or amend the IP Licenses.
- Permitted junior secured debt up to $750m subject to rating-agency confirmation; beyond
  $750m subject to total secured debt / Collections greater than 1.6x (**A-99.1** slide 33).
- **F**: "The Indenture and the Credit Agreement restrict United's ability to terminate or
  modify the intercompany agreements governing the relationship between United and the
  MileagePlus program, including the agreement governing the rate that United must pay MPH
  for the purchase of miles and United's obligation to make certain seat inventory
  available to MPH for redemption."

### 1n. The intercompany licence and use agreements
Structure (**A-99.1** slide 29; **A-99.2**; **F**): United and MPH contributed their
MileagePlus intellectual property to **MIPA / "IPCO"**, a newly formed bankruptcy-remote
Cayman SPV that is an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of MPH. The IP includes MileagePlus
customer data, domain names (mileageplus.com), copyrights, patents, trademarks
("MileagePlus", "Premier", "Premier Executive") and the MileagePlus software including the
MileagePlus X mobile app.

**MPH License** (IPCO to MPH), **A-99.1** slide 34: exclusive, royalty-bearing, worldwide,
perpetual until a Termination Event.
> "**License Fee**: Monthly fee equal to **all revenues of MPH during the previous month**.
> The License Fee will be paid in daily installments through daily sweeps from the MPH
> Revenue Account to the Collection Account."

**United Sublicense** (MPH to United), **A-99.1** slides 36-37: non-exclusive,
royalty-bearing, worldwide, perpetual until a Termination Event.
> "**Sublicense Fee**: Amounts due and payable by United to MPH every month under the
> Intercompany Agreements."

**The liquidated-damages clause**, the single most quantitative statement in the filings
about what the program is worth to United, **A-99.1** slide 37:
> "If the United Sublicense is terminated due to an Automatic Termination Event or a
> Termination Event due to non-payment of the Sublicense Fee or a payment event of default
> under the Facility, a License Termination Payment will be automatically due and payable.
> The amount of liquidated damages will be equal to (x) the present value of all future
> payments of the Sublicense Fee, **assuming a fixed annual Sublicense Fee of $1.4 billion**
> from the date of termination through the date that is **the 30th anniversary** of the date
> of the United Sublicense, discounted to the termination date at a rate of **10% per
> annum** minus (y) the recovery value of the MileagePlus IP."

**F** (the closing 8-K) characterises the same clause without the numbers: termination of
the IP License "would trigger a liquidated damages payment in an amount that is **several
multiples of the principal amount** of the Notes and the Term Loan Facility."

**Non-compete**, both licences: neither MPH nor United (nor their subsidiaries) may
establish, create or operate any other loyalty program unless substantially all of that
program's revenues, IP, member data, third-party contracts and intercompany agreements are
pledged as first-lien collateral. Carve-out for a loyalty program acquired with another
commercial airline carrier, subject to conditions; **A-99.2** adds that if such a Permitted
Acquisition Loyalty Program's cash revenues exceed a threshold, United must announce the
merger of that program into MileagePlus and use commercially reasonable efforts to
