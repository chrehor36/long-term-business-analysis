**Honesty — binary, permanent, filings-based [E5-16].** Each matter dated to when it became PUBLIC:
- **The 2015 restatement (public 2015-02-02 to 2015-06-25). The weightiest matter in the file, read in full.** By a press release of 2015-02-02 (cited in the 8-K of 2015-05-07) the company disclosed that controls over revenue recognition for *"Software License Maintenance and Service Agreements"* were not effective; it filed its December 2014 10-Q late (NT 10-Q 2015-02-09; Nasdaq non-compliance letter 2015-02-12, 8-K Item 3.01) and again in May 2015 (NT 10-Q 2015-05-12, a second Item 3.01). **On 2015-03-23 it told owners: *"While the Company does not expect any material changes to its previously reported financial results, there can be no assurance as to that outcome."*** Six weeks later, 2015-05-06 (8-K Item 4.02): the fiscal 2012-2014 statements *"should no longer be relied upon due to the identification of material errors."* The 10-K/A's Note 15: *"revenue being recognized on these contracts before all licenses, for which no VSOE exists, had been delivered"*; *"net income for the fiscal years ended June 30, 2014, 2013 and 2012 was overstated by $14,421 , $9,035 , and $2,944 respectively"* (thousands; 7.7% of restated fiscal 2014 net income), with a further *"$44,373"* thousand taken out of retained earnings for years before 2012; fiscal 2014 licence revenue fell from *"53,009"* to *"2,184"*.
