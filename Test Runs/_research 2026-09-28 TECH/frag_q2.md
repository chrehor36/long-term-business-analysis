## Q2 — IS IT A FRANCHISE? **[E3-03]**
*The analyst's incentives, stated before the reading (operator rule 9, [E4-27], [E4-26]): the quote is pinned to a cash deal, so nothing this file concludes can be bought at an owner-earnings price, which invites a hurried close; and Bio-Techne's reagent margins are famous, which invites a reader to wave the franchise through on reputation. The register also holds far more Q2 closes than gate-clearers. None of these belongs in the question. Because the reading below passes the gate, **the case for OUT is written first and at full strength** [E4-51], and the pass is written as narrow, with its weakest link named.*

### THE STRONGEST EVIDENCE AGAINST THIS VERDICT (the case for OUT), stated as its holders would state it [E4-51, E4-26, E3-41]
1. **The filer never says its customers lack a close substitute; it says the opposite.** *"Although our segments both generally operate in highly competitive markets"*; key factors *"typically also include price"*; *"We have seen increased competition in a number of our markets as a result of the entry of new companies into certain markets, the entry of competitors based in low-cost manufacturing locations, and increasing consolidation in particular markets"* (FY2026). The FY2020 10-K names the reagent rivals: *"a number of large companies supply the worldwide market for protein-related and chemically-based research and diagnostic reagents, including BD Biosciences, Merck KGaA/EMD Chemicals, Inc., PeproTech, Inc., Abcam plc., and Thermo Fisher Scientific, Inc, as well as a number of smaller, niche competitors. Market success is primarily dependent upon product innovation and quality, selection of products, price and reputation."* The risk factors since FY2021 list the need to *"differentiate our offerings from our competitors’ offerings and avoid our products from becoming commodities"*. In the 1990s the company credited its own growth to *"their recognized quality and competitive pricing"* (FY1995-FY2000): it priced to meet rivals, not above them.
2. **No filed year credits price as a driver of growth except two, and those were small.** Searched in every 10-K FY1995-FY2026 for a price attribution in the results discussion (`pgrep.py` → `price_out.txt`): *"Approximately $1.3 million of the increase in biotechnology net sales for fiscal 2006 was the result of price increases"* and *"Approximately $1.2 million of the increase in biotechnology net sales for fiscal 2007 was the result of price increases"*, about 1% of the division's sales (FY2007: *"Biotechnology net sales in fiscal 2007 increased $12.2 million (9.1%)"*); **no other instance found**, including FY2022-FY2023, when inflation let many sellers raise prices. The protein segment's organic growth by year: FY2020 3%, FY2021 24%, FY2022 19%, FY2023 4%, FY2024 −2%, FY2025 5%, FY2026 −1%, each explained by demand (*"strong BioPharma demand"*, *"broad based headwinds"*), none by price.
3. **In the one year the filings put [E2-44]'s test (flat demand), the price went the wrong way.** FY2026, Protein Sciences: *"Organic revenue for the segment decreased 1% for the fiscal year ... Segment revenue was impacted by unfavorable product mix and pricing pressures."*; the segment's gross margin fell *"primarily attributable to unfavorable product mix and pricing pressure within the segment"*; the company's adjusted earnings *"primarily impacted by unfavorable product mix and pricing pressures"*. In the same market year Danaher's Life Sciences segment, with core sales down 1.5% (*"Lower funding levels at emerging biotechnology customers and in the academic and government end-markets reduced demand"*), still filed *"Price increases in the segment contributed 0.5% to the change in sales"* (Danaher FY2025 10-K). The risk factor has said since FY2011 that *"The entry into the market of manufacturers in China and other low-cost manufacturing locations is also creating increased pricing pressures"*; FY2026 is the first MD&A in which the pressure is named as a cause.
4. **The direction is down.** Protein Sciences segment margin 46.7% (FY2021), 45.4%, 44.2%, 42.7%, 42.6%, **41.1% (FY2026)**: five successive declines. The segment's revenue was $832.3M in FY2022 and $874.6M in FY2026 (with 2 points of currency in the last year). The consolidated operating margin, 51-58% every year FY2004-FY2013, has been 8-27% since FY2017.
5. **More than a quarter of the company is plainly not a franchise.** Diagnostics and Spatial Biology earned segment margins of **5.9%, 8.1%, 16.9%, 17.8%, 14.7%, 7.5%, 6.2% and 11.2% (FY2019-FY2026)** before any amortization, on businesses bought for at least $905M (ACD about $258M, Exosome $251.6M, Asuragen and Eminence $225.4M, Lunaphore $169.7M, before smaller ones). Spatial biology is a field in which the listed rivals lose money every year (10x Genomics, Akoya, row below) and in which, the filer says, *"our future success depends, to a large extent, on our ability to keep pace with changing technologies and market needs"*: [E4-04]'s continuously rebuilt class.

### THE READING
- **Needed or desired [x]** · **no close substitute [x], held NARROW** · **not price-regulated [x]**
  - (1) Needed or desired: $1,215.0M of revenue from laboratories, drug makers and diagnostic manufacturers, 81% consumables reordered as they are used up; passes.
  - (2) No close substitute: **the filer does not claim it, and the customers' conduct, as far as filings can show conduct, says they act as though they had none for twenty years.** The evidence is the margin record, which is what [E3-43] says the three conditions produce: *"The existence of all three conditions will be demonstrated by a company’s ability to regularly price its product or service aggressively and thereby to earn high rates of return on capital."* From the filed five-year tables (`hist.py` → `hist_out.txt`), the reagent company earned an operating margin of **37.9% (FY2000), 40.0%, 26.8% (FY2002, after a $17.5M Amgen litigation settlement), 46.7%, 51.0%, 54.7%, 53.6%, 55.6%, 56.1%, 57.1%, 58.1% (FY2010), 56.2%, 52.8% and 51.0% (FY2013)**: cumulatively **$1,540.0M of operating income on $2,966.6M of revenue FY2000-FY2013, 51.9%**, against the rivals named in item 1, with the Biotechnology Division's gross margin at *"79.2%"* in FY2002 and consolidated gross margin 77.4-79.6% FY2004-FY2011. After the purchases the reagent core stayed where it was: the Biotechnology segment earned **52.5% (FY2015), 53.1%, 48.1% and 47.2% (FY2018)**, and Protein Sciences (the reagents plus the ProteinSimple instruments) **41.1-46.7% every year FY2019-FY2026**. A product that customers could easily replace does not carry a 50% operating margin for a decade against Thermo Fisher, Merck KGaA and BD. The mechanism is in the filer's words: *"We believe we are the world leader in providing high quality proteins"*, the aim *"to serve as the standard for biological content in the research market"* (FY2019-FY2026), and *"R&D Systems believes that it is the dominant worldwide supplier of cytokine related products in the research marketplace"* (FY1995). Clause (2) is **held NARROW**, not wide, because of case items 1 to 3: rivals are named and funded, the company competes partly on price, and in FY2026 it lost price.
  - (3) Price regulation: none on the reagents; the diagnostics products are regulated for safety, not price (*"Medical devices approved or cleared by the FDA"*), with reimbursement a factor only for the tests now largely sold (Exosome). Passes.

- **[E3-43]'s other class, "a business":** the Diagnostics and Spatial Biology segment is that class on its record (case item 5); the old Techne hematology and clinical-controls line, now inside it, earned 25.7-30.1% (FY2015-FY2018) in a market it called *"intense"* with *"We compete based primarily on product performance, quality, and price."* (FY2016). It is 27.7% of revenue and 9.5% of FY2026 segment operating income. **The franchise is the reagent catalog; the company priced here is that franchise plus a business attached to it.**
- **[E2-44], the two characteristics:** (1) *"an ability to increase prices rather easily (even when product demand is flat and capacity is not fully utilized)"*: **not shown, and contradicted once**: in FY2026, with demand flat, pricing pressure is filed (case item 3). (2) *"an ability to accommodate large dollar volume increases in business ... with only minor additional investment of capital"*: **shown for the reagent business** (tangible operating capital $142.6M at FY2010 against $156.3M of operating income; capital additions $3.6-6.6M a year FY2009-FY2012), **not shown for the company**, which bought its growth after FY2013. One of two, held against the class.
- **[E2-45], the attacker's test.** The best-equipped attacker in the filer's own competitor list, Merck KGaA (owner of MilliporeSigma; *"Merck KGaA/EMD Chemicals, Inc."* is in the FY2013 10-K's list), indicated *"$92 to $95 per share"* in 2023 and has now agreed to pay $73.00 in cash, *"a 36% premium to Bio-Techne’s one-month volume weighted average trading price"*, rather than compete. Recorded as consistent with the test, with the ESI caution: an acquirer's price is a fact about its judgment and its synergies (*"annual cost synergies of approximately EUR 140 million"*), not proof of the incumbent's position. The attack that the filings do show is from below: low-cost makers in Asia (case item 3).
- **[E2-58], the commodity class:** the reagent catalog is not in it on the record (25 years of 40-58% margins are not *"persistent over-capacity without administered prices"*), but the filer's own risk factor names the drift toward it (*"avoid our products from becoming commodities"*), and the FY2026 pricing pressure is what that drift looks like when it begins. Carried to Q6.
- **[E4-04], applied as the competence limit it is.** The reagent catalog's basis is **maintained, not replaced**: the FY1999 10-K describes the same products for the same buyers (*"purified cytokines (proteins), antibodies and assay kits which are sold to biomedical researchers and clinical research laboratories"*) that the FY2026 10-K does, and the catalog accumulates (*"In fiscal 2026, we introduced over 1,900 new products"*; *"hundreds of thousands of diverse products"*): [E5-23]'s defended moat, not [E4-04]'s rebuilt one. Its durability can be judged from twenty-five years of filings, so the perimeter close does not apply to it. The spatial-biology and instrument lines (about $110.6M of instruments and part of the $336.4M segment) are the rebuilt class and are recorded as such; they do not carry the company's economics and are not what the verdict rests on. [E4-36]: the record fits *"Extreme maximization or minimization of one or two variables"* (quality of a small, cheap-to-make consumable whose price is trivial against the experiment it serves) more than wave-riding.
- **[E4-23], key person: not a defect.** The reagent margins held across three chief executives: Thomas Oland (*"Chairman of the Board, President, Treasurer and Chief Executive Officer of the Company since December 1985"*), Charles Kummeth (*"President and Chief Executive Officer of the Company since April 1, 2013"*) and Kim Kelderman (*"promoted to President and Chief Executive Officer of the Company on February 1, 2024"*). The business does not need a superstar; [E5-18]'s capacity to stand a mediocre manager is shown by the core margin surviving a decade of dilutive purchases around it.
- **[E4-32], direction, and [E4-55], units: narrowing.** No unit series is filed. The segment margin has fallen five years running (case item 4); the price, flat or down, is carrying no growth; the protein segment's revenue was flat in dollars from FY2022 to FY2026. [E3-30]'s question (cycle or slip) is open: the filer and Danaher both describe a funding-driven demand slump in the same year, and only Bio-Techne describes lost price. **Recorded as the first Q6 metric.**
- **Primary moat metric, filing-sourced, and its trend:** the reagent segment's operating margin before amortization and stock compensation: 52.5% (Biotechnology, FY2015) to 47.2% (FY2018); Protein Sciences 46.7% (FY2021) to 41.1% (FY2026). Trend: down about a point a year for five years, still above every filed peer segment below.
