### Perimeter and recast warnings (read before using any row)

1. **Two recasts of the segment series.** K25 recast FY2024 and FY2023: *"Effective January 1, 2025, we updated our segment reporting to reflect our international business based on product category alignment, as opposed to the entirety of international results historically being reported in the Reynolds Cooking & Baking segment. All prior period segment disclosures have been recast to reflect this reassignment."* (K25, Note on segments). So FY2023 and FY2024 have two sets of figures: as first filed (K23, K24) and as recast (K25). Both are shown.
2. **Hefty Waste & Storage (HWS) is not "trash bags".** It holds Hefty-branded trash bags, Hefty food storage bags, and some store-brand bags. The Presto Products segment ALSO sells store-brand trash bags. K25: *"Our Presto Products segment primarily sells store brand products in three main consumer categories: food storage bags, trash bags and plastic wrap."*
3. **2026 resegmentation.** From Q1 2026, HWS and Presto were dissolved into "Hefty Waste & Clean-Up" (all trash bags, branded plus store brand) and "Hefty Storage & Organization" (food bags). Q26: *"In January 2026, we reorganized the previous Hefty Waste & Storage and Presto Products segments by consolidating waste bags into the new Hefty Waste & Clean-Up segment and food bags and storage products into the new Hefty Storage & Organization segment in an effort to increase efficiencies, sharpen focus on innovation and establish a structure that can better facilitate entry into adjacent categories. Comparative segment disclosures have been recast to reflect this realignment. Prior periods will be similarly recast in our future reports for the remainder of 2026."* The 2026 Waste & Clean-Up figures are therefore NOT comparable with the HWS series; a full-year recast of FY2025 on the new basis has not yet been filed.
4. **Product-line view (waste vs storage).** K25 also gives net revenues by product line across HWS plus Presto combined: Waste products $936M (2025), $917M (2024), $917M (2023); Storage products $681M, $639M, $612M. Footnote: *"(1) Waste and storage products are comprised of our Hefty Waste & Storage and Presto Products segments."* (K25). This is the nearest filed figure to "all REYN trash-bag revenue" before 2026. Earlier 10-Ks combine waste and storage into one line ("Waste and storage products"), so no waste-only product line exists before K25.

## Item 5. Does REYN make store-brand (private-label) bags itself?

Yes, as stated in its own filings:

- K25 Item 1, HWS: *"Through our Hefty Waste & Storage segment, we produce both branded and store brand trash and food storage bags."*
