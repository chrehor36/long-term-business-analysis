## Segment scope by year (the Lysol perimeter changes; read before comparing rows)

- **FY2021 to FY2024: operating segment "Hygiene"** (one of three: Hygiene, Health, Nutrition). Segment profit is adjusted operating profit.
  - AR21 (segment note 2): "The Group's operating segments comprise of the Hygiene, Health and Nutrition business units".
  - AR21 scope: "Hygiene is the foundation of health and our purpose-led portfolio works to eliminate dirt, germs, pests and odours with marketleading products such as Lysol, Finish, Air Wick, Harpic and Mortein." (OCR/extraction artifact: "marketleading" as extracted.) Brands discussed in the AR21 Hygiene review: Lysol, Finish, Air Wick, Vanish, Harpic, Pest.
  - AR22 lists six Hygiene categories: "OUR CATEGORIES Surface & Disinfection Lavatory Care ... Fabric Additives ... Air Care ... Auto Dishwashing ... Pest Control" (extracted as scattered labels; ellipses mark page-layout breaks, not omitted words).
  - AR22 segment note footnote: "Segmental information for the year ended 31 December 2021 has been restated to reflect the Group's current operating segments, the composition of which changed with effect from 1 January 2022 when the Vitamins, Minerals and Supplements (VMS) business was moved from Nutrition to Health". This did not touch Hygiene (FY2021 Hygiene 5,911 in both AR21 and AR22).
  - AR23 Hygiene category profile: "Surface & Disinfection  Auto Dishwash  Air Care  Fabric Additives  Lavatory Care Other".
  - **Dettol is NOT in Hygiene in 2021 to 2024**; it sat in Health (AR23 Health review: "Dettol declined mid-single digits in the year").
  - No re-segmentation in 2021 was found in AR21: its note 2 presents 2021 and 2020 on the same Hygiene/Health/Nutrition basis. The document evidence places the re-segmentations at 1 Jan 2022 (VMS, Health/Nutrition only) and 1 Jan 2025 (below).
- **FY2025 onward: operating segments are geographic.** AR25 note 2: "On 1 January 2025, the Group's operating segments changed from Hygiene, Health and Nutrition to Core Emerging Markets, Core Europe, Core North America, Essential Home and Mead Johnson Nutrition."
  - Lysol now sits in the **"Germ Protection" category** (a revenue category of Core Reckitt, not an operating segment, so no adjusted operating profit is reported for it). AR25: "GERM PROTECTION Lysol, Dettol and Harpic protect against the spread of germs and drive widespread adoption of good hygiene habits, the foundation of healthy lifestyles." AR24 already described it: "Germ Protection brings together Lysol, Dettol and Harpic."
  - Finish and Vanish moved to "Household Care"; Air Wick, Calgon, Cillit Bang, Mortein, Easy-Off, Woolite went to "Essential Home" (divested 31 Dec 2025, see section 3).
  - AR25 goodwill note: "Prior to 1 January 2025, the Group's GCGUs/CGUs were Health, Hygiene and MJN ... The Hygiene goodwill has been split amongst Germ Protection and Household Care on the basis of relative values."
  - RNS26 (H1 2026) note 4: "The Group's operating segments comprise of Core Emerging Markets, Core Europe, Core North America, Mead Johnson Nutrition and Vestacy Transitional Services". RNS26 Q2 reconciliation footnote 2 on the 2025 comparative: "Updated for certain products that are being managed as part of household care rather than germ protection".
- Consequence: **the 2021 to 2024 "Hygiene" rows and the 2025 to H1 2026 "Germ Protection" rows are different perimeters.** FY2024 is available on both: Hygiene 6,140 (AR24) versus Germ Protection 3,086 (AR25 restated comparative).

## 3. Essential Home: sale and whether Lysol stays

- AR24 (FY2024): "We are working to exit a group of strong homecare brands, now called Essential Home, which we are on track to do by the end of 2025." And: "We plan to exit Essential Home by the end of 2025 while exploring all options for Mead Johnson Nutrition".
- AR24 Essential Home scope: "This portfolio of market-leading brands plays in growing and resilient categories such as Air Care, Laundry, Surface Care and Pest ... At the core of the portfolio is Air Wick".
- AR25 audit committee: "the Committee reviewed the accounting for the disposal of the Essential Home business following the announcement to divest in July 2025."
- AR25: "Essential Home brings together longestablished brands including Air Wick, Calgon, Cillit Bang, Mortein, Easy-Off and Woolite, with around 75 other brands across c.70 markets." (artifact: "longestablished" as extracted.)
- AR25: "Reckitt owned 100% of Essential Home for the entirety of the financial year 2025, with the divestment to Advent completing on 31 December 2025."
- AR25: "Under the majority ownership of global private equity firm, Advent, supported by Reckitt's retained 30% equity stake in Advent's acquisition vehicle, Essential Home will benefit from an agile operating model".
- AR25: "IFRS operating profit was impacted by the completion of the sale of Essential Home to Lavender Bidco B.V. for total consideration, net of disposal costs, of GBP2,172 million on 31 December 2025."
- AR25 note: "On 31 December 2025, the Group completed the sale of Essential Home and retained a 30% shareholding in Lavender Dutch TopCo B.V."
- RNS26: "Following the announcement on 31 December 2025 confirming the completion of the divestment of the Essential Home business ... the Group returned GBP1,578 million to shareholders on 20 February 2026 by way of special dividend of 235 pence per ordinary share."
- **Lysol stays in the retained perimeter**: AR25 places Lysol in Core Reckitt's Germ Protection ("Lysol, Dettol and Harpic protect against the spread of germs"), it is not in the Essential Home brand list, and RNS26 reports Lysol inside Core Reckitt/Germ Protection for H1 2026.
