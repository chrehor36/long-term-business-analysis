### THE PRIOR THE BRIEF ASKED TO BE ARGUED AGAINST, AND HOW IT FARED
The brief expected the hard question to be whether a publisher can hold a franchise against the platform owners who control distribution. **Half right.** The platform
record is real (Facebook hours 428M, 184M, 58M in 2021-23; the founder's 2023 memo: *"slow to accept that the big platforms wouldn't provide the distribution or
financial support"*; AI summaries that *"provide answers directly on the search results page"*). **But the question was not hard, and the platforms were not the
decisive substitute**: the paying customers were. Branded-content buyers keep 41-50% of their spend a year, Amazon (28% of revenue) sets the commission, and People
Inc., the competitor BuzzFeed's own 10-K names, grew its Digital revenue 11.4% a year at a 27.7% segment margin through the same search decline while BuzzFeed's
continuing revenue fell 17.2% a year.
