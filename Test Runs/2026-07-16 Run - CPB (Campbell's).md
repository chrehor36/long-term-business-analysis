# Company Run — The Campbell's Company (CPB) — 2026-07-16
Framework v3.0. SP400-600 screen, sleeper set #4. Source: FY2025 10-K
(fiscal year ended Aug 3, 2025, CIK 16732, filed 2025-09-18).

## STEP 0 — RATE REFRESH
US 30-yr **5.10%**. Mkt cap $6,530M → small/mid hurdle **6.10%**.

## GATE 1 — CIRCLE OF COMPETENCE
Branded food and beverage manufacturer — heritage to 1869 (soup,
Pepperidge Farm/Goldfish, Prego, V8, and Rao's via the March 2024 Sovos
acquisition, $2.899B). Classic consumer-staples economics.
**VERDICT: PASS.**

## GATE 2 — MOAT
From CPB's own 10-K: **"We operate in a highly competitive industry...
includes producers of private label products... Private label products
are generally sold at lower prices than branded products... Our principal
areas of competition are brand recognition, taste..."** — real brand
franchise (the franchise test's original home turf), honestly disclosed
against the private-label price gap that has pressured all branded food.
NI down 40% from FY2021 ($1,002M → $602M) — input-cost inflation and
category softness are visible in the numbers, partially offset by Rao's
(a genuinely growing premium brand).
**Class: NARROW** (real brands, real private-label pressure; the
Coca-Cola-clause question of a "generational moat" is NOT claimed here),
**Direction: NARROWING slowly.** **VERDICT: PASS.**
