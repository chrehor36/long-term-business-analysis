## What the row shows
1. **WEYS is the best operator in the set**: highest operating margin (10.6% vs 7.7% /
   2.6% / 0.2%) and the only net-cash balance sheet. In the FY2025-26 tariff shock the
   whole set's margins compressed; Weyco's least.
2. **And it is still shrinking.** The subject's six-year sales trend is −9% nominal
   (roughly −25% real against CPI) with a 10% price increase inside the last year of it.
   The direct dress comp (J&M) is flat at ~2.6% margins. The category is not growing for
   anyone; the profit pool differences are execution, not position.
3. **The attack comes from below** (retailer private-label programs at the opening price
   point, named in the subject's own filing against Nunn Bush) **and from scale/outdoor
   adjacency** (RCKY's Muck/XTRATUF against BOGS: BOGS FY2025 sales −11% to $32.0M while
   RCKY's boot portfolio grew to $482M with XTRATUF called out as its growth brand in
   its releases).
4. **Row limit [E3-61]:** the row shows position, not conduct; identical structures can
   produce opposite outcomes.
