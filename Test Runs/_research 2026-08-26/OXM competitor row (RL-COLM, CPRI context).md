## What the row gives and takes
- **Gives OXM:** the second-best gross margin in the set (60.7%) — the DTC-heavy model
  and the brands' price points are real; still solidly profitable ex-impairment at the
  segment level (TB + Lilly ≈ $147M of segment EBITDA).
- **Takes away:** the premium attacker with scale grew **+14.6% to a record at a 69.9%
  gross margin and 14.5% operating margin through the identical tariff year** in which
  OXM shrank for the third year running and earned ~2% adjusted operating margin. The
  premium-lifestyle wallet did not shrink; it went elsewhere. COLM shows what the
  mid-market melt looks like when it runs five years.
- Honest caveat, both directions: RL's aesthetic is not Tommy Bahama's; no same-aesthetic
  competitor of TB exists at scale — which is TB's narrow-moat claim AND its key-person
  analogue (the moat is one brand's niche, in a fashion category).
