### The row's own limits, stated
- **Fiscal-year offsets are real**: RPM reports to May-2026, FUL to Nov-2025, the rest to
  Dec-2025. RPM's year therefore contains five months the others do not. On cyclical measures the
  row is not read across naively.
- Gross margins are **not** comparable — SHW's COGS carries depreciation, distribution and R&D;
  PPG's caption is expressly "exclusive of depreciation and amortization"; CSL states R&D
  separately while FUL puts it in SG&A. **Only the operating-margin line is like-for-like**,
  which is why it, and not gross margin, is the row's metric.
- **SHW's tangible-capital lead narrows on a lease-adjusted basis.** SHW carries $1,995.2M of
  operating-lease right-of-use assets against roughly 4,850 stores; PPG $604M; AXTA $112M; RPM
  $396.9M. Adding ROU assets to the tangible denominator: SHW 42.9%, PPG 29.8%, AXTA 26.6%,
  **RPM 24.2%**. RPM's relative position is unchanged.
- **AXTA is under a signed merger agreement with AkzoNobel**, which bars it from repurchasing
  shares; and its 30.6% effective rate is a break in series (Bermuda's 15% corporate income tax
  from 2025). Its FY2025 is not a clean comparison year.
- **[E3-61] governs:** the row shows position, not conduct.

### Price versus volume across the row — what the peers publish and RPM does not

- **RPM, FY2026 10-K, footnote to the net-sales table, verbatim:** *"Organic growth (decline)
  **includes the impact of price and volume**."* No split is given anywhere — not consolidated,
  not by segment, not in any earnings release. RPM's narrative is qualitative only: the Consumer
  segment "experienced organic sales declines in fiscal 2026 due to softness in DIY and European
  markets, product rationalization, and weak demand in our Color Group, **partially offset by
  improved pricing to recover cost inflation**."
- **PPG, verbatim, consolidated:** *"Net sales increased $30 million due to the following: ●
  Higher selling prices (+1%) ● Higher sales volumes (+1%) ● Favorable foreign currency
  translation (+1%) … Partially offset by: ● Divestitures (-3%)."* And for the segment that maps
  onto RPM's Consumer business — **Global Architectural Coatings:** *"● Divestitures (-3%) ●
  **Lower sales volumes (-2%)** Partially offset by: ● **Higher selling prices (+2%)** ●
  Favorable foreign currency translation (+1%)."*
- **H.B. Fuller, verbatim:** *"The flat organic revenue was driven by a **0.8 percent increase in
  product pricing offset by a 0.8 percent decrease in sales volume**."*
- **Sherwin-Williams, verbatim, Paint Stores Group:** *"Net sales in the Paint Stores Group
  increased 3.2% primarily due to **selling price increases, which impacted Net sales by a
  mid-single digit percentage, partially offset by a low-single digit decrease in sales
  volume**."* No split for Consumer Brands or Performance Coatings.
- **Axalta, verbatim, consolidated:** *"The decreased net sales were driven by **lower volumes of
  4.6%**."* Segment price is fused with mix.

**The industry signature is uniform and it is the [E4-55] signature: price up, volume down.**
