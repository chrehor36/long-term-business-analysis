# NITORI FY2024 - work order result
Nitori Holdings Co., Ltd. (TSE 9843; ADR NCLTY). Compiled 2026-08-27. All figures JPY millions as published, no conversion. Primary sources only (Nitori's own English IR site, nitorihd.co.jp/en/ir/). Source register at the bottom; every figure below carries a source tag [S1]-[S4].

## 0. Correction to the work order premise
The work order says "fiscal year ended ~Feb 2024". That is out of date. Nitori changed its fiscal year-end from February 20 to March 31; the transition period ran **February 21, 2022 to March 31, 2023** (13.4 months, presented on the IR site as "Fiscal year ended March 2023"), and **FY2024 is the 12 months April 1, 2023 to March 31, 2024** [S3, income statement header; S4 library page headings]. FY2024 is therefore the first regular 12-month April-March year, which matters for owner-earnings averaging: the FY2023 comparative is a 13.4-month period and is **not** comparable without adjustment.

A second wrinkle: **Nitori's own standalone FY2024 English statements are J-GAAP.** The company adopted IFRS with a transition date of April 1, 2023, first applied in the FY2025 annual securities report [S2, p.1 and first-time-adoption note]. FY2024 IFRS figures therefore exist only as the audited comparative year inside the FY2025 report. Both bases are given below; the IFRS column is the one consistent with the FY2025/FY2026 statements already on disk.

Conservatism is spent once.

## 2. Share-based compensation, FY2024-FY2026 - DISCLOSED, and it is nil
It **is** disclosed in the English materials, in Note 33 "Share-based payment(s)" of both annual securities reports. No Japanese-only document is needed. Three plans exist (performance-linked director plan; employee share grant/delivery plan; J-ESOP trust), all equity-settled.

- **FY2024:** performance-linked plan and employee share grant plan - "No expenses ... were recognized" for the year; J-ESOP - "Expenses ... recognized in the consolidated statements of profit or loss amounted to ¥0 million in the previous fiscal year and were included in 'Selling, general and administrative expenses'" (previous fiscal year = FY2024). Grant activity: 36 points granted at weighted-average fair value ¥23,600 (≈ ¥0.8m total). [S2, Note 33]
- **FY2025:** no expenses recognized under any of the three plans. [S2, Note 33; confirmed in S1, Note 33]
- **FY2026:** performance-linked and employee share delivery plans - no expenses; J-ESOP - "such expenses amounted to ¥0 million," recorded in SG&A. Grant activity: 360 points (post-split basis) at weighted-average fair value ¥2,517.5 (≈ ¥0.9m total). [S1, Note 33]

Conclusion for the owner-earnings inputs: SBC rounds to ¥0m in every year FY2024-FY2026; no adjustment line is warranted. It was correct that nothing appears on the statement face - the amounts are sub-millions against ¥90bn-scale profit.

## 5. What was NOT obtained, and why
1. **A precise "official publication date" for S1-S3.** Nitori's English library pages carry no posting dates. Best available evidence recorded instead: PDF internal creation timestamps + server Last-Modified headers (they agree within two days in every case). The Japanese originals on EDINET carry legal filing dates (annual securities reports are filed late June each year); the English PDFs are translations posted several weeks later.
2. **J-ESOP shares within treasury at March 31, 2024.** The English Note 24 gives total treasury (1,431,638) but breaks out the trust holding only for FYE2025 and FYE2026. Immaterial to per-share work (≈0.3% of shares). The Japanese FY2025 securities report (EDINET) would carry it if ever needed.
3. Nothing else. Share-based compensation was fully resolved in English (section 2) - no UNRESEARCHED remains on it.
