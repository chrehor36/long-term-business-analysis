## Structure and tax — the K-1/1099 answer, verbatim
FY2025 10-K: "Although Star Group, L.P. is a partnership for state law purposes, it has
elected to be treated as a corporation, rather than a partnership, for federal income tax
purposes (commonly referred to as a 'check-the-box election')." Effective 2017-11-01,
approved by unitholder vote at a special meeting 2017-10-25. And: **"Unitholders will
receive a Form 1099-DIV and will not receive a Schedule K-1 as in tax years prior to
December 31, 2017."** SA (Star Acquisitions) and subsidiaries pay federal and state
corporate income taxes; cash taxes paid FY2023/24/25: $15.1M / $17.1M / $22.0M.

GP structure: general partner is Kestrel Heat, LLC; its board is appointed by sole member
Kestrel Energy Partners, LLC ("a private investment partnership owned by Yorktown Energy
Partners XII, L.P., Paul A. Vermylen Jr. and other investors"). "Unitholders do not
directly or indirectly participate in our management or operation or elect the directors
of the general partner." Ownership: public common 87.7%, officers/directors common 12.3%,
GP units 1.0% of combined (0.33M GP units, not publicly traded). Item 404 related-party:
none besides one lease agreement, FY2023-FY2025.

Distribution waterfall (Partnership Agreement, per 10-K): 1st — 100% to common to MQD
$0.0675/qtr; 2nd — arrearages; 3rd — GP units to MQD; then to the first target
distribution $0.1125/qtr; **"thereafter, 80% to the common units, pro rata, and 20% to
the general partner units, pro rata."** Management Incentive Compensation Plan (adopted
fiscal 2007 "following our recapitalization"): participants receive **50% of the GP's
Incentive Distributions** and 50% of Kestrel Heat's Gains Interest on any GP-unit sale.
Incentive distributions paid to the GP: FY2023 $1.2M, FY2024 $1.3M, FY2025 $1.5M —
"exclusive of amounts paid subject to the Management Incentive Plan" (so the total
incentive pool is roughly double). Common distributions FY2025: $24.5M.

## Weather and hedging
Approximately 93% of full-service customers on automatic delivery; ~25% on budget plans.
Weather hedge, Nov 1-Mar 31: FY2024 received $7.5M (warm); FY2023 benefit $12.5M (the
contract maximum; FY2023 was "the third warmest in the last 123 years in the New York
City metropolitan area", 16.3% warmer than normal); FY2025 and FY2026 PAID $5.0M (cold).
FY2026 contract: receive up to $15.0M, pay capped at $5.0M. FY2026 nine months were 2.9%
COLDER than normal — the only colder-than-normal stretch in the series read; FY2025 was
still 8.3% warmer than normal.
