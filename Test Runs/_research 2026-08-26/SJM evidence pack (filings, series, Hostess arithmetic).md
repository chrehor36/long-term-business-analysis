## 4. STAGE 0 (c) — THE BOOM/BUST WINDOW ARTIFACTS [E4-41]

### 4.1 Hostess Brands — acquisition accounting, amortisation, impairments

**The deal** (10-K FY2024 Note 2, acc. 0000091419-24-000054; repeated in FY2026 10-K):
- Closed **2023-11-07**. Exchange offer at **$34.25 per Hostess share** = $30.00 cash +
  0.03002 SJM shares (priced off SJM's 2023-09-08 close ≈ $141.6/share implied).
- **Total purchase consideration $5.4 billion**: $3.9bn cash net of cash acquired +
  **4.0M SJM shares valued at $450.2M** + $991.0M of assumed Hostess debt + $67.8M other
  debt-like item.
- **New debt of $5.0 billion borrowed**: $3.5bn senior notes + $800.0M delayed-draw term
  loan + $700.0M commercial paper.
- Purchase price allocation: **$1.8bn to indefinite-lived intangibles** (the Hostess brand
  trademark), **$1.2bn to customer and contractual relationships**, the balance largely
  goodwill. (E&Y made the allocation its FY2024 critical audit matter.)
- Promised **~$100.0M of cost synergies "by the end of 2026"**; $11.0M achieved in FY2024.

**What has been written off since:**

| item | amount | when | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Goodwill impairment, Sweet Baked Snacks reporting unit | **$1,661.6M** | FY2025 (Q4) | FY2025 10-K MD&A |
| Hostess brand indefinite-lived trademark impairment | **$320.9M** | FY2025 (Q4) | FY2025 10-K MD&A |
| Goodwill impairment — **the full remaining carrying value** of SBS goodwill | **$507.5M** | FY2026 Q3 | FY2026 10-K, critical audit matter |
| Hostess brand trademark impairment; brand then **reclassified as finite-lived** at 2026-01-31 | **$454.2M** | FY2026 Q3 | FY2026 10-K |
| Pre-tax loss on divestiture of **Voortman** (acquired with Hostess) | **$265.9M** | FY2025 (sold 2024-12-02) | FY2025 10-K |
| Pre-tax loss on divestiture of certain **SBS value brands** | **$44.2M** | FY2025 (sold 2025-03-03) | FY2025/FY2026 10-K |
| **TOTAL destroyed / lost** | **$3,254.3M** | within 3 years of closing | |

**$3.25bn of a $5.4bn purchase consideration — about 60% — is gone in under three years.**
Add the Indianapolis bakery closure (**$83.5M** cumulative restructuring cost, FY2026) and
cumulative Hostess **integration costs of $187.4M**.

The FY2026 10-K's own explanation of the second write-down, quoted:
> "both net sales and segment profit continued to underperform as compared to plan for the
> Sweet Baked Snacks reportable segment, reflecting **sustained challenges in the sweet
> baked goods category, ongoing executional and operating challenges**… The declines are
> reflective of both near-term underperformance and long-term expectations… driven by the
> sustained reduction in consumer discretionary income due to inflationary pressures and
> an overall shift in consumer sentiment related to sweet baked goods."

**How this run treats the acquisition accounting:**
1. **Impairments are non-cash and are already excluded from operating cash flow.** They do
   not enter owner earnings directly. They are the *record of capital allocation* and are
   scored at Q3, and they stay in the GAAP ROE series — [E5-33]: *"to tell owners year
   after year, 'Don't count this' … is misleading."*
2. **Amortisation of acquired intangibles ($191.1M FY2024, $219.3M FY2025, $210.6M FY2026)
   is carried inside the (c) band, not deleted.** The conservative end of (c) is full D&A
   precisely because the filed record proves these acquired brand assets are consumed.
   The generous end uses depreciation / total capex only.
3. **Integration, restructuring and divestiture costs are real costs** [E5-33] and remain
   inside owner earnings because owner earnings is computed from operating cash flow.
4. **What the business earns without any acquisition accounting** (FY2026 basis, judgment
   labelled as one): total segment profit **$2,006.4M** − corporate administrative
   expenses **$328.1M** = adjusted operating income **$1,678.3M**; − net interest
   **$381.2M** = $1,297.1M; − recurring special project costs (3-yr mean **$88.9M**) =
   $1,208.2M; − cash tax at the guided 24.2% = $915.8M; − SBC $23.6M ≈ **$890M**. Charge
   the acquired-intangible amortisation as the real cost the impairments show it to be and
   it is **≈ $730M**. That bracket — **$730M to $890M** — is the P&L-side cross-check on
   the cash-side owner-earnings range in §6.
