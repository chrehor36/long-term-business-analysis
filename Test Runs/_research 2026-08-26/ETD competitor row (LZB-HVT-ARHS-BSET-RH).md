## What the row shows
1. **The vertical-integration signature is real and shows exactly where predicted:** ETD's
   61.2% gross margin is the top of the set — above pure-retail HVT (60.7%) while ETD's
   consolidated figure also contains the manufacturing margin, and far above every other
   maker-retailer (BSET 56.3%, LZB 44.0%, ARHS 38.9%). Its trough operating margin (7.8%)
   leads every unleveraged peer; the closest structural comp (BSET, also a vertically
   integrated maker-retailer with design services) ran operating losses in the same slump.
2. **The volume/direction signature is also real and runs the other way:** ETD's revenue
   fell 29% from the FY2022 peak — worse than every peer except BSET — while ARHS grew
   +12% straight through the same premium-furnishings market and RH held ~flat. The growth
   in the category went to the attackers.
3. **Row limit [E3-61]:** identical structures produce opposite outcomes (ETD 7.8% OM vs
   BSET losses, same model); the row shows position, not conduct.
