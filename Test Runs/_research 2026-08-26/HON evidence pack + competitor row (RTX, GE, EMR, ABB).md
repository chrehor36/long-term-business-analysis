## 2. THE FACT THAT GOVERNS EVERY NUMBER IN THIS FILE

**The company described in the FY2025 10-K no longer exists in that form.** The Aerospace
separation, announced 2026-02-06 and pending throughout the FY2025 10-K, has since **completed**.
From the Q2 2026 10-Q (accession 0000773840-26-000124), Note 3, verbatim:

> "In the third quarter on June 29, 2026, the Company completed the previously announced
> separation of its Aerospace Technologies business (the Aerospace Spin-Off) into an independent,
> publicly traded company named Honeywell Aerospace Inc. (Honeywell Aerospace). The Company
> completed the Aerospace Spin-off, through a pro rata distribution of all of the issued and
> outstanding shares of common stock of Honeywell Aerospace to the holders of common stock of the
> Company. Each Honeywell Technologies shareowner received one share of Honeywell Aerospace common
> stock for every two shares of Honeywell Technologies common stock held of record as of the close
> of business on June 15, 2026 … **Honeywell Aerospace is now an independent publicly-traded
> company under the symbol "HONA" on the Nasdaq Stock Market LLC.**"

And from Note 1 of the same filing:

> "Following the Aerospace Spin-Off, **Honeywell International Inc. now operates as Honeywell
> Technologies.**"

Timing note, from the Q2 2026 10-Q Note 1: HON closes its quarters on a Saturday under a
predetermined fiscal calendar. "Honeywell Technologies' closing dates for the three and six months
ended June 30, 2026 and 2025, were **June 27, 2026** and June 28, 2025, respectively." The spin-off
on **June 29, 2026** therefore fell two days into HON's fiscal third quarter and is **not** in the
Q2 2026 balance sheet or income statement. The fiscal year end remains December 31.

Two further Q2 2026 events, both filed:
- **Reverse split.** "In the third quarter on June 29, 2026, following the Aerospace Spin-Off …
  the Reverse Stock Split became effective. As a result of the Reverse Stock Split, **every two
  shares of common stock issued and outstanding … were automatically combined into one share**."
  Shares outstanding at 2026-06-30: **316,940,010** (split-adjusted).
- **Quantinuum deconsolidated.** "The Company recognized a gain of **$6,629 million** during the
  three and six months ended June 30, 2026, as a result of the deconsolidation of Quantinuum …
  The initial fair value of the **retained investment of $7,525 million** at the date of
  deconsolidation was determined using a market approach based on the observable quoted market
  price of Quantinuum's common stock."
- **Johnson Matthey closed.** "On July 17, 2026, the Company acquired 100% of the outstanding
  equity interests of Johnson Matthey's Catalyst Technologies business segment (Johnson Matthey),
  for total consideration of **$1,750 million**, net of cash acquired."

**Consequence for this pack.** Everything in sections 3 to 7 is measured on the FY2025 10-K, which
is the last audited full-year record and the only basis on which a like-for-like competitor row
can be built. **Aerospace Technologies, which is 47% of FY2025 revenue and 53% of FY2025 segment
profit, is no longer inside HON.** Any run must decide which entity it is valuing. That decision
is not made here.

## 13. CONSTRUCTION NOTES AND WHAT THIS ROW CANNOT SAY

1. **The operating-income numerator is a choice, and the choice moves the answer materially.** On
   HON's own segment profit rather than the uniform construction, HON's return on total capital
   including goodwill rises from 16.5% to 20.4%; GE's rises from 22.1% to 26.0% or 29.2%; RTX's
   from 7.8% to 9.4%; Emerson's from 10.3% to 13.6%. **The ranking across the five is unchanged by
   the choice.** Both numbers are given in every column so nobody has to take one on trust.
2. **The narrow working-capital formula understates capital employed for the long-cycle names and
   is generous to them net.** Section 8.1 shows the sensitivity for all three December filers.
3. **Emerson's year ends 30 September 2025, three months before the other three.** ABB's ends
   31 December 2023, two years before. Neither is adjusted.
4. **GE's total assets include a run-off insurance book.** Two versions of the goodwill ratio are
   given for that reason. GE's operating numerator excludes insurance revenue and cost entirely.
5. **GE's revenue and balance sheet exclude the unconsolidated half of CFM International.** RTX's
   revenue includes collaborator share on the top line and removes it in cost of sales. **The two
   engine businesses are not consolidated on the same basis and the row cannot fix that.**
6. **Every number here is a position at a point in time. None of it is conduct.** Whether HON's
   $27.8bn goodwill wedge represents capability bought well or capital destroyed, whether GE's
   $9.1bn of goodwill reflects discipline or reflects two decades of impairment already taken, and
   whether RTX's $85.2bn wedge is the Rockwell Collins and Raytheon mergers working or not working,
   are judgments about people and history that this row does not contain and does not attempt.
7. **No verdict, moat class or recommendation appears anywhere in this file, by design.**
