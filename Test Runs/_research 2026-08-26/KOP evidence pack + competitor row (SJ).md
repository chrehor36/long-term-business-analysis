## Restructuring (10-Q Note 2)
- **Stickney shutdown** (announced 2026-05-08): discontinue coal tar distillation and
  chemical manufacturing at Stickney, IL by 2026-12-31; production shifts to Nyborg,
  Denmark. Filed reasons: "unit operating costs outpacing our ability to capture higher
  pricing, reduced raw material supply from North American steel manufacturers and
  increased capital requirements." Expected charges $233-252M ($176-185M non-cash;
  $52-62M plant cleaning/waste/demolition cash; $5M severance cash).
- Phthalic anhydride shutdown (Dec 2024, completed Q2-2025): $50-54M ($28M non-cash,
  $21-25M cleanup cash, $1M severance).
- Q2-2026 charge $215.8M (impairment $164.6M); cumulative program $292.4M.
- Stickney liability $38.4M accrued at Q2-2026, $0 paid to date; ~$18.5M of it a
  short-term ARO.
- Plants idled Feb 2026: Vance, AL (UIP; consolidation into Kennedy, AL) and Florence,
  SC (RUPS crossties; "due to lower overall future forecasted demand from the
  facility's largest customer"; production to Guthrie, KY, complete by Nov 2026).
