# REIT INTERNALIZATION COMPARABLES — 29 confirmed, 2014-2026
**Compiled 2026-08-31** for the RMR run's Q2/Q4 work. 69 raw filings on disk with the
`RMRpeer_internalization_` prefix; every row below carries its SEC accession. Facts as
filed; no estimates.

## THE FINDING THAT MATTERS FOR RMR
**RMR's own precedent is a ZERO-fee termination.** Equity Commonwealth (then
CommonWealth REIT) terminated RMR on 2014-09-30 after the entire board was removed by
shareholder written consent (Related/Corvex). RMR received **no internalization or
termination fee** — only $1.2M/month transition services for five months and the $15.3M
pro-rata incentive fee already accrued under the pre-existing agreement, with "no future
obligation to pay an incentive fee to RMR." 8-K 2014-10-01, acc. 0001104659-14-069514.

Read against the "20-year contracts are a moat" claim: when a managed vehicle's board
actually turned, the contract produced nothing. The fee stream is only as durable as the
board's composition.

## The fee is BIMODAL
**Where the REIT bought the manager entity** — 3-4x annual fee run-rate, or ~7-10% of
equity market cap: AGNC $562M cash (2016) · GNL/AR Global $375M (2023) · Broadstone
~$209.5M + $90.5M debt (2020) · Preferred Apartment $154M + $25M earnout (2020) ·
American Healthcare ~$134.7M + uncapped 7-yr earn-out (2021) · Watermark $125M (2020) ·
Steadfast $125M (2020) · National Healthcare Properties $113.1M (2024) · BrightSpire
$102.3M cash (2021) · Granite Point $44.5M cash (2020) · Independence Realty $43M cash
(2016) · Jernigan $42.1M (2020) · Bluerock ~$41.2M (2017) · Sila $40M cash (2020) ·
Global Medical $18.1M (2020) · Medalist $1.6M (2023).

**Where the REIT exercised a termination right, or the manager had no independent value
— ZERO:** Equity Commonwealth/RMR (2014) · InvenTrust/Inland (2014, "we will not pay an
internalization fee") · NorthStar Healthcare (2022, "no payment will be made") · Cherry
Hill (2024, "no termination fee is payable") · ACRES (2026, "for no additional
consideration") · Annaly ($1.00 nominal, 2020).

## Contested / litigated
- **Equity Commonwealth/RMR** — the most contested in the set; board removed by written
  consent, replacement board terminated the manager; extensive prior arbitration.
- **Granite Point/Pine River** — the term-loan 8-K names "the potential arbitral award"
  as a use of proceeds; the $44.5M settled a disputed process.
- **The Parking REIT/MVP** — renegotiated twice after the fact; manager principal later
  subject of SEC action.
- **GNL/AR Global** — publicly opposed by Blackwells during the vote; supplemental proxy
  disclosures 2023-08-09 (0001104659-23-089315).
- **Monogram/Behringer Harvard** (2013, outside window) — sued years later over
  internalization terms; the precedent that these agreements get litigated late.

## CONFIRMED NOT internalized (searched, negative)
All five current RMR-managed REITs — **SVC, OPI, ILPT, DHC, SEVN** — still describe
themselves as "managed by RMR" in 2026 filings; none appears in any internalization
full-text sweep. Also negative: Ashford Hospitality/Braemar (still Ashford Inc.-advised;
the single "internalization" hit is a 2025 board-seat cooperation agreement), American
Strategic Investment/NYC, Inland Real Estate Income Trust (2024 8-K *amends* the manager
agreement), Owens Realty (never completed), Farmland Partners (internal since IPO).

## Method note
Six full-text sweeps across 8-Ks 2015-2026 ("internalization fee", "internalization of
management", "internalization transaction", "completed the internalization",
"internalization of the Company's management", "became internally managed"), plus
per-candidate 8-K item review. Near-misses separated where the internalization was
bundled into a REIT-to-REIT merger (Necessity Retail into GNL; Cottonwood/RealSource;
Griffin-American III/IV). One gap recorded, not estimated: American Farmland Co's own
internalization (SEC returned HTTP 503 on acc. 0001564590-16-014139).
