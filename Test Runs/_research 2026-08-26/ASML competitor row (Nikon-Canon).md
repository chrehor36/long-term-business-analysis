**Research note for Q2 (franchise). A moat is a relative claim; this is the relative row.**
Compiled 2026-08-28 under work order dated 2026-08-26. All figures filing-sourced or from the
issuer's own English IR materials; every figure carries its document in the source register below.
No currency conversion is performed anywhere in this note: JPY stays JPY, EUR stays EUR.
The same-metric row that needs no FX is **units**.

**Fiscal window caveat, stated once:** ASML and Canon report calendar years (ended December 31).
Nikon's fiscal year ends March 31; its "FY2026/3" (April 2025 to March 2026) overlaps CY2025 by
nine months. The rows below align ASML/Canon CY2023-2025 with Nikon FY2024/3-FY2026/3. This is
the closest same-window alignment the filings permit.

**DERIVED, for scale only (arithmetic on the sourced figures above, no new data):**
revenue per litho system recognized, 2025 window: ASML (24,474.3 - 824.6 M&I) / 327 units is
about EUR 72M per system. Nikon 167,258 JPY millions covers 27 semi + 32 FPD units plus all
segment service, about JPY 2.8B per unit gross. Canon Optical Equipment 256.4 JPY billions
covers 234 semi + 33 FPD units, about JPY 1.0B per unit gross. The three companies sell
machines an order of magnitude (or two) apart in price. Unit counts alone understate the
revenue gap; revenue alone understates Canon's unit-share in trailing-edge i-line/KrF.

## 6. WHAT THE ROW SHOWS (facts only; verdict language belongs in the run file)

1. **EUV: one seller.** ASML recognized 48 EUV systems (11,602.7 EUR millions) in 2025. Neither
   competitor has an EUV product; neither discloses any EUV development program for sale.
   Nikon instead sells EUV-related *components* through its Components/Industry segment.
2. **ArF immersion, the leading-edge DUV workhorse: effectively one seller.** ASML 131 units in
   2025; Nikon 0 units in FY2026/3 (3 the prior year, 11 the year before); Canon has never had
   an immersion tool and has no ArF row in its unit table at all.
3. **The unit competition that exists is at the trailing edge.** Canon's 234 units are 188
   i-line + 46 KrF, driven by back-end/advanced-packaging and power/memory devices. Nikon's 27
   units are mostly i-line-class. In i-line and KrF units combined, Canon (234) actually
   out-ships ASML (132), but at the per-unit revenue an order of magnitude lower shown in the
   derived-scale note of section 3.
4. **Profitability of the litho business:** ASML company operating margin 34.6% (2025). Canon's
   Industrial BU (litho plus deposition/bonders) 17.3% (62,525 / 361,128). Nikon's pure-litho
   segment: 0.8% margin in FY2025/3 and a loss in FY2026/3.
5. Both competitors' litho segments are a minor share of their parent: Canon Industrial is 7.8%
   of Canon net sales (361,128 / 4,624,727); Nikon Precision Equipment is 24.7% of Nikon
   revenue (167,258 / 677,163) and is loss-making.
