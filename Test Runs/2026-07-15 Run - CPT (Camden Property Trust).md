# Company Run — Camden Property Trust (CPT) — 2026-07-15
Framework v3.0. From the REIT mechanical screen. OE from real FY2025 10-K
data (Archives/edgar/data/906345), with a flagged approximation on
maintenance capex (see Gate 4 — CPT's disclosure is less clean than AVB/
EQR/ESS/AMH).

## STEP 0 — RATE REFRESH
US 30-yr **5.10%**. Mkt cap $11.36B — no size premium.

## GATE 1 — CIRCLE OF COMPETENCE
Owns and operates apartment communities concentrated in Sun Belt markets
(Houston headquarters; Texas, Georgia, Florida, North Carolina, Colorado
concentrations). Same rent × occupancy − opex − capex economics as the
other apartment REITs. **VERDICT: PASS.**

## GATE 2 — MOAT
Franchise test [E3-03]: needed/desired — yes; no close substitute — **weak**
(same Sun Belt supply-elasticity concern already flagged at MAA — CPT's
markets overlap significantly with MAA's); unregulated — largely yes, low
rent-control exposure. Company's own 10-K explicitly characterizes new
multifamily supply in its markets as currently "manageable" — a real,
disclosed, and directly relevant statement given the supply concerns
already flagged for the Sun Belt REIT in this batch (MAA). **Class:
NARROW-to-weak**, similar concern profile to MAA though CPT's own
disclosure is more sanguine about current supply/demand balance.
**VERDICT: PASS with NARROW classification**, Sun Belt supply risk flagged
consistently with MAA's run.

## GATE 3 — MANAGEMENT
CEO **Alexander J. Jessett** — board listing shows 2026 as his director
start year, indicating a **very recent CEO transition**, the same pattern
already seen at MAA (Brad Hill, April 2025). **D. Keith Oden, Executive Vice
Chairman, has served since 1993** — CPT's co-founder-era leadership remains
present in a senior board role even as day-to-day CEO leadership just
changed, a genuine continuity-plus-transition mix worth naming rather than
treating as purely one or the other. No litigation/restatement/integrity
flags found.
**VERDICT: PASS**, recent-CEO-transition risk flagged explicitly, partially
offset (not eliminated) by continued co-founder board presence.

## GATE 4 — FINANCIAL QUALITY (full OE formula, approximated maintenance capex)
**CPT's capex disclosure is less clean than the other REITs run so far** —
the cash flow statement blends "Development and capital improvements,
including land" into one figure ($440.4M in 2025, mostly growth/development
spend, not maintenance). The 10-K's forward-looking MD&A separately
guides **$113–117 million of "additional recurring capital expenditures"
for 2026** — this is the only recurring-capex-specific figure disclosed.
**This run uses the guidance midpoint (~$115M) as an approximation for
recent-year recurring capex, applied to both years shown, since a clean
historical breakout was not found** — flagged as a real limitation, not
presented as precise:

| FY | Net income (common) | Real estate D&A | Recurring capex (approximated) | **OE = NI+D&A−capex** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | $163.293M | $569.998M | ~$115M | **~$618.291M** |
| 2025 | $384.462M | $597.925M | ~$115M | **~$867.387M** |

**Sensitivity check (not run elsewhere in this batch, but warranted given
the approximation here):** if actual FY2024 recurring capex were $150M
instead of $115M, OE falls to ~$583M (yield 5.13%, still barely passes);
at $200M, OE falls to ~$533M (yield 4.70%, **fails**). **The Gate 6 verdict
below is genuinely sensitive to this approximation** in a way none of the
other REIT runs have been.
**LOWEST VERIFIED TIER (approximated): ~$618.291M (FY2024).**
Fortress (financial/leverage-ceiling track): Total assets $9,042.989M ÷
Total equity $4,438.253M = **2.038:1** — comfortably under the 10:1 ceiling.
**VERDICT: PASS**, with the capex approximation flagged as a material
open item, not a resolved input.

## GATE 6 — VALUATION

11,360 = **5.44%** vs. hurdle **5.10%** → **PASSES**, but by only **0.34pp**
— and per the Gate 4 sensitivity check, this pass could flip to a fail if
true recurring capex runs meaningfully above the guidance figure used here.
**Book Two DCF (Ruling 4 — built 2026-07-15 PM)**: Gate 3 carried a real
caveat (brand-new CEO, already scored [P] at Gate 5) → discount rate = 5.10%
+ 2.5% NARROW spread + 0.75% caveat bump = **8.35%**. g1: the raw YoY OE
growth (FY2024→FY2025) computes to +40.3%, but this is an artifact of the
already-flagged FY2024 NI anomaly ($163.293M vs. $384.462M in FY2025 — a
swing far too large to be organic operating growth) compounding the Gate 4
capex approximation already flagged as a documented [U]. **Rather than feed
an untrustworthy 40% figure into the DCF, g1 is deliberately capped at the
2% terminal growth rate** — i.e., no above-terminal growth assumed at all.
This is a conservative override, not a data point, and is flagged as such
explicitly (consistent with the Gate 4/Gate 5 caution already on record for
this company). 10yr NARROW fade (though with g1=g_terminal, the fade is
flat).
- **IV = $9,931.6M** | Max buy (20% MOS) = **$7,945.3M**
- Current mkt cap $11,360M → **above raw IV**, let alone the max-buy line.

**Scream Test**: Book One PASSES by only 0.34pp — already flagged as the
least reliable Statute pass in the batch given the approximated capex input.
Book Two, even under a deliberately conservative g1, still finds the price

even on its most cautious construction** → **WHISPER, pass — do not act.**
**VERDICT: STATUTE-ONLY (starter size) — the least reliable "pass" in this
batch**, and Book Two does not rescue it. Treat with more caution than
