# Company Run — Marubeni Corporation (MARUY) — 2026-07-15
Framework v3.0. From the Japan ADR mechanical screen. Fourth of five sogo
shosha runs — same shared primary-source basis as MTSUY/MITSY/ITOCY.

## STEP 0 — RATE REFRESH
Japan 30-yr JGB **3.76%**, floored at **4.00%**. Mkt cap $49.80B — no size
premium (above the $2B threshold, though the smallest-by-market-cap of the
five sogo shosha after Sumitomo).

## GATE 1 — CIRCLE OF COMPETENCE
Same sogo shosha structure — Marubeni is weighted toward **grain/food
trading (one of the largest grain traders globally), power generation, and
agribusiness**, a notably different emphasis than Mitsubishi/Mitsui's
heavier metals/energy focus. Same Berkshire-analogy circle-of-competence
argument [2023 Letter] — named explicitly in Buffett's list ("Itochu,
**Marubeni**, Mitsubishi, Mitsui and Sumitomo").
**VERDICT: PASS.**

## GATE 2 — MOAT
Same scale/deal-flow-based advantage as the other four. Marubeni's global
grain-trading position is a genuine, specific scale advantage in that
sub-sector (grain trading has real infrastructure/relationship barriers to
entry — port facilities, farmer relationships, shipping logistics) — worth
naming as a more concrete moat candidate than the generic "diversified
scale" argument applied to the others. **Class: NARROW**, with the grain-
trading sub-business arguably closer to genuine structural advantage than
the group average.
**VERDICT: PASS with NARROW classification.**

## GATE 3 — MANAGEMENT
Same primary-source basis: named explicitly in Buffett's 2023 Letter
commentary on buyback discipline, modest compensation, ~1/3 payout ratio,
and reluctance to issue shares, applying to all five including Marubeni.
**VERDICT: PASS**, same evidence base as the other sogo shosha.

## GATE 5 — INVERSION
Same four-factor scoring as the other sogo shosha (commodity-cycle exposure,
management/key-person, unverified balance sheet, currency risk), all **[P]**.
Marubeni's grain/agribusiness weighting introduces a **distinct cyclical
driver (weather/harvest cycles, agricultural commodity prices)** different
from the metals/energy cycle the other four are more exposed to — a real
diversification benefit for a basket holder, though this run assesses
Marubeni individually and that diversification benefit doesn't reduce
Marubeni's own standalone agribusiness-cycle exposure.
**Thesis-breaking metric:** same as the other sogo shosha, plus a sustained
global grain-trading margin compression specific to Marubeni's core
business.
**VERDICT: PASS**, same evidentiary caveats as the other four.
