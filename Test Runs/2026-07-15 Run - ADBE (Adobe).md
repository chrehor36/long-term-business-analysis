## GATE 2 — MOAT
Franchise test [E3-03], from Adobe's own 10-K: **"...competition from
productivity tools and consumer-focused creative tools including general
productivity platforms and apps, web- and mobile-first design platforms,
an array of easy-to-use desktop, web and mobile content creation apps,
AI-first creativity and productivity tools, presentation tools, and social
media platforms that offer built-in media editing capabilities. Our
solutions for creators and creative professionals face broad competition
from both established and emerging players including... AI-first creative
tools..."** — Adobe's own filing names AI-first creative tools as a
named, current competitive category, not a speculative future risk.
- **No close substitute**: **historically very strong, now genuinely
  tested**. Professional creative workflows (Photoshop/Illustrator file
  formats, plugin ecosystems, decades of trained-user muscle memory) carry
  real, substantial switching costs — one of the strongest software moats
  of any name run today on that basis alone. But generative-AI creative
  tools are a genuinely new kind of substitute (not just a cheaper
  Photoshop clone, but a different creation paradigm) that the company's
  own filing names directly. **Unlike Gartner (run separately, where a 42%
  NI decline shows the named AI-disruption risk already materializing in
  the numbers), Adobe's net income has continued growing through FY2025
  ($4,756M FY2022 → $7,130M FY2025, +50%)** — the named risk is real and
  disclosed, but not yet visible in the financial results, a meaningfully
  different evidentiary situation than Gartner's.
**Class: NARROW-to-WIDE** (very strong historical professional-workflow
moat, tempered by a real, named, not-yet-materialized AI-disruption risk),
**Direction: NARROWING** on the named risk specifically, even though
current results don't yet show it.
**VERDICT: PASS with NARROW-to-WIDE classification**, resolved to the
conservative NARROW end given the named AI risk, while explicitly noting
the risk is disclosed-but-not-yet-realized rather than already showing up
in earnings (unlike Gartner's case).
