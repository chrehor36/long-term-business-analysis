## What the series shows, and what it does not

- **Sales did not fall in the downturns.** System sales were flat in 2015 (-0.1%) and 2024 (-0.8%) and rose in every
  other year, through the 2019 and 2023 slowdowns. Service sales fell once, in 2023, by 2.1%.
- **The swings the run recorded are in cash, not in sales.** Owner cash was EUR 10,257M, 7,834M, 4,569M, 10,075M and
  11,430M (2021 to 2025, the run's maintenance case); the run attributes the swing to customer down payments, which rose
  from EUR 1.8B to 19.4B over the decade.
- **Service is about a quarter of sales** (23.0% to 27.5% across 2014 to 2025) and grew from EUR 1,613.5M to 8,193.0M,
  about 15.9% a year compounded over eleven years.
- **It does not decide Q1 by itself.** Under Q1's paragraph on parts, the part that matters is the one with more than half
  of five-year operating profit, or else the largest; system sales are about three quarters of the total.
- **It does not answer the ten-year question.** The series is the past. Q1 asks where the earning power will be in ten
  years, and the run's evidence is that the insiders' own written forecasts missed by half (2025 revenue modelled in 2018 at
  EUR 15 to 24B; actual EUR 32.7B).
