Sources: each company's latest 10-K via SEC XBRL + primary text (ABG filed

Hurdle: **6.10%** (5.10% + small/mid premium) throughout.

## Shared sector analysis
**Gate 1 (all PASS)**: franchised new/used dealerships + parts/service +
F&I — understandable retail economics with a high-margin recurring
service anchor.
**Gate 2 — the real moat mechanism**: state franchise laws + manufacturer
framework agreements legally protect dealer territories (manufacturers
cannot sell direct or freely add competing dealerships) — a genuine
regulatory/contractual barrier, unusual among retailers. Offsetting
erosion vectors, from the filings themselves: ABG names "our relationships
with manufacturers... and our ability to renew... framework and dealer
agreements" as a principal risk (the moat is licensed, not owned); AN
concedes ~17,000 franchised dealers plus twice as many independents
compete on "location, service, price, selection"; the EV-direct-sales
model is the structural threat to the franchise-law wall itself.
**Class: NARROW (statutory/contractual), Direction: NARROWING** — all five.
**Gate 3 (all PASS provisional)** — none verified against proxies; caveat
bumps retained. **Gate 5 (all PASS, one [U] each)**: cycle normalization
[P — realized, see every NI series], EV-direct erosion [P], manufacturer
dependence [P].
