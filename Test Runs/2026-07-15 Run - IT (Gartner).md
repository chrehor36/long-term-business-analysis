## STEP 0 — RATE REFRESH
US 30-yr **5.10%**. Mkt cap $8.90B — no size premium (above $2B large-cap
threshold, smallest name run today though).

## GATE 1 — CIRCLE OF COMPETENCE
Subscription research/advisory (Magic Quadrant-style syndicated research,
enterprise IT decision-support), plus consulting and conferences segments.
Unit economics: subscription contract value, renewal rate, and per-client
expansion. Understandable, though genuinely dependent on enterprise clients
continuing to value paid research over free/AI-generated alternatives (see
Gate 2). **VERDICT: PASS.**

## GATE 2 — MOAT
Franchise test [E3-03], from Gartner's own 10-K risk factors: **"...we face
competition from a significant number of independent providers of
information products and services. We compete indirectly with consulting
firms and other data and information providers, including electronic and
print media companies... we face competition from free sources of
information that are available to our clients through the internet. We
anticipate encountering more competition with increased adoption of AI
services in the markets in which we compete. Limited barriers to entry
exist in the markets in which we do business."**
- **No close substitute**: **weak, and the company says so itself** — "free
  sources of information," AI-services competition, and an explicit
  admission of "limited barriers to entry" are Gartner's own words, not an
  inference. This is a real, named erosion risk, not boilerplate dismissed
  by this run.
- **Real, narrower advantage**: subscription research businesses of this
  type historically retain embedded enterprise relationships (a client's
  procurement/IT-strategy process gets built around syndicated research
  access) — a genuine switching-cost mechanism, though this run did not
  independently pull Gartner's disclosed contract-value retention rate to
  confirm it directly this pass.
- Net income fell from $1,253.7M (FY2024) to $729.2M (FY2025) — a **42%
  single-year decline**. This run did not independently confirm the cause
  (one-time item vs. genuine business deterioration), but the magnitude and
  timing align with the AI-competition risk the company names in its own
  filing — flagged as a real, not hypothetical, concern rather than
  smoothed over.

**Class: NARROW**, **Direction: NARROWING** — both by the company's own
admitted competitive-entry language and by a large, unexplained recent
earnings decline consistent with the named risk.
**VERDICT: PASS with NARROW/NARROWING classification**, carrying real,
company-disclosed erosion evidence forward rather than defaulting to a
comfortable "subscription business = wide moat" assumption.

facility. Debt/lowest-tier-OE ≈ 3.3x, a manageable multiple for an
investment-grade-profile services business, and current liabilities
include substantial deferred subscription revenue (a benign, pre-funded
liability for this business model, not distress debt) rather than being
entirely debt. Credit rating not independently pulled this pass — flagged.
**VERDICT: PASS**, with the thin-equity balance sheet named explicitly as a
real characteristic requiring the termed-out-debt context to interpret
correctly, not a silent pass-through.

   syndicated research (the company's own named risk, plus the 42% NI
   decline already observed). Scored **[P]**, arguably the most live
   inversion risk of any company run today given it is already showing up
   in the numbers, not just the risk factors.
2. **Management/key-person** — not independently verified. Scored **[U]**.
3. **Balance-sheet stress** — thin equity, but termed-out debt and 3.3x
   OE coverage. Scored **[P]**, not [D] — genuinely thinner cushion than
   most names run today, even though not disqualifying.
4. **Client-budget cyclicality** (enterprise research/consulting spend is
   discretionary and gets cut in downturns). Scored **[P]**.

**One [U], three [P]s, no [D]** — the most uncertain qualitative profile of
any S&P 500 name run today, driven by a real, already-materializing AI-
disruption signal rather than a purely hypothetical one.
**Thesis-breaking metric:** a second consecutive year of NI decline, or
contract-value retention rate (if/when independently pulled) falling below
historical norms.
**VERDICT: PASS, with a documented [U] and the most serious [P] set of any
name run today** — proceeding to Gate 6 with this uncertainty carried
loudly forward, not resolved by assumption.
