### THE FINDING: the disclosed advantage is real, it was measured for the first time, and it is smaller than the cost gap

The 2026-07-16 v3.0 note recorded CRC as *"the closest call of any E&P run in this project"* and
quoted the company's own claim:

> "We believe that our **proximity to the California refineries gives us a competitive advantage

### THE SECOND FINDING: the company publishes its own maintenance-capital number, and it invalidates the capex end

**[E5-20]** asks whether spending depreciation keeps a depleting business in place, and the answer
had to come off the filing rather than the category. **CRC answers it itself**, in the 8-K EX-99.1
of 2026-08-10:

> "**Lowered California long-term maintenance capital outlook by reducing drilling, completions and
> workover capital expectations by approximately 5% to a $450 million to $475 million range with
> six drilling rigs**, compared to seven previously"

and the 2026 guidance table pairs **capital investments $520-560M with DD&A $520-540M**.

**So the capex end of the 2023-2025 owner-earnings band is INVALID, not merely optimistic.** Actual
capital investments of **$185M, $255M and $322M** are all far below the company's own stated
maintenance requirement, because **California stopped issuing new-well permits** - the 10-K records
the resumption: *"Following the resumption of permitting for new wells in January of this year, we
expanded our drilling program to include new well development in Kern County."* Under-spending
forced by a permit freeze is deferred maintenance, not owner earnings.
**`tools/run.py` reported OE hi of $504M for 2025 and $413M for the three-year window on that
end.** The tool computed and concluded nothing, which is exactly its remit; **the finding is that a
reader taking the top of the band would be taking a number the company's own 8-K contradicts.**
The reverse also holds and is worth recording: **the D&A default [E3-44] genuinely holds for this
business**, and for once the filing proves it instead of the run assuming it - guided DD&A sits
inside guided capex and just above disclosed maintenance. **The reason is a real asset-quality
fact:** the production bridge shows natural decline of only **(4), (7) and (6) MBoe/d** on bases of
110, 86 and 91, roughly **4-8% a year**, against 30%+ first-year shale declines.

On the company's own (c), owner earnings are **$185M to $234M**, or **3.94% to 4.97%** on a
re-struck cap of $4,708.6M, against a **5.34%** sovereign and nowhere near the **~10% floor
[E4-28]**.

### THE NAMED DEATH IS AT THE DEMAND END, AND NO SURVIVAL SHAPE WAS ADDED

as much as the California refineries that buy the barrels - and the filing publishes their

crude. **A ten-point widening of the Brent discount - from 97.5% to 87.5%, below where Amplify

why it is not rated lower: the benign 98/96/98 realization history is *experience*, and the exposure
is five buyers and one suspended pipeline.
