## Q1: CAN I UNDERSTAND IT? Where its economics will be in ten years, inside my perimeter. STOP.
**The test as the framework states it.** Understanding is "a reasonable fix on about what the earning power and competitive position will look like in five or 10 years" **[M2012-065]**, "where the business will be in 10 years" **[M2000-037]**; the first step is to identify "the key variables" and to evaluate "how predictable they were first", and "If something is not very predictable, forget it." **[M1998-044]**; the past statements must tell "what the future financial statements are going to look like" **[M2008-033]**; and the question's own list of what it rules OUT opens with start-ups: "Start-ups are not our game." **[L2007-015]**; "We don’t do startups." **[M2008-088]**.

**The filing facts.**
- **The business has never reached earning power, in any of the four businesses it has begun.** Irrigated farming (from 2009): the produce business DFP was sold in 2017 "based on three years of operational losses". Greenhouse leasing to marijuana growers through GrowCo (from 2014): tenants evicted for non-payment in December 2017 and July 2018, and GrowCo deconsolidated from 2018-04-01 "due to change of management and control". Water management, called "our core business" from October 2016: "During fiscal year 2017 we had negligible revenues from our water business as our new initiatives have not yet gained traction. We also experienced negligible water revenues in 2018"; Item 1 calls the basin "our first water-focused project" that "we plan to develop". Hemp (from 2019): Vaxa and its two subsidiaries were bought on 2019-07-31 for 30,000,000 shares; the 10-Q calls Ekstrak "an emerging company in isolate extraction". (FY2018 10-K Item 1 and Item 7, `0001493152-19-005266`; 10-Q `0001493152-19-018289`.)
- **What it earned.** Revenue $66 thousand in 2018, "the majority" from "farm and grazing leases" (Item 7), $145 thousand in the nine months to 2019-09-30 (10-Q). Net cash from operations was negative in every year 2012 to 2018 on the filed statements (tagged series: from $-4.291 million in 2012 to $-1.060 million in 2018). Accumulated deficit $95.596 million at 2019-09-30. The 10-K's own risk factor: "There is no assurance that we will generate revenues or profits. We have not been profitable in the past".
- **The asset the water plan rests on was removed.** The state engineer took the Cucharas dam down for want of the company's capital: "Two Rivers was able to empty all the water in the Dam but was not able to meet the requirements of the agreement by March 31, 2018 due to lack of capital", and the State's deconstruction "was completed in January 2019" (Item 1). The 8-K of 2020-04-29 (`0001493152-20-007292`) states that "the Cucharas Dam’s structure was razed by the Colorado State Office of Engineers as a result of insufficient capital needed to comply with reconstruction", and that a buyer of the controlling interest would be "responsible for securing an estimated $40,000,0000 financing for future Cucharas Dam construction obligations" (the figure is printed with that digit grouping).
- **No accounts after 2019-09-30.** The 10-K for FY2019 was promised "on or before May 14, 2020" (8-K `0001493152-20-005339`) and never filed; the 8-K of 2021-07-14 (`0001493152-21-016792`) said "We intend to become current with our filing as soon as possible"; the Commission found that the company had "not filed any periodic reports since it filed a Form 10-Q for the period ended September 30, 2019", that it "failed to file an answer" and did not respond to the order to show cause, and revoked the registration (Release No. 95549, 2022-08-18). Whether the HCIC controlling interest was sold under the agreement of 2020-02-27, whether the hemp business still operates, and what the company owns and owes today: no filing states it.

**The key variables, and whether they are foreseeable.** Three decide the ten-year economics: (1) whether and at what cost the Cucharas storage is rebuilt (the company's own estimate is a $40 million obligation, against cash of $401 thousand at 2019-09-30); (2) what water from these rights would sell or lease for, and to whom, a market the company has never sold into at more than "negligible" revenue; (3) the economics of CBD hemp extraction bought in 2019 with no filed annual result. None has a history in the filings from which a forecast could be read: the past statements tell nothing of "what the future financial statements are going to look like" **[M2008-033]**, and the latest statement is seven years old. "If something is not very predictable, forget it." **[M1998-044]**

**Routing.** This is not the case of a fast-changing industry sent to TOO HARD: water rights change slowly, and the framework's Q1 list rules start-ups OUT by name **[L2007-015]**, **[M2008-088]**. In substance every business this company has run is a start-up: each was begun with outside money (the 10-K: "Since inception, we have raised and invested over $81 million to acquire, improve, integrate farm/water assets, launch related businesses, and support operations"), and none has reached a year of positive operating cash in the seven years 2012 to 2018 (2015 to 2018 read on the filed statements, 2012 to 2014 from the tagged series). A decade of operations (2009 to 2019) does not make a start-up a going business when the earning power has never appeared. The doubt rule points the same way: "if you have doubts about something being into your circle of competence, it isn’t." **[M2002-092]**. The verdict falls on the business, not on the analyst's work: more reading would not supply a record that the company never filed (the WORK cause does not apply), and the question is not one the box sends to TOO HARD.

**Contrary evidence, written down as found** **[M1997-127]**: the water rights are old, senior and decreed, and land and water in Colorado are bought and sold "much like real property" (Item 1); a business that owned such rights and simply leased the water could in principle be understood. Against that, the company's own record over a decade is that it could not turn the rights into revenue, and the storage that would have made them saleable in volume is gone. The rights may have a sale value; Q1 asks about a business's earning power, and a liquidation value is not an earning power; the share is bought as a business **[M1997-109]**, not as a claim on a sale of its parts.

- **VERDICT: OUT.** A start-up in substance, with no earning power in any year of the filed record and no filed accounts since 2019-09-30 **[L2007-015]**, **[M2008-088]**, **[M1998-044]**. The file closes here; Q2 to Q12 are NOT REACHED.
