## 0. THE METRIC, STATED ONCE AND APPLIED IDENTICALLY

**Return on unleveraged net tangible operating assets (NTOA)** =

> fiscal-year OPERATING INCOME ÷ (total assets − goodwill − other intangible assets −
> cash & equivalents − short-term investments − non-interest-bearing operating liabilities)

**Non-interest-bearing operating liabilities subtracted** (listed per company below):
accounts payable; accrued expenses/liabilities; income taxes payable; deferred income tax
liabilities; and other non-debt operating liabilities. **NOT subtracted:** any funded debt
(short- and long-term borrowings, finance lease obligations) and **operating lease
liabilities** (current and noncurrent).

**Operating-lease treatment, stated explicitly and applied uniformly:** operating lease
right-of-use assets **STAY IN** the asset base; operating lease liabilities are **NOT
subtracted** (treated as financing, not as an operating liability). This treats the rented
estate as capital the business must earn on, at every company alike. All companies in the
row report under ASC 842, so the treatment is available identically for all.
