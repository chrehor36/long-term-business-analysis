## 2. LTIP (2026 proxy, acc. 0001193125-26-126620)

Structure FY2025: 50% PSUs, 25% stock options, 25% RSUs (options vest ratably over 3 years, 10-year term; RSUs vest ratably over 3 years).

PSU metrics, verbatim: "The performance metrics for the PSUs are growth in net sales and growth in earnings per diluted share, with each of the metrics weighted 50%." And: "The achievement of the performance metrics for PSUs granted in fiscal 2025 ... is dependent upon the Company's annual net sales and earnings per diluted share for fiscal 2027 as compared to the target level of achievement set by the Board." Plus a relative TSR modifier: "the application of the TSR modifier may result in the number of shares earned from PSU awards being reduced or increased by up to 25% if TSR over the three-year performance period relative to the S&P 500 on the first day of the performance period ('comparator group') falls outside of a defined range" (≥75th pctile +25%; 25th–75th no change; <25th −25%).

Non-GAAP usage: **no adjusted/non-GAAP measure is named in either the CIP or the PSU metrics.** The 2023 PSU achievement table (measured on FY2025) uses actual reported figures — net sales $15,524 (millions) and diluted EPS $2.06 — matching the GAAP 10-K numbers. The only adjustment levers disclosed, verbatim:
- CIP: "the Compensation and Human Capital Committee may exclude any extraordinary or unusual items when determining whether the net income and net sales targets are met" (not exercised in FY2024 or FY2025 — both proxies state no such adjustments were made).
- PSU EPS: "The earnings per diluted share target also includes an assumption of share repurchase activity over the performance period." And: "Once the performance period ends, the Compensation and Human Capital Committee will consider all relevant factors, including the impact of share repurchases, in order to determine whether the targets were met."

PSU rigor datapoint: 2023 PSUs (FY2025 measurement) — net sales threshold $16,252M / target $17,300M / max $18,348M vs actual $15,524M; diluted EPS threshold $2.39 / target $2.53 / max $2.67 vs actual $2.06. Verbatim: "As threshold performance was not achieved for either the net sales or the earnings per diluted share metric, the 2023 PSUs that were granted to the Named Executive Officers were forfeited."

Forward-looking PSU targets are withheld, verbatim: "The performance targets for the net sales and earnings per diluted share metrics applicable to the PSUs granted in 2025 represent proprietary information and the Company has determined that their disclosure at this time poses competitive harm concerns to the Company. Consistent with the Company's historical practice, the applicable performance hurdles and actual performance results will be fully disclosed by the Company following the completion of the performance period."

One-time award (public 2025-11-05 grant, disclosed in 2026 proxy): CEO Retention Equity Awards to Lawton, target grant value $20 million — Retention PSU Award $12,000,000 target (vests 2031-02-05 on TSR relative to S&P 500 constituents over 2026-01-01 to 2030-12-31; target requires ≥55th percentile; threshold 30th; max 80th; capped at 100% if absolute TSR negative; value cap 5x target) plus Retention RSU Award $8,000,000 (vests thirds on 3rd/4th/5th anniversaries). Proxy notes: "there have been no comparable special or one-time retention equity awards granted to a chief executive officer or any of the Company's executive officers in the past decade."
