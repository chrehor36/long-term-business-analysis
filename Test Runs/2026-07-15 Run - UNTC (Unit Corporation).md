# Company Run — Unit Corporation (UNTC) — 2026-07-15
Framework v3.0 per _TEMPLATE. From the OTCQX mechanical screen (non-bank survivor).
Price/mkt cap $304.28M (Jul 15, same-day). Ruling 2 in effect (non-financial —
survival track applies, not the leverage ceiling).

## STEP 0 — RATE REFRESH
US 30-yr **5.10%** (unchanged, Jul 13–15 checks).

## GATE 1 — CIRCLE OF COMPETENCE
Tulsa-based oil & gas E&P (Anadarko Basin, Permian/Scurry County), now
**single-segment following the October 2025 sale of its drilling subsidiary (Unit
Drilling Co.) to Cactus Drilling for $119.7M.** Unit economics are readable at a
surface level (production × realized price − operating cost, hedged ~60% of
2025 gas production) — but **genuine circle-of-competence honesty check**: commodity
E&P requires real judgment about reserve quality, decline curves, and basin
economics that a surface-level filing read does not confer. This is closer to
Buffett's "quantitative/statistical" mode than a franchise business [E1-15] — worth
naming rather than waving through.
**VERDICT: PASS, with the above caveat carried forward** (not a disqualifier, but a
genuine limit on conviction).

## GATE 2 — MOAT
Franchise test [E3-03]: needed/desired — yes, generically (energy is always
needed); no close substitute — **NO, oil and gas are fungible commodities, this
fails cleanly**; not price-regulated — yes. **Two of three fail the strict test.**
This is a commodity business with no durable pricing power. At best, a **narrow
cost-advantage** exists in specific low-cost acreage (Anadarko, Scurry County) — the
framework does not require a moat to proceed (NONE is a defined, valid
classification with its own harsher DCF treatment), but the honest read is
**Class: NONE-to-NARROW (cost advantage in specific basins only).**
**VERDICT: PASS with NONE/narrow classification carried into Gate 6 (5yr fade,
30% MOS floor, not the generous wide-moat treatment).**

## GATE 3 — MANAGEMENT
**Material history: the company itself filed Chapter 11 in May 2020** (oil price
crash; ~$700M of debt became unserviceable) and emerged September 2020 with
**>$650M of debt eliminated**, noteholders converted to equity. This is not a
hypothetical commodity-cycle risk for this specific business — it already happened
once, at the company level. Post-emergence: **debt-free ($0 total debt)** as of
year-end 2025, disciplined dividend + buyback program, and the October 2025 UDC
sale (which appears well-timed — sold a lower-quality, commodity-adjacent services
business at a large gain) reads as sound capital allocation. No current integrity
flags found.
**VERDICT: PASS** — with the bankruptcy history weighed explicitly at Gate 5, not
treated as fully in the past.

## GATE 4 — FINANCIAL QUALITY (full formula, major caveat)
FY2025: **total** net income $98.292M includes a **$56.738M discontinued-operations
gain** (UDC sale, $106.5M taxable gain, largely offset by $104.5M of NOLs).
**Continuing operations (the E&P business alone, post-divestiture) net income =
$41.554M** — this is the correct OE anchor, not the headline $98.3M.
D&A/depletion and precise maintenance-vs-growth capex split were **not obtained
from primary filings this session** (9-month 2025 capex was $19.7M, up sharply
from $7.0M in 9M 2024 — consistent with "high-return drilling inventory" pursuit,
i.e. likely growth-weighted, not pure maintenance — flagged, not resolved).
**Growing?** Cannot be properly assessed — **this is the ONLY full year that exists
for the current, post-divestiture entity.** 2024's reported $47.2M total NI
included the now-divested drilling segment and is not comparable on a like-for-like
basis. **Range anchoring (P47) is not meaningfully possible yet** — there is
exactly one verified tier for the business as it exists today.
Fortress (Ruling 2, survival track — non-financial): **debt-free, cash $181.7M
(≈60% of market cap)** — trivially passes; no leverage risk exists.
**VERDICT: PASS, but flagged as resting on a single data point for the current
entity — the weakest data foundation of any company run in this project to date.**

## GATE 5 — INVERSION
1. **Moat destruction** — n/a in the traditional sense (no moat to destroy); the
   real risk is **commodity price collapse**, which is not hypothetical — it
   already forced this company through Chapter 11 once. Scored **[DS]** —
   addressed structurally via the zero-debt balance sheet (the direct, disclosed
   lesson from 2020) and a hedging program covering ~60% of gas production, not
   by any pricing-power moat.
2. **Management failure/key person** — no single dependency; post-emergence board.
   **[DS]**.
3. **Balance-sheet stress** — already stress-tested by history and passed
   (survived Chapter 11, emerged debt-free). **[D]** — the strongest possible
   form of "addressed," since it is not hypothetical.
4. **Commodity price cycle** (mandatory addition for E&P) — same as #1; the
   debt-free structure is the mitigant, not elimination of exposure. **[DS]**.

No [U]. **Thesis-breaking metric:** net debt turning positive (re-leveraging) OR
realized oil/gas price collapse sustained 2 consecutive quarters without
hedge protection. **Thesis-confirming metric:** continued debt-free operation with
stable-to-growing production.
**VERDICT: PASS** — but every score above rests on structural/historical mitigants
(zero debt), not on the business having any pricing power of its own.

## GATE 6 — VALUATION
**6-B Statute** (single-tier anchor $41.554M — see Gate 4 caveat): yield =
41.554/304.28 = **13.66%** vs. hurdle 6.10% (5.10% + 1% small-cap) → **PASSES by a
wide margin.** Statute-max mkt cap $681.2M (+124%).

**Book Two** (WACC 11.6% = 5.10 + 5.5 ERP + 1.0 small-cap; **NONE/narrow moat → 5yr
fade** per Gate 2; net cash $181.7M added to enterprise PV):
- BEAR (g1 −30%, reflecting a realistic commodity-down-year, though a true crash
  could be worse — see caveat below): equity IV **$283.1M** (−7%)
- BASE (g1 0%, flat production/price): equity IV **$607.2M** (+100%)
- BULL (g1 +15%): equity IV **$1,009.2M** (+232%)

Max buy (30% MOS, no/narrow-moat floor) = **$425.0M** (+40% vs current).

**⚠ Why this result should NOT be read as a clean buy signal:** the entire Base/
Bull case is built on ONE year of continuing-operations earnings for a commodity
business with no pricing power and a bankruptcy in its own history. The BEAR case
here (−30%) is a modeling choice, not a demonstrated floor — 2020's actual
commodity collapse was more severe than that. **A single strong year, discounted
at a rate that doesn't fully price commodity-cycle risk, will always look
statute-cheap for an E&P company between cycles.** This is closer to a
Graham-style statistical bargain (net cash ≈ 60% of market cap; the balance sheet
itself is much of the margin of safety) than a Buffett/Munger "wonderful business"
conviction case [E1-15/E2-16 distinction].

**Scream Test:** Statute PASS + Book Two PASS on Base/Bull, marginal FAIL on Bear
→ **verdicts do not cleanly agree** — closer to a whisper than a scream, given the
single-year data foundation.

**VERDICT: STARTER SIZE AT MOST, NOT A FULL-CONVICTION ENTRY.** The arithmetic
passes; the qualitative picture (no moat, one data point, commodity exposure) does
not support the kind of position size the framework reserves for genuine
conviction. Appropriate treatment: **watchlist for a second full year of
post-divestiture data**, or a small, explicitly asset-backed (net-cash-aware)
starter position sized accordingly — not a "buy a meaningful amount" call
[E3-14].

## GATE 7 — SIZING (if any position taken)
Given the single-year data limitation and commodity exposure, any position should
be sized as a small statistical/asset-backed allocation, not a core holding — Gate
7's "wider MOS justifies a larger position" logic is undercut here by the
uncertainty in the MOS calculation itself, not just its magnitude.
