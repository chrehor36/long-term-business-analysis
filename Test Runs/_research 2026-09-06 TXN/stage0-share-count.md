## 2. Single class of common stock — confirmed

**One class only.** Two independent confirmations from the filed documents:

**(a) The Section 12(b) registration table on the cover** lists exactly one line item:
"Common Stock, par value $1.00 / TXN / The Nasdaq Global Select Market." There is no Class A /
Class B, no tracking stock, and no second registered equity security.

**(b) The balance sheet, verbatim** (Consolidated Balance Sheets, Form 10-Q for the period ended
June 30, 2026, Stockholders' equity section):

> Preferred stock, $ 25 par value. Shares authorized – 10 ; **none issued** — $—
> Common stock, $ 1 par value. Shares authorized – 2,400 ; shares issued – 1,741 — $1,741
> Paid-in capital — 5,129
> Retained earnings — 53,161
> Treasury common stock at cost
>   Shares: June 30, 2026 – **828** ; December 31, 2025 – **834** — $(41,941) / $(42,130)
> Accumulated other comprehensive income (loss), net of taxes (AOCI) — (83)

(Figures in millions except par values.)

### Preferred stock: authorized but NONE OUTSTANDING

The filed line reads **"Shares authorized – 10 ; none issued"** and carries a carrying value of
`—`. TI has 10 million shares of $25-par preferred authorized and **zero issued or outstanding**.
There is no preferred dividend, no preferred liquidation preference, and no preferred conversion
overhang in the capital structure.

### Arithmetic tie-out (cover vs. balance sheet)

| | Shares |
|---|---|
| Common shares issued, 2026-06-30 (balance sheet, millions) | 1,741 |
| Less treasury shares, 2026-06-30 (balance sheet, millions) | (828) |
| = Shares outstanding, 2026-06-30 | **913** |
| Cover-page count as of 2026-07-15 | **913,247,686** |

The two agree to the balance sheet's rounding. **Stage 0 clears.** The cover count is the number
to use; the two-week gap between the balance-sheet date and the cover date is immaterial at this
share count.

### Cross-check against the FY2025 10-K balance sheet

FY2025 10-K (accession `0000097476-26-000059`, period 2025-12-31): common shares issued 1,741
million, treasury 834 million → 907 million outstanding at 2025-12-31. Shares outstanding have
**risen** by ~6 million over H1 2026 (net issuance from option exercises and RSU vesting exceeded
repurchases in the period).

## 3. Capital stock description — 10-K Item 5 and the equity footnote

The FY2025 10-K's Item 5 heading is:

> ITEM 5. Market for Registrant's common equity, related stockholder matters and issuer
> purchases of equity securities

and its opening sentence:

> TI common stock is quoted on The Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker symbol TXN.
> At December 31, 2025, we had **10,238 stockholders of record**.

Note the direction of that number: stockholders of record have fallen every year on the filed
record — 25,107 (2008) → 17,213 (2013) → 12,624 (2020) → 11,261 (2023) → 10,729 (2024) →
**10,238 (2025)**. That is street-name migration, not a change in the shareholder base's size.

No provision for a second class, no poison pill disclosed in the FY2025 10-K equity note, and no
convertible security disclosed in the capital structure. The only dilution sources named in the
equity footnote are stock options, RSUs, the ESPP, and director deferred stock — all settled out
of treasury:

> Our practice is to issue shares of common stock from treasury shares upon exercise of stock
> options, distribution of director deferred compensation and vesting of RSUs.

## 5. One flag raised at Stage 0

The Q2 2026 income statement carries a new line, **"Acquisition charges"** ($17M in Q2 2026,
$34M in H1 2026, nil in the prior-year periods). Tied to the FY2025 10-K subsequent-event
disclosure:

> As announced on February 4, 2026, we have entered into a definitive agreement to acquire
> Silicon Labs for $231.00 per share in an all-cash transaction, representing a total enterprise
> value of approximately $7.5 billion. ... We expect to fund the transaction with a combination
> of cash on hand and **debt financing to be arranged prior to closing.**

Expected to close in the first half of 2027. This is a $7.5 billion debt-funded acquisition
landing on a balance sheet that already carries $12.9 billion of long-term debt (2026-06-30) and
is already returning more cash than it generates. It must be carried forward into Q4 (survival)
and Q5 (what it is worth), not treated as a footnote.
