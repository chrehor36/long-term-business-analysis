Regarding the Release of Nintendo Switch 2 
 
Nintendo Co., Ltd. (HQ: Kyoto, Japan, President and Representative Director: Shuntaro Furukawa; hereinafter “Nintendo”) 
hereby announces that it has decided today to release the new game system, Nintendo Switch 2 (hereinafter “the product”) as below.  
 
1. 
About the product 
Nintendo Switch 2 is the successor to Nintendo Switch. It has a larger screen, new Joy-Con controllers with magnetic connectors, 
powerful processing speed, and graphical performance which enables new gameplay. 
Nintendo Switch 2 Japanese-Language System (Japan Only) will be released on June 5, 2025 at a manufacturer’s suggested retail 
price of 49,980 yen (including tax). For more information on the product, please visit the Nintendo website at  
< https://www.nintendo.co.jp/corporate/release/en/2025/250402.html >. 
 
 
Nintendo Switch 2 
 
2. 
Impact of the product on consolidated net sales 
The projected impact of the product on Nintendo’s consolidated sales has not yet been determined. Nintendo plans to disclose the 
consolidated financial forecast for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2026, at the time of our financial results announcement for the 
fiscal year ended March 31, 2025 (scheduled for May 8, 2025). In the aforementioned announcement, Nintendo will disclose the 
consolidated sales forecast for this fiscal year, which will include the impact of sales of the product.

Expenses specifically incurred to commercialize the product

Future Business Projection 
 The consolidated financial forecast for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2026 is scheduled to be disclosed at the financial results
