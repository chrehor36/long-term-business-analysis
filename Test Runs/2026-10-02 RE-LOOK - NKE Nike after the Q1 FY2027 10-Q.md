## 2. THE 2026-09-02 RUN'S OWN Q6 METRICS, CHECKED ONE BY ONE

All figures below are from the 10-Q, accession `0000320187-26-000193`, three months ended August 31, 2026
against the same quarter of 2025. Currency-neutral changes are the registrant's own words; reported changes
are arithmetic on the segment table.

### Thesis-confirming metric: Greater China revenue, currency-neutral

> *"Greater China revenues decreased 26% on a currency-neutral basis. Wholesale revenues decreased 31%. NIKE
> Direct revenues decreased 18% due to declines in digital sales of 28% and declines in store sales of 14%.
> • Footwear revenues decreased 26% on a currency-neutral basis. Unit sales of footwear decreased 26%, while
> ASP per pair was flat as lower discounts were offset primarily by channel mix."*

| Greater China | Q1 FY2026 | Q1 FY2027 | change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenues, reported ($M) | 1,512 | 1,180 | -22.0% |
| Footwear revenues ($M) | 1,109 | 866 | -21.9% |
| EBIT ($M) | 377 | 248 | -34.2% |

**Reading:** the 2026-09-02 run recorded a 13% currency-neutral fall for the year. The first quarter of
the next year is twice as steep. **This confirms the OUT.** The run's confirming test also asks whether
adidas, ANTA and Li Ning are growing in the same period. **Not refreshed here:** their latest filed
periods end before August 2026, so there is no same-quarter competitor row yet. Nike's own number is the
only new evidence, and it moves one way.

### Thesis-breaking metric: footwear ASP rising on flat or growing units, two consecutive fiscal years, with Greater China at or above zero

> *"NIKE Brand footwear revenues ... decreased 6%. Unit sales of footwear decreased 5%, while lower average
> selling price ("ASP") per pair reduced footwear revenues by approximately 1 percentage point. Lower ASP
> per pair was primarily due to channel mix and higher discounts, partially offset by strategic pricing."*

**Not met on any limb.** Units fell, ASP fell, the stated cause includes *"higher discounts"*, and Greater
China is down 26%. By construction the test needs two fiscal years; one quarter can only move toward it or
away from it, and this quarter moved away. The gross-margin bridge says the same in the registrant's words:
*"Lower NIKE Brand ASP, decreasing gross margin approximately 30 basis points, primarily due to higher
discounts and channel mix."* The [E2-44] pricing limb and the [E4-37] metric the run rested on are
unchanged in direction.

**One region against the grain, recorded because a re-look that reports only confirming evidence is not a
re-look [E4-18]:** North America revenue rose 2% currency-neutral, footwear units up 2%, EBIT from $1,134M
to $1,170M (+3.2%), with *"Gross margin expansion of 110 basis points primarily due to higher ASP"* in that
segment. The 2026-09-02 run already said *"North America says it may"* recover. It still says so. It is one
region for one quarter, and footwear ASP there was still *"lower ... primarily due to channel mix and higher
discounts."* It does not reach the breaker.

### Secondary breaker: demand creation below 8.5% of revenue while revenue grows

| TOTAL NIKE, INC. revenues ($M) | 11,720 | 11,213 |

**Not met, and moving the other way:** revenue fell and the cost of holding the brand rose, the [E4-04]
test running in the direction the run recorded as *"cost rising."*
