### The finding
- **[E3-03] criterion (2) fails in the registrant's own words, every 10-K FY2014-FY2026**: *"Our customers are
  accustomed to purchasing from multiple suppliers and channels concurrently"*; *"Since switching costs are very low,
  customers can make supplier and channel changes very quickly"*; *"There are few barriers to market entry."* **US
  Foods and Chefs' Warehouse file the same sentence**: *"The cost of switching distributors is very low, as are the
  barriers to entry into the U.S. foodservice distribution industry."*
- **The customers acted on it.** Sysco's U.S. local cases (the class it earns most on) trailed Performance Food Group's
  organic independent cases in all eight fiscal years FY2019-FY2026 (-1.4% against +4.6% in FY2025; +1.7% against +5.9%
  in FY2026) and US Foods' independent cases in seven of eight pairs.
- **Pricing is cost plus a mark-up**, in its own risk factor, with *"our limited ability to increase prices in the
  current, highly competitive environment"*. Operating margin 5.08-5.31% in FY2009-FY2010, 3.66% in FY2026.
- **The row, the part that cuts for Sysco and the part that answers it.** Sysco has out-earned US Foods on operating
  margin every year 2016-2025, but the gap narrowed from 1.84 points to 0.62-0.76, the 15-18 point lead on tangible
  returns (2016-2019) is gone (US Foods 41.5% in CY2025 against Sysco's 37.7-39.8%), and Sysco's operating costs per
  sales dollar (14.84%) are **higher** than US Foods' (14.37%): its edge is a point of gross margin from local mix,
  which is the class it has been losing. Not [E3-43]'s low-cost operator; not [E2-58]'s wide and sustainable advantage.
- **The FTC's 2015 block of the US Foods merger** was weighed as evidence for a franchise and read the other way: it
  preserved competition between the only two national networks, which is evidence that each is the other's close
  substitute for national customers (SYGMA, the chain business, earned 1.1% in FY2026).

- **What it does**: Jetro's audited 2025 cash after capital spending is about $1,266M unlevered and after tax, about

- **The target is one of the substitutes the registrant names** (*"club stores; cash and carry stores"*), and US Foods
  runs its own cash-and-carry stores (CHEF'STORE, formerly Smart Foodservice).
