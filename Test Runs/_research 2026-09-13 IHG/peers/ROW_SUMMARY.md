## Basis breaks a reader must carry

- WH: system size, RevPAR and royalty rate re-based in Q2 2025 to exclude Super 8 China; 2019-2020 figures are on the old basis.
- CHH: revenue lines reclassified in K25 (2023-2025) - not comparable line-for-line to K21 (2019-2021); RevPAR and royalty rate are U.S./domestic only; figures filed in $000.
- MAR: 2020 OCF includes $920M co-brand card prepayment; D&A add-back re-presented in K24 for 2022-2023.
- HLT: 2020 OCF includes $1.0bn Honors points pre-sale.
- H: no operating income subtotal; large acquisitions (ALG 2021 $2,916M; Playa 2025 $1,274M) and real-estate sale proceeds; RevPAR 2024/2025 on comparable hotels excluding all-inclusive.
- WH, CHH: 2019 OCF includes WH's (195) La Quinta tax payment; CHH 2019 operating income re-presented (318,642 in K19 vs 318,538 in K21, $000).
