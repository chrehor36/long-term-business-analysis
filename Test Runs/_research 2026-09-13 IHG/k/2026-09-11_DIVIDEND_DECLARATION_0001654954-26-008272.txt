InterContinental Hotels Group PLC (IHG) 
Interim Dividend - Pence Sterling Amount Payable 
On 11 August 2026, IHG's Board announced an interim dividend
for 2026 of 64.5 cents per share and that the corresponding pence
sterling amount payable would be announced on 11 September 2026.
This 
 has been determined
with reference to the average of the market exchange rates on the
three working days commencing 8 September 2026, using the WMR
closing mid-point spot rate as at 4:00pm (London time), as
published in the Financial Times, resulting in an applicable
exchange rate of £1:US$1.3547. Accordingly, the amount payable
will be 47.6 pence per ordinary share. The interim dividend will be
paid on 1 October 2026. 
Registered shareholders are reminded that they can elect to receive
their dividend payments in US Dollars (USD) instead of British
Pounds (GBP). Elections can be made by completing the Currency Form
at www.shareview.info/products/directdividends .
Alternatively, registered shareholders can contact the Company's
Registrar, Equiniti, by telephone on +44 (0) 371 384 2132 to
request a Currency Form. For shares held in CREST, an election for
USD will be permitted using the CREST dividend election process.
CREST participants should ensure a USD CREST Memorandum Account has
been enabled.
