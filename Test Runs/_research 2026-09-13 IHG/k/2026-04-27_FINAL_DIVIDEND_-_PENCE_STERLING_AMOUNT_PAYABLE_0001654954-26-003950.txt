InterContinental Hotels Group PLC ("IHG") 
Final Dividend - Pence Sterling Amount Payable 
On 17 February 2026, the Board of IHG announced a final dividend
for 2025 of 125.9 cents per share and that the corresponding amount
in pence sterling per share would be announced on 27 April 2026.
Subject to shareholder approval at IHG's Annual General
Meeting on 7 May 2026, the final dividend will be paid on 14 May
2026 to shareholders on the register at the close of business on 10
April 2026. 
 The pence sterling amount payable in respect of
the final dividend has been determined with reference to the
average of the market exchange rates on the three working days
commencing 22 April 2026, using the WMR closing mid-point spot rate
as at 4:00pm (London time), as published in the Financial Times,
resulting in an applicable exchange rate of £1:US$1.3504 . 
Accordingly, the pence sterling amount payable to shareholders in
relation to the final dividend will be 93.2 pence per ordinary
share.

About IHG Hotels & Resorts: 
IHG Hotels & Resorts (tickers: LON:IHG for Ordinary
Shares; NYSE:IHG for ADRs) is a global hospitality company, with a
purpose to provide True Hospitality for Good. 
 With a
family of 21 hotel brands and IHG One
Rewards , one of the world's largest hotel loyalty programmes
with over 160 million members, IHG has more than one million rooms
across 6,963 open hotels in over 100 countries, and a development
pipeline of a further 2,300 properties.
