## STEP 0 — RATE REFRESH
US 30-yr **5.10%**. Mkt cap $177.34B — no size premium.

## GATE 1 — CIRCLE OF COMPETENCE
Wireless and wireline telecommunications carrier. Unit economics: network
infrastructure (enormous fixed cost) monetized via subscription service
revenue, minus network operating/content/device-subsidy costs.
**VERDICT: PASS.**

## GATE 2 — MOAT
Franchise test [E3-03], from Verizon's own 10-K: **"The telecommunications
industry is highly competitive. The rapid development of new technologies,
services and products has eliminated many of the distinctions among
wireless, cable, internet and traditional telephone services and brought
new competitors to our markets. We expect competition to remain intense as
traditional and non-traditional participants seek increased market
share."**
- **No close substitute**: **mixed**. Real, enormous infrastructure/
  spectrum-license barriers exist against genuinely NEW entrants (no one
  is building a fifth nationwide wireless network from scratch) — but the
  company's own words admit the boundary between wireless, cable, and
  internet providers has collapsed, meaning EXISTING large players
  (AT&T, T-Mobile, and cable companies via wireless resale/MVNO and fixed-
  wireless) compete directly for the same subscribers. This is an
  oligopoly-with-real-entry-barriers structure, not a pricing-power
  franchise — years of promotional wireless price wars (general industry
  knowledge, consistent with Verizon's own "intense" competition language)
  have compressed industry-wide pricing power.
**Class: NARROW** (infrastructure/spectrum barriers against new entry,
weak pricing power among existing large rivals), **Direction: STABLE-to-
NARROWING** given the explicitly named wireless/cable/internet convergence.
**VERDICT: PASS with NARROW/NARROWING classification.**
