### THE SKIP REASON, FROM THE FILED RECORD
- **Four readings tested; three confirmed, one refuted, and a fifth found.** (a) per-class cover plus a stale **pre-IPO** count (56,508,441 of 2021-09-30);
  (b) the **1-for-20 split** of 2024-09-04, mixed vintages in companyfacts' weighted averages, and the house rule's split factor turning the stale count into a
  quarter of the true one; (c) the **lower yield bound** trips on the correct count as well (-237% to -299%); (d) no shell year; **(e) the numerator was a sold
  business**: the triage divided the footwear business's FY2021-25 losses, from 10-K facts not yet recast, by the market value of the company that had sold it.
- **A NEW DEFECT CLASS for the tooling: the completed disposition.** Nothing in the screens reads a sale of the whole operating business between annual reports:
  the continuing/discontinued split reaches companyfacts only as half-year facts, and `ocf_continuing()` acts only on annual facts, so every registrant that sells
  its business and keeps its listing is priced on the sold business until its next 10-K. **Not fixed here** (the run does not change tooling); recorded for the
  operator. The 8-K Item 2.01 route was already tested and rejected as a signal (RESUME STATE item 5).
- **The house split rule has a blind spot beside it**: it corrects a count measured before a split, but not one measured before an IPO. Any backtest anchor that
  fell back to a pre-IPO balance-sheet count would be understated by the IPO's shares and the converted preferred, and then scaled by any later split. Untested
  whether any panel did.
