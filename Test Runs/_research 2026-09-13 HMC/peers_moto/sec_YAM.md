## 2. Yamaha Motor Co., Ltd. (TSE 7272) - NOT an SEC filer; fiscal year = calendar year (Dec 31)

**Rung used:** company IR site, English (https://global.yamaha-motor.com/ir/library/). Yamaha Motor is not an SEC
filer. The English "Financial Report" is the company's translation of its Kessan Tanshin; its cover states: "This
document has been translated from the Japanese original, Kessan Tanshin (Flash Report), for reference purposes only. In
the event of any discrepancy between this translated document and the Japanese original, the original shall prevail."
The Japanese securities report (Yuho) was not retrieved. Consolidated figures throughout. JPY.

**Accounting basis changes (flagged, material to comparability):**
1. FY2021-FY2023 reports are Japanese GAAP ("(Japanese GAAP)" on cover); segment row label "Segment income".
2. Yamaha adopted IFRS from FY2024 (Reference FY2024: "The Company has adopted International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) from the fiscal year of 2024."); FY2023 restated to IFRS in the FY2024 report; row label "Segment profit (loss)", "Revenue".
3. From FY2025, ATVs/ROVs moved out of Land mobility into a new "Outdoor land vehicle" segment; FY2024 restated in the FY2025 report. FY2025 report: "all-terrain vehicles and recreational off-highway vehicles previously included in "Land mobility," and golf cars previously included in "Others," are now included in "Outdoor land vehicle.""

**Brief check:** there is no audited segment called "Motorcycle". The reportable segment is "Land mobility", which FY2025 describes as "Motorcycles, intermediate parts for products, knockdown parts for overseas production, electrically power assisted bicycles, electrically power assisted bicycle drive units (e-Kit), electrically powered wheelchairs, automobile engines, and automobile components" (and, through FY2024, ATVs/ROVs/snowmobiles). A "Motorcycle" business line (revenue and operating income) is disclosed only in the earnings PRESENTATIONS (not the segment note).

### E. Pricing statements (verbatim)
- YAM-FR-22 (FY2022, motorcycles): "Operating income increased, despite soaring production costs, such as raw material prices and logistics costs, due to the implementation of price pass-on efforts and the positive effect of yen depreciation."
- YAM-FR-23 (FY2023): "Operating income increased mainly due to a growth in unit sales, along with price pass-on efforts and positive effect of the yen's depreciation."
- YAM-PR-23 slide 16: "2023 Results: Demand was steady and unit shipments increased thanks to improved procurement of semiconductors. Passing on prices led to higher sales and profits."
- YAM-FR-24 (FY2024): "Revenue increased due to higher unit sales in Brazil and India and also higher unit price per unit. Operating profit was flat year on year due to an increase in selling, general and administrative expenses"
- YAM-FR-25 (FY2025): "As a result, total unit sales remained largely flat year on year, and revenue was also flat year on year. However, operating profit decreased due to higher procurement costs, increased SG&A expenses, including R&D and personnel expenses, and the impact of U.S. tariffs."
