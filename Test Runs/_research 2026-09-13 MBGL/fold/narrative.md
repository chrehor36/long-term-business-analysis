### WHY UNKNOWABLE AND NOT IN OR OUT
- **For IN:** CARFAX at 43-45% before acquired amortization, capex ~1% of revenue, unleveraged net tangible assets ~$68M; a data estate grown
  from 17bn to 38bn records since FY2016; company-compiled revenue growth near 10% a year through a decade of flat vehicle sales; a 2013 dealer
  antitrust complaint that shows customers once believed there was no close substitute.
- **For OUT:** two wholesale registrants offer AutoCheck beside CARFAX; Listings (14%) is a follower on three registrants' lists; B2B (35%) is
  no franchise on its own competitor list and falling margin.
- **What decided it:** the missing peer is the ONLY direct competitor in the product that carries the claim (unlike CTAS, TSCO and EFX, where a
  private peer was one of several and a filed unit series carried the verdict), and **the unit disclosures are a frozen "more than 40,000" dealer
  floor and a monthly report-view figure that fell from "more than 31 million" (TTM Sep-2025) to "more than 28 million" (TTM Dec-2025)** while
  growth became "primarily driven by price increases". An IN needed three narrowing assumptions; an OUT needed the opposite three. The deciding
  record - a defined CARFAX unit and price/volume series over two to three standalone years - does not exist yet.

### REFUTED OR NARROWED PRIORS
- **"A spin-off with a short record is the HHH shape"**: refuted for the business, kept for the company. HHH's forward entity was not its filed
  entity; MBGL's business is the filed perimeter with a new balance sheet and head office. Q1 is IN; the too-little-history feature applies only
  to standalone costs, which Q4 bridges from the company's own estimates.
- **"Registrant-named substitutes fail criterion (2)" (the ACVA rule)**: narrowed. It held at ACVA because the returns agreed with the list;
  at MBGL they do not, and the gate cannot be closed OUT on the list alone.
- **"Data businesses are capital-light, so the D&A end is fine"**: confirmed here, with the maintenance cost located in operating expense
  (data acquisition and advertising), which is why (c) is small.
