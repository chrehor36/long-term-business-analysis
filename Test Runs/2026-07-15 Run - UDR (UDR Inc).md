this batch. **VERDICT: PASS.**

**Class: NARROW**, similar to AVB/EQR — diversified enough that no single
regulatory regime dominates the risk picture, unlike ESS's concentrated

**VERDICT: PASS with NARROW classification.**

governance difference to weigh, not a disqualifier on its own.

## GATE 5 — INVERSION
1. **Moat destruction** — diversified geography helps here more than for
   ESS. Scored **[D]**.
2. **Management/key-person** — combined Chairman/CEO with long tenure is a
   genuine key-person and governance-structure dependency simultaneously.
   Scored **[P]**.
3. **Balance-sheet stress** — 3.23:1 assets/equity is the highest in the
   batch; still passes the mechanical ceiling but deserves a **[P]** rather
   than a clean [D] given the relative (not absolute) elevation.
4. **Unexplained FY2024 NI collapse** — a genuine open question this run
   did not resolve. Scored **[U]** — an unresolved mandatory inversion.

**One [U] present.** Per the protocol, an unresolved mandatory inversion
requires a written justification to proceed rather than an automatic stop —
the justification here: the lowest-tier anchor already reflects this
weak year mechanically (it's the anchor, not an outlier being excluded), so
the valuation gate is not relying on an assumption that the 2024 event won't
recur; if anything, using it as the anchor is the conservative choice. This
is judged sufficient to proceed to Gate 6, but the underlying cause remains
a genuine open research item.
**Thesis-breaking metric:** a repeat of a similar-magnitude NI event without
a disclosed one-time cause, or assets/equity rising materially above 3.2:1.
**VERDICT: PASS, with a documented [U] and written justification** — the
softest Gate 5 clearance of any REIT in this batch.
