  mat, and turns them into two things: **big rolls of rubber and plastic sheet that go on the flat

  very often together and with a five-to-forty-year warranty attached, to a roofing contractor who

  **66% of cost of goods sold** — at a **35.7% gross margin**, spends 14.8 points of revenue on

  every revenue dollar**. Interest takes 1.0 point net and tax 4.1, leaving **14.8 cents of income

- **The scarce input this business controls: a place in a four-firm oligopoly for a product that is
  specified, warranted and installed by a trained crew — and, underneath it, the physical fact that
