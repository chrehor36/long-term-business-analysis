**CONCLUSION: Teledyne discloses no traffic/ITS revenue, no traffic margin, and no traffic
asset base.** Teledyne "files", but it is **unreadable as a competitor to AATC**: the
relevant business is buried at well under 1% of a $6.1bn revenue base with zero disclosure.
Counting TDY as a readable competitor row would be false precision.
