### WHY Q2 CLOSED, ON SNOWFLAKE'S OWN RECORD (the PLTR run's Snowflake figures were not imported)
- **The company says its lock-in is falling, by its own choice.** FY2026 10-K Item 1A: open formats like Apache Iceberg mean *"there is less customer 'lock in'
  when our products are used in external environments"*, and *"Our support of open data formats may also reduce switching costs between us and our competitors."*
- **Its suppliers are its largest competitors.** *"We currently offer our platform on the public clouds provided by AWS, Azure, and GCP, which are also some of our
  primary competitors. Currently, a substantial majority of our business is run on the AWS public cloud"*; costs and gross margins depend on *"the prices we are able
  to negotiate with these public cloud providers"*. Their segment margins are 24-41%.
- **Price is a competitive factor and efficiency is passed on.** *"We compete based on various factors, including price"*; it may not *"offer as many discounts or free
  services as our competitors"*; and every 10-K since FY2022 says efficiency gains let customers *"consume fewer compute, storage, and data transfer resources to
  accomplish the same workloads"* [E3-62].
- **No return on capital to demonstrate [E3-43, E3-46]**: operating loss every year since inception; operating margin before stock pay **2.6%, 0.6%, 3.5%
  (FY2024-26)**, the lowest of six software filers in the row (MongoDB 13-17%, Elastic 9-15%, Datadog 21-23%, Teradata 17-19%, Palantir 27-47%), while its stock pay
  (34-42% of revenue) is the highest.
- **The full-strength case, recorded first**: realised capacity prices rose **8% (FY2021), 4% (FY2022) and 3% (FY2024)** by discount discipline and dearer editions;
  retention 125-126%; RPO $9.8bn; product revenue +37% in Q2 FY2027; Teradata, the shrinking legacy incumbent, names Snowflake; growth needs no balance-sheet capital
  because customers prepay. **No list-price increase was found in 23 documents swept.** Retention has fallen from 178% (FY2022) to 125%, and the company forecasts
  further decline. **[E4-04]** engaged in its own words, including a new FY2026 risk: *"Frontier AI model providers may seek to vertically integrate their offerings
  by expanding into the data storage and management layers"*.
- **Databricks, the named private rival, is a Form D filer**: $5.0bn of equity sold, filed 2026-08-27, revenue *"Decline to Disclose"*. The one filed fact about it
  is that it is funded; its margins are not on any rung of the ladder, and the gate does not close on that gap.
