===== 2001
 - Engine operating profit decreased $319 million from 2000. The decline was primarily due to the lower price realization, impact of manufacturing inefficiencies related to sharp swings in production levels and the nonrecurring charges ($55 million)
 - Profit of $1.05 billion was $107 million more than 1999. Operating profit improved due primarily to higher physical volume, improved price realization (excluding currency) and manufacturing efficiencies
 - Engine sales were $7.06 billion, an increase of $202 million or 3 percent from 1999. The increase was due to a 2 percent increase in physical sales volume and higher price realization
