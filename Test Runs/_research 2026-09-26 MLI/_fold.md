### The finding
- **[E3-03] criterion (2) fails in the registrant's own words**: *"Prices for our products are affected by overall supply
  and demand in the market for our products and for our competitors' products"*; *"we may be unable to control the
  timing and extent of pricing changes"*; plastics *"an increasing share of consumption"*; *"not materially dependent on
  patents, trademarks, licenses, franchises, or concessions held"*. A former rival, Wolverine Tube, called the same line
  *"commodity-type plumbing and refrigeration tube products"* in its 2008 10-K, the year it lost $97.0M.
- **The pounds say the same.** Piping Systems core unit volume fell in each of 2022-2025, about a fifth in all, while its
  margin rose; the registrant has said every year since FY2015 that its pricing *"results in lost sales opportunities and
  lower volume"*. [E2-44]'s first characteristic is price raised without losing volume; this is the reverse.
- **Where the step came from.** Piping margin 6-10% FY2008-FY2020, 18.7-28.5% FY2021-FY2025. The named domestic tube
  competitors in Item 1 went from five (FY2007-FY2012) to three to two (FY2020 on); antidumping orders on Chinese and
  Mexican tube from 2010, Mueller among the petitioners ([E2-59]); Encore Wire, a commodity copper-wire maker, stepped in
  the same two years; Mueller's own brass rod, where a larger rival still competes, did not. [E3-43]: *"Tightness in
  supply usually does not last long."*
