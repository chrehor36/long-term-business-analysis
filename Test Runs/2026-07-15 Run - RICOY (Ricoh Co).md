## GATE 1 — CIRCLE OF COMPETENCE

transitioning toward digital services/workplace solutions as the legacy

secular-decline dynamic already flagged for Dai Nippon Printing's

structurally fallen with digitization — a real, ongoing, disclosed
industry-wide trend); no close substitute — **weak**, a commodity, mature
hardware category with several credible competitors (Canon, Xerox, HP,
Konica Minolta); unregulated — largely yes. **Class: NONE** — the harshest
moat classification of any company in this Japan batch, consistent with
the legacy hardware business facing genuine secular decline, similar to the
"legacy printing" component already flagged (more favorably, given
diversification) at Dai Nippon Printing.
**VERDICT: PASS with NONE classification** — an honest, not generous, read
given the secular pressure on the core business.
