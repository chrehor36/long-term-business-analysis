Source notes for this folder
=============================

Buffett Partnership Ltd. letters to limited partners, 1957-1970.

Downloaded 2026-07-13 as a single compiled PDF from the Ivey Business School
archive (https://www.ivey.uwo.ca/media/2975913/buffett-partnership-letters.pdf)
- the same source the Framework document's Appendix A cites - then converted
with pdftotext and split into one file per letter.

Naming: dated letters are "{YYYY-MM-DD} Partnership Letter.txt" (the date is
the letter's own dateline; note January letters report on the PRIOR year -
e.g., 1962-01-24 is the 1961 annual letter). The earliest letters (1957-1960),
which the compilation groups under year headings without full datelines, are
"{YYYY} Partnership Letter.txt"; "1960 Partnership Letter (2).txt" is the
second letter in the compilation's 1960 section.

Provenance caveat: this is a third-party compilation of historical letters,
not an official Berkshire/Buffett publication. Text fidelity is good but not
authoritative; treat as personal-study copies and flag any OCR-suspect
passage rather than smoothing it (Prime Rule 1).

29 letters, ~74,000 words. Covers Phase 2 era E1 of the Framework v3.0
rebuild (see CLAUDE.md in the BRK root).
