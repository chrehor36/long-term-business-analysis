## The discipline (Graham's documented practice)
- **Sell at the gain target below, or +50%, whichever comes first.**
- **Time stop: out by ~July 2028 (2 years) regardless of price** — the
  business deteriorates while you hold; the appraisal shrinks toward the
  price if the re-rating doesn't come fast.
- **Basket logic**: Graham held 30+ at once so no single collapse
  mattered; holding 1-3 means each name's "broken if" line replaces his
  diversification — a triggered line is an immediate exit, not a review.
- Buffett's verdict on the method [1989 letter, "time is the friend of
  the wonderful business, the enemy of the mediocre"] is the reason for
  the clock.
