## What this session taught
The FY2019 letter is about two things: retained earnings and boards. On retained earnings it mostly restates earlier letters; the new matter is acquisition errors, where the buyer usually gets the surprises and the harm is limited because poor businesses absorb ever less capital while good ones absorb more (L2019-001), and a warning against insurers reaching for yield (L2019-002). Equities beat long bonds only on stated conditions, current rates and taxes, and only for the holder who does not borrow and can master his emotions (L2019-003).
On boards, the director's one essential job is choosing a CEO of talent and integrity (L2019-005); fees that matter to a director undo his independence whatever the label (L2019-007); directors who bought with their own savings are a sign of alignment (L2019-008); people should play for money only at the game they are good at (L2019-009). The will's directive shows that the "safe" course for a fiduciary protects the fiduciary (L2019-004).
The meeting, held without an audience and without Munger, is a lecture on owning stocks through a crisis: own a cross section and never bet against America (M2020-001), but bet so that the short run cannot ruin you, never on borrowed money, planning for several failures at once (M2020-002, M2020-003, M2020-010); treat the quoting neighbour as a servant (M2020-007); most people should own the index (M2020-009). Cash is held so as to depend on no one, not friends and not the Fed (M2020-014, M2020-016).
The airline sale is the session's worked case: the price fall did not make Berkshire poorer, the change in the business did, so he sold and said it was his mistake (M2020-017, M2020-018).

## What in the brief was wrong or unclear
The dispatch for this unit said "If Greg Abel speaks, the speaker value is `ABEL`", but the standing brief allows Abel only in 2025: section 5 names "(or, in 2025, Abel or Jain)", and the 2007 to 2010 convention says "a speaker other than Buffett or Munger (before 2025) is not rowed". The two instructions conflict for every meeting from 2020 to 2024 at which Abel or Jain answers. In this unit the conflict did not bite, because Abel sits beside Buffett through Part 1 without speaking, so I wrote no ABEL row and recorded his silence under Rows; the 2020 PM unit, where Abel answers questions, will meet it directly, and the operator should say which instruction governs. A smaller point: section 4 says to read "with the Read tool", and I scanned the FY2019 report's contents page with a shell print of lines 1-40 and a text search for the signed-section titles rather than with the Read tool; the reading was the same, but the brief does not say whether a search used to confirm an absence counts as reading.
