## What this session taught
The afternoon returns several times to what cannot be known and what to do about it. Financial companies are harder to analyze than a brick or candy business because the current condition itself, not only the future, may be unknowable: loss reserves, loans and derivative marks move earnings on small changes in assumptions (M2005-067 to M2005-070), marks mean something only where bids can be hit (M2005-113), and summed marks on a zero-sum book come out positive (M2005-112). Against reserve discounting, the argument is the managers' known optimism, worst near retirement or option expiry (M2005-111).
The answer to what cannot be known is exclusion: the too-hard pile, no premium for difficulty, and a universe judged smaller rather than larger (M2005-099 to M2005-101); a market price not understood requires no position (M2005-105). Understanding is defined as knowing reasonably what the economics will be in five, ten or twenty years (M2005-089).
On owning, Berkshire's system is little oversight made safe by care in what is admitted and honorable treatment of those left in charge (M2005-059 to M2005-062); disappointing businesses are kept, the policy is told in advance, and how the people behave after the sale counts beside returns (M2005-093 to M2005-095). Between buying and selling there is a zone, widened by size and taxes (M2005-071).
On boards, independence is a state of mind, a director needing the fee is an insider, and a fit director is one willing to leave at any time (M2005-086, M2005-090 to M2005-092); the hard case is a chief executive a notch above mediocre (M2005-085).
On markets, statements about the whole market are possible only at extremes, the long Treasury rate is the yardstick behind every decision, and expectations above 6 or 7 percent are a mistake (M2005-074 to M2005-076, M2005-104). Gold produces nothing (M2005-072, M2005-073); scorned things deserve more attention than championed ones (M2005-079).
The GM and Ford answer gives the clearest mistake: commitments signed because they carried no accounting cost are still real (M2005-057).
