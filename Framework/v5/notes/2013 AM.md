## What this session taught
The FY2012 letter turns from what the business is to what to do with what it earns. Reinvestment comes first and widens the moat; managers fail at it when they start from the answer they want and work back, as Buffett did with textiles, "wishing ... it's poison in business" (L2012-011, L2012-012). Then acquisitions, then repurchases, and dividends last; the dividend section works the arithmetic: where a company earns well on retained net worth and sells above book, owners selling a slice each year get more cash and more capital, keep their own payout choice and pay tax only on the gain (L2012-013, L2012-014), but whatever the policy it must stay consistent (L2012-015).
On newspapers the letter and the meeting give a full case for a business in decline: news defined, primacy kept only in local news for a real community, giving away online what is sold in print as self-destruction, no cutting of content, and purchase only at a very low multiple of current earnings because earnings will fall, which the meeting calls an exception (L2012-006 to L2012-010, M2013-026).
On price, Buffett confesses twice: to bargain-hunting against Munger's wonderful-business rule, and to skipping extraordinary businesses that looked dear (L2012-004, M2013-008).
On survival, redundant liquidity costs return in 99 years of 100 (L2012-005), cash stays in Treasurys (M2013-013), and in panics capital plus willingness to say yes sets Berkshire apart (M2013-017, M2013-022).
The meeting's strongest thread is behavior as advantage: stay sane while others go crazy, shrink 80 percent when prices fail and pay no one to write business, resist the social proof of a bubble, refuse envy, and go where competition is thin (M2013-018, M2013-020, M2013-031 to M2013-035, M2013-037).
On managers and structure: culture carried by self-selection, decentralization that makes the count of subsidiaries irrelevant, bolt-ons and minority buy-outs as the best acquisitions, and full freedom with full responsibility for investment managers (M2013-003, M2013-010, M2013-011, M2013-023).
