## What this session taught
The afternoon is about owners, managers and the honesty of what a company says about itself. A repurchase is the fair way to pay the partner who wants out, provided the price favours those who stay (M2021-030), and Munger makes the motive the test (M2021-031); a payout policy the owners bought on is not changed by a change of tax law (M2021-032). The corporate tax is the government's Class AA share of the earnings, borne by owners except in a utility (M2021-035).
Management is the first risk factor and the one never listed (M2021-042); a board's work is the culture and the power to remove the wrong CEO, hardest when he is a nice person (M2021-050). A company's repeated story to analysts becomes a catechism no successor can contradict (M2021-051), and Munger adds that the speaker comes to believe it (M2021-052). Decentralization keeps size manageable (M2021-056) only with the right culture (M2021-057) and managers who love the business (M2021-058).
On risk: book a loss when you believe it has happened (M2021-037); some risks are too big to insure (M2021-036); where failure costs lives, prepare for the improbable with a margin of safety (M2021-044); admit not knowing and choose the course with a decent result whatever happens (M2021-048). Buffett restates the PCC error as a misjudged average earning power (M2021-039) and accepts Munger's charge that turnover is still too much (M2021-062).
