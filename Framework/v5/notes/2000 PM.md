## What this session taught
Understanding is restated as the predictability of a business's economics ten years out, not knowledge of its product (M2000-104), with a working test: would the industry's own leaders write down where ten of its companies will be (M2000-105); knowing a field will grow is not knowing who wins in it (M2000-126), and gains in efficiency can flow to society rather than to owners (M2000-107).
Options are costed at the expectable cost, about a third of the stock's value for a ten-year grant, as a retained interest in the upside (M2000-079, M2000-080, M2000-082); pay ratchets because consultants and proxy comparisons only move it up (M2000-091, M2000-109, M2000-110).
The castle and knight image joins the moat to the manager and asks what share of the castle the knight takes (M2000-108). Franchise strength is judged by what bad can happen (M2000-083), and a self-reinforcing franchise survives mismanagement (M2000-084).
Spending is split into a maintenance guess and the growth spending beyond it, disclosed to owners as a best guess (M2000-144), each dollar required to create more than a dollar (M2000-142), limited by what the organization can absorb (M2000-143).
On people: never buy an organization to change it (M2000-152); the test is whether managers tell themselves the truth (M2000-153); self-deception at the top becomes the norm and kills a financial firm (M2000-154); love of the business against love of the money remains the crucial, uncheckable test (M2000-098).
The cigar-butt purchase of Berkshire is narrated as a mistake with the lesson to start from a good business (M2000-116, M2000-117), and Munger adds that wrong decisions are survived by recognising them promptly (M2000-118).
Insurance: float is free to go anywhere only because capital stands behind it (M2000-132), the two questions are cheap money without ever endangering payment and then the best deployment (M2000-133), and reinsurance discipline is no volume goals, letting misevaluators have the business, and underlining evil (M2000-156, M2000-157, M2000-160).
