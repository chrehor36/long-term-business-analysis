## What this session taught
The afternoon is mostly about conduct and capital rather than valuation. On capital: buy once and avoid continuing reinvestment in inflation (M2015-049); do not lower standards because money is cheap, and decline debt the arithmetic seems to favour (M2015-077); repurchase only below intrinsic value, as a partner would buy out a partner (M2015-074); price a cyclical purchase on a bad year's earnings (M2015-079).
On judgment: understand a business 5 to 15 years out and know one's limits, compare every new idea with what is held, prefer the certain decent result to the hoped-for brilliant one (M2015-086, M2015-087); distrust paper synergies, break-up premiums and single-variable comparisons (M2015-071, M2015-073, M2015-081).
On industries: ask why capital is entering (M2015-060), price to the contract's term (M2015-053), and expect prices to rise to what can be collected until an incentive stops them (M2015-082).
On people: reputation as a slow investment that ends as deserved (M2015-051, M2015-052), managers with skin in the game (M2015-078), hating to lose other people's money (M2015-094), and Munger's claim that keeping removable ignorance is dishonorable (M2015-093).
Two narrated failures of others: Dow Jones letting a commanding position go for want of imagination (M2015-098), against Munger's caution that new modalities are hard to invent (M2015-099); and universities overstaffed in prosperity (M2015-083).

## Tensions seen
- Rowed: M2015-099 (Munger: entirely new modalities are hard to invent, even for Murphy) against M2015-098 (Dow Jones had every advantage and lacked only imagination). Buffett does not reconcile them; he answers that Gates would have done well with Dow Jones too.
- Not rowed: M2015-077 ("Logically, we probably should take on more debt at these prices, but that’s just not something that appeals to us") pulls against the rest of the session's insistence on following the arithmetic (M2015-074, M2015-079). Buffett states the gap himself in the same sentence, so it is reconciled by preference, not left open.
- Not rowed: M2015-087 (a near-certain decent result over a hoped-for brilliant one) against M2015-098 (the cost of a strong position run without imagination). They pull on caution against ambition; they are about different actors (an investor choosing, a controlling owner running), and both rows are already written, so no tension row (2004-2005 convention).
