## What this session taught
The afternoon is mostly about incentives and the shape of contracts. Insurance underwriters are promised their jobs whatever the volume, because the silent message that volume protects jobs produces silly prices and a culture of bad business that is hard to undo (M2004-077, M2004-078); National Indemnity had nothing structural to set it apart and won by discipline (M2004-079).
On pay, there is no single yardstick: the owner's purchase price and the ease of the business distort return on equity, so the bar is set at what anyone would earn there, and an owner who does not understand the business is fooled by manager and consultant alike (M2004-094 to M2004-098). The Salomon and Maxwell story gives the same point from the other side: people paid to bring in business do not run morality checks (M2004-110).
On contracts, downgrade-triggered collateral clauses are called a margin account by another name, and one unmet call is enough (M2004-107, M2004-108); notional size does not measure danger (M2004-127). The closing answer repeats the morning: only leverage or crazy valuation ruins a disciplined investor (M2004-128).
On buying, the auction-versus-negotiation contrast is used twice, for whole businesses (M2004-072) and for IPOs timed by informed sellers (M2004-100); country risk is a number put in the valuation, and a big enough discount pays for it (M2004-082, M2004-083).
On capital, buybacks below business value with honest disclosure, stable public dividends, and payout of earnings that cannot be employed (M2004-085 to M2004-089); value is the present value of distributable cash, with retention worth it only above the discount rate (M2004-119).
On the investor, macro lists are ignored in both directions (M2004-101, M2004-102), and investors do themselves in (M2004-103, M2004-104). Munger's batting away of whole categories of folly and Buffett's hanging up at the first sentence describe a method of exclusion (M2004-117, M2004-118).
Four personal-conduct rules on habits, debt and associates are rowed for the operator to keep or drop (M2004-123 to M2004-126).
