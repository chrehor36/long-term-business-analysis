## What this session taught
The FY2013 letter's "Some Thoughts About Investing" is the year's centre: two small unquoted properties, a farm and a building by NYU, used to teach that one judges an asset by what it will produce, passes when a rough estimate of future earnings cannot be made, calls a focus on price change speculation, ignores macro opinion and the daily quote, and treats a stock as a small piece of a business one would not dump in a panic (L2013-005 to L2013-011).
The letter also gives the purchase test in its plainest form so far: a sensible earnings range five or more years out, and a reasonable price against the bottom of that range, or move on (L2013-012). For the non-professional it gives the index fund, bought over time and never sold on bad news, and Buffett puts it in his own will (L2013-013, L2013-014, M2014-016).
On survival the letter fixes the $20 billion floor and bars material short-term obligations; the meeting adds cash as oxygen, knowing where the cash comes from for every commitment, and never spending cash because it is there, while also calling idle cash in a panic the dumbest thing to own (L2013-002, L2013-003, M2014-020, M2014-021, M2014-032).
On managers and boards the meeting is about trust and social forces: promises to sellers kept and kept small, light supervision accepted with its occasional miss, a culture of deserved trust, boards as social organizations whose paid directors are not independent, pay ratcheted by published comparisons, and stock issuance as the mark of a promoter (M2014-011, M2014-012, M2014-018, M2014-022, M2014-023, M2014-033).
Four confessions: the EFH bonds bought without consulting Munger, stock issued for BNSF, See's failure to travel, and Munger's "pretty damn stupid when we bought See’s" (L2013-004, M2014-030, M2014-024, M2014-026).

## What in the brief was wrong or unclear
The 2013 convention says that when a reprint drops a sentence an earlier R-row quotes and the unit writes no new R-row, the drop is recorded under Rows and an erratum "not reprinted in the FY<year> report", nothing more, is appended to the dropped row. It does not say whether this reaches drops that are years old and recur in every reprint, or only a drop first seen this year. Reading the helper's seventeen DRIFT ids against this file, six R-rows are truly dropped (R1996-004, R1996-012, R1996-021, R1996-022, R1996-023 and the second sentence of R2009-001), none of them new this year; the rest are rewordings with the lesson intact. I applied the convention literally to all six, so each now carries "not reprinted in the FY2013 report", and every later AM unit through 2025 will append the same erratum to the same rows again unless the brief limits it. Two smaller points: "nothing more" does not fit a row whose quote is only partly dropped, so R2009-001's erratum names the dropped sentence; and R1996-004's lesson (per-share progress will diminish with size) survives reworded in principle 3 while its 15% figure is gone, which is neither a clean repeat nor a clean drop, so I treated it as a drop.
