## D. Six more specifics for Q7's range (stands in Q7 "The range, not the point", after "Four specifics the PG re-test asked for")

> **Six more specifics, from the small-cap runs** *(CONVENTION, ours in method; the principle of each is a row's)*.
> (a) **Cycles.** Where the first or last year of the five-year window is a cyclical peak or trough, or otherwise aberrational
> **[L2005-003]**, **[L2003-004]**, the base is the average over the last full cycle, peak to peak or trough to trough, and the
> growth is measured between the averages of successive cycles **[M2011-101]**, **[M2011-102]**, **[M2021-039]**; the literal
> window is shown beside it.
> (b) **Decline.** A negative growth shown is carried as negative and makes the bottom of the range; the no-growth case is then
> the top **[M2006-058]**, **[M2006-059]**. Where the series changes sign, or the compound rate between end years is
> meaningless, the growth is measured between the first and second halves of the window, and where that too is meaningless the
> range is the no-growth case alone, said so.
> (c) **Acquisitions.** Cash paid for businesses, net of businesses sold, is capital spending for the range. Either it is
> deducted and the total growth is credited, or it is left out and only organic growth, as the filer reports it, is credited;
> never left out with the total growth credited **[M1998-080]**, **[M2014-068]**, **[M2023-081]**. **The default pair is the
> first: acquisitions deducted, total growth credited**; the second is shown beside it where the filer reports organic growth.
> A deal financed by debt is valued as if paid with equity: unlevered owner cash, less today's net debt **[L2017-004]**. A merger
> inside the window is valued on the combined companies' own averages, with the deal's financing deducted.
> (d) **Working capital.** The increase in working capital is capital spending for the range **[M2008-036]**, **[M2003-122]**;
> a single year's draw that the filer ties to one contract or event is an aberrational year by (a), and the run shows the range
> at the business's working-capital intensity before the event beside it.
> (e) **Growth spending.** Where the filing allows the maintenance guess **[M2000-144]**, **[L1999-024]**, **the central case is
> owner cash after maintenance only**, with the growth spending charged against the growth it buys; the all-spending case is shown
> beside it. Never charge all spending and also cap the growth that spending bought.
> (f) **The cap.** No rate above the discount rate is carried after year ten **[M1997-095]**; inside the ten years the shown
> rate is the cap, and any result that traces to an absurdity is cut back **[M1999-067]**, **[M1997-096]**. A price above the
> top of the range closes OUT however wide the range, by the floor convention above.

## H. On what the floor is earned (stands in Q7 "The floor or hurdle", after "Whose tax")

> **On what the floor is earned** *(CONVENTION, ours in its extension to shares; the principle is **[L2017-004]**'s)*. Q7's
> central figure is built on the all-equity basis: owner cash before interest and after the company's tax, against the market
> value of the shares plus net debt (borrowings and finance leases less cash and securities, as the filing states them;
> operating leases left out and said so). The range per share is the whole-business range less net debt, and the fair price is
> computed on the same basis. The equity-only figure, owner cash after interest against the market value, is shown beside it
> and never decides alone. Rationale: a debt-financed business shows its discount per share magnified, since the lenders take
> the first dollar of value, and the rows refuse exactly that flattery of per-share figures **[L2017-004]**; the floor rows that
> name a figure name it on what is paid for the business **[M2011-062]**, and the target's debt is still weighed on its own at
> Q9 **[R1997-001]**.
