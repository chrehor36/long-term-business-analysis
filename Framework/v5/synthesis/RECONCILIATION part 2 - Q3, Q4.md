# RECONCILIATION part 2 — v4.1 Q3 and Q4 against v5 Q3 to Q6 and Q9. 2026-10-04

**Scope.** v4.1 `Framework/THE FRAMEWORK v4.md` Q3 (the weight declaration to the humility clause) and Q4 (owner
earnings to the named death), with `Framework/v4/THE MANAGER STANDARD - Q3.md` (MS below), which v4.1 incorporates by
reference. Against them: `Framework/v5/DRAFT - THE FRAMEWORK v5.md` Part III (the standing rule) and Part IV Q3, Q4,
Q5, Q6 (Parts A and B) and Q9, and the rows of `principle_ledger_v5.csv` behind them. Not blind: both were read.

**Method.** Classes and the load-bearing test are those of `Framework/v5/PREREGISTRATION - v5 blind read and
comparison.md`, section "Pre-registered standards": REPRODUCED, ABSENT, CONTRADICTED, NEW IN V5, plus PARTLY where a
v5 row carries part of a rule and not the rest (the part missing is named). Load-bearing means stated three or more
times across the source, or framed by Buffett or Munger as central, **and** capable of changing a run's verdict. A
v4.1 flag is by v4.1's own rule a prompt to read, never a verdict, so a single flag is marked not load-bearing; the
rule that makes flags prompts is classed separately (row 23). Every ABSENT names the regular-expression searches run
over the `quote_verbatim` column of all 4,279 rows of the v5 ledger, which cover all 64 units of the order file. The
calibration record `Framework/v5/synthesis/_work/calibration.txt` is cited where it shows an E-row the blind read
re-found (HIT) or did not (MISS). A v5 row that is Berkshire describing its own conduct is marked "(own conduct)".
