## Steering noticed
Yes, some. The auto-loaded map names six questions (understand, franchise, honest and rational management, survival, value against a bond, what proves me wrong and when to sell), and my candidate list has a similar order and coverage. I checked the order against the speakers' own combined lists in this slice (T25: M2008-039, M2010-061, M2011-085, M2013-045, M2013-066, M2010-084, L2014-036). They put understanding, durable advantage, management and price, in roughly that order, with price compared against cash and bonds (M2010-061). They do not put survival in those purchase lists. Survival is the largest theme here (T01), but nearly all of it is stated as Berkshire's own conduct (cash floors, no collateral calls, no leverage), not as a test applied to a company bought. I placed it sixth as a question about the buyer's exposure, but a reader should know that putting it in the list at all follows the row count and the map, not the speakers' checklists. Where the map would have folded management's use of retained earnings into a management question, the rows keep it separate (T09, T13), and I kept it separate. Selling (T18) is a small theme in this slice and I joined it with sizing rather than give it its own question. Theme splits (moat against capital needs; retention against deals; insurance and banking as their own industry tests) came from how the rows cluster, not from the map. The word "franchise" was not used for a theme name because the rows say "moat", "barrier" and "competitive advantage".
