# STRUCTURE DECISION, 2026-10-04 — the v5 question set, approved by the operator
**Under PRIME RULE 5.** The candidate set in `Framework/v5/synthesis/MERGED THEME MAP and CANDIDATE QUESTIONS.md`
(section 5) was put to the operator with the four structural alternatives the slices disagreed on. The operator
chose, 2026-10-04, in the parent session:

1. **The merged set as proposed:** a PREAMBLE of foundations (not a gate), then Q1 to Q11 in the speakers' order,
   Q12 optional, each drafted from its rows with STOP or WEIGHING stated and sourced.
2. **Ruin lives in both places:** a STANDING RULE of the buyer's conduct above the questions (never risk what you
   have and need; no practices that can bring sudden demands for large sums), and Q9 as a WEIGHING of the target's
   own debt and exposures.
3. **Selling is Q11, after purchase**, as the rows state it (has the business changed, or only its price), and the
   spine of the v5 holdings draft. The pre-committed "what would prove me wrong" trigger is recorded as ABSENT from
   the meetings (no instance found in the four maps); the analyst habits the rows do carry go in the preamble.
4. **Preamble yes; personal conduct as a closing note.** The 55 reputation rows are optional Q12; the 110
   personal-conduct rows become a closing note on the investor, not a question.
