**How a row is classed** (the pre-registered four classes, plus PARTLY as the brief allows): REPRODUCED, a v5 row states the same substance; ABSENT, worded "no instance found in the v5 ledger (searched: ...)", the searches being regular-expression searches of the `quote_verbatim` column of all rows of `principle_ledger_v5.csv`, run 2026-10-04, unless another sweep is named; CONTRADICTED, a v5 row cuts against the rule with no resolution in the meetings or the reports; PARTLY, part of the rule is reproduced and part is absent or cut against, the note says which. **Load-bearing** is the Test D definition the preregistration keeps: stated three or more times across the source, or framed by the authors as central, AND capable of changing a run's verdict. A confessed CONVENTION is by its own label not stated in the source, so it can meet the first limb only where a v4.1 row cites corpus for it.

## D. The verdict for this slice, as pre-registered

**The test.** The hypothesis FAILS on the first ABSENT or CONTRADICTED rule that is load-bearing by the definition: stated three or more times across the source, or framed by the authors as central, and capable of changing a run's verdict.

1. **Q6's pre-committed "what would prove me wrong" metric (row 29): ABSENT.
