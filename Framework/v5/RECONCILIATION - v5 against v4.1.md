## 2. The verdict, by the pre-registered rule
The hypothesis FAILS on the first load-bearing v4.1 rule that is ABSENT from or CONTRADICTED by the meetings.
Load-bearing means stated three or more times across the source, or framed by the authors as central, and capable of
changing a run's verdict.

- **ABSENT, load-bearing: none.** The 28 absences are confessed conventions of v4.1 itself (the UNRESEARCHED and
  UNKNOWABLE split, the thin-evidence rule, the evidence ladder), single-source refinements (the cash-tax tell,
  dividends paid by issuing shares, "broke but flush"), rules resting on one Munger talk outside the meetings, or
  protocol and project record. **Q6's pre-committed "what would prove me wrong" metric and the sell rule written at
  purchase are ABSENT** (part 3, resting in v4.1 on the single row E1-02, which is about measuring performance) and
  **do not meet the load-bearing definition.** Part 3 says what should be said: the hypothesis survives there because
  the definition's frequency limb excludes rules v4.1 treats as structural, so the definition is doing the work.
- **CONTRADICTED, load-bearing: one, conditionally.** Part 2, row 23: v4.1's "each flag is a prompt to read, never a
  verdict" (E5-36, E5-38, E4-13) against the meetings' M1995-063, M1995-065, M2003-029 and M2002-026 ("If you ever get
  suspicious about accounting, just go onto the next company"). The meetings' only boundary (M1994-018) protects mere
  preoccupation with accounting, not suspicion. **It is load-bearing only if E4-13 counts as one of its three
  supporting ids, which the manager standard does and the framework's own text may not.** That ruling is the
  operator's, and the verdict turns on it: with E4-13 the hypothesis FAILS narrowly on one rule; without it the
  hypothesis holds with no load-bearing contradiction.
- **The other four contradictions are not load-bearing:** the mandatory checklist (E3-68), the "moves no value" half
  of the position-size convention, the analyst's 25 percent borrowing ceiling (E1-18), and **the PERMANENT designation
  for marketable securities, which the authors withdrew themselves in 2016 (L2016-010, R2016-001).** That last one is a
  finding against v4.1 and the holdings framework whatever becomes of v5.

**Stated as the pre-registration requires:** the meetings reproduce or partly reproduce 182 of 215 v4.1 rules, add 87
the whole-shelf read did not carry, and contradict five, one of them load-bearing on a ruling the operator has not yet
made. The two hard cases named in advance came through: the owner-earnings number is reached from M1998-127 with
L2015-004 and M2000-144, and the named-death test from L2015-008 with M2004-054, with a timing dimension added.
