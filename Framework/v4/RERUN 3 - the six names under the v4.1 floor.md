## What changed and what did not

**Nothing actionable changed today** — nothing was buy-eligible before the floor and nothing
is after it. **What changed is the shape of every future add decision:** under v4.0 an add
argued "best available spread over the sovereign." Under v4.1 it must first write down a
pre-tax expectancy of roughly 10% and the growth belief that carries it, then rank. The 2003
answer's own caveat travels with it: the figure is arbitrary, a judgment, and revisable only
on a permanent regime change **[E4-28]**.

**Holds are untouched.** The floor is an entry rule. Q6 governs holds: HRB per the 2026-08-28
hold read; V/NCLTY/MITSY per their standing lines; the permanent-designation option
**[E2-39]** now exists for anchor holdings if you choose to invoke it — in writing, in
advance, never retro-fitted.
