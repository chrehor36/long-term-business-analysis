# CASE D — business description (de-identified, from the filing of record at the anchor)

The company is a large oil and gas producer with two smaller adjacent businesses, run through three segments.

The oil and gas segment explores for, develops and produces oil and condensate, natural gas liquids and natural gas. Domestic upstream operations are concentrated in one prolific onshore basin region spanning two states of its home country; international operations are in four countries, three of them in one hydrocarbon-rich region overseas and one on another continent. Proved reserves at year-end are roughly 2.75 billion barrels of oil equivalent, of which about 62% is domestic; on an equivalent-barrel basis the mix is roughly 58% oil, 18% natural gas liquids and 24% gas, with the filing noting that gas converts at six thousand cubic feet per barrel but currently sells for far less than price equivalence. The filing describes its products as commodities sensitive to global and local market conditions, sold at market prices or on a forward basis, against numerous public, private and government producers. Its stated competitive strategy is to grow production through developing conventional and unconventional fields, applying primary and enhanced oil recovery techniques, and making strategic acquisitions where its existing operations or shared infrastructure give it an advantage.

The chemical segment manufactures and markets basic chemicals of the chlorine and caustic soda chain and vinyls, operating plants at 22 domestic sites and two international sites. The filing states its market position in the home country was first or second in its principal basic chemical products and in a key vinyl intermediate, and among the top three in a widely used plastic resin. Its stated competitive strategy is to be a low-cost producer in order to compete on price. A jointly owned ethylene plant recently completed its first full year of operations.

The midstream and marketing segment purchases, markets, gathers, processes, transports and stores oil, gas liquids, natural gas, carbon dioxide and power, primarily to support and enhance the other two segments, while also serving third parties and trading around its assets. Assets include gas processing plants, carbon dioxide fields and pipeline systems feeding enhanced oil recovery operations, power and steam generation, and equity investments in pipeline entities. During the year the company sold several non-core midstream assets, including a pipeline and storage system and a terminal, while retaining flow assurance and export capacity through its marketing business. A new venture within this segment seeks to build on the company's stated leadership in enhanced oil recovery by developing carbon capture, utilization and storage projects.

The company employs approximately 11,000 people, about 7,000 of them in the home country; roughly 500 domestic and 900 international employees are represented by labor unions. The filing describes its midstream and marketing markets as competitive and highly regulated, and directs readers to extensive environmental regulation and remediation disclosures elsewhere in the report.
