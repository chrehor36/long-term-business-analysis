SHEET 7 DCF COMPUTATION | Gate 6

Only reach this sheet if Gates 1­5 are all passed. The DCF is the final confirmation -- not the starting point.
A note on method: running a DCF in isolation would be a Benjamin Graham approach -- not inherently wrong, but Graham used valuation as the primary
filter, buying cheap businesses without requiring a durable moat or exceptional management. This framework is more advanced. Five qualitative gates
have already confirmed the business is within your circle, the moat is real, management is honest, the financials are sound, and the thesis survives
inversion. The DCF here is not the analysis -- it is the final check that you are not overpaying for a business you already know is exceptional. That
distinction is the Buffett/Munger synthesis of Graham: the same tool, used last instead of first.

ERP = Equity Risk Premium = 5­6% | Company-Specific = 0­2%
