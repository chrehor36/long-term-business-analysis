## Addendum — 2026-07-15 (Ruling 2 ratified: the Fortress Test, two tracks)
- **New Principle 48 — The Fortress Test, Two Tracks.** Field amendment, ratified
  2026-07-15 (Framework/PENDING RULINGS.md, Ruling 2). Replaces the blanket
  net-cash-positive requirement in Gate 4's balance-sheet check with a split test:
  - Non-financial businesses pass on SURVIVAL — service debt and fund operations
    through a 50% owner-earnings decline sustained 2 consecutive years without
    existential risk (credit rating, undrawn liquidity vs. near-term maturities,
    a termed-out debt schedule). Ordinary investment-grade leverage is not itself
    a disqualifier.
  - Financial businesses (banks, insurers, anything balance-sheet/float-levered)
    must ALSO clear a hard mechanical ceiling: assets ÷ equity > 10:1 = automatic
    FAIL, no exception. Corpus basis: the 1990 letter's 20:1 bank-leverage warning
    [E3-02 context].
- **Why:** the strict net-cash rule had eliminated Home Depot (2026-07-14) and
  Sherwin-Williams (2026-07-15) for carrying ordinary, serviceable corporate debt —
  a false-positive-generating rule in the conservative direction. The backtest
  (Backtests/2026-07-14 Batch 2, episode #12) showed the SAME strict rule
  mechanically rejects Citigroup mid-2007 at 19:1 leverage (−95% followed), which
  is exactly the danger the original net-cash rule existed to catch. The two-track
  split keeps the Citigroup catch (via the mechanical leverage ceiling, which
  applies with no judgment call for financials) while no longer penalizing
  ordinary industrials for using normal debt.
