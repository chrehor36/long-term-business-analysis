## What it does

Hubbell makes the hardware that utilities and electrical contractors use: arresters, insulators, connectors, switches, smart meters and protection controls for the grid, and wiring devices, boxes and fittings for buildings. Utility Solutions (Grid Infrastructure and Grid Automation) is about 63% of its $3.67 billion of 2025 sales and Electrical Solutions about 37%. Products are sold mainly through distributors or directly to utilities.

## Why it passed

- It earns 40% to 55% before tax on the net tangible capital it uses (42% in 2021, 55% in 2025), and did so through the cycle.
- Its position has held against its nearest rival across seventeen years of record, though the analysis calls the advantage moderate.
- Its economics can be foreseen ten years out from slow-moving variables such as utility spending on the grid.
- The analysis found no sign of dishonesty and judged management's ability modestly positive.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

At $482.23 a share against a value range of $204 to $405, the price is above the top. A buyer would earn about 2.5% to 4.6% a year after tax on the cash the business produces, below a 30-year US Treasury bond (5.63%). The price assumes cash growing about 10% a year for ten years.

## What to watch

- Its recent profit level (2023 to 2025) is unproven, and its earlier profits were about flat from 2008 to 2017 while about $1.5 billion went into acquisitions.
- It paid about $3.0 billion for NSI in 2026 (about 15.5 times expected earnings before interest, tax and depreciation), and goodwill and intangibles of $7.5 billion now exceed shareholders' equity of $3.9 billion.
- It has bought back shares at prices above its value with no stated price discipline.
