## What it does

Enerpac makes high-pressure hydraulic and mechanical tools such as cylinders, pumps, bolt tensioners and torque wrenches, plus heavy-lifting systems and bolting and machining services. It sells mainly through distributors to maintenance and industrial projects in refining, power, infrastructure and mining. Fiscal 2025 sales were $617M, and the largest customer was about 3% of sales.

## Why it passed

- Its return on tangible capital was about 99% to 107% before tax in fiscal 2023 to 2025.
- Manufacturing is mostly light assembly of sourced parts, so capital spending was only $8M to $19M a year, 1.5% to 3.1% of sales.
- The industrial tools segment's operating margin rose from 14.9% in fiscal 2022 to 27.5% in fiscal 2025.
- Sales are spread across the US (37%), Europe (28%), the Middle East (13%) and Asia (11%).

## Why we are not buying at today's price

The analysis values the shares at $18.00 to $25.08 against a price of $35.23. A buyer at that price would earn only about 3.5% to 6.6% a year, below the 5.64% a 30-year US Treasury bond pays in all but the most generous case.

## What to watch

- A Dutch sanctions proceeding, which the company disclosed itself, remains open.
- The principal accounting officer was removed in July 2026 without a stated reason.
- A planned acquisition (SFE) would raise net debt to about 2.8 times adjusted EBITDA, and the credit facility matures in September 2027.
