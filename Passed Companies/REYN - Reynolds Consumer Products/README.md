## What it does

Reynolds makes aluminum foil, parchment and wax paper, disposable pans, Hefty trash and food-storage bags, plastic wrap, plates, cups and cutlery, and store-brand versions of many of these. Its brands include Reynolds Wrap, Hefty and Presto. Segment sales were about $3.7 billion in 2025.

## Why it passed

- It earned about 56% before interest and tax on its tangible capital in 2025 ($479M on about $848M).
- Its Reynolds Wrap foil has been the top trusted brand in consumer foil for over 75 years.
- The Hefty bags segment's margin rose from 23.4% to 27.6% as its volume grew, and the store-brand segment improved from 15.6% to 20.7%.
- Total segment profit was $761M on $3.7 billion of sales (20.5%) in 2025.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

This run found the price of $22.23 slightly below its value range of $23.84 to $27.93, which is built at the 5.66% a 30-year US Treasury bond pays. But at the price the expected return is only 7.4% to 9.1% before tax, below the roughly 10% the analysis requires, and the analysis's fair price is $16.14.

## What to watch

- About 48% of sales come from one customer group.
- The foil and tableware segments are narrowing: margins fell from 23.5% to 17.4% in foil and 25.0% to 15.6% in tableware over the years compared.
- After price rises of about 25%, overall volume fell about 4% over the period the analysis compared, and $1.9 billion of goodwill sits on the balance sheet.
