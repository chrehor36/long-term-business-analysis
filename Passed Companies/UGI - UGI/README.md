## What it does

UGI owns regulated gas utilities (UGI Utilities with about 694,000 customers, and Mountaineer with about 210,000), the AmeriGas retail propane business in the US, and gas and propane businesses in Europe. The utility is the largest part by profit, at 30% of five-year segment profit.

## Why it passed

- Its gas utility is a franchise monopoly that rivals cannot cross, and its rates are set by regulators years ahead.
- The utility's segment earnings rose from $242 million to $403 million.
- It added more than 9,000 residential heating customers in fiscal 2025.
- No fact was found that raises doubt about management's honesty.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

A regulator caps what the utility may earn, so the price is far above what that capped return justifies. At $38.43 a share the price is above the top of the analysis's value range ($12.85 to $33.00), and the best expected return is about 5.0% a year, a little below a 30-year US Treasury bond (5.43%).

## What to watch

- AmeriGas has written off $851 million of goodwill in two years, and its customers keep leaving.
- Europe faces continued structural conservation of gas, and AmeriGas refinanced at 9.5% in May 2025.
- The company features an adjusted earnings figure, and net debt exceeds what its cash is worth at a 10% return.
