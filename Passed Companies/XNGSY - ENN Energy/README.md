## What it does

ENN Energy distributes piped natural gas in mainland China to 32.76 million connected households and 316,000 commercial and industrial customers. It sold 26,606 million cubic metres of gas in 2025 on revenue of RMB 111,905 million. It buys gas from the three major oil companies, LNG contracts and the spot market, and resells it at prices that for homes are set by local government.

## Why it passed

- It holds legal gas franchises that a rival with money cannot simply cross.
- Gas is a basic need for the households it serves, and substantially all revenue and profit come from mainland China.
- Retail gas volume rose 1.5% in 2025, and 71.6% of residential volume had completed a gas price adjustment by the end of 2025.
- The analysis found management honest and able.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

Local governments cap what the company may earn on residential gas. At US$25.54 per ADS a buyer would earn about 5.4% to 6.6% a year, below the roughly 10% the analysis requires, and the analysis's fair price is US$12.11, about half the current price.

## What to watch

- Its profit per unit of gas is being squeezed, and new connections have collapsed.
- Borrowings are RMB 18,944 million, and current liabilities exceed current assets by RMB 17,575 million, funded by customer prepayments.
- The capital invested over the last five years earned a negative return, and pay has no charge for capital.
