## What it does

IDACORP, through Idaho Power, serves about 664,000 retail customers in southern Idaho and eastern Oregon. It runs 17 hydroelectric projects, plus gas and co-owned coal-to-gas plants. Rates are set on cost of service by the Idaho regulator, which covers about 95% of retail revenue.

## Why it passed

- It has a legal franchise, so customers have no other supplier.
- Hydropower supplies 52% to 55% of its own generation, which keeps power costs low.
- Its return on equity was between 9.1% and 9.8% every year from 2016 to 2025, above Avista (7.6% average) and NorthWestern (8.8%).
- Summer peak demand set a record of 3,793 MW in 2024.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

A regulator caps what this utility may earn, so the price is far above what that capped return justifies. The analysis values the shares at $70.72 to $91.45 against a price of $142.06, and a buyer at that price would earn only about 2.6% to 3.7% a year, below the 5.29% a 30-year US Treasury bond pays.

## What to watch

- In 2024 and 2025 it reached its return only with $29.8M and $40.3M of extra tax-credit amortization allowed by the regulator.
- Interest was $204.4M in 2025 against pre-tax income of $310.4M, and one rating agency's outlook is negative.
- Wildfire claims could exceed its insurance, and recovering uninsured losses in rates is not assured.
