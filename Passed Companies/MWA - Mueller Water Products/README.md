## What it does

Mueller Water Products makes iron gate valves, specialty valves, fire hydrants, meters and leak-detection and pressure-management products for water systems. It sells through waterworks distributors. About 60% to 65% of its $1.43 billion of sales goes to repairing and replacing city water systems.

## Why it passed

- Cities approve a limited number of hydrant brands so they can use the same tools and spare parts, and its hydrants and gate valves are specified in the 100 largest US metropolitan areas.
- It held its prices through the 2009 downturn.
- It earned 40.5% a year before tax on the tangible capital it needs in fiscal 2025 (about 25% counting goodwill and intangibles still on its books).
- Its principal competitors are two private companies, McWane and American Cast Iron Pipe, in a heavy, bulky product that is costly to import.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

At $22.02 a share the price is above the top of the analysis's value range of $10.73 to $16.76. The cash the business produces is 2.7% of the price on a five-year average, against 5.63% on a 30-year US Treasury bond. The analysis's fair price is $7.47.

## What to watch

- It owes an environmental indemnity of undetermined size to the buyer of its former U.S. Pipe business (the North Birmingham Superfund site).
- Two distributors account for 37% of gross sales.
- It has a history of past acquisitions that were later written off, and its recent buybacks were made at prices above the analysis's value range.
- A 2021 fatal accident at its Albertville plant was noted in the analysis.
