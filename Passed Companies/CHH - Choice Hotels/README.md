## What it does

Choice Hotels licenses its hotel brands to independent owners and collects fees. Owners pay a royalty of about 5% to 6% of room revenue plus a 3% to 4% fee for marketing and reservations, on contracts of 10 to 30 years. It has 22 brands, about 497,000 US rooms, and a few owned hotels that support its newer brands.

## Why it passed

- Franchising needs almost no capital: the franchising segment earned $603.1M in operating income in 2025 while spending about $39M on property and equipment.
- The average US royalty rate rose from 4.41% in 2016 to 5.14% in 2025.
- US rooms in the system grew from 404,498 in 2016 to 496,979 in 2025.
- Fees are a share of room revenue, so income grows with hotel sales without Choice carrying hotel costs.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

The analysis values the shares at $23.63 to $60.29 against a price of $103.88. At that price a buyer would earn about 2.4% to 4.5% a year, below the 5.56% a 30-year US Treasury bond pays.

## What to watch

- The US system lost 141 hotels and 14,760 rooms (-2.9%) in 2025.
- Payments to win franchisees rose from $38.9M in 2019 to $83.4M in 2025, so the royalty Choice keeps after those payments is slightly lower than in 2019.
- The chief executive left in May 2026 without an explanation in the filing, and debt is about $2.0 billion with most due 2029 to 2031.
