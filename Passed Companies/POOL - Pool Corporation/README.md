## What it does

Pool Corporation distributes pool chemicals, equipment and supplies to the contractors and stores that maintain, remodel and build pools. About 64% of sales are for maintenance and minor repair, 22% for remodels and upgrades and 14% for new construction. It serves about 5.5 million in-ground pools in the US.

## Why it passed

- Its gross margin stayed between 27.3% and 31.3% every year from 2003 to 2025, through a housing collapse, a boom and an unwind.
- Most of its sales depend on a large, slowly growing base of existing pools that need chemicals and replacement parts.
- It earns high returns on its tangible capital through the cycle, and the working capital growth needs earns well.
- Its operating margin (10% to 11%) sits well above SiteOne, the national landscape distributor (3.8% to 6.6%).

## What to watch

- Home Depot owns Heritage Pool Supply Group, the second national pool distributor; the analysis named this attack as the live threat to its margin and share.
- Its chief executive left by mutual agreement in May 2026 after two years; the successor joined only in January 2026 from another company and is unproven.
- It bought back shares in 2021 to 2025 at prices above its value, and holds $1.3 billion of debt.
