## What it does

Ecolab sells water treatment and cleaning products and services to factories, restaurants, hotels, hospitals and food retailers. Most of its revenue is consumable product delivered by a field force of about 25,000 sales and service staff out of 48,000 employees. It also runs a commercial pest service and supplies contamination control to drug makers.

## Why it passed

- Its biggest business, water treatment, earns about 68% a year before tax on the tangible capital it uses.
- Its rivals in hygiene have struggled while it stayed profitable: Diversey made operating losses in 2021 and 2022, while Ecolab's operating margin stayed about 11% to 13%.
- No customer is 10% of sales, and the largest raw material is only about 5% of purchases.
- Customers keep paying for a service they depend on, and the company's strong pricing outran cost inflation.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

The price was $279.25. The analysis's central value range is about -$4 to $31 a share (the low end is negative because heavy capital spending and acquisitions eat up the cash), and even a more generous version tops out at $136.82, still half the price. The cash the business produces is only 1.86% of the price, against 5.64% on a 30-year US Treasury bond.

## What to watch

- It carries $13.2 billion of debt, including $1.3 billion due within a year.
- Capital spending has risen from 5.0% to 6.5% of sales since 2021, which holds down the cash left for owners.
- A lawsuit over the 2019 TPC Group explosion involves Nalco, with the plant owner's own estimate of its liability at $152 million to $520 million.
