## What it does

Spire's Gas Utility segment delivers natural gas under exclusive regulated franchises and produced 88% of recent operating profit. Residential customers are 66% of its revenue. It sold its gas marketing business on 30 April 2026.

## Why it passed

- It holds exclusive franchises, and regulators set its rates, for example a $210.0M base rate increase for Spire Missouri effective October 2025.
- Regulatory mechanisms let it recover pipe-replacement spending without waiting for a full rate case.
- Gas Utility net income held between $198.6M and $237.2M over fiscal 2021 to 2025.
- Its deciding economics (rate base and allowed return) can be read from public filings.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

A regulator caps what this utility may earn, so the price is far above what that capped return justifies. The analysis values the shares at $39.45 against a price of $85.37. The central expected return at the price is only 2.45% a year, and the most generous reading 5.76%, against the 5.31% a 30-year US Treasury bond pays.

## What to watch

- Borrowings were $7.3 billion against $3.6 billion of common equity, and $1.3 billion of commercial paper must be rolled continuously.
- Customers grew only about a quarter of a percent a year, and capital spending ($922M in 2025) far exceeded depreciation ($298M).
- Its return on equity averaged 8.0% over ten years, in the lower half of comparable gas utilities, and goodwill is about a third of its equity.
