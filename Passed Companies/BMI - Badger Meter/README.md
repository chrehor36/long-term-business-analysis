## What it does

Badger Meter sells water meters, the radio devices that read them, and cloud software that receives the readings, mainly to US water utilities. Utility water was about 89% of 2025 sales and industrial flow instruments about 11%.

## Why it passed

- Water utilities are risk-averse and change their meter and software systems only on a 12 to 15 year cycle, so the installed base keeps customers in place.
- A rival with money and a sales channel lost money in every year the analysis examined, and Badger has passed cost increases on to customers each time.
- Its gross margin has widened over twenty years.
- It earned about 68% before tax on the $271.6 million of tangible capital it needs, and has had no borrowing since 2020.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

The price of $123.73 is about 1.95 times the top of the analysis's value range ($36.93 to $63.29), and it is above the top of every cut-back case. A buyer at that price would earn less than the roughly 10% return the analysis requires.

## What to watch

- 2025 was the top of a wave of meter replacements and a margin peak, so earnings may not stay that high.
- It has bought back shares at $119.61 to $182.15, well above the analysis's value range.
- It faces seven named rivals, including larger groups.
