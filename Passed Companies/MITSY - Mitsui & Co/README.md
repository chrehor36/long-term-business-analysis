## What it does

Mitsui & Co. is a Japanese trading company with many businesses. The part that carries its profits is mineral and metal resources, led by low-cost iron ore from Australia's Pilbara region, and it also earns from gas and other resources.

## Why it passed

- Its iron ore is among the lowest-cost in the world, which protects it when prices fall.
- Its returns across a full commodity cycle were excellent.
- No doubt about honesty was found in the record the analysis read.
- The volume and cost of its main mines are foreseeable, even though the price of ore is not.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

At ¥4,775 a share, the price sits near the top of the analysis's value range (¥2,723 to ¥4,796). The analysis found an expected return of only 2.8% to 4.9% a year, below the roughly 10% the analysis requires and below the 4.14% a 30-year Japanese government bond pays at the low end.

## What to watch

- The price of ore and gas sets most of its cash, and no one in the business will forecast it.
- The accounts feature an adjusted cash figure and guidance, and show recurring write-downs; the analysis weighed these against.
- It carries about ¥4.46 trillion of net debt.
