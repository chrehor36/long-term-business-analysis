## What it does

New Jersey Resources owns New Jersey Natural Gas, which delivers gas to 588,975 customers in six New Jersey counties and supplies 54.8% of the group's five-year earnings. It also has a wholesale gas marketing business (24.5%), commercial solar projects (14.7%) and gas storage and pipeline interests (6.0%).

## Why it passed

- The gas utility is a franchise, and a regulator-approved program (the Conservation Incentive Program) protects its margin from weather and usage.
- Its gas cost is passed through to customers without markup, so commodity swings do not hit its earnings.
- The regulator's order of November 2024 allowed 9.6% on equity with a 54.0% equity share, and a new case filed in June 2026 asks for 10.10%.
- Group earnings averaged a 13.1% return on capital over five years, against 7.7% to 9.0% for three comparable gas utilities.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

A regulator caps what this utility may earn, so the price is far above what that capped return justifies. The analysis values the whole business at -$18.22 to $15.91 a share ($5.26 to $37.87 counting only shareholders' money) against a price of $52.19. The best defensible case yields 3.20% a year, below the 5.43% a 30-year US Treasury bond pays.

## What to watch

- Debt is $3.7 billion, 58% of debt plus equity, and the company holds little cash and relies on short-term bank borrowing and commercial paper.
- New Jersey's clean-energy plan, which pushes toward electrification, is a long-term political threat to gas distribution.
- Cleanup of old gas plant sites is estimated at $144.3M to $200.2M, recovered through a regulator-approved rider.
