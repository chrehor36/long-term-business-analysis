## What it does

CSW makes heating, cooling and plumbing products for installers and repairers, such as thread sealant, condensate pumps, vent grilles, coils, motors and capacitors. Its contractor products brought in $810.3 million, or 75% of fiscal 2026 revenue. It also has an engineered building products segment.

## Why it passed

- Its contractor products have earned segment margins of 23% to 27% for a decade on a small base of tangible capital.
- It held its prices through the 2021 to 2023 inflation.
- Distributors that carry competitors' products have not taken its margin.
- It needs little capital to stand still, with capital spending of only $14 million to $17 million a year on $0.8 to $1.1 billion of revenue.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

At $305.83 a share, the price is more than twice the top of the analysis's value range ($74 to $145). The analysis's fair price is about $85, and no case it could support gives the roughly 10% a year return the analysis requires.

## What to watch

- The newest third of the business (motors and coils from recent purchases) has no track record under CSW.
- Distributors decide what sits on the shelf, and the analysis could not ask competitors about it.
- Its engineered building products segment shows a narrowing edge, and it features an adjusted profit measure (EBITDA) that the analysis weighed against.
