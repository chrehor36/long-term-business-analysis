## What it does

Alliant Energy owns two regulated utilities, Interstate Power and Light in Iowa and Wisconsin Power and Light, serving about 1,010,000 electric and 435,000 gas customers. Customers cannot choose another electric supplier, and the utilities must serve everyone in their territories.

## Why it passed

- Customers have no other electric supplier, so demand is protected by law.
- Return on equity ran between 10.0% and 11.9% over the nine years measured.
- The Iowa utility earned 54% and the Wisconsin utility 47% of operating profit.
- The regulators set the return it may earn, which makes future earnings easy to follow.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

A regulator caps what this utility may earn, so the price is far above what that capped return justifies. The analysis values the shares at $17.87 to $21.48 on the whole business, or $34.97 to $47.59 counting only shareholders' money, against a price of $68.32. The best return a buyer could expect is about 3.6% a year, below the 5.43% a 30-year US Treasury bond pays.

## What to watch

- Debt is 62% of total capital, and earnings covered interest only about 2.3 times in 2025.
- Short-term borrowings were $1.1 billion at mid-2026 ($708M commercial paper and $400M other).
- All earnings come from two utilities in two adjacent states, so a single catastrophic event could hurt it badly.
