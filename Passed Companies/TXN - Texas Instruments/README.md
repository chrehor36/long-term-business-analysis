## What it does

Texas Instruments makes analog and embedded-processing chips, more than 80,000 products sold to industrial, automotive and other customers, with over 80% of 2025 revenue through its own channels. Revenue was $17.7 billion in 2025, and about 20% comes from China-headquartered customers.

## Why it passed

- Its operating margin ran from 30.3% in 2014 to 50.6% in 2022, with no loss year.
- After-tax returns on tangible capital were 60% to 105% a year from 2014 to 2022.
- Its operating margin averaged 39.5% from 2014 to 2025, against 26.5% for Analog Devices, 18.9% for Microchip and 20.4% for NXP.
- Revenue rose 13% in 2025 and 23% in the second quarter of 2026.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

The analysis values the shares at $116.70 to $163.88 against a price of $283.74. A buyer at that price would earn about 2.4% to 4.3% a year, below the 5.60% a 30-year US Treasury bond pays.

## What to watch

- It agreed to buy Silicon Labs, backed by a $5 billion loan that, if drawn, would take debt to about $19 billion.
- Debt was $14.15 billion at mid-2026, and its CHIPS Act award agreement includes clawback rights.
- Revenue swings widely with the chip cycle ($20.0 billion in 2022, $15.6 billion in 2024), and China is about 20% of revenue.
