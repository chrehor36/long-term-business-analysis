## What it does

Colgate sells toothpaste, toothbrushes, mouthwash, soap, shower gel, deodorant, dish liquid and cleaners. Through Hill's it also sells therapeutic pet food. Oral care was 44% of 2025 sales, pet nutrition 23%, personal care 17% and home care 16%.

## Why it passed

- It holds about two fifths of global toothpaste sales.
- It raised prices about a fifth through the last bout of inflation while holding sales volume and gaining share.
- It earned $5,023 million of segment profit in 2025 on only $4,336 million of tangible capital.
- No doubt about management's honesty came up in its accounts, proxy statement or record.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

At $87.25 a share, the price is above the top of the analysis's value range ($49.64 to $83.25). A buyer at that price would expect only 3.4% to 5.4% a year in cash, below the roughly 10% the analysis requires and, at the low end, below a 30-year US Treasury bond.

## What to watch

- It has lost some ground in North America and spends more to hold its place.
- About two thirds of its cash is earned abroad, 45% in emerging markets.
- Restructuring charges recur and are presented as one-offs, and it has net debt of about $6.4 billion.
