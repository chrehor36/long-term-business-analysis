## What it does

Global Water Resources runs 39 water, wastewater and recycled-water systems in Arizona, serving 68,577 active connections. All of its revenue ($55.8 million in 2025) is regulated by the Arizona Corporation Commission, and 87.3% of its connections are in one service area.

## Why it passed

- It holds an exclusive certificate to serve its territory, and no rival would pay to duplicate the pipes.
- All revenue is regulated, so the next ten years of its business are easy to picture.
- It found and disclosed on its own a rate-base error carried for fourteen years, which went against its own revenue, so honesty was not in doubt.
- It has no share buybacks.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

A regulator caps what a utility may earn, so the price is far above what that capped return justifies. At $7.74 a share the price is above the top of the analysis's value range, and the company earned only about 4% on its capital against an allowed return more than twice that.

## What to watch

- The regulator's staff once recommended a decrease and its largest rate case was withdrawn, so the regulatory climate is an open risk.
- Cities have twice taken over a franchise by condemnation.
- Management's ability was weighed against, and it has about $127 million of net debt and refundable advances of $155.7 million from builders.
