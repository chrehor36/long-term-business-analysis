## What it does

Eaton makes the electrical equipment that distributes and protects power, such as breakers, switchgear, backup power units and panelboards, for buildings, data centers, utilities and factories. It also makes fuel and hydraulic systems for aircraft. It is selling off its vehicle business and recently bought Boyd Thermal, which makes liquid cooling for data centers.

## Why it passed

- Its main electrical business in the Americas raised its profit margin from 15.7% in 2008 to 29.9% in 2025, and held up through the 2009 recession.
- Electrical equipment sits in the safety path of a building or data center, so buyers do not choose on price alone.
- Its order backlog in the Americas grew to $13.2 billion from $10.1 billion a year earlier.
- It earns about 26% a year before tax on the tangible assets it uses, though the last three years are boom years and the quieter years earned about 20%.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

The analysis values the shares at $118 to $238 against a price of $437.83, well above the top of the range. A buyer at that price would expect only about 1.5% to 3.2% a year, far below a 30-year US Treasury bond (5.63%).

## What to watch

- Debt jumped from $9.9 billion to $20.6 billion after the $9.55 billion Boyd Thermal purchase, and interest cost has nearly tripled.
- It bought back shares at $320 to $327, far above the top of its value range, and has paused buybacks for 2026.
- The 2023 to 2025 profits are boom-year results, so margins could come down; a few big rivals (Schneider, Siemens, ABB) compete for the same work.
