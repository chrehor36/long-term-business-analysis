## What it does

Wabtec has two segments. Freight (72% of 2025 sales, $8.0 billion) makes and modernizes diesel-electric locomotives, supplies parts and services for an installed base of nearly 24,600 locomotives, and makes brakes and train control. Transit (28%, $3.1 billion) supplies brakes, doors and air conditioning for passenger trains.

## Why it passed

- About 58% of Freight sales are aftermarket parts and services for locomotives already in service.
- It earned 68% to 114% before tax on net tangible capital over 2019 to 2025, and customer deposits fund much of its working capital.
- Its gross margin rose from 28.3% in 2020 to 34.1% in 2025, even through the cost inflation of 2021 to 2023.
- Backlog grew to $27.4 billion at the end of 2025.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

The analysis values the shares at $104.21 to $245.51 against a price of $287.32, above the top of the range. A buyer at that price would earn about 2.3% to 4.8% a year, below the 5.43% a 30-year US Treasury bond pays.

## What to watch

- Debt was $6.6 billion at mid-2026, $1.7 billion of it current.
- Sales in Kazakhstan and nearby countries were $431M in 2025 and carry sanctions and nationalization risk.
- The top five customers are about 30% of sales, and asbestos claims cannot be estimated.
