## What it does

Pentair makes pool equipment (pumps, filters, heaters, lights, cleaners), water treatment products (commercial filtration, ice machines, softeners) and pumps for homes, irrigation and industry. Pool is the largest part at about 49% of five years of segment income. It is an Irish-registered company.

## Why it passed

- From 2019 to 2025 its tangible capital was only $146 million to $795 million against operating income of $459 million to $916 million, so the return on that capital topped 100% before tax every year.
- Capital spending is only 1.5% to 2.1% of sales.
- Pool segment income grew from $452.7 million in fiscal 2021 to $527.1 million in 2025, and Flow's grew from $213.3 million to $362.1 million.
- The analysis found its integrity sound.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

At $52.47 a share the price is above the top of the analysis's value range of $36.95 to $49.63, and the central case earns 4.50% to 5.41% a year. That is below the 5.60% a 30-year US Treasury bond pays. The analysis's fair price is $13.43.

## What to watch

- It agreed in July 2026 to buy Taco Group Holdings for $1.425 billion, adding about $1.4 billion of debt to bring the total near $3.0 billion.
- One customer, Pool Corporation, took 18% of sales in 2025.
- It bought back $350 million of its shares in the first half of 2026 at about $87.50, above the analysis's value range, and the analysis judged management's ability as weighing against.
