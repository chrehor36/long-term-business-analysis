## What it does

H World franchises and runs hotels. At the end of 2025 it had 12,858 hotels with 1.26 million rooms in 1,222 cities, almost all in greater China. Only 4.5% of the hotels are leased or owned by the company. The rest are franchised, and the owners pay it an upfront fee plus a monthly fee of about 3% to 6.5% of each hotel's revenue.

## Why it passed

- Franchisees pay for developing and running their hotels, so the fee business needs very little of the company's own money.
- Cash produced in 2025 was RMB 7.06 billion on about RMB 7.8 billion of tangible operating assets, a return of the order of 90%.
- Its main Chinese hotel business carried the group: about RMB 23.9 billion of adjusted operating profit over 2021 to 2025, while the European business lost money.
- Its loyalty program and direct booking channels keep hotels and guests inside its system.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

At $44.17 per ADS (a share certificate traded in the US) the price sits inside the analysis's value range of $37.59 to $91.53, but even the central growth case earns only about 4.2% a year. That is well short of the roughly 10% the framework requires, and the analysis's fair price is $14.08.

## What to watch

- The European business (leased hotels in Germany) had negative operating profit in three of five years, and its brand was written down RMB 391 million in 2024.
- Leases are a large commitment: RMB 27 billion of lease liabilities, with rent equal to 16.5% of revenue in 2025.
- It has bought back its own shares at about $44.4 per ADS, well above the analysis's fair price.
- The value depends heavily on which years are treated as normal: counting the post-COVID recovery would move the fair price from $14 to about $38.
