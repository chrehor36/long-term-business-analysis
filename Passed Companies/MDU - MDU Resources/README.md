## What it does

MDU Resources owns regulated gas utilities serving 1,082,329 customers in eight states, an electric utility serving 146,550 customers in four states, and a 3,800-mile interstate gas pipeline and storage system. Regulators set what it may charge.

## Why it passed

- Each part is a franchised local monopoly, so customers cannot choose another supplier.
- Gas customers have grown from 1,049,275 in 2023 to 1,082,329 in 2025.
- Regulators allowed returns of 9.5% to 9.65% in the gas cases decided in 2025.
- The analysis found management honest.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

A regulator caps what a utility may earn, so the price is far above what that capped return justifies. At $19.00 a share the best case earns only about 4.3% a year, less than the 5.43% on a 30-year US Treasury bond, and the analysis's value range is only $7.16 to $9.70.

## What to watch

- Washington and Oregon are slowly narrowing what the utility can earn, and a gas segment return on assets of about 1.4% is thin.
- Borrowings are $2,577 million; one gas subsidiary breached an interest coverage covenant in September 2025, until waived.
- A proposed Bakken East pipeline would cost $2.7 to $3.2 billion with financing not settled.
