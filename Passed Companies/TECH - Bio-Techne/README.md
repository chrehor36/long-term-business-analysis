## What it does

Bio-Techne makes proteins, antibodies, reagents and instruments used in life-science research and drug manufacturing, along with diagnostic controls. It sells hundreds of thousands of products and launched over 1,900 new ones in fiscal 2026. Its protein sciences business is the deciding part.

## Why it passed

- The company says it is the world leader in high-quality research proteins, both for research and for drug manufacturing.
- Its pre-tax return on the capital it needs was between 34.4% and 55.6% each year from fiscal 2016 to 2023.
- Capital spending is light, $28.9 million to $62.9 million a year (2.4% to 5.4% of sales).
- Its products are small, repeat-purchase supplies, so labs tend to stay with the brands they know.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

At $72.46 a share the price is far above the analysis's value range of $25.69 to $26.87, and the expected return is only 1.8% to 3.3% a year, against 5.60% on a 30-year US Treasury bond. The price is pinned by Merck KGaA's agreed $73.00 cash purchase offer, not by the business's own earnings.

## What to watch

- Merck KGaA's $73.00 cash merger was approved by shareholders on 2026-09-23 but had not closed; while it is pending the company cannot buy back shares or pay dividends, and it owes a $230.5 million fee in specified cases.
- It is committed to buy the rest of Wilson Wolf for about $1 billion or more by the end of 2027, which would take net debt to roughly four years of the cash it produces.
- Its returns fell to 17.0% in fiscal 2025 after a held-for-sale write-down and a litigation charge, and its customers' research budgets can fluctuate.
