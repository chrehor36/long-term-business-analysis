## What it does

ONE Gas delivers natural gas through buried pipe networks to homes and businesses. It serves about 89% of gas-distribution customers in Oklahoma, 72% in Kansas and 13% in Texas. State regulators set the rates it may charge.

## Why it passed

- It holds the gas-distribution franchise in its areas, and a rival would have to duplicate the whole buried pipe network street by street.
- Regulators allow it set returns on equity: 9.40% in Oklahoma, 9.60% in Kansas and 9.80% in Texas.
- It earned 7.5% to 9.0% a year on its book equity across 2016 to 2025, a steady regulated return.
- Spending above depreciation, about $690 million a year against $246 million, grows the base on which regulators let it earn a return.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

A regulator caps what this utility may earn, so the price is far above what that capped return justifies. At $78.36 a share the price is above the analysis's value range of $66.59 to $68.70, and the expected return is only about 4.7% a year, against 5.31% on a 30-year US Treasury bond.

## What to watch

- Debt was about $3.4 billion in mid-2026, and $771 million of commercial paper must be rolled over within 270 days.
- Winter Storm Uri in February 2021 forced it to find about $2 billion for gas costs and raise $2.5 billion in new notes.
- All its business is in three adjoining states, and an event such as a major pipeline accident could hurt a single utility badly.
