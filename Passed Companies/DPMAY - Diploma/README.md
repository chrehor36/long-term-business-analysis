## What it does

Diploma is a UK group of specialist businesses that distribute low-cost but critical parts, such as cable, aerospace and specialty fasteners, interconnect products and seals, together with the technical service around them. Its Controls sector, the largest part, supplies more than half of five-year profit. It keeps growing by buying more businesses.

## Why it passed

- The parts are cheap compared with the value the distributor adds, so customers pay for service and stock availability rather than shopping on price.
- Its gross margin rose from 44.6% in 2022 to 46.7% in 2025, even through the 2021 to 2023 inflation, so it passed cost increases on to customers.
- Its operating margin held between 13.0% and 18.6% for ten years, including the 2020 downturn, when profit fell only 10%.
- The Controls sector earned returns that larger, well-funded distributors of cable, such as WESCO and Graybar, did not match over ten years.

## Why we are not buying at today's price

At 7,785p a share the market values the company at about £10.4 billion, and the cash it produces for owners is only about 1.5% of that. Even if it grew about a quarter a year for ten years, a buyer would earn only about 6%, and the analysis's value range is 1,150p to 1,652p.

## What to watch

- Growth depends on buying businesses, and the analysis doubts that the price of that bought growth will keep paying.
- Part of the recent jump in margins in Controls may be a passing aerospace shortage.
- Pay is tied to earnings per share growth, which acquisitions can supply, and it pays a dividend while raising new shares.
