Source notes for this folder
=============================

1977-2025 Letter.txt
  Official letters from berkshirehathaway.com (letters/{year}.html for
  1977-1997, letters/{year}ltr.pdf or equivalent for 1998-2025).
  2025 Letter.txt is Greg Abel's first letter as CEO (letters/gealetters.html).

1965-1976 Letter.txt
  Berkshire Hathaway did not publish these pre-1977 letters on its own
  website. These copies were kept from an earlier pull off a third-party
  archive (berkshire.memorex.ai) - see brk-reports.ps1 in this folder for
  that process. Treat these as unofficial/personal-study copies; everything
  from 1977 onward above is sourced directly from Berkshire's own site.

Corporate Genealogy.txt
  Also from the earlier berkshire.memorex.ai pull, not from
  berkshirehathaway.com.
